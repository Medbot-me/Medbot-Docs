# Patient Notes Workspace

The notes workspace is a full-page writing area attached to a patient's chart. It lists the notes filed for one patient and lets clinicians claim an existing note into the chart. Open it with **Notes** under the patient's name in the sidebar, or from **Notes** at `/notes`, where you first search for the patient.

> **Note:** The notes workspace is available only when your Medbot deployment has turned it on. If it is off, the **Notes** entry is missing and the page shows "not found".

## Who can do this

| Task | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open a patient's notes and read the list | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Claim an existing note into the chart | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Start a new note | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Remove or delete a note | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |

A Receptionist opens the workspace in read-only mode and does not see the buttons for changing notes.

## Finding a patient's notes

1. Select **Notes** in the sidebar. This opens a page that says "Search for a patient to view the notes claimed into their chart."
2. Type in **Search patients by name...** and select the patient. From a patient's chart, select **Notes** under their name to go straight there.

The workspace is laid out as a workbench with three areas:

- On the left, the **Notes** dock, with the tabs **Notes**, **Chart**, **Templates** and **Resources**. Only Clinic Admins, Doctors and Nurses see **Chart**.
- In the centre, the note you are writing.
- On the right, the **AI** dock, with the tabs **Chat** and **Agents**.

The **Notes** tab lists the patient's notes under **Search notes**. An empty chart says "No notes in this patient's chart yet." Type in **Search notes** to filter the list by title. Each row shows the title and when it was last updated.

### Previewing a note or encounter

Select the eye icon on a note (**Preview beside the open note**) to open it read-only in a second tab beside the note you are writing. Select **Open for editing** in the preview to switch to that note.

The **Chart** tab shows the patient, their allergies, problems and medications, and their encounters. Select a past encounter to preview its orders and latest note in the centre. Only one preview is open at a time.

> **Note:** Your deployment may also turn on a bottom dock with **Medications** and **Orders** tabs, for drafting orders and prescriptions and then signing them. It is off by default. Prescriptions drafted there are checked against the patient's allergies. See [Allergies & Medication Safety](/cms/allergies-and-medication-safety#allergy-alerts-when-prescribing).

## Claiming an existing note

A note you wrote elsewhere in Medbot can be filed into a patient's chart.

1. Select the link icon (**Claim an existing note**).
2. In **Claim a note into this patient's chart**, search under **Note** ("Search your notes by title...") and pick one of your own notes.
3. Optionally, fill in **Encounter id (optional)** to link the note to a visit. Leave it blank to link to the patient only.
4. Select **Claim**.

If claiming fails, the window shows the reason.

## Starting and removing notes

Select **New** to start a note. It is available only while the patient has an open encounter. Otherwise the button is disabled and its tooltip says "This patient has no open encounter to note against." See [Encounters & Clinical Notes](/cms/encounters).

Use the three-dot **Note actions** menu on a row to choose **Remove from chart** (for a claimed note, which keeps the note itself) or **Delete note**.

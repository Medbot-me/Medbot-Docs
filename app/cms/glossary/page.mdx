# Glossary

Terms used across the CMS documentation, each with one meaning. Where a term is a control on screen, the label is quoted as it is rendered.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization** | A clinic's account: its staff, locations, and patients. The screen spells it **Organisation**. A person's role is set per organization. |
| **Clinic location** | One physical site of an organization. Sessions, rooms, and the queue are scoped to one location at a time. |
| **Member** | A person's place in one organization in one role. Someone with two roles in the same organization, such as Doctor and Clinic Admin, has two memberships. |
| **Role** | Clinic Admin, Receptionist, Doctor, or Nurse, held per organization. A person can hold more than one role in an organization and works as one at a time, switching from the **Working as** menu. See [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions) and [Switching role](/cms/switching#switching-role). |
| **Session** | A clinic location's working day: which staff are on duty and which rooms are open. Opened from [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup). |
| **Shift** | One rostered block of work for one staff member. See [Rostering](/cms/rostering). |
| **Queue ticket** | The number a checked-in patient is called by. See [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue). |
| **Stage** | Where a checked-in patient is in the visit: Nurse, Doctor, Investigations, Dispensary, or Completed. |
| **Appointment** | A booked time slot for a patient. |
| **Booking request** | A patient's request from the portal that is not yet an appointment. |
| **Encounter** | The clinical record of one visit, started from the chart or the queue. See [Encounters & Clinical Notes](/cms/encounters). |
| **Chart** | Everything the CMS shows for one patient, split into sections. See [Patient Records & Consent](/cms/patient-records). |
| **Void** | Mark a record as made in error while keeping it for the history. Different from deleting it. |
| **Owed** | What a patient still has to pay on a visit: the bill's cash due, plus anything a payer refused or paid short, less the payments taken. Shown in the queue's **Take payment** dialog and on **Claims today**. See [Taking payment](/cms/reception-queue#taking-payment). |
| **Sensitive photo** | A clinical photo only doctors and nurses can open. Other roles see a locked tile. Turned on by hand or by a breast, genital or perineal body site. See [Clinical Photos](/cms/clinical-photos#sensitive-photos). |
| **Result explanation** | A plain-language explanation of one or more results for the patient, drafted with AI and approved by a doctor before it is sent to the patient or printed. See [Diagnostic Results](/cms/diagnostic-results#explaining-results-to-the-patient). |
| **Explanation library** | Approved wording, by test, that result explanations reuse as written. See [Diagnostic Results](/cms/diagnostic-results#the-explanation-library). |
| **Publish (roster)** | Make a draft week of shifts official. Only a Clinic Admin can publish. |
| **Finalize (attendance)** | Lock a day's attendance records. See [Attendance](/cms/attendance). |
| **Order set** | A saved group of orders a clinician can apply to an encounter at once. See [Orders & Order Sets](/cms/orders-and-order-sets). |
| **Formulary** | The clinic's list of medications it can prescribe. See [Formulary](/cms/formulary). |
| **Ingredient (RxNorm)** | An active ingredient of a formulary medication, as RxNorm names it. Allergy and duplicate checks match medications on their ingredients. See [Ingredients and ATC classes](/cms/formulary#ingredients-and-atc-classes). |
| **ATC class** | A group from the Anatomical Therapeutic Chemical classification, which sorts medicines by the body system they act on and how they work, for example J01CA, Penicillins with extended spectrum. See [Formulary](/cms/formulary#ingredients-and-atc-classes). |
| **Allergy group** | A family of related drugs, such as Penicillins or NSAIDs, recorded as one allergy so every drug in it is checked. See [Allergies & Medication Safety](/cms/allergies-and-medication-safety#adding-an-allergy). |
| **No known drug allergies** | A recorded statement that the patient was asked and has no drug allergies. Different from allergy status not recorded. |
| **Allergy alert** | A message shown when a prescribed drug matches a patient's allergy. **Stop:** alerts block signing until the order is amended or overridden; **Warning:** alerts only inform. See [Allergies & Medication Safety](/cms/allergies-and-medication-safety#allergy-alerts-when-prescribing). |
| **Override reason** | The prescriber's recorded reason for going ahead with a prescription despite a **Stop:** alert. It is shown on the signed prescription. |
| **Lot** | A batch of stock received into inventory, with its own quantity and expiry. See [Inventory & Stock](/cms/inventory-and-stock). |
| **Ping** | A short task or message sent to a staff member. See [Tasks & Staff Pings](/cms/tasks). |
| **Patient portal** | A separate, patient-facing app where a clinic's patients manage their care. See [Patient Portal](/cms/patient-portal). |
| **MCR, SNB** | Registration numbers for doctors (Medical Council) and nurses (Nursing Board), entered on the staff form. |
| **Caregiver** | A family member or carer who uses the patient portal for a patient with their own account, read-only or able to make changes. See [Caregiver access](/cms/patient-portal#caregiver-access). |
| **SmartCMS** | Synapxe's gateway to Singapore's national health systems. See [SmartCMS](/cms/smartcms). |
| **HE code** | A clinic location's MOH Health Establishment code, which SmartCMS identifies it by. See [Singapore Registration](/cms/smartcms/registration). |
| **CHAS** | The Community Health Assist Scheme. Medbot verifies a patient's CHAS card at check-in and claims CHAS subsidies from the bill. See [Claims Workflow](/cms/smartcms/claims). |
| **Medisave (CDMP)** | Paying part of a chronic disease bill from a patient's or family member's Medisave account, claimed from CPF. See [Claims Workflow](/cms/smartcms/claims#medisave). |
| **ICD-10-AM** | The diagnosis coding MOH requires on claims and reports. Coded on the visit's **Diagnoses (ICD-10-AM)** card. |
| **Claims queue** | Every SmartCMS claim the organisation has prepared, with its status. At **SmartCMS → Claims**. |
| **Claims today** | One clinic day's checked-in patients, with each bill, its claims and what the patient still owes. At **SmartCMS → Claims → Claims today**. See [Claims today](/cms/smartcms/claims#claims-today). |
| **Resubmission** | A corrected claim an admin sends after MHCP rejects one. The rejected claim shows **Rejected, resubmitted**. See [Resubmitting a rejected claim](/cms/smartcms/claims#resubmitting-a-rejected-claim). |
| **Claims agent** | The automated run, or AI agent, that prepares claims and reports under the organisation's agent policy. See [Claims Agent](/cms/smartcms/claims-agent). |
| **Entitlement** | Synapxe's approval for one clinic location to use one SmartCMS service. Recorded on [SmartCMS Settings](/cms/smartcms/settings). |
| **Held submission** | A SmartCMS claim or report waiting out a gateway outage, shown as **Waiting for SmartCMS to recover**. It is sent by itself when the gateway answers again. See [Gateway Outages & Deadlines](/cms/smartcms/outages). |
| **Nurse-led hold** | A scheme claim on a visit led by a nurse, held because it has no attending doctor's MCR, until a doctor takes part or a Clinic Admin submits it as nurse-led. See [Claims on a nurse-led visit](/cms/smartcms/claims#claims-on-a-nurse-led-visit). |
| **Status banner** | The amber (degraded) or red (major outage) strip at the top of every page when Medbot has a problem. See [System Status](/cms/system-status). |
| **Medbot CMS desktop app** | The installed app for clinic laptops that shows Medbot CMS while Medbot answers and the downtime workspace when it does not. See [Medbot CMS Desktop App](/cms/downtime/cms-desktop). |
| **Downtime workspace** | The offline part of the desktop app, for looking up charts and recording care during an outage. See [Working During an Outage](/cms/downtime/working-offline). |
| **Downtime laptop** | A computer enrolled to keep an encrypted copy of the charts for use during an outage, with a desk profile. Managed at **Administration → Downtime devices**. See [Downtime Laptops](/cms/downtime/devices). |
| **Desk profile** | What a downtime laptop holds and can record: **Consultation room**, **Reception** or **Dispensary**. See [Desk profiles](/cms/downtime/devices#desk-profiles). |
| **Offline passphrase** | What a person types to unlock a downtime laptop offline. Not their Medbot password. |
| **Downtime entry** | Vitals, an allergy, a note, a visit record or a walk-in recorded on a downtime laptop, signed by its author and uploaded when Medbot is back. |
| **Verify (downtime entry)** | The author confirming in the recovery inbox that an uploaded entry is an accurate record of care, within 72 hours. See [Recovery After an Outage](/cms/downtime/recovery). |
| **Break-glass guest** | A colleague let in on a downtime laptop by someone signed in, view only, for up to 12 hours. |
| **Outage** | Medbot's record of one period of downtime for the organisation. Ended by a doctor or Clinic Admin, and **reconciled** once everything it affected is dealt with. See [Outages](/cms/downtime/recovery#outages). |

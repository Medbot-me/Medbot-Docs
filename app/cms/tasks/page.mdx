# Tasks & Staff Pings

A ping is a short message from one staff member to another, like a walkie-talkie. Staff use pings to ask a colleague to come to a room, clarify an order, or call back. Your pings appear in the bell at the top of the screen and on the **Tasks** page, in the **CMS** tab at **Clinical → Tasks** (`/tasks`). On a phone, **Tasks** is on the CMS tab's bottom bar.

Care services have tasks of their own, such as approving a plan or answering a patient's question. Those are in the [Task Inbox](#the-task-inbox) on the **Home** tab, not here.

## Who can do this

| Task | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Send a ping | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Receive, acknowledge, and finish pings | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| See the pings you sent | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Only the person a ping was sent to can acknowledge it or mark it done. Nobody can read another person's pings.

## Sending a ping

There are two places to start:

- The **Ping staff** button at the top of a patient's chart. The ping links to that patient.
- The ping icon at the end of an order row in a patient's encounter. The ping names that order.

1. Select **Ping staff**. From an order row, select the ping icon (**Ping about** the order).
2. Under **Choose who to ping**, pick a person. The list shows their role and, if they are assigned to a room in today's open session, the room.
3. Type a message under **What do you need?**, or select a quick message: "Please come chaperone.", "Can you clarify this order?", "Patient would like to speak with you again.", or "Can we talk over a call?".
4. Select **Send**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/tasks/02-ping-dialog.png" alt="The Ping about a patient window with a staff member chosen, a quick message selected, and the Send button" />
_Sending a ping from a patient's chart_

You can ping any active Clinic Admin, doctor, nurse, or receptionist who can work at the current clinic location, except yourself. A message can be up to 500 characters. When you cannot send, you see:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Write a message first. | Type something. |
| Keep it under 500 characters. | Shorten the message. |
| That person is not available to ping. | Choose someone else. |

## Receiving a ping

A new ping shows as a red number on the bell. Medbot also shows a short notice and plays a quiet beep. When several arrive at once, it shows one notice such as "3 new pings". The list updates about every five seconds, so you do not need to refresh.

Select the bell to see your pings. Each shows the message, who sent it, the room it was sent to, and how long ago. If it is about a patient, select **Open** followed by the patient's name to go to the chart.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/tasks/03-ping-bell.png" alt="The Pings list opened from the bell, showing one ping with Acknowledge and Done buttons, next to the My tasks page" />
_A new ping in the bell and on the Tasks page_

Then choose one:

- **Acknowledge** keeps the ping on your list, marked **To-do**. Use it for "I have seen this and will do it".
- **Done** removes the ping from your list.

You can select **Done** straight away, without acknowledging.

## The Tasks page

**My tasks** lists your open and acknowledged pings, newest first. Under **Sent** you see the last 20 pings you sent and how far each has gone: **Waiting**, **Acknowledged**, or **Done**. When nothing is waiting, the page says "Nothing pending." or "Nothing sent yet."

At the bottom of the bell list, **Open Tasks as a drawer** or **Open Tasks as a page** changes what the **Tasks** menu item does for you. Your choice is remembered in your browser.

## The Task Inbox

The **Task Inbox** (`/services/inbox`) is the care services' list of work for you: "Tasks assigned to you directly, or unclaimed tasks assigned to a role you hold in a service of this organisation." Open it with **Tasks** in the **Home** tab sidebar, which shows how many are waiting. On a phone, **Tasks** is on the Home tab's bottom bar.

Every role can open it. Each task under **Pending** names its service and, when it is about one, the patient, and is marked **Overdue** once past its due date. Act on it from the row, for example **Complete**. With nothing waiting, it says "No pending tasks."

The tasks come from the services' own work, such as [referrals](/cms/services/referrals) and [pre-visit check-ins and escalations](/cms/services/pre-visit-and-escalations). Old Task Inbox links from Medbot web, including those in task emails, open this page.

If a task disappears while the inbox is open, for example because a [demo reset](/cms/services/demo-services#remove-or-reset-the-demo) removed it, acting on it shows the server's reason, and the inbox refreshes and drops it.

### Redirecting a task to a doctor

A nurse can pass a patient's task to a doctor when it needs a doctor's decision.

1. On the task, select **Redirect to doctor**.
2. Choose the **Doctor**. Medbot suggests one when the service's care team makes it clear: the patient's assigned doctor, else whoever is covering the service today, else the only doctor on the care team, and says why ("Suggested: …"). If more than one could take it, it says "More than one doctor could take this. Choose who should answer." With no doctor on the care team: "No doctor is on this service's care team. Add one to the care team to redirect tasks."
3. Say **Why does this need a doctor?**, for example "missed antagonist dose last night, lead follicle 16 mm. Needs a decision on timing."
4. Select **Redirect to doctor**.

The task moves to the doctor's Task Inbox with a **Redirected to you** badge. Others see "Redirected to &lt;doctor&gt; by &lt;nurse&gt;" and the reason. The doctor sees who sent it and why, the latest messages with the patient, the latest results, and **Open the plan context** and **Full message history**. They can:

- **Reply to the patient** and **Send reply**. The reply goes to the patient's portal thread (WhatsApp, SMS or email only tell them a message is waiting) and closes the task.
- **Hand back**, with a **Note for** the nurse, for example "in theatre until 3 pm; Dr Ng is covering", then **Hand back to** the nurse. The nurse sees "Handed back by &lt;doctor&gt;:" with the note, and can redirect it again.

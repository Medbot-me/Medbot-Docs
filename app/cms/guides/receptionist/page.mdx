# Receptionist Guide

This guide follows a receptionist's working day and links to the full page for each task. For who can do what, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

## Start of the day

After you sign in, the CMS opens on the **Patients** page (the [Workstation](/cms/services/workstation)) of the service you work in. If you work in several services, choose one on **Where are you working today?**, or **Open Org Level** to work the whole organisation's front desk. Change it later from the switcher at the top of the sidebar. See [Choosing & Switching](/cms/administration/switching#choosing-a-service).

1. Start each service's session for the day, and check who is on duty and in which room. See [the Session tab](/cms/services/workstation#the-session-tab).
2. Open the [Reception Queue](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue): **Patients** under the organisation's name in the Home tab, narrowed to one service if you like. This is your main screen for the day. A USB card scanner works anywhere on it.

## As patients arrive

1. Scan the patient's [clinic card](/cms/front-desk/clinic-card-check-in), or find their row and [check them in](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue#checking-a-patient-in). Patients who checked themselves in with the Medbot app or at the kiosk are already on the queue. A patient with no appointment can be registered on the spot; see [Patient Records & Consent](/cms/clinical-care/patient-records#registering-a-patient), then select [Add to queue](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue#adding-a-walk-in).
2. [Give the patient their number](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue#giving-the-patient-their-number) by printing the ticket, or tell them it and record that you did.
3. [Call the patient to a room](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue#calling-a-patient-to-a-room) when a room is ready, and [route them](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue) through the visit stages. If a called patient does not come, choose [**Did not come**](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue#when-a-called-patient-does-not-come): **Requeue** or **Move to Missed Queue**. When they return, use **Return to queue** in the Missed Queue; if they went home, record **Patient left**.
4. Generate the visit's invoice from [Billing](/cms/billing#generating-an-invoice).
5. [Take payment](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue#taking-payment) from the **Bill** column of the queue. The dialog starts at what the patient still owes; choose the method and select **Record payment**. A patient can pay in parts, and the row shows **✓ Paid** once the payments cover the bill. If a payer later refuses or pays short, it changes to an amber **Owes $X**; select it to take the balance. A payment entered wrongly is voided and entered again, never edited.
6. For a receipt, print the invoice from [Billing](/cms/billing#finding-and-reprinting-invoices).

## Booking and requests

1. [Book, edit, or cancel appointments](/cms/front-desk/appointments) for patients who call or drop in.
2. [Answer booking requests](/cms/front-desk/appointments#responding-to-portal-booking-requests) that patients send from the portal and from Find care, linking a new patient's record first.

## Throughout the day

- Keep an eye on the bell: [Notifications](/cms/notifications) holds your pings and the clinic inbox's patient messages. Read and reply to them in the [clinic inbox](/cms/clinical-care/messaging).
- [Invite patients to the portal](/cms/clinical-care/patient-portal) so they can manage their own care.
- File incoming [diagnostic results](/cms/clinical-care/diagnostic-results) and print or email [documents](/cms/clinical-care/documents-and-certificates) a patient asks for.
- [Photograph a paper report](/cms/clinical-care/clinical-photos#taking-photos-for-a-patient) a patient hands over with **Photo capture**, then [file it as a result](/cms/clinical-care/clinical-photos#filing-a-paper-report-as-a-result).

## Singapore clinics (SmartCMS)

- Check the patient's [CHAS badge](/cms/smartcms/claims#1-check-in-and-chas-verification) after check-in, and select **Verify now** if it is missing or stale.
- [Bill each visit once](/cms/smartcms/claims#3-bill-the-visit), choosing its CHAS, Screen for Life, subsidy and Medisave claims on **Generate invoice**.
- Work the [Claims queue](/cms/smartcms/claims#5-the-claims-queue): submit prepared claims, fix and **Re-prepare** held ones, and resolve **Notes for staff** the [claims agent](/cms/smartcms/claims-agent) left.
- [Void and correct a bill](/cms/billing#voiding-and-correcting-a-bill) that was issued wrongly.
- Open **Claims today** from the claims queue to see each checked-in patient's bill, claims, payments and what they still owe, including anything a payer refused or paid short. Take a balance for today's patients from the [queue](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue#taking-payment). See [SmartCMS Claims](/cms/smartcms/claims).

## Not in this role

A receptionist cannot start encounters, record clinical care, publish a roster, or manage staff, the formulary, or the catalog. A receptionist can draft a roster but not publish it. For the full boundary, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

# Receptionist Guide

This guide follows a receptionist's working day and links to the full page for each task. For who can do what, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

## Start of the day

1. Open today's session for your location, and mark which doctors and rooms are working. See [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup).
2. Open the [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue) and choose your location at the top. This is your main screen for the day.

## As patients arrive

1. Find the patient's row and [check them in](/cms/reception-queue#checking-a-patient-in). A patient with no appointment can be registered on the spot; see [Patient Records & Consent](/cms/patient-records#registering-a-patient).
2. [Call the patient to a room](/cms/reception-queue#calling-a-patient-to-a-room) when a room is ready, and [route them](/cms/reception-queue) through the visit stages.
3. Record payment with [Mark paid](/cms/reception-queue#routing-a-patient-and-marking-payment), and generate an invoice from [Billing](/cms/billing) when needed.

## Booking and requests

1. [Book, edit, or cancel appointments](/cms/appointments) for patients who call or drop in.
2. [Answer booking requests](/cms/appointments) that patients send from the portal.

## Throughout the day

- Read and reply to patient messages in the [clinic inbox](/cms/messaging).
- [Invite patients to the portal](/cms/patient-portal) so they can manage their own care.
- File incoming [diagnostic results](/cms/diagnostic-results) and print or email [documents](/cms/documents-and-certificates) a patient asks for.

## Not in this role

A receptionist cannot start encounters, record clinical care, publish a roster, or manage staff, the formulary, or the catalog. A receptionist can draft a roster but not publish it. For the full boundary, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

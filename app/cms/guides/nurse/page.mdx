# Nurse Guide

This guide follows a nurse's working day and links to the full page for each task. For who can do what, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

## Start of the day

- After you sign in, the CMS opens on **Patients**, the [Workstation](/cms/services/workstation) of the service you work in: today's queue, with the service's **Patient list**, **Appointments**, **Referrals** and **Session** as tabs. If you work in several services, choose one on **Where are you working today?**, or change it later from the switcher at the top of the sidebar. See [Choosing & Switching](/cms/administration/switching#choosing-a-service).
- Work the [Queue tab](/cms/services/workstation#the-queue-tab). Choose **My room**, and choose the step you run, such as **Running Vitals**, under **I am running**: patients waiting for it are marked **Waiting for you** and listed first. On a nurse-led visit you lead, you can also call the patient to a room and use **Did not come**; see [When a called patient does not come](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue#when-a-called-patient-does-not-come). Leave a [remark](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue#remarks) for the other staff where it helps.

## Per-patient work

1. When a patient is routed to you, they show as **Vitals now**. Open the patient and [record their vitals](/cms/clinical-care/vitals#recording-vitals).
2. Ask about drug allergies and [record them](/cms/clinical-care/allergies-and-medication-safety#adding-an-allergy) on the **Allergies** card, or select **No known drug allergies**. A doctor corrects or resolves them.
3. [Record vaccinations](/cms/clinical-care/vaccinations), drawing doses from stock where the service is set up.
4. Add a [progress note](/cms/clinical-care/encounters#writing-a-note) to the encounter when you need to record what you did.
5. [Receive or adjust stock](/cms/pharmacy/inventory-and-stock) when a delivery arrives or a count is off.
6. [Photograph a wound, rash or injury](/cms/clinical-care/clinical-photos#taking-photos-for-a-patient) from your phone with the camera button on the patient's chart, and tick the patient's consent.
7. Select **Explain to patient** on a result to start a plain-language explanation for the patient. A doctor approves it before the patient sees it. See [Diagnostic Results](/cms/clinical-care/diagnostic-results#explaining-results-to-the-patient).

## Nurse-led visits

A visit booked to you is nurse-led: no doctor is assigned, and you run it from start to finish. See [Nurse-led visits](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue#nurse-led-visits).

1. Select **Start nurse visit** on the row to open the patient's chart at [Encounters](/cms/clinical-care/encounters#encounters-and-the-queue).
2. Record vitals, vaccinations and a progress note as needed.
3. [Start an AI encounter](/cms/clinical-care/encounters#starting-an-ai-encounter) if the patient needs one, then select **Complete visit** to send the outcome to the patient.

Only you, a doctor or a Clinic Admin can close a visit you lead. If a doctor sees the patient, the visit becomes doctor-led and the doctor closes it. You cannot close another nurse's visit or a doctor-led one.

## Throughout the day

- [Read a patient's chart, vitals history, and notes](/cms/clinical-care/patient-records).
- [Verify a patient's identity](/cms/clinical-care/patient-records#verifying-a-patients-identity) against their NRIC, FIN card or passport when their account's name or date of birth is not verified.
- Compose a letter or memo from a patient's [Documents](/cms/clinical-care/documents-and-certificates#composing-a-letter-memo-or-referral).
- File incoming [diagnostic results](/cms/clinical-care/diagnostic-results).
- Send and answer [pings](/cms/clinical-care/tasks) with the rest of the team.

## Not in this role

A nurse cannot register patients, book appointments, sign off results, issue referrals or medical certificates, or manage staff, the formulary, or roster publishing. For the full boundary, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

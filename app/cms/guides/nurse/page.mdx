# Nurse Guide

This guide follows a nurse's working day and links to the full page for each task. For who can do what, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

## Start of the day

- Open **Today's Queue** from the [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue#what-the-doctor-and-nurse-see). A nurse sees every patient at the location.

## Per-patient work

1. When a patient is routed to you, they show as **Vitals now**. Open the patient and [record their vitals](/cms/vitals#recording-vitals).
2. [Record vaccinations](/cms/vaccinations), drawing doses from stock where the service is set up.
3. Add a [progress note](/cms/encounters#writing-a-note) to the encounter when you need to record what you did.
4. [Receive or adjust stock](/cms/inventory-and-stock) when a delivery arrives or a count is off.

## Throughout the day

- [Read a patient's chart, vitals history, and notes](/cms/patient-records).
- Compose a letter or memo from a patient's [Documents](/cms/documents-and-certificates#composing-a-letter-memo-or-referral).
- File incoming [diagnostic results](/cms/diagnostic-results).
- Send and answer [pings](/cms/tasks) with the rest of the team.

## Not in this role

A nurse cannot register patients, book appointments, sign off results, issue referrals or medical certificates, or manage staff, the formulary, or roster publishing. For the full boundary, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

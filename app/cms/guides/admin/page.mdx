# Admin Guide

This guide follows a Clinic Admin's work and links to the full page for each task. A Clinic Admin can act as a backstop for most workflows another role owns, so this guide covers the admin-only setup and oversight. For who can do what, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

## Setting up the clinic

1. [Add staff members](/cms/staff) and set each person's role and registration number.
2. Add clinic locations, rooms, and public displays in [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup) and [Patient-Facing Displays](/cms/display-boards).
3. Build the [billable catalog](/cms/billable-catalog), [services and consumables](/cms/services-and-consumables), and the [formulary](/cms/formulary).

### Singapore clinics (SmartCMS)

1. Choose the [SmartCMS modules](/cms/smartcms/modules) and enter the [Singapore registration](/cms/smartcms/registration) details.
2. Once Synapxe approves each location, switch its services on, check accreditation and set the agent policy in [SmartCMS Settings](/cms/smartcms/settings).

## Running the week

1. Draft and [publish the roster](/cms/rostering#publishing-a-week-clinic-admin-only). Only a Clinic Admin can publish.
2. Review and correct [attendance](/cms/attendance), then finalize and export timesheets.
3. Manage the [results review rota](/cms/diagnostic-results) so incoming results reach a doctor.

## Oversight during the day

- Step into any front-desk or clinical task as a backstop: [reception queue](/cms/reception-queue), [appointments](/cms/appointments), [vitals](/cms/vitals), and [documents](/cms/documents-and-certificates).
- Manage [inventory and stock](/cms/inventory-and-stock), including reorder thresholds.

## Not in this role

A Clinic Admin cannot sign off a result, arrange a session, or complete a visit; those belong to a doctor. A Clinic Admin cannot reopen or correct their own attendance record. Platform administration is not a clinic role at all; see [Platform Administration](/cms/platform-administration). For the full boundary, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

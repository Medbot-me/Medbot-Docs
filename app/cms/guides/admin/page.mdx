# Admin Guide

This guide follows a Clinic Admin's work and links to the full page for each task. A Clinic Admin can act as a backstop for most workflows another role owns, so this guide covers the admin-only setup and oversight. For who can do what, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

## Setting up the clinic

1. [Add staff members](/cms/staff) and set each person's role and registration number. To give an existing member a second role (for example, a doctor who also helps run the clinic), use **Add another role** on their row.
2. Add clinic locations, rooms, and public displays in [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup) and [Patient-Facing Displays](/cms/display-boards).
3. Build the [billable catalog](/cms/billable-catalog), [services and consumables](/cms/services-and-consumables), and the [formulary](/cms/formulary).

### Singapore clinics (SmartCMS)

1. Choose the [SmartCMS modules](/cms/smartcms/modules) and enter the [Singapore registration](/cms/smartcms/registration) details.
2. Once Synapxe approves each location, switch its services on, check accreditation and set the agent policy in [SmartCMS Settings](/cms/smartcms/settings).

### Preparing for outages

1. Enroll a downtime laptop at each desk that needs one, with the right desk profile, and set the replica window, grant lifetime and automatic downtime wait in [Downtime Laptops](/cms/downtime/devices). Enroll yourself and at least one other person on every laptop.
2. Make sure staff sign in on the laptops while they are online, so they can unlock them offline. See [Before an outage](/cms/downtime/working-offline#before-an-outage).

## Running the week

1. Draft and [publish the roster](/cms/rostering#publishing-a-week-clinic-admin-only). Only a Clinic Admin can publish.
2. Review and correct [attendance](/cms/attendance), then finalize and export timesheets.
3. Manage the [results review rota](/cms/diagnostic-results) so incoming results reach a doctor.

## Oversight during the day

- Step into any front-desk or clinical task as a backstop: [reception queue](/cms/reception-queue), [appointments](/cms/appointments), [vitals](/cms/vitals), and [documents](/cms/documents-and-certificates).
- Manage [inventory and stock](/cms/inventory-and-stock), including reorder thresholds.
- Review how allergy alerts are answered in the [Medication safety report](/cms/allergies-and-medication-safety#medication-safety-report) (**Pharmacy → Medication safety**).
- When the status banner shows a problem, check **Administration → System status** for what is down, the SmartCMS backlog, the downtime laptops and entries awaiting verification. See [System Status](/cms/system-status).
- Decide claims held for a [nurse-led visit](/cms/smartcms/claims#claims-on-a-nurse-led-visit) (Singapore).

## After an outage

1. End the outage once every laptop has uploaded, then work through [what it affected](/cms/downtime/recovery#what-an-outage-affected) until it is reconciled.
2. Verify or withdraw entries for absent authors, and review entries flagged for authorization that you did not write. See [Recovery After an Outage](/cms/downtime/recovery).
3. Print the [outage report](/cms/downtime/recovery#the-outage-report) for the clinic's records.

## Not in this role

A Clinic Admin cannot sign off a result, arrange a session, or complete a visit; those belong to a doctor. A Clinic Admin cannot reopen or correct their own attendance record. Platform administration is not a clinic role at all; see [Platform Administration](/cms/platform-administration). A Clinic Admin can see a patient's allergies but cannot record or change them. A Clinic Admin does not issue medical certificates; an admin who is also a doctor switches to their Doctor role to issue one. For the full boundary, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

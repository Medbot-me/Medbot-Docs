# Admin Guide

This guide follows a Clinic Admin's work and links to the full page for each task. A Clinic Admin can act as a backstop for most workflows another role owns, so this guide covers the admin-only setup and oversight. For who can do what, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

## Your home page

The CMS opens on the **Patients** page (the Workstation) of the service you are working in, or on the organisation's **Patients** page at Org Level. Change service, organisation or role from the switcher at the top of the sidebar. See [Choosing & Switching](/cms/administration/switching).

## Setting up the clinic

If you are creating the organisation, its setup steps let you fill in its public profile, set up the first service from a template and invite your colleagues or load them from a CSV file. See [Create an Organisation](/cms/administration/create-organisation#after-creating).

1. [Add staff members](/cms/administration/staff) and set each person's role and registration number, or profession for an Allied health professional. To give an existing member a second role (for example, a doctor who also helps run the clinic), use **Add another role** on their row. To let a nurse sign prescriptions, [authorise them to prescribe](/cms/administration/staff#authorising-a-nurse-to-prescribe).
2. Add clinic locations and their rooms, and choose the default clinic, under **Clinic locations** in [Organisation Settings](/cms/administration/settings#clinic-locations). Add public displays on a service's Session tab: see [Patient-Facing Displays](/cms/front-desk/display-boards).
3. Fill in the organisation's [public profile](/cms/administration/settings#public-profile): what patients see in Find care, and whether they can book there. Choose when patients may [check in with one tap](/cms/administration/settings#patient-check-in).
4. Build the [billable catalog](/cms/billing/billable-catalog), [services and consumables](/cms/billing/services-and-consumables), and the [formulary](/cms/pharmacy/formulary). Publish [dosing rules](/cms/pharmacy/dosing-rules) for any oral liquid the clinic doses by weight.
5. Under **Queue** in [Organisation Settings](/cms/administration/settings#queue), set how many places a requeued patient moves back and how long queue remarks are kept.

### Singapore clinics (SmartCMS)

1. Choose the [SmartCMS modules](/cms/smartcms/modules) and enter the [Singapore registration](/cms/smartcms/registration) details.
2. Once Synapxe approves each location, switch its services on, check accreditation and set the agent policy in [SmartCMS Settings](/cms/smartcms/settings).

### Preparing for outages

1. Enroll a downtime laptop at each desk that needs one, with the right desk profile, and set the replica window, grant lifetime and automatic downtime wait in [Downtime Laptops](/cms/downtime/devices). Enroll yourself and at least one other person on every laptop.
2. Make sure staff sign in on the laptops while they are online, so they can unlock them offline. See [Before an outage](/cms/downtime/working-offline#before-an-outage).

## Running the week

1. Draft and [publish the roster](/cms/front-desk/rostering#publishing-a-week-clinic-admin-only). Only a Clinic Admin can publish.
2. Approve or reject staff [time off](/cms/front-desk/rostering#time-off), and fix what the roster's **Issues** tab lists.
3. Review and correct [attendance](/cms/front-desk/attendance), then finalize and export timesheets.
4. Manage the [results review rota](/cms/clinical-care/diagnostic-results) so incoming results reach a doctor.

## Oversight during the day

- Place the booking requests patients sent without a service, on the **Needs a service** tab of [booking requests](/cms/front-desk/appointments#requests-that-need-a-service). You are emailed when one arrives, and it shows in [Notifications](/cms/notifications).
- Step into any front-desk or clinical task as a backstop: [reception queue](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue), [appointments](/cms/front-desk/appointments), [vitals](/cms/clinical-care/vitals), and [documents](/cms/clinical-care/documents-and-certificates).
- Manage [inventory and stock](/cms/pharmacy/inventory-and-stock), including reorder thresholds.
- Read the [dose overrides log](/cms/pharmacy/dosing-rules#the-dose-overrides-log) (**Pharmacy → Dose overrides**).
- Review how allergy alerts are answered in the [Medication safety report](/cms/clinical-care/allergies-and-medication-safety#medication-safety-report) (**Pharmacy → Medication safety**).
- When the status banner shows a problem, open **System status** from **Downtime and status** in [Organisation Settings](/cms/administration/settings) for what is down, the SmartCMS backlog, the downtime laptops and entries awaiting verification. See [System Status](/cms/downtime/system-status).
- Decide claims held for a [nurse-led visit](/cms/smartcms/claims#claims-on-a-nurse-led-visit) (Singapore).

## Seeing what your team sees

To check what a Doctor, Nurse or Receptionist opens on and sees in the sidebar, [preview their role](/cms/administration/switching#previewing-another-role) from the **Role** column of the sidebar switcher, and select **Back to admin** when done. A preview changes navigation only: you keep your own access, and anything you change is recorded as you.

## After an outage

1. End the outage once every laptop has uploaded, then work through [what it affected](/cms/downtime/recovery#what-an-outage-affected) until it is reconciled.
2. Verify or withdraw entries for absent authors, and review entries flagged for authorization that you did not write. See [Recovery After an Outage](/cms/downtime/recovery).
3. Print the [outage report](/cms/downtime/recovery#the-outage-report) for the clinic's records.

## Not in this role

A Clinic Admin cannot sign off a result, arrange a session, or complete a visit; those belong to a doctor. A Clinic Admin cannot reopen or correct their own attendance record. Platform administration is not a clinic role at all; see [Platform Administration](/cms/administration/platform-administration). A Clinic Admin can see a patient's allergies but cannot record or change them. A Clinic Admin does not issue medical certificates; an admin who is also a doctor switches to their Doctor role to issue one. Previewing the Doctor view is not enough: it never lets you act as a doctor. For the full boundary, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

# Doctor Guide

This guide follows a doctor's working day and links to the full page for each task. For who can do what, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

## Start of the day

- Open **Today's Patients** from the [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue#what-the-doctor-and-nurse-see). A doctor sees only the patients assigned to them.

## Seeing a patient

1. Select **Start consultation** on the patient's row to open their chart at [Encounters](/cms/encounters#starting-an-encounter).
2. Review [vitals](/cms/vitals) and the chart, then write a [consultation note](/cms/encounters#writing-a-note).
3. Place [orders or apply an order set](/cms/orders-and-order-sets) for the encounter.
4. [Sign off diagnostic results](/cms/diagnostic-results#signing-off-a-result) assigned to you, and reassign or withdraw one when needed.

## Finishing the visit

1. [Arrange a session](/cms/reception-queue#arranging-a-session-and-completing-the-visit) for the patient if they need financial counselling or health education.
2. [Complete the visit](/cms/reception-queue#arranging-a-session-and-completing-the-visit) to send the outcome to the patient.
3. Compose a [referral or a medical certificate](/cms/documents-and-certificates) where the patient needs one.

## Throughout the day

- Send a message from a patient's chart, or send and answer [pings](/cms/tasks).
- Look up a medication in the [formulary](/cms/formulary).

## Not in this role

A doctor cannot register patients, book appointments, operate the queue, or manage staff, sessions, the formulary, the results rota, or roster publishing. For the full boundary, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

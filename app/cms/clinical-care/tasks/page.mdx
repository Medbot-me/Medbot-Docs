# Staff Pings

A ping is a short message from one staff member to another, like a walkie-talkie. Staff use pings to ask a colleague to come to a room, clarify an order, or call back. Your pings appear in the header bell and in [Notifications](/cms/clinical-care/notifications), with the care services' tasks and patient messages.

> **Tasks moved to Notifications.** The old **Tasks** page (`/tasks`) and the **Task Inbox** (`/services/inbox`) are now part of [Notifications](/cms/clinical-care/notifications). Their old links redirect there.

## Who can do this

| Task | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Send a ping | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Receive, acknowledge, and finish pings | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| See the pings you sent | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Only the person a ping was sent to can acknowledge it or mark it done. Nobody can read another person's pings.

## Sending a ping

There are four places to start:

- **Ping staff** on [Notifications](/cms/clinical-care/notifications). The ping is not about a patient.
- **Ping staff** on a service's notifications. The ping is filed under that service.
- The **Ping staff** button at the top of a patient's chart. The ping links to that patient.
- The ping icon at the end of an order row in a patient's encounter. The ping names that order.

1. Select **Ping staff**. From an order row, select the ping icon (**Ping about** the order).
2. Under **To**, choose a person ("Choose who to ping"). The list shows their role and, if they are assigned to a room in today's open session, the room.
3. Under **Show under**, choose where the ping is filed: **General** ("The organisation as a whole, not one service.") or one of the services you can open ("It shows in that service's notifications, and under All for the whole organisation."). From a service's notifications, that service is already chosen.
4. Type a **Message** ("What do you need?"), or select a quick message: "Please come chaperone.", "Can you clarify this order?", "Patient would like to speak with you again.", or "Can we talk over a call?".
5. Select **Send**.

You can ping any active Clinic Admin, doctor, nurse, or receptionist who can work at the current clinic location, except yourself. A message can be up to 500 characters. When you cannot send, you see:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Write a message first. | Type something. |
| Keep it under 500 characters. | Shorten the message. |
| That person is not available to ping. | Choose someone else. |
| You cannot file a ping under that service. | Choose another service under **Show under**, or **General**. |

## Receiving a ping

A new ping adds to the red number on the bell. Medbot also shows a short notice and plays a quiet beep. When several arrive at once, it shows one notice such as "3 new pings". Pings update about every five seconds, so you do not need to refresh.

Select the bell, or open [Notifications](/cms/clinical-care/notifications), to see your pings. Each shows the message, who sent it, the room it was sent to, and how long ago. If it is about a patient, select **Open** followed by the patient's name to go to the chart. A ping filed under a service also shows in that service's notifications.

Then choose one:

- **Acknowledge** keeps the ping on your list, marked **To-do**. Use it for "I have seen this and will do it".
- **Done** removes the ping from your list.

You can select **Done** straight away, without acknowledging.

## Pings you sent

On [Notifications](/cms/clinical-care/notifications), **Sent** lists the pings you sent and how far each has gone: **Waiting**, **Acknowledged**, or **Done**. When you have sent none, it says "Nothing sent yet."

## The Task Inbox

The care services' tasks, which used to be in the **Task Inbox** (`/services/inbox`), are now in [Notifications](/cms/clinical-care/notifications), on the organisation page and on each service's own notifications. Old Task Inbox links, including those in task emails, open Notifications. Redirecting a task to a doctor is described in [Redirecting a task to a doctor](/cms/clinical-care/notifications#redirecting-a-task-to-a-doctor).

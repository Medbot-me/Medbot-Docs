# Patient Records & Consent

Patient records are the list of everyone registered at your clinic and each person's chart. At Org Level, the list is the **Patient list** tab of **Patients** in the **Home** tab (`/patients/lists`; the old `/patients` address opens it), and you can find a patient from **Search** (⌘K). A chart opens in the **Home** tab too. Every role can open a chart: Clinic Admins, receptionists, doctors, nurses, patient service associates and allied health professionals. Front desk staff register patients.

## Who can do this

| Task | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See the patient list and open a chart | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Register a patient | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Invite a patient to self-register | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Deactivate a patient | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Edit contact details | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Record or change communication consent | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Add, change or remove a problem on the problem list | No | No | Yes | No |
| Reveal a masked NRIC, FIN or passport number | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Verify a patient's identity | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |

Patient service associates and allied health professionals also open charts and reveal identity documents. A patient service associate can verify identity too. Sections such as vitals, coverage and medications are shown to them only where their role may read them.

Doctors and nurses who open `/patients/new` or `/patients/invite` see the message "This action requires one of the following roles: admin, receptionist."

## Finding a patient

The list shows each patient's name, contact details, birth date, gender, address and status. Under each name is the masked NRIC, FIN or passport number (for example `S****345D`), so you can tell same-named patients apart; on a narrow screen their age and sex show there too.

The search box ("Search name, NRIC/FIN, phone or DOB") takes any of these, in any combination, and every part must match:

- a name, or part of one, in any order
- a full NRIC or FIN
- the last four characters of an NRIC or FIN, typed or as shown masked (`345D` or `S****345D`)
- a phone number, or at least four digits of one
- a date of birth, day first (`14/03/1992`) or as `1992-03-14`

For example "Tan Mei Lin 345D" or "14/03/1992 Tan". The last four characters of an NRIC or FIN are not enough on their own: add the name, phone number or date of birth, and if too many patients match, add more of the name or the date of birth. The page says so when that happens. The same search is used by the appointment booking and notes workspace patient pickers. Email addresses are not searched.

Use the status filter to show **Active**, **Inactive** or **Deceased** patients. The newest updates appear first, 20 patients per page.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/patient-records/01-patients-list.png" alt="The Patients page with the name search box, the status filter and a table of patients" />
_The Patients page_

Patients with no phone or email on file carry a **No contact on file** badge.

The **Latest AI encounter** column shows each patient's most recent [AI encounter](/cms/clinical-care/encounters#ai-encounters-across-services), or **None**. A row's actions menu starts a [consult or an AI encounter](/cms/clinical-care/encounters#starting-a-consult-from-the-patient-list) for that patient.

A **Demo** badge beside a name marks a demo patient: a made-up record created for a product demo, not a real person. The same **Demo** chip shows in the patient's header, and every chart page of a demo patient starts with a strip saying **Demo patient**, which explains the record is synthetic and is removed from the service's Demo panel. Demo patients behave like any other patient while you work with them. Nothing in the staff app creates or deletes them.

## Registering a patient

1. Select **Add Patient**.
2. Choose the **Identifier type**: **NRIC**, **FIN** or **Passport**, then enter the number. The form says **Valid** when the number is acceptable, or explains why not.
3. Enter **Given name**, **Family name** and **Date of birth**. These are required. **Sex** defaults to **Unknown**.
4. Optionally add a phone number, an email address, the **Preferred contact channel** (**Phone** or **Email**), communication consent and an address.
5. In a Singapore organisation that subscribes to SmartCMS, the form also asks for **Race**, **Residential status**, **Nationality** and **Country of birth**. Race and nationality are searchable lists with the common choices first: race shows **Main groups**, then **Detailed groups**, then **Other races** A to Z, and nationality shows **Singapore**, then **Southeast and East Asia**, then **Other nationalities** A to Z. Every code stays selectable, and the same lists are used when you edit a patient's demographics from the chart.
6. Select **Register patient**. The new patient's chart opens.

In a [physician family organisation](/cms/administration/physician-family-organisation#patients-yourself-and-family), the button is **Register yourself or family**, the form also asks **Who this patient is to you**, and **Invite to self-register** is not offered.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/patient-records/02-register-patient-form.png" alt="The Register patient form with identifier type, names, date of birth, contact details, consent grid and address" />
_The Register patient form_

Rules the form enforces:

- The identifier must match the selected type. A wrong check digit shows "Identifier doesn't match the selected type (NRIC)." and keeps **Register patient** disabled.
- A patient with the same identifier can exist only once per clinic. Otherwise you see "A patient with this identifier already exists in this clinic," and the message notes that the patient may be archived and points you to the clinic's medical record system to view or restore them. Names are not compared. Search by name first (including the **Inactive** filter) to avoid a second record for the same person. If a matching patient is inactive, restore the existing record rather than registering a new one.
- A Singapore mobile number has 8 digits and starts with 8 or 9. Other numbers start with a plus sign and have 8 to 15 digits. A number with seven or more zeros in a row is refused.
- If consent could not be saved, the patient is still registered. A warning tells you to record consent again from the chart.

_The duplicate identifier message_

## The patient chart

A chart opens in the **Home** tab, with the patient as a card at the top of the sidebar listing the chart's sections: **Overview**, **Problem list**, **Medications**, **Accounts**, **Encounters**, **AI encounters**, **Notes**, **Results**, **Photos**, **Vaccinations**, **Documents** and **Messages**, and for some roles **Shared by patient** and **Audit log**. **Problem list** and **Medications** are for clinical staff: see [The problem list](#the-problem-list) and [Prescribing & Medications](/cms/clinical-care/prescribing). **AI encounters** lists the patient's [AI encounters across services](/cms/clinical-care/encounters#ai-encounters-across-services). **Notes** appears only when your deployment has turned the [notes workspace](/cms/clinical-care/patient-notes) on. **Photos** holds [clinical photos](/cms/clinical-care/clinical-photos). **Accounts** is visible to Clinic Admins and Receptionists only. **Audit log** is visible to Clinic Admins only (see [Audit log](#audit-log)). Select the cross on the card (**Close patient**) to leave the chart. On a phone the sections are a row of tabs under the patient's name.

### The patient workspace

**Overview** (`/patients/<id>`) opens the patient workspace:

- **The header** names the patient, with their sex, age, birth date, MRN and phone number where recorded, the identity document (masked, see [National ID numbers on the chart](#national-id-numbers-on-the-chart)), the patient's status when it is not active, up to two allergy chips such as "Allergy: Penicillin", and **Encounter in progress** with a link when one is under way. Its buttons are **Portal invite**, **New note**, **Start Encounter**, **Ping staff** (a [staff ping](/cms/clinical-care/tasks)) and, for the front desk and nurses, **Verify identity** (see [Verifying a patient's identity](#verifying-a-patients-identity)).
- **The left rail** holds the **Patient** section, open by default: the patient's allergies, problems, medications, results and earlier encounters, then **Vitals** (the latest of each measure), **Vaccinations**, **Documents**, **Photos** and **Contact and consent**, each expanding in place. A section is shown only to the roles its chart page admits, and one that fails to load says so. Below it are the patient's **Services**, each opening the patient's page in that service, and **More**, linking to what the Patient section does not show: the problem list, accounts, records the patient shared and the audit log, each for the roles its page admits. A link with `?section=vitals` (or vaccinations, documents, photos, contact) opens the workspace with that section expanded.
- **The centre** shows the chart Overview described below.

The chart's own section pages stay for recording and editing, and each section in the rail links to its page. On the other sections the header leads with the section's name, with the patient's name and details beside it. Each section's own buttons sit at the top right; on a phone they are a bar along the bottom of the screen, in place of the app's bottom bar.

| Button | What it does | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Portal invite** | Makes a one-time link that, once the patient signs in to the patient app, links their account to this chart. See [Patient Portal](/cms/clinical-care/patient-portal). | Everyone |
| **New note** | Opens the [notes workspace](/cms/clinical-care/patient-notes). | Clinic Admins, Doctors and Nurses |
| **Start Encounter** | Starts an encounter in the patient's service (see below), or a visit for a patient in no service. See [Encounters & Clinical Notes](/cms/clinical-care/encounters#starting-an-encounter). | Clinic Admins, Doctors, Nurses and Allied health professionals, while no encounter is in progress |

**Start Encounter** belongs to a service. With one service it starts there straight away; with several it asks which (**Service**), listing only the patient's services that you may open. If the patient waits in a service's queue today, the button reads **Start consult** and opens that visit's consult instead of a second encounter. A patient in no service keeps the chart's own encounter dialog.

### The Overview

A **Services** card lists the patient's services, each with **Open**. **Allergies** comes next, across the full width. Below it are two columns (one on a narrow screen):

| Left column | Right column |
| --- | --- |
| **Problem list**, **Medications**, **Vitals**, and in Singapore **Diagnoses (ICD-10-AM)** | **Recent results**, **Clinical notes**, **Contact**, **Communication consent**, **Demographics and identifiers** |

| **Patient-reported intake** | Answers from a self-registration, only for patients who registered themselves. Shown under **Allergies** |
| **Problem list** and **Medications** | The patient's problems, with a count of active ones (see [The problem list](#the-problem-list)), and their medicines, with a filter between active and all. Shown to doctors, nurses, Clinic Admins and clinical staff in the other roles. Each medicine has a menu to amend, stop or repeat it, which opens the [Medications page](/cms/clinical-care/prescribing) with the form filled in, where your deployment lets you write orders. | **Recent results** | The patient's three latest results, each with when it was received and **Awaiting review** or who reviewed it. Select one to open it on **Results**, or **All results**. See [Diagnostic Results](/cms/clinical-care/diagnostic-results) |
| **Clinical notes** | See [Encounters & Clinical Notes](/cms/clinical-care/encounters#clinical-notes) |
| **Contact** | Phone and email with a **Preferred** badge, and the addresses on file, plus **Caregivers**, **Messaging numbers** and **Edit contact**. See [Caregiver access](/cms/clinical-care/patient-portal#caregiver-access) |
| **Communication consent** | Consent by purpose and channel, plus **Edit consent** |
| **Demographics and identifiers** | Birth date, gender, and marital status, preferred language and date of death when recorded, the masked **Identity document** when the patient has an NRIC, FIN or passport, then the clinic's own identifiers, such as the MRN, in full |

**Messaging numbers** shows the numbers the patient's care services message them on, their own and any guardian's.

## The problem list

The **Problem list** section of a chart (`/patients/<id>/problems`) is the clinic's list of the patient's problems. It stays on this clinic's chart for the patient's future visits.

- **Active** and **Past** problems. Each shows its name, its code (for example "ICD-10-AM J44.9 · Since 2019"), any comment, and who added or confirmed it and when.
- **Diagnosed at visits**: the diagnoses coded at this clinic's visits, one row each, marked **On problem list** when they are already there. A doctor can **Add &lt;diagnosis&gt; to the problem list**.

Doctors add, edit and remove problems; the other clinical roles read the list. The notes workspace's **Chart** tab and the consult screen's Patient section show the same list.

To add a problem, select **Add problem**:

1. Search **Diagnosis code (ICD-10-AM)** ("Code or name, e.g. J44 or asthma") and choose a match, or describe the problem in your own words under **Problem, in your words**. A code not on MOH's lists is offered as **Not on MOH's lists** ("Use this code and name the problem."), and one on MOH's lists with no title held as **On MOH's lists, no title held**.
2. Check the **Name on the chart** (the code's title unless you change it).
3. Choose the **Status**: **Active**, **Inactive** or **Resolved**.
4. Optionally enter **Started (optional)** and, for a problem that is not active, **Resolved (optional)** or **Inactive since (optional)**: a year, with or without a month. A date first recorded as free text (such as "since childhood") stays unless you enter a year.
5. Optionally add a **Comment (optional)**.
6. Select **Add to problem list**.

To change a problem, open its menu (**Actions for &lt;problem&gt;**) and select **Edit or mark resolved** (**Edit or reactivate** for a past problem), then **Save changes**. Changes are kept in the record's history; saving a different code replaces the old one. If someone else changed the problem since you opened it, the save is refused ("This problem was changed by someone else since you opened it.").

**Remove (entered in error)** asks **What was wrong**, for example "Recorded on the wrong patient", then **Remove**. Use it only for a problem that should never have been recorded; mark a problem that has gone as **Resolved** instead.

A condition the patient reported and shared with the clinic joins the list once a doctor codes or confirms it on **Shared by patient**: see [Reported conditions](/cms/clinical-care/patient-concerns#reported-conditions).

## National ID numbers on the chart

A patient's NRIC, FIN or passport number is masked everywhere on the chart, for example `S••••345D`: in the header, the identity line and the Overview's **Demographics and identifiers** card. The patient list, the pickers and search show the same masked value. A number shorter than an NRIC is masked whole.

To see it, click the masked value or the eye button. One reveal shows the identity documents in all three places, and is written to the patient's audit log. Hiding and showing it again adds no new entry. Opening another patient, or leaving the chart, masks it again.

Every role that opens a chart can reveal: Clinic Admins, receptionists, doctors, nurses, patient service associates and allied health professionals, who confirm a patient's identity before a procedure, a test or a referral. The MRN and the clinic's other identifiers are never masked.

A medical certificate downloaded or printed from **Documents** names the patient by NRIC or FIN, else passport. See [Documents & Certificates](/cms/clinical-care/documents-and-certificates).

## Verifying a patient's identity

Patients edit their own name and date of birth in their Medbot account. Until someone checks them against an identity document, they are shown as not verified. Identity change requests are retired.

For a patient whose chart is linked to a Medbot account, Clinic Admins, receptionists, patient service associates and nurses see **Verify identity** in the workspace header (**Identity verified** once done):

1. Check the patient's original NRIC, FIN card or passport against **Their Medbot account** (shown beside **This chart**). If the chart differs from their account, verifying confirms the account; correct the chart separately.
2. Choose the **Document**.
3. Tick **The name matches the document**, **The date of birth matches the document** and **I kept no copy of the document**.
4. Select **Verify identity**.

Scanning a patient's clinic card at the desk also verifies them. A Medbot platform superadmin can verify a patient from the admin panel: see [Platform Administration](/cms/administration/platform-administration#identity-verification).

## Editing contact details

1. On **Overview**, select **Edit contact**.
2. Change **Phone**, **Email** or **Preferred channel**. Clearing a field removes that entry.
3. Select **Save contact**.

Invalid numbers are refused with "Invalid phone number." or "Invalid email address." If a colleague saved the chart after you opened it, you see "This record changed since you opened it. Please refresh and try again." Refresh the chart and repeat the change.

## Communication consent

Consent controls how the clinic may contact a patient. It is deny by default: the clinic may contact a patient only for the purpose and channel combinations ticked. Purposes are **Appointment reminders**, **Clinical recalls**, **Clinical updates**, **Documents sent to the patient**, **Documents sent to a third party** and **Health promotion**. Channels are **Email**, **SMS** and **WhatsApp**. You can record SMS and WhatsApp consent even when the clinic does not send on those channels yet.

1. On **Overview**, select **Edit consent**.
2. Tick the purpose and channel combinations the patient agreed to.
3. Select **Save consent**. Saving replaces the whole set, so untick anything the patient withdrew.

A purpose with nothing ticked shows **No consent**. Treat any combination not shown as not permitted.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/patient-records/05-edit-consent-dialog.png" alt="The Communication consent dialog with a grid of purposes and channels" />
_The consent grid_

Saving needs your account to be linked to a clinical profile. Otherwise you see "Your account is not linked to a practitioner profile. Contact an administrator." Ask a Clinic Admin for help. If the consent changed after you opened the chart, you see the same "record changed" message as for contact details.

## Inviting a patient to register themselves

Use this for a person who asked for an appointment but is not registered yet.

1. Select **Invite to self-register** on the Patient list. The **Invite to self-register** page opens.
2. Select **Invite to self-register** again, optionally enter a given and family name, choose **SMS** or **Email**, and enter the phone number or email address.
3. Select **Send link**.

The patient receives a one-time link that expires after 7 days. The **Sent registration links** table shows each invite as pending, completed, expired or revoked, and a **View patient** link once completed. If the clinic's text message or email service is not set up, the dialog shows an error and the patient does not receive the link.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/patient-records/07-invite-to-self-register.png" alt="The Send a self-registration link dialog with name fields, SMS or email choice and a phone number field" />
_Sending a registration link_

What the patient sees: a registration form on the patient app. See [Patient Portal](/cms/clinical-care/patient-portal).

When the patient submits the form, their chart shows the **Patient-reported intake** card labeled **Unverified**. It lists drug allergies, reported health conditions, current treatment and medications, and hospitalization history. These answers are patient-reported and not checked against clinical records. Confirm allergies, conditions and medications with the patient before you rely on them. Until a drug allergy is recorded, the **Allergies** card also shows the reported allergies, and doctors and nurses can select **Record this allergy**. See [Allergies & Medication Safety](/cms/clinical-care/allergies-and-medication-safety#allergies-the-patient-reported-at-registration).

## Deactivating a patient

1. On the Patient list, select the actions menu (three dots) on the patient's row.
2. Select **Deactivate patient**, then **Deactivate**.

The patient's history stays intact and the status becomes **Inactive**. An inactive patient can no longer be selected for care: **Start Encounter** is disabled. Use the **Inactive** filter to find them. The CMS has no button to reactivate a patient; if someone was deactivated in error, restore the record in the clinic's medical record system rather than registering them again (which the app refuses as a duplicate).

## Audit log

A Clinic Admin can see who read, changed, sent or approved anything in a patient's record. Open **Audit log** in the chart sidebar. For every other role the section does not exist.

The list shows chart reads, record changes, messages sent and approvals, newest first, with the time, who did it, the action, the resource and the outcome. When an event carries one, columns show the plan version and the service configuration version in force. Filter by **Action** (for example `chart.read`) and a **From** and **To** date, then **Apply**, or **Clear**. Older events load with **Load older events**.

To see everything one staff member did, open [Staff](/cms/administration/staff), open the person's actions menu and select **Audit log**.

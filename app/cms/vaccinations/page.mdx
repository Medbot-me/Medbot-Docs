# Vaccinations

A patient's detail page shows vaccination history to admins, receptionists, doctors, and nurses. Admins, doctors, and nurses can record, edit, and void records — stored as FHIR `Immunization` resources — receptionists have view-only access.

## Recording a vaccination

Recording a vaccination doesn't require an in-progress encounter — clinics record a same-day vaccine and backfill a patient's prior history alike — but an optional encounter link is verified to belong to the same patient.

Admins can register a vaccination administration service in the catalog as a FHIR `ActivityDefinition` with a stable Medbot catalog slug. During an active encounter, clinical staff can order one of these services, producing a `ServiceRequest`. Selecting that active order while recording the vaccination performs it atomically: the `Immunization` is created, the order is completed, and a FHIR `Task` records the order-to-fulfilment trace. Historical entries can still be recorded without an order.

Vaccine stock allocation, lot-derived expiry, and charge capture require the stock-item and billing foundations and aren't represented as complete in this workflow yet.

## Coding

The vaccine product is coded to CVX, the CDC "Vaccine Administered" code set — a curated catalog of commonly-given vaccines covers the common case, with a free-text "Other" option for anything not listed. Route of administration and anatomical site use the HL7 v3 RouteOfAdministration and ActSite code systems; dose volume is recorded in millilitres (UCUM).

## Editing and voiding

Editing a record is a true FHIR update (HTTP PUT) with optimistic concurrency (`If-Match` on the current version) that rejects a blind overwrite if the record changed since it was loaded. The original performer and recorded timestamp are preserved, so an edit corrects the record without re-attributing who gave the vaccine or when it was first entered.

Voiding never deletes the resource — it sets `status` to `entered-in-error` with an audit note recording who voided it, when, and why, keeping the record and its version history for audit while it drops out of the active list.

## Date rules

The administration date can't be in the future: the date picker caps at the browser's local date, and the server independently accepts dates up to one day past the UTC date, since a clinic in a timezone ahead of UTC (e.g. Singapore, UTC+8) can be a full calendar day ahead of the server's UTC clock during early-morning local hours.

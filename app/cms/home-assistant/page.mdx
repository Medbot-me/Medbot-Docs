# Home Assistant

The Home assistant is a chat on the **Home** tab. Ask it about the organisation's patients, today's queue, appointments or billing, and it answers from the records, drawing what it found as cards. In an ordinary chat it can only read: it never books, checks in, bills, signs, orders or changes anything. Where your deployment turns on [running a clinic day](#running-a-clinic-day), a doctor or nurse can also have it propose steps of the day, which only they can confirm.

Selecting **Home** opens it on a new chat (`/home`). Each conversation you start gets its own address, `/home/chats/<id>`, and is listed under **Chats** in the sidebar.

## Who can do this

Every role can use the Home assistant. What it can look up follows your role, the same way the matching pages do:

| What it can look up | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Find a patient by name, NRIC or FIN, phone number or birth date | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| A patient's summary (allergies, conditions, medications, last visit, next appointment) | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| A patient's latest results | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| The appointments on a day | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| The live queue | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Unpaid invoices | Yes | Yes | No | No |

A Patient service associate can look up unpaid invoices, as a Receptionist can. If you ask for something your role cannot see, the assistant says it has no way to look it up. Under the message box it reminds you: "Medbot reads &lt;organisation&gt;'s records and shows only what your role may see. It cannot change the record. Check before you act."

## Starting a chat

1. Select **Home**, or **New chat** in the sidebar.
2. Type your question in the box ("Ask about a patient, the queue, appointments or billing") and send it, or select one of the suggestions:
   - "Who is waiting in the queue?"
   - "What appointments do we have today?"
   - "Show a patient's latest results"
   - "Which invoices from today are unpaid?"
   - "Who is booked for tomorrow?"
3. Medbot reads the records and replies with a sentence or two on what matters, and a card for each thing it looked up.

To ask about a patient, name them. The assistant searches for the patient first. If several match, it lists them and asks which one you mean.

Days are the organisation's own calendar days, in its time zone.

## The cards

| Card | What it shows | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Patient matches | Each patient found, with their details and masked identifier | **Open chart** |
| Patient summary | **Allergies**, **Conditions**, **Active medications**, **Last visit** and **Next appointment** | **Open chart** |
| Results | The patient's most recent report: each **Test**, its **Result**, **Reference range**, the previous value and the lab's **Flag**, with how many are flagged | |
| **Queue** | How many patients are at each stage, and each patient's **Ticket**, **Stage**, **Doctor** and how long they have **Waited**, longest wait first | **Open queue** |
| **Appointments** | The day's total, how many are booked, arrived, fulfilled, cancelled or no-show, and the next appointments (**Next up**) | **Open appointments** |
| **Unpaid invoices** | Issued invoices not yet paid, with the total owed and the largest invoices | **Open billing** |

Values are shown exactly as the record has them, never rounded or converted. A card lists only the first few rows; follow its link for the full page.

## Replies

Each reply has **Copy reply**, and the latest has **Ask again**, which asks the same question afresh. If the assistant cannot answer, it says so with **Try again**.

The assistant always runs on the organisation's default model. The box shows **Organisation default model**: you cannot choose another. A Clinic Admin sets that model in the [AI policy](/cms/ai-policy).

## Running a clinic day

> **Note:** Running a clinic day is available only when your deployment has turned it on. If it is off, the Home tab has only the read-only assistant described above.

A doctor or nurse can run a day of clinic from a Home chat: call the next patient, record the consult and add its summary to the note, draft orders and prescribe, end the consult and move on to the next patient, and send follow-ups after clinic. Medbot's assistant proposes each step as a card in the chat, and nothing happens until you press the button on the card. It never calls a patient, signs, sends or ends a consult by itself.

### Starting

1. Set the CMS to a service that runs a queue, a clinic or a hybrid service. See [Choosing & Switching](/cms/switching#choosing-a-service).
2. On a new chat on **Home**, select **Start my clinic**. It is offered to a Doctor or Nurse, and to a Clinic Admin covering for support. Without a service set, it is not offered, and the ordinary assistant works as before.

This opens a conversation for that service, you and today. Starting your clinic again the same day reopens it. It appears under **Chats** like any other conversation. The suggestions in it are "Who is waiting?", "Who is next?", "Show any red flags" and "What appointments do we have today?"

### The panel beside the chat

A panel beside the chat has two tabs. On a narrow screen it opens from a button in the header.

- **Today** shows tiles for **Booked**, **Waiting**, **In progress** and **Seen**, the day's steps (**Morning brief**, **Consults**, **Orders and notes to finish** and **Post-clinic follow-ups**), **Needs you** (red flags nobody has acknowledged, issues raised through the portal, and check-ins that went unanswered) and **Today's list**. **Open workstation** goes to the service's [Workstation](/cms/services/workstation).
- **Patient** shows the patient whose consult the run has open. It opens by itself when a consult starts.

### The cards

| Card | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Call patient** | Choose a room and select **Start consult**, or **Not now**. The patient is called in and the consult opens with them. A patient who has not arrived cannot be called. |
| **Recording** | Record and transcribe the consult. While it is listening, the recorder replaces the message box. When it stops, review the drafted summary and add its sections to the consult note with **Add all** (or **Add remaining** once some are in), **Regenerate** it, or **Discard** it. **Open note editor** takes you to the consult screen. |
| **Note change** | When you ask for a change to a summary section by voice, **Accept change** applies it to the draft, or select **Not now**. |
| **Orders and prescriptions** | The assistant drafts orders for the open consult and proposes a prescription. **Review and sign** shows the unsigned medications. A prescription is checked and signed as in [Prescribing & Medications](/cms/prescribing#signing): only a doctor, a Clinic Admin or an [authorised nurse](/cms/staff#authorising-a-nurse-to-prescribe) can sign it. Any other nurse can draft it and leave it for a prescriber. |
| **End the consult** | Choose the next steps to add for the patient, then confirm. Ending the consult also calls the next arrived patient in the same press. The card says if that call did not go through, with a **Call** button to try again. Unsigned orders are not signed by ending the consult. |
| **Follow-ups** | After clinic, the assistant proposes an AI encounter for each patient seen today, with its kind and reason open to change. A patient with no phone number is skipped. Links go out only when you send them. See [Starting an AI encounter](/cms/encounters#starting-an-ai-encounter). |

### Closing the clinic

Select **Close the clinic** in the panel. Medbot then stops proposing calls, recordings, orders and follow-ups for the day. You can still ask it questions, and starting your clinic again reopens it.

If someone else saved the consult note while you were working, for example from the Home chat, the consult screen refuses to save over it: "This note was changed elsewhere, for example added to from the Home clinic run. Reload it to see the changes."

## Your conversations

**Chats** in the Home sidebar lists your 20 most recent conversations, newest first, under **Today**, **Previous 7 days** and **Older**. A new conversation appears there as soon as you send its first message. Select one to carry on where you left off.

Conversations are yours alone, in that organisation. Nobody else sees them, and they are not listed when you work in another organisation. Opening a conversation that is not yours, or that belongs to another organisation, shows "This conversation is not here", with **Start a new chat**.

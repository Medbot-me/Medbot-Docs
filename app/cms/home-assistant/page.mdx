# Home Assistant

The Home assistant is a chat on the **Home** tab. Ask it about the organisation's patients, today's queue, appointments or billing, and it answers from the records, drawing what it found as cards. It can only read: it never books, checks in, bills, signs, orders or changes anything.

Selecting **Home** opens it on a new chat (`/home`). Each conversation you start gets its own address, `/home/chats/<id>`, and is listed under **Chats** in the sidebar.

## Who can do this

Every role can use the Home assistant. What it can look up follows your role, the same way the matching pages do:

| What it can look up | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Find a patient by name, NRIC or FIN, phone number or birth date | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| A patient's summary (allergies, conditions, medications, last visit, next appointment) | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| A patient's latest results | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| The appointments on a day | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| The live queue | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Unpaid invoices | Yes | Yes | No | No |

If you ask for something your role cannot see, the assistant says it has no way to look it up. Under the message box it reminds you: "Medbot reads &lt;organisation&gt;'s records and shows only what your role may see. It cannot change the record. Check before you act."

## Starting a chat

1. Select **Home**, or **New chat** in the sidebar.
2. Type your question in the box ("Ask about a patient, the queue, appointments or billing") and send it, or select one of the suggestions:
   - "Who is waiting in the queue?"
   - "What appointments do we have today?"
   - "Show a patient's latest results"
   - "Which invoices from today are unpaid?"
   - "Who is booked for tomorrow?"
3. Medbot reads the records and replies with a sentence or two on what matters, and a card for each thing it looked up.

To ask about a patient, name them. The assistant searches for the patient first. If several match, it lists them and asks which one you mean.

Days are the organisation's own calendar days, in its time zone.

## The cards

| Card | What it shows | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Patient matches | Each patient found, with their details and masked identifier | **Open chart** |
| Patient summary | **Allergies**, **Conditions**, **Active medications**, **Last visit** and **Next appointment** | **Open chart** |
| Results | The patient's most recent report: each **Test**, its **Result**, **Reference range**, the previous value and the lab's **Flag**, with how many are flagged | |
| **Queue** | How many patients are at each stage, and each patient's **Ticket**, **Stage**, **Doctor** and how long they have **Waited**, longest wait first | **Open queue** |
| **Appointments** | The day's total, how many are booked, arrived, fulfilled, cancelled or no-show, and the next appointments (**Next up**) | **Open appointments** |
| **Unpaid invoices** | Issued invoices not yet paid, with the total owed and the largest invoices | **Open billing** |

Values are shown exactly as the record has them, never rounded or converted. A card lists only the first few rows; follow its link for the full page.

## Replies

Each reply has **Copy reply**, and the latest has **Ask again**, which asks the same question afresh. If the assistant cannot answer, it says so with **Try again**.

The assistant always runs on the organisation's default model. The box shows **Organisation default model**: you cannot choose another. A Clinic Admin sets that model in the [AI policy](/cms/ai-policy).

## Your conversations

**Chats** in the Home sidebar lists your 20 most recent conversations, newest first, under **Today**, **Previous 7 days** and **Older**. A new conversation appears there as soon as you send its first message. Select one to carry on where you left off.

Conversations are yours alone, in that organisation. Nobody else sees them, and they are not listed when you work in another organisation. Opening a conversation that is not yours, or that belongs to another organisation, shows "This conversation is not here", with **Start a new chat**.

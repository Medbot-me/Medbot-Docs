# Medbot-Hosted Organisations

A clinic that has no Medplum server of its own can have Medbot host one. When you create an organisation and choose **Hosted by Medbot**, Medbot makes a Medplum project for it at once and hands the organisation over with you as its first **administrator**. Nobody at Medbot approves it and nothing waits in a queue. From then on it works exactly like any other organisation, except that Medbot manages its Medplum credentials.

> **Where this is offered.** "Hosted by Medbot" appears only on a deployment that runs Medbot's own Medplum server, which is Medbot's production service. A staging or self-run deployment without one does not offer it: there, every organisation brings its own Medplum project, and the create form asks for its client ID and secret. See [Create an Organisation](/cms/create-organisation).

## Creating one

1. Open **Create organisation** from the [organisations screen](/cms), the [welcome screen](/cms/switching#the-welcome-screen), the sidebar switcher or `/organisations/new`.
2. Enter the **Organisation name**, **Country** and **Time zone**, and for a Singapore clinic choose its SmartCMS modules or skip them.
3. On the **Medplum server** step, leave **Hosted by Medbot** selected. It says "Medbot makes a Medplum project for this organisation now. Nothing else to enter." The other choice, **My own Medplum project**, asks for a client ID and secret instead.
4. Select **Create organisation**.

Medbot creates the Medplum project, its client, the organisation, your `admin` membership and a default "Main Clinic" location, then switches you into the new organisation. If any step fails, whatever was made in Medplum is removed again and the form shows the reason. The Medbot web app's **Create organisation** form offers the same choice.

The same **one organisation per person** limit applies as for a self-managed organisation: see [Create an Organisation](/cms/create-organisation#how-many-you-can-create).

## How a hosted organisation differs

Only in its Medplum connection. On [Organisation Settings](/cms/settings), the **Medplum credentials** card says "This organisation's Medplum server is managed by Medbot. Contact Medbot to rotate its credentials." It shows the base URL and a **Verify connection** button, and no credential fields. The organisation's own admins cannot read or change the credentials, even through the API.

Everything else (staff, join code, patients, every clinic workflow) behaves exactly as for a self-managed organisation.

## For Medbot platform administrators

Hosted organisations are looked after in the CMS's **Platform** sidebar group, which appears only for a **platform superadmin**. That is a Medbot-wide role on the user account, separate from any organisation role, and granted by an existing superadmin. For anyone else, `/admin/*` answers **not found**. Some of the same queues are also in the admin area of the Medbot web app.

The **Platform** group has three items:

| Screen | Route | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hosted organisations** | `/admin/organisations` | Every Medbot-hosted organisation, with its Medplum project. Set or rotate its Medplum credentials, **Offboard** or **Reactivate** it, and grant the platform superadmin role to someone by email. |
| **Caregiver access** | `/admin/caregivers` | Look up, revoke or invite caregiver access across organisations. |
| **Record services** | `/admin/record-services` | Paid requests from patients for the Medbot team to upload documents or reconcile history. |

Each screen is described in [Platform Administration](/cms/platform-administration).

There is no longer a request-and-approve step: the former **Provisioning requests** screen, the "Request a Medbot-hosted organisation" form and the emails around them have been removed. Records of requests made before the change are kept but nothing writes to them now.

# Patients & Cohort

## The organisation's patient directory

A service's patients come from **the organisation's own patient directory**, the same patients the clinic sees in the CMS. They are not owned by any one service, and the same patient can be in several services' cohorts.

New patients are added to the organisation, not to a service. An organisation admin or receptionist registers them in Medbot CMS (see [Registering a patient](/cms/clinical-care/patient-records#registering-a-patient)), or an admin adds them in bulk by [seeding from a CSV file](/cms/administration/seed-data). On their home page in a service, admins and receptionists have an **Add patient** shortcut: "Register a patient, then add them to &lt;service&gt;".

## Adding a patient to the cohort

From **Patients** under the service's name in the CMS sidebar (or the wizard's last step, **Patients and publish**), click **Add patient to cohort**. In **Add patient to cohort**:

1. **Search by name…** among the organisation's patients. Patients already in this cohort are not offered.
2. Check the enrolment preview (below), which shows how Medbot will tell the patient.
3. Optionally add **Clinical notes**: referral letters, consult notes, discharge summaries. They are saved as the patient's **first encounter note** for this service, and their **patient context** is generated from it.
4. Click **Add to cohort**.

A toast says "Patient added to service" and "Medbot is sending them the enrolment notice."

To take a patient out of the cohort, use the **X** on their row and confirm. They stay in the organisation's directory and in any other service.

### The enrolment preview

Once you choose a patient, the dialog shows two columns. On the left:

- whether the patient **Has a patient portal account** or has **No patient portal account**
- **Notify by**: the channels Medbot will try (WhatsApp, SMS, email), with the patient's preferred one marked "preferred"
- **After you add** the patient: what happens next

On the right, **Preview** shows the exact message for the channel. It also warns you if nothing will be sent, for example when enrolment notices are off for the service ("Turn them on in Patient notifications"), when the patient has opted out of messages, or when there is no phone number or email on file.

### What happens when a patient is added

Configured on **Settings → On patient added**:

- **One enrolment notice.** Medbot sends the patient a single notice by their preferred channel (WhatsApp, SMS or email), naming your organisation and the service. If they have no patient portal account, it links to portal sign-in. If they do, it points them to their portal notifications. Which channels are used, and whether this notice is sent at all, is set in [Patient Notifications](/cms/services/patient-notifications).
- **A note from the care team** (**Add a note from the care team to the enrolment notice**). Turn this on and type the **Message**; it is added to the notice. You can use `{{firstName}}`.
- **Onboarding agent** (**Assess the patient with the onboarding agent**). Runs the chosen **Onboarding agent** against the new patient. The default one raises a task only when the patient clearly does not belong in the service. It no longer messages the patient itself. See [Service Agents](/cms/services/agents#the-onboarding-agent).

## The cohort roster

The roster shows, per patient: **Patient**, **Pending tasks**, **Notes**, **Messages** and **Last message**, so you can see who needs attention without leaving the page. Click a name (or the eye icon) to open the patient's page. Made-up patients in a [demo service](/cms/services/demo-services) carry a **Demo** chip.

### Patient identifiers

Each patient's ID number (such as NRIC) is masked everywhere it appears. Click the eye button (**Show patient identifiers**) to reveal it; click again (**Hide patient identifiers**) to mask it. The full view lists each identifier and the **Patient ID** Medbot uses internally, each with a copy button. The same control appears on the patient page, the Workstation boards, the visit panel and the referrals list. It is available to anyone who can open the service.

## A patient's page

Each patient has a page per service (`/services/<id>/patients/<patient id>`), the patient workspace. It opens from the patient's name on the roster, or **View patient** in a Workstation row's menu.

- **Header**: name, gender, age, phone and address, with the masked ID (use the eye button to reveal), a **Demo** chip for demo patients, the service's name, and **In today's queue** with the queue number when the patient is in today's queue.
- **Header buttons**: **Add note**, then one way to start the patient's encounter: **Resume consult** while their consult is under way today, **Start consult** while they wait in today's queue, otherwise **Start Encounter** (see [Starting an encounter](/cms/services/workstation#starting-an-encounter)). **Start consult** and **Start Encounter** are for Clinic Admins, Doctors, Nurses and Allied health professionals. Clinic Admins and Patient service associates also have **Start financial counselling** (see [Financial counselling](/cms/services/workstation#financial-counselling)). On a phone the buttons sit in a grid under the name.
- **Draft awaiting review**: pinned under the header if the patient has a stored reply waiting for approval. See [Patient Agent](/cms/services/patient-agent#drafts-awaiting-review).

The centre shows the patient's chart overview, with a **Notes** tab for this service's encounter notes. **Add note** opens **Notes**.

The left rail opens on **Patient**: the chart's lists as the consult screen shows them, then vitals, vaccinations, documents, photos and contact and consent. Each part shows only for the roles that may open the matching chart page, and links to its page for recording and editing. Below **Patient** come the service's own parts:

| Part | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **In this service** | **Escalations** from the patient agent (with a count of open ones), and for [Inpatient services](/cms/services/modes-of-care#inpatient) an **Admission** card. |
| **AI encounters** | The patient's AI encounters in this service. See [AI Encounters](/cms/services/encounters). |
| **Cycle** | For a patient with an IVF cycle. See [IVF Cycles](/cms/services/ivf-cycles). |
| **Messages** | The message thread, and a box to send a message. |
| **Appointments** | Upcoming and past bookings, and **Book for this patient**. See [Appointments](/cms/services/appointments). |
| **Health plan** | The plan the patient shares with this service, and proposing one. See [Health Plans](/cms/services/health-plans#proposing-a-plan-after-a-visit). |
| **Service** | The AI-maintained **agent context** for this patient, which can be edited or regenerated, and the **Messaging settings**. |
| **More** | Links to the chart's other pages, such as **Problem list**, **Accounts**, **Shared by patient** and **Audit log**, by role. |

Old links to the page's former tabs (`?tab=general`, `?tab=messages` and so on) open the matching part.

### Encounter notes

Encounter notes are the patient's running clinical record in this service, under **Notes** in the centre. Click **New encounter note**, write it, and **Save note** (or **Update note** when editing). Every save **folds the note into the patient context**. Notes can be deleted individually.

### Messages

Type in "Send a message to this patient…", choose WhatsApp or SMS, and send. Each message in the thread is badged by who or what sent it: **Staff**, **Agent** (the patient agent), **Service agent**, **Emergency escalation**, **Encounter link** or **Encounter summary**.

### Messaging settings

Several patients can share one phone number, for example a parent messaging for two children. **Messaging settings** shows everyone reachable on this patient's number, which service their replies are currently routed to, and the recent routing changes. Staff can **Pin to this service** so replies from that number come here, or **Reset context** to let routing start afresh.

# Staff

**Staff**, under the service's name in the sidebar (`/services/<service>/staff`), brings together the people who work in the service, their coverage and their availability. It sits above **Roster**. Staff-facing pages used to call this the service's **care team**; some settings and dialogs still say care team.

## Roles come from the organisation

A service has no roles of its own. Each staff member acts under **the role or roles they hold in the organisation**: Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Receptionist, Patient service associate or Allied health professional.

A member can hold more than one organisation role. The staff table's **Role** column shows one badge for each. If someone's organisation roles change, their roles on every service change with them. The same roles appear wherever a service asks for a role: coverage, the patient agent's escalation role, and the appointment role filter. Roles are managed in the organisation, not the service; see [Members & Roles](/cms/administration/members-and-roles).

## Members

Click **Add staff**. The dialog says "Tick everyone to add. Each joins with the role they hold at the organisation."

1. Tick one or more organisation members. Only people who are already members of the service's organisation can be added, and people already on the service are marked as such.
2. With exactly one person ticked, you can add **Additional information on &lt;name&gt;'s preferences**, such as their specialty or current focus on this service. AI turns it into that member's **agent context**, which service agents are given when they work with or for this person.
3. Click **Add &lt;name&gt; to staff** (or **Add &lt;N&gt; to staff**).

Someone who was invited to the organisation but has not signed up yet can be ticked too: **Keep a place for &lt;name&gt;** keeps their place on the service, shown as **Invited**, until they join.

The table lists **Name**, **Email**, **Role**, **Agent context** (which can be edited) and **Actions**. To take someone off the service, click **Remove** on their row and confirm in **Remove from staff?**.

Staff are not invited to a service by email: add them to the organisation first, then to the service.

## Coverage / rota

Coverage says **who is covering a role at a point in time**. Several behaviours, such as patient agent escalations, address "whoever is covering the Nurse role right now" rather than a named person, so a day off does not leave notifications with nowhere to go.

Add an entry from the **Coverage / rota** card: **Organisation member**, **Role**, **Starts**, and optionally **Ends**, then **Add coverage**.

## Availability

The **Availability** card defines each staff member's **recurring weekly booking windows**. Staff booking, the patient agent and patients booking in the patient portal all read these to work out open slots. See [Appointments](/cms/services/appointments).

Times are in **clinic time**: your organisation's timezone, or Singapore time if none is set.

1. Click **Add availability**.
2. Pick a **Staff member**.
3. Pick a **Day of week**, and set **Start (clinic time)** and **End (clinic time)**.
4. Set the **Slot length (minutes)**, between 5 and 480, e.g. 30.
5. Click **Add**.

The table shows **Member**, **Role**, **Day**, **Window (clinic time)**, **Slot**, **Active** and **Actions**. Toggle a window's **Active** switch to take it offline temporarily (for leave) without losing it, or delete it with the trash icon. If nobody has any availability, patients cannot book, and the card says so.

### "Not bookable — fix in Members"

A window with this badge belongs to a member who has no practitioner record in the clinic's schedule, so their times are not offered for booking. An organisation admin can create the record on the [Staff](/cms/administration/staff) page.

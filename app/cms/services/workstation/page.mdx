# Workstation

The **Workstation** is a service's working view of the day. It shows who needs attention, where each patient is in their visit, and what needs doing next. From it, staff move patients through the visit journey and run the consult.

What the Workstation shows depends on the service's **Mode of care** and on whether it runs a queue. This page covers an **Outpatient** service, where it shows **today's clinic queue**. A **Hybrid** service has the same queue plus a caseload. For ward rounds see [Inpatient](/cms/services/modes-of-care#inpatient), for remote follow-up see [Remote](/cms/services/modes-of-care#remote), and for the two together see [Hybrid](/cms/services/modes-of-care#hybrid).

Open it from **Home** in the sidebar, under the service's name. The Workstation is every role's home page in a service, so it is not listed again under its own name (see [Role home pages](/cms/services/getting-started#role-home-pages)). A row of tabs at the top switches between its views. **Home** always comes first:

| Service | Tabs |
| --- | --- |
| **Outpatient** | **Home**, **Appointments**, **Referrals**, **Session**, **Progress**, **AI encounters**, **Escalations**, **Health plans** |
| **Hybrid** | **Home**, **Appointments**, **Referrals**, **Session**, **Progress**, **AI encounters**, **Caseload**, **Escalations**, **Health plans** |
| **Outpatient** or **Hybrid** with its queue turned off | **Home**, **Appointments**, **Referrals**, **AI encounters**, then **Caseload** for a Hybrid service, **Escalations**, **Health plans** |
| **Inpatient** | **Home** (the census), **Referrals**, **Rounds**, **Escalations**, **Health plans** |
| **Remote** | **Home** (the caseload), **Appointments**, **Referrals**, **Escalations**, **Health plans** |

**AI encounters** holds today's AI encounters and the pre-visit histories patients answer before a booked visit; it and **Escalations** are described in [Pre-visit History & Escalations](/cms/services/pre-visit-and-escalations). **Health plans** holds plans patients sent for review: see [Health Plans](/cms/services/health-plans). A service with IVF cycles turned on also has a **Cycles** tab, before **Escalations**: see [IVF Cycles](/cms/services/ivf-cycles).

There is no **Patients** or **Queue** tab: **Home** holds both. An old link to the Workstation's patients opens **Home**.

## How it connects to the clinic

The Workstation does not keep its own queue. It reads the **Medbot CMS queue** for the service's clinic location, the same queue the front desk runs, so:

- a patient checked in at reception, who **checked themselves in** on the patient portal, or who checked in at the [self-service kiosk](/cms/services/kiosk), appears here
- a patient with a booked appointment (including one booked by the patient in the portal) appears as **Not yet arrived**
- starting a step that maps to a CMS queue stage (nurse, doctor, dispensary) moves the patient to that stage in the CMS
- completing every step of the journey completes the visit in the CMS

### The patient's side

On the day of the visit, patients follow the same journey on the patient portal:

- **Self check-in** ("Register your arrival"), either by location when they are at the clinic or with the 6-digit arrival code shown at the counter. They get a **queue number**.
- A **live queue tracker** that tells them where to go next.
- **Sessions your clinician arranged**: AI financial counselling or health education sessions that staff arrange for this visit.
- A **visit outcome** screen at the end: what was found, medicines started, next steps and billing.

In the CMS, the front desk can **Arrange session** for a patient and **Complete visit** with findings, medicines started and next steps (pre-drafted by AI). See [Reception Queue](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue).

## Home

**Home** puts the service's patients in one table. Today's queue comes first. Below it, **Not in today's queue** lists the service's other patients (**Not admitted** under an Inpatient census). A clinic whose queue is turned off has no queue: **Home** lists its patients.

What the queue part looks like depends on your role. The front desk (Clinic Admins, Receptionists and Patient service associates) gets the reception queue for the service, with **Add to queue** ("Check in, or add a walk-in, to issue the next &lt;service&gt; queue number."); see [Reception Queue](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue). Allied health professionals see the queue read-only. Doctors and nurses get the board described below.

The board refreshes itself every few seconds, and shows how fresh it is. **Refresh** reloads it immediately.

| Control | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Day** | **Today** or **Tomorrow**. Tomorrow is a read-only list of who is booked, with their appointment, doctor and pre-visit status. |
| **Show** | **This service's patients** (the cohort) or **Everyone in today's queue** (**Everyone booked tomorrow** on the Tomorrow view) |
| **Filter** | **All patients**, **Waiting**, **In progress**, **Escalated**, **Completed**, **Not arrived**, each with a count |
| **My room** | Appears when the clinic has rooms. Choose the room you are working in. |

Click the **Queue** or **Appointment** column header to sort.

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Queue** | The patient's queue number. |
| **Patient** | Name, with the masked ID and a **Demo** chip for demo patients. |
| **Appointment** | The booked time. |
| **Notifications** | An **Urgent** or **Acknowledged** escalation, and unanswered portal **Message**s. |
| **Pre-visit** | The state of the patient's pre-visit history. |
| **Progress** | How many journey steps are left, of how many. Hover for the breakdown. |
| **Now and next** | The current and next step. |

Banners at the top say when:

- patients have raised an issue through the patient portal and need acknowledging
- patients on today's list did not answer the pre-visit check-in ("Ask about how they have been before the consult.")

Row actions:

- **Mark arrived** checks in a patient who is booked but not yet here.
- **Add to service** enrols a patient from the queue into this service's cohort.
- **Start consult**, for a doctor, on a patient waiting in the queue, starts their consult and opens the consult screen. **Resume consult** returns to one under way. Choose **My room** first to also call the patient in.
- **Open visit** opens the patient panel. It is the action nurses and other roles see on a checked-in patient.
- **Call** (then **Call again**) calls the patient to your room. Choose **My room** first, otherwise the button explains "Choose "My room" above to call patients to it".

### Starting an encounter

On the board, a doctor has **Start consult** for a patient waiting in today's queue. A patient listed under **Not in today's queue** or **Not admitted**, or on **Home** in a clinic whose queue is turned off, has **Start Encounter**, next to **Start AI encounter**. The patient's page in the service has it too (see [A patient's page](/cms/services/patients#a-patients-page)). What it starts depends on the service:

| Service | **Start Encounter** |
| --- | --- |
| Runs a queue | Checks the patient in as a walk-in (to their appointment today, if they have one) and opens the visit's consult. |
| Remote | Opens a review. |
| Clinic with its queue turned off, or a ward patient who is not admitted | Opens a consult with no visit. |

The usual start needs no dialog: select **Start Encounter** and the consult screen opens. The arrow beside it opens **More options** first ("Choose how this encounter is recorded. Anything left as it is uses the default."):

- **Encounter class**: **Ambulatory** (the default), **Emergency**, **Virtual**, **Home health** or **Inpatient**.
- **Priority**: **Routine** (the default), **Urgent**, **ASAP** or **STAT**.
- **Reason (optional)**, for example "Wound review, results follow-up".

Where the service runs a queue, **More options** offers only **Priority**: any priority other than **Routine** adds the walk-in as urgent. Select **Start Encounter** in the dialog, or **Cancel**.

Clinic Admins, Doctors, Nurses, Patient service associates and Allied health professionals can start an encounter. A Patient service associate's is always manual financial counselling.

### Financial counselling

Clinic Admins and Patient service associates can start manual financial counselling for a patient. It has no **More options**, and opens the consult screen as **Financial counselling**, with no visit.

- **Counsel** on a row of the front desk's queue that is not finished.
- **Start financial counselling** in the header of the patient's page in the service.
- For a Patient service associate, **Start financial counselling** also takes the place of **Start Encounter** on **Home**.

If it cannot start, Medbot says "Could not start financial counselling" with the reason.

### The Progress view

**Progress** shows each of today's patients against the service's journey steps, one column per step, including education and counselling sessions running on the portal or the kiosk. Tiles count **Needs a person**, **Session in progress**, **Steps left** and **Visit complete**; click a session cell to open its details and step in.

## The patient panel

**Open visit** opens a side panel for that patient's visit:

- **Escalation**, if there is one, with **Acknowledge** and **Resolve**. Resolving asks for an optional resolution note.
- **Unanswered portal message**, if the patient is waiting on a reply.
- **Today's journey**: every step of the visit with its status. For each step: **Start** followed by the step name in lower case (for example **Start vitals**), **Mark complete** or **Skip**.
  - A step that runs as an **AI encounter** (history taking, health education, financial counselling) opens the in-clinic encounter dialog, so the patient can do it on a clinic device. See [AI Encounters & Kiosk](/cms/services/encounters#in-the-clinic-the-kiosk).
  - The consult step has **Start clinical consult**, or **Resume clinical consult** once started. If you have chosen **My room** and the consult has not started, **Start consult and call in** also calls the patient.

## The clinical consult

**Start clinical consult** opens the consult screen for the visit: Medbot's notes editor, with the patient's note for this visit, under the service's breadcrumb. Beside the note are the patient's chart and **Orders** and **Medications** tabs, where you draft and sign prescriptions, lab and imaging orders and service orders (see [Orders & Order Sets](/cms/clinical-care/orders-and-order-sets)). If the patient did a pre-visit history, a **Pre-visit history** section offers **Add to note** and **Open full note**, and a pre-visit check-in summary offers **Add to note**.

A consult started with **Start Encounter** in a service without a queue, or for a ward patient who is not admitted, has no visit. The consult screen calls it **Clinical consult**, and financial counselling **Financial counselling**. Both end the way a review ends: Medbot proposes next steps, and you confirm them.

- The note **saves automatically**.
- **Record** the consult with the [encounter recorder](/cms/services/encounter-recording). On this screen it transcribes every 45 seconds, and a **Transcript** pane beside the note shows the conversation almost live. Pause and resume without losing the microphone.
- When the recording ends, review the AI-drafted note section by section and add what you want.

### Ending the consult

Click **End consult** when you are done. Medbot reads your plan in the note and proposes next steps, but **nothing reaches the patient until you confirm**. The dialog **End consult: next steps for &lt;patient&gt;** shows:

- the rationale, and a checklist of proposed steps. Each carries a badge: **Portal**, **Kiosk**, **In clinic** or **Task**. Untick anything that should not happen.
- **Add another step**, to add one of the service's other journey steps yourself.
- **Unsigned orders**: any prescription, lab, imaging or service drafts. "Drafts are not sent. Sign them on the Orders and Medications tabs before you end the consult."
- **Tell the patient by SMS or WhatsApp that steps are waiting, with a portal link only.** This is disabled if the patient is not on the portal. Its starting value comes from your Workstation settings.
- **Propose a health plan next**, when the patient is on the portal. Ending the consult then opens the patient's **Health plan** tab with targets suggested from their recent results, to check and send. See [Health Plans](/cms/services/health-plans#from-the-end-of-a-consult).

Click **Confirm &lt;N&gt; step(s) and end consult** (or **End consult with no new steps**). Medbot adds the steps, completes the consult step and shows "Consult ended". If notifying the patient fails, the consult still ends.

## Settings

**Workstation settings** (from the Workstation):

- **Clinic location**: whose queue to show (for an Inpatient service, whose wards the census reads from), or **Organisation default**.
- For an Outpatient or Hybrid service: **Default to only my assigned patients** and **When ending a consult, tell the patient about new steps by default**.
- For an Inpatient service: **Wards this service rounds on** and **Census order**.
- For a Remote or Hybrid service: **Unanswered message needs attention after (hours)**.

See [Modes of Care](/cms/services/modes-of-care).

Click **Save settings**.

### The visit journey

For an Outpatient or Hybrid service, the same page defines the service's **visit journey**: the steps a visit can go through. The standard steps are:

1. Registration (included by default)
2. Vitals (included by default)
3. Investigations
4. Consult (included by default)
5. Financial counselling
6. Health education
7. Medications collection
8. Follow-up appointment

Only the steps marked **Included by default** start every visit. The others are added when needed, for example by **End consult**.

For each step:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Shown to staff, e.g. "Vitals". |
| **Key** | A short identifier, e.g. `vitals`. Every step needs one. |
| **Order** | Position in the journey. |
| **CMS queue stage** | **None**, **Nurse queue**, **Doctor queue**, **Investigations**, **Dispensary queue** or **Completed**. Starting the step moves the patient to that stage in the CMS. |
| **AI encounter kind** | For a step the patient does with the AI assistant: **History taking (AI)**, **Health education (AI)** or **Financial counselling (AI)**. |
| **Included by default** | Whether every visit starts with this step, or it is only added when needed. |
| **Active** | Turn a step off without deleting it. |

**Add step** adds a custom step; **Save steps** saves the journey ("Steps saved."). Changes apply to new visits. A visit already under way keeps the steps it started with.

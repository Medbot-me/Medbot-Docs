# Kiosk Self-Service

A **kiosk**, the self check-in kiosk, is a clinic device, such as a tablet in the waiting area, that patients use themselves to check in and to complete the sessions arranged for their visit. It serves patients with the Medbot app and without it: it shows the counter check-in QR code, reads clinic cards, and takes an ID number and date of birth. It is separate from handing a device to one patient during a consult, which is covered in [AI Encounters](/cms/services/encounters#in-the-clinic-the-kiosk).

Who does what: a member of staff signs in on the device and sets it up. After that, patients use it without signing in to anything.

The kiosk is part of Medbot CMS. Kiosk addresses (`/kiosk/...`) are unchanged from when it was in the Medbot web app, and old links and QR codes to the web app's kiosk redirect to the CMS.

## Set up a kiosk

1. On the device, open the kiosk sign-in page ("Medbot kiosk - staff sign-in"), sign in, and click **Continue to kiosk setup**.
2. On **Set up this kiosk session**, complete the numbered form:
   - **1 - Organisation**: choose the organisation.
   - **2 - Service this kiosk serves today**: choose a service. Each service card lists the kiosk sessions turned on for it (for example "Pre-visit history and Health education"), or says "No kiosk sessions turned on for this service."
   - **3 - Kiosk name**: e.g. "Kiosk 2 - Level 3 waiting area".
   - **4 - Languages offered**: Medbot speaks with the patient in the language they choose.
   - **5 - Session ends**: when you sign out, or at this time today.
3. Click **Start kiosk session**.

A service only offers a session at the kiosk if you turned it on. For a pre-visit history, turn on **Offer it at the clinic kiosk on the day** (see [Pre-visit History & Escalations](/cms/services/pre-visit-and-escalations#offering-a-pre-visit-history)). Sessions that staff arrange for a patient's visit also appear.

## What patients see

The kiosk home has two choices:

- **Check in for today**: "Get your queue number — appointments and walk-ins".
- **Start a session**: "One your doctor arranged for you today".

Below them, the home screen offers two quicker ways to check in:

- **Have the Medbot app?** The rotating counter check-in QR code of the service's clinic location. The patient scans it with their phone camera and taps **Check in** in the app. The code changes every minute and is taken down if the kiosk stops getting new ones. See [Clinic Card & Check-in](/cms/front-desk/clinic-card-check-in#checking-in-with-the-medbot-app).
- **Or show your Medbot card here**: the patient holds their [clinic card](/cms/front-desk/clinic-card-check-in) up to the kiosk's barcode scanner, if it has one, or selects **Use the camera** to read it with the kiosk's camera.

A clinic card at the kiosk only checks the patient in: nobody compares their photo, so it is accepted only for a patient the clinic has linked and whose identity was checked. Anyone else is asked to show their card at the front desk ("We could not check you in here. Please show your card at the front desk."), and the code stays unused so the desk can scan it. An expired code asks the patient to open their card again.

### Checking in

On **Check in for today** the patient enters their NRIC, FIN or passport number and their date of birth, then clicks **Check in and get my queue number**. A patient with no appointment today is checked in as a walk-in.

Whichever way they checked in, the kiosk then says "You're registered, &lt;first name&gt;." with their queue number, and prints a ticket where the kiosk has a printer ("Your ticket is printing", **Print again**). If it cannot print, it says "This kiosk cannot print a ticket right now" and asks them to remember the number or take a photo of the screen. It asks them to take a seat and watch the screen. A walk-in is told they may wait a little longer than booked patients. They then appear on the [Workstation](/cms/services/workstation) queue, with **Self check-in** under their ticket.

### Starting a session

On **Start a session** the patient first enters their particulars to see the sessions their doctor arranged (the last four characters of their ID). They click **Find my sessions**, check "Matched: &lt;name&gt;", choose a session, then answer by typing or speaking (**Type** or **Speak**). **Ask for a staff member** is always available.

When the session is done, the kiosk shows "This kiosk session has ended".

## Ending a kiosk session

Staff can use **Staff: sign out and set up a new session** on the kiosk to end the session and return to setup, for example to switch the device to a different service.

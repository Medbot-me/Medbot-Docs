# Modes of Care

A service's **Mode of care** says how it delivers care, and decides which boards the [Workstation](/docs/services/workstation) shows. Set it when you create a service, or change it later with **Edit service details**. The mode appears as a badge next to the service's status, and as the **Mode** column in the Services list.

| Mode | What it means | Main board |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clinic** | Patients book or walk in, join a queue for the day, and are seen in a consult. | **Queue** |
| **Inpatient** | Patients are admitted to a ward, reviewed on ward rounds, and discharged. | **Census** (and **Rounds**) |
| **Remote** | Patients are followed remotely: messages, portal sessions and video reviews, with no queue. | **Caseload** |

Services that existed before modes were introduced are **Clinic**. Every mode also has an **Escalations** view; see [Pre-visit History & Escalations](/docs/services/pre-visit-and-escalations#concerns-from-patients-not-booked-here).

## Clinic

The Queue, Progress and Pre-visit views are covered on the [Workstation](/docs/services/workstation) page.

## Inpatient

The **Census** board lists the service's admitted patients. Columns: **Bed**, **Patient**, **Admitted**, **Attending**, **Last round** and **Flags** (a **For discharge** badge marks patients expected to go home).

The toolbar has:

- **Show**: **This service's patients** or **Everyone admitted**
- a ward select (**All wards** by default)
- a filter: **All patients**, **Not seen today**, **Round in progress**, **Escalated** or **For discharge**

A banner says how many patients on the ward have not been seen today.

### Admit, transfer and discharge

- **Admit patient** (on the board, or **Admit** on the patient's page): search for the **Patient**, choose the **Ward** and **Bed**, and optionally an **Expected discharge**. A bed can be left as "Not assigned yet". Click **Admit**.
- **Transfer** on a row opens **Transfer or update**: change **Ward**, **Bed** or **Expected discharge** and click **Save**.
- **Discharge** on a row asks **Discharged to** (for example Home, Transferred to another facility, Rehabilitation, Long-term care) and then **Discharge**.

On the patient's page, the **General** tab shows an **Admission** card ("Not admitted" until the patient is admitted) with **Admit**, **Today's ward round** and **Draft discharge summary**. The draft summary is written for you to review in the notes before you discharge.

### Ward rounds

**Start round** (or **Resume round**) on a census row opens the consult screen, titled **Ward round**. It works like a clinic consult, including recording. Click **End round** when finished ("Ward round ended").

The **Rounds** view is a grid of patients by day showing **Rounded**, **In progress**, **No round** or **Not admitted**, with a **Today** column.

### Settings

In the Workstation settings:

- **Wards this service rounds on**: leave none selected to include every ward.
- **Census order**: **By ward, then bed** or **Longest stay first**.
- **Unanswered message needs attention after (hours)**.

A **Wards** section lets you define the wards themselves; click **Save wards** to keep changes.

## Remote

The **Caseload** board ranks patients by how much attention they need. The **Show** filter offers **Needs attention**, **Scheduled today**, **Active sessions** and **All patients**.

Columns: **Patient**, **Flags**, **Scheduled** (for example a video review with a clinician, or a visit), **Last contact** and **Last review** (**In progress** or **Reviewed**).

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/services/modes-of-care/02-remote-caseload.png" alt="The Remote caseload with patients needing attention, their flags, scheduled contacts and the Start review and Message buttons" />

Flags include **Escalation**, **Unanswered message**, **Session needs a person**, **Task overdue**, **Task due**, **Scheduled today**, **Waiting on patient**, **Review in progress** and **No contact for a while**.

**Start review** (or **Resume review**) on a row opens the consult screen, titled **Remote review** (**Video consultation** when it belongs to an appointment). Click **End review** when finished. The **Unanswered message needs attention after (hours)** setting decides when an unanswered patient message raises a flag.

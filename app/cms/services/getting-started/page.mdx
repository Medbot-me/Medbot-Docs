# Creating a Service

## 1. Start a new service

In Medbot CMS, open the context switcher at the top of the sidebar and select **All services** (or **Choose a service** in the sidebar, when no service is set). The **Services** list shows each service's name, specialty, setting, **Mode** and status, with a **Continue setup** link next to any of your own drafts. Demo services carry a **Demo** chip.

Click **New Service**. Administrators can also select **New service** in the context switcher. Under **How to start this service**, choose:

- **Blank service**: start empty and set up your staff, protocols, agents and settings step by step.
- **A template** (Chronic disease management, Joint replacement and recovery, Medical retina clinic, or IVF and assisted reproduction): comes fully configured for one clinical area. In a demo organisation it is a demo service, which can be filled with made-up patients; elsewhere it is an ordinary service with a configured starting point. See [Demo Services](/cms/services/demo-services).

For a blank service, fill in:

- **Name**: required, e.g. "Colorectal SOC: Referral to Discharge".
- **Specialty**: optional, e.g. "Colorectal Surgery".
- **Mode of care**: **Outpatient**, **Inpatient**, **Remote** or **Hybrid**. This sets what the Workstation shows. See [Modes of Care](/cms/services/modes-of-care).
- **Setting**: optional, e.g. "SOC, inpatient, primary care".
- **Clinic location**: where this service sees patients: its queue, rooms, displays and bookings. Choose one of the organisation's locations, or **New location…** and give it a **Location name**. Only a Clinic Admin can add a location here.
- **Run a queue**: for an **Outpatient** or **Hybrid** service, on by default. Checked-in patients get a queue number and are called from the Workstation. Optionally set a **Queue prefix** for its numbers; Medbot suggests one from the service's name. The prefix must be unique at the location.

Click **Create Service**. Any member of the organisation can create one. The service is created as a `draft`, with:

- four default agents: **Onboarding**, **Health education**, **Financial counselling** and **Patient agent** (see [Service Agents](/cms/services/agents#default-agents))
- default AI encounter settings

and you go straight into the setup wizard. The organisation's first service becomes its **default clinic** (see [Organisation Settings](/cms/administration/settings)). A service created from a template skips the wizard and opens its page as a draft, with a **Finish setting up** card listing what is left; see [Demo Services](/cms/services/demo-services#finish-setting-up).

### Your organisation's first service

When someone sets up a new organisation, or a demo organisation, in the first-sign-in flow (see [Getting Started](/cms/getting-started)), its first administrator is asked to set up its first service at `/onboarding/services`: **Set up your first service**, or **Set up a demo service** in a demo organisation. The step appears only while the organisation has no services.

1. Under **Start from**, choose **Basic service** ("Only the essentials. You choose the care setting, specialty and everything after.") or one of the specialised templates.
2. Under **Service details**, fill in or check the **Service name**, the **Care setting** (**Outpatient**, **Inpatient**, **Remote** or **Hybrid**), the **Specialty** and **Where care happens** (optional). A template fills these in for you ("Filled from the &lt;template&gt; template. You can change any of it."), and anything you change is saved onto the service it makes.
3. Click **Create service** (**Create demo service** in a demo organisation), or **Skip for now**.

The service starts as a draft. A real organisation then goes on to its team step; a demo organisation opens on Home.

## 2. Walk through the setup wizard

The wizard has four steps, shown as a list you can jump between freely, each with a one-line status such as "Just you so far" or "Using defaults". Medbot has filled in a working default for each, so you can publish at any point and come back; select **Finish later** to leave. Only the service's owner (or a Medbot platform administrator) can open the wizard. A service made from a template is further along: its wizard says the template filled in how the service runs and its patient agent, and what is left is the staff and the patients.

### Step 1: Staff

Add people from your organisation to the service. Each joins with the role or roles they already hold in the organisation; there is no role to choose. See [Staff](/cms/services/care-team).

### Step 2: How the service runs

Plain-language rules every agent in this service follows, each starting from a default written for the service's name and specialty:

- **Protocols and instructions**: who does what, when to escalate, and what agents should tell patients. Medbot turns it into the agent context that every service agent run is given. See [Service Context & Content](/cms/services/context-and-content).
- **What this service looks after**: the **Conditions you treat** and the **Symptoms you assess, even before a diagnosis**, and the patients you do not see (**Patients you don't see**). It decides which red-flag alerts and Ask Medbot referrals reach the service: Medbot will not alert you about, or suggest your service to, the patients you exclude. **Required before you publish**: a service that lists nothing it looks after cannot be published (a demo service is exempt), and the publish buttons say so and link here. Setting it needs the service's owner or a Clinic Admin.
- **Referral criteria**: which referrals the service accepts. The screening agent drafts a verdict against them; a person always decides. See [Referrals](/cms/services/referrals#referral-criteria).
- **When a patient is added**: the enrolment notice and the onboarding agent. See [Patients & Cohort](/cms/services/patients#what-happens-when-a-patient-is-added).

### Step 3: Patient agent

- **Chat in the patient portal**: whether enrolled patients can chat with the service's patient agent in the patient portal (on by default), and how it hands over. See [Patient Agent](/cms/services/patient-agent).
- **Education and counselling**: the service's content library, where one set of references and education materials is added once, with the settings for the education encounter. See [Service Context & Content](/cms/services/context-and-content#content-library) and [AI Encounters](/cms/services/encounters#settings).
- **Pre-visit history**: the questions patients answer before a booked visit, and where any red flags go. See [Pre-visit History & Escalations](/cms/services/pre-visit-and-escalations).

Agent instructions, models and subagents are on the service's **Agents** page; setup does not need them. See [Service Agents](/cms/services/agents).

### Step 4: Patients and publish

**What patients will get** summarises the staff, protocols, patient agent, education, pre-visit history and the notice sent when a patient is added, so you can check it before anyone is enrolled. **Add patients** adds them to the cohort from the organisation's patient directory; each gets the enrolment notice as soon as they are added. This step is deliberately **last**, so everything else is in place before real patients start receiving notices. See [Patients & Cohort](/cms/services/patients). In a demo organisation, the step also has the **Demo patients** panel.

Select **Publish service** to publish (from `draft` to `published`), or **Keep as draft**. Every step remains editable afterwards. Earlier links to the seven-step wizard's steps open the step that now holds their content.

## 3. Continue setup later

If you left the wizard, the **Services** list shows a **Continue setup** link next to your own drafts, and a service made from a template shows a **Finish setting up** card on its page, with **Mark setup complete**.

## 4. The service page

Every page of a service is on the **Home** tab. The pages of the service you chose in the context switcher sit at the top of the sidebar, under the service's name (with **Draft** beside it while it is a draft), below **Back to Org Level**. Opening a service's page from a link shows that service's pages instead.

The sidebar lists only the pages your role uses. **Patients** comes first: it is every role's home page in the service (see [Role home pages](#role-home-pages)).

| Sidebar item | Shown to | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Setup** | The owner, while the service is a draft | The setup wizard. |
| **Patients** | Everyone on the service | The service's [Workstation](/cms/services/workstation): today's queue (or census, or caseload), the **Patient list**, **Appointments**, **Referrals**, the **Session** and the other views for its mode of care. |
| **Overview** | Clinic Admins | A **Notifications** card for this service, the **Patient agent** summary card, then the **Demo patients** panel (demo services only). **SOP and instructions** and **Referral intake criteria** sit side by side, closed until you select **Open** (**Hide** closes them again), each with a one-line summary such as "3 sections · 412 words." or "Nothing written yet." When the patient agent is on, the summary card shows **Turns (7 days)**, **Pending drafts**, **Open escalations**, **Upcoming appointments** and **Waiting on a patient's choice**. |
| **Staff** | Clinic Admins, Doctors, Nurses and Receptionists | The service's staff, **Coverage / rota** and **Availability**. See [Staff](/cms/services/care-team). |
| **Roster** | Clinic Admins, Doctors, Nurses and Receptionists | The service's roster. See [Rostering](/cms/front-desk/rostering). |
| **Agents** | Clinic Admins | **Agent runs**, **Daily agent schedules**, the **Default agents** cards and the **Agents** table. See [Service Agents](/cms/services/agents). |
| **AI content library** | Clinic Admins, Doctors and Nurses | The service's documents and pictures, with **Add content**. See [Service Context & Content](/cms/services/context-and-content#content-library). |

### Service settings

Clinic Admins open the service's settings from the gear beside its name in the sidebar. They open as a dialog over the page you are on (`/services/<service>/settings`). The sections are:

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | The name, specialty, setting and mode of care (the owner edits them), and the service's **Clinic location**. |
| **Queue and sessions** | For an Outpatient or Hybrid service: **Run a queue**, the queue prefix, when the session starts and closes, how new patients are shared among doctors, and **Self check-in**. See [Queue and sessions settings](/cms/services/workstation#queue-and-sessions-settings). |
| **Patient agent** | The assistant enrolled patients talk to in the patient portal, and which of its messages wait for staff. See [Patient Agent](/cms/services/patient-agent). |
| **Education and counselling** | What the patient agent teaches and explains, from the AI content library. See [AI Encounters](/cms/services/encounters#settings). |
| **Pre-visit history** | The questions patients answer before a booked visit, and the warning signs checked on every answer. See [Pre-visit History & Escalations](/cms/services/pre-visit-and-escalations). |
| **Patient notifications** | Which events tell patients, over which channel, and the **Channel** for invitations and appointment reminders. See [Patient Notifications](/cms/services/patient-notifications). |
| **When a patient is added** | The note from staff in the enrolment notice, and the onboarding agent. See [Patients & Cohort](/cms/services/patients#what-happens-when-a-patient-is-added). |
| **What this service looks after** | The conditions and symptoms that decide which red-flag alerts and Ask Medbot referrals reach the service. |
| **Configuration history** | Every change to the service's agent prompts, approval policy, red-flag rules and content, with who made it and when. |
| **Delete service** | The owner only. See [Deleting a service](#5-deleting-a-service). |

An administrator previewing the CMS as another role sees that role's sidebar. See [Choosing & Switching](/cms/administration/switching).

### Role home pages

Every role's home page in a service is its **Patients** page, the [Workstation](/cms/services/workstation) (`/services/<service>/workstation`), whatever template the service was made from. Choosing a service in the context switcher, or on **Where are you working today?** after signing in, opens it. What it opens on depends on the service:

- a service that runs a queue opens on its **Queue** tab, which the front desk, clinicians and allied health each see in their own way (see [The Queue tab](/cms/services/workstation#the-queue-tab))
- an Outpatient service with its queue off opens on its **Patient list**
- an Inpatient service opens on its **Census**, and a Remote service on its **Caseload**

Older addresses (`/services/<service>/queue`, `/services/<service>/appointments`, `/services/<service>/referrals` and the service's old Patients tab) open the matching Workstation tab.

## 5. Deleting a service

The owner can delete a service from the **Delete service** section of its settings. This permanently removes it with its staff, roles, agents, cohort membership, encounter notes and message history. It cannot be undone. Patients stay in the organisation's patient directory.

A service that still has demo patients cannot be deleted until you remove them first. See [Demo Services](/cms/services/demo-services#remove-or-reset-the-demo).

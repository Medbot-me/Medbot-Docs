# Creating a Service

## 1. Start a new service

In Medbot CMS, open the context switcher at the top of the sidebar and select **All services** (or **Choose a service** in the sidebar, when no service is set). The **Services** list shows each service's name, specialty, setting, **Mode** and status, with a **Continue setup** link next to any of your own drafts. Demo services carry a **Demo** chip.

Click **New Service**. Administrators can also select **New service** in the context switcher. Under **How to start this service**, choose:

- **Blank service**: start empty and set up your care team, protocols, agents and settings step by step.
- **A template** (Chronic disease management, Joint replacement and recovery, Medical retina clinic, or IVF and assisted reproduction): comes fully configured for one clinical area. In a demo organisation it is a demo service, which can be filled with made-up patients; elsewhere it is an ordinary service with a configured starting point. See [Demo Services](/cms/services/demo-services).

For a blank service, fill in:

- **Name**: required, e.g. "Colorectal SOC: Referral to Discharge".
- **Specialty**: optional, e.g. "Colorectal Surgery".
- **Mode of care**: **Outpatient**, **Inpatient**, **Remote** or **Hybrid**. This sets what the Workstation shows. See [Modes of Care](/cms/services/modes-of-care).
- **Setting**: optional, e.g. "SOC, inpatient, primary care".

### AI settings

Below the fields, a closed **AI settings** card ("Optional. Medbot applies these unless you change them, here or later in the service's settings.") shows a summary of its choices and the badge **Recommended defaults**, or **Changed** once you change something. Select **Open** to see the choices:

| Setting | Recommended default |
| --- | --- |
| **Patient agent in the portal**: enrolled patients can chat with the service's assistant | On |
| **Hands over to**: who the agent passes a patient to when it should not answer itself (shown while the patient agent is on) | Nurse |
| **Pre-visit history**: questions patients answer before a booked visit; red flags go to the hand-over role | **Offered when booked** (or **On request**, **Off**) |
| **Education and counselling**: the agent teaches from the content library once you add resources to it | On |
| **Fit check when a patient is added**: an onboarding agent checks the new patient against your referral criteria | On |
| **Agent messages to patients** | **Care team approves first** (or **Send without approval**) |

With **Care team approves first**, someone on the care team approves an agent's reply before it is sent. With **Send without approval**, routine replies are sent as drafted; replies that need clinical judgement are never sent by an agent. Models are Medbot's recommended ones; change them per agent on the service's **Agents** page after it is made. **Reset to defaults** puts every choice back. Left closed, the service is made with the recommended defaults, and every choice can be changed later in the service's settings.

Click **Create Service**. Any member of the organisation can create one. The service is created as a `draft`, with:

- four default agents: **Onboarding**, **Health education**, **Financial counselling** and **Patient agent** (see [Service Agents](/cms/services/agents#default-agents))
- default AI encounter settings

and you go straight into the setup wizard. A service created from a template skips the wizard and opens its page as a draft, with a **Finish setting up** card listing what is left; see [Demo Services](/cms/services/demo-services#finish-setting-up).

### Your organisation's first service

When someone sets up a new organisation, or a demo organisation, in the first-sign-in flow (see [Getting Started](/cms/getting-started)), its first administrator is asked to set up its first service at `/onboarding/services`: **Set up your first service**, or **Set up a demo service** in a demo organisation. The step appears only while the organisation has no services.

1. Under **Start from**, choose **Basic service** ("Only the essentials. You choose the care setting, specialty and everything after.") or one of the specialised templates.
2. Under **Service details**, fill in or check the **Service name**, the **Care setting** (**Outpatient**, **Inpatient**, **Remote** or **Hybrid**), the **Specialty** and **Where care happens** (optional). A template fills these in for you ("Filled from the &lt;template&gt; template. You can change any of it."), and anything you change is saved onto the service it makes.
3. Check the **AI settings** card, the same as on [the new service form](#ai-settings). A template sets its own AI settings instead ("This template sets its own AI settings. You can change them in the service's settings after it is made.").
4. Click **Create service** (**Create demo service** in a demo organisation), or **Skip for now**.

The service starts as a draft. A real organisation then goes on to its team step; a demo organisation opens on Home.

## 2. Walk through the setup wizard

The wizard has four steps, shown as a list you can jump between freely, each with a one-line status such as "Just you so far" or "Using defaults". Medbot has filled in a working default for each, so you can publish at any point and come back; select **Finish later** to leave. Only the service's owner (or a Medbot platform administrator) can open the wizard. A service made from a template is further along: its wizard says the template filled in how the service runs and its patient agent, and what is left is the care team and the patients.

### Step 1: Care team

Add people from your organisation to the service. Each joins with the role or roles they already hold in the organisation; there is no role to choose. See [Care Team](/cms/services/care-team).

### Step 2: How the service runs

Plain-language rules every agent in this service follows, each starting from a default written for the service's name and specialty:

- **Protocols and instructions**: who does what, when to escalate, and what agents should tell patients. Medbot turns it into the agent context that every service agent run is given. See [Service Context & Content](/cms/services/context-and-content).
- **What this service looks after**: the **Conditions you treat** and the **Symptoms you assess, even before a diagnosis**, and the patients you do not see (**Patients you don't see**). It decides which red-flag alerts and Ask Medbot referrals reach the service: Medbot will not alert you about, or suggest your service to, the patients you exclude. **Required before you publish**: a service that lists nothing it looks after cannot be published (a demo service is exempt), and the publish buttons say so and link here. Setting it needs the service's owner or a Clinic Admin.
- **Referral criteria**: which referrals the service accepts. The screening agent drafts a verdict against them; a person always decides. See [Referrals](/cms/services/referrals#referral-criteria).
- **When a patient is added**: the enrolment notice and the onboarding agent. See [Patients & Cohort](/cms/services/patients#what-happens-when-a-patient-is-added).

### Step 3: Patient agent

**Chat in the patient portal** is "The only decision this step needs. Everything else starts from a working default." Its switch decides whether enrolled patients can chat with the service's patient agent in the patient portal. Below it, **Ready to use as it is** describes what is already in place, for example "Default patient agent, hands over to Nurse, emergency message points to" the emergency number. See [Patient Agent](/cms/services/patient-agent).

Under **Advanced AI settings** ("Optional. Open a section only if the defaults do not suit this service."), each row is closed, with a one-line summary and a **Default** badge while it still holds Medbot's recommended value. Select **Open** on a row to change it:

| Row | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Hand-over and emergencies** | The patient agent's hand-over settings. Its summary shows the role it hands over to, the staff hold time in minutes and the emergency number. See [Patient Agent](/cms/services/patient-agent). |
| **Education and counselling** | The service's content library, where one set of references and education materials is added once, and the settings for the education encounter. See [Service Context & Content](/cms/services/context-and-content#content-library) and [AI Encounters](/cms/services/encounters#settings). |
| **Pre-visit history** | The questions patients answer before a booked visit, and where any red flags go. See [Pre-visit History & Escalations](/cms/services/pre-visit-and-escalations). |
| **Education outreach after a visit** | **On** or **Off**. Off is the default. |
| **Agents and models** | Agent instructions, models and subagents are on the service's **Agents** page; setup does not need them. See [Service Agents](/cms/services/agents). |

### Step 4: Patients and publish

**What patients will get** summarises the care team, protocols, patient agent, education, pre-visit history and the notice sent when a patient is added, so you can check it before anyone is enrolled. **Add patients** adds them to the cohort from the organisation's patient directory; each gets the enrolment notice as soon as they are added. This step is deliberately **last**, so everything else is in place before real patients start receiving notices. See [Patients & Cohort](/cms/services/patients). In a demo organisation, the step also has the **Demo patients** panel.

Select **Publish service** to publish (from `draft` to `published`), or **Keep as draft**. Every step remains editable afterwards. Earlier links to the seven-step wizard's steps open the step that now holds their content.

## 3. Continue setup later

If you left the wizard, the **Services** list shows a **Continue setup** link next to your own drafts, and a service made from a template shows a **Finish setting up** card on its page, with **Mark setup complete**.

## 4. The service page

Every page of a service is on the **Home** tab. A service's page has no tab bar: the pages of the service you chose in the context switcher sit at the top of the sidebar, under the service's name (with **Draft** beside it while it is a draft). Opening a service's page from a link shows that service's pages instead. The page also shows the service's status and **Mode of care** as badges.

The sidebar lists only the pages your role uses. **Home** comes first and opens your role's home page (see [Role home pages](#role-home-pages)), which is not listed again under its own name.

| Sidebar item | Shown to | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Setup** | Whoever may set the service up, while it is a draft | The [setup wizard](#2-walk-through-the-setup-wizard). |
| **Home** | Everyone | The service's [Workstation](/cms/services/workstation): today's queue, then the service's other patients, with its other tabs (appointments, referrals and more). |
| **Overview** | Admins | The **Notifications** card with how many are pending for the care team and **Open notifications**, the **Patient agent** summary card, then the **Demo patients** panel (demo services only). **SOP and instructions** and **Referral intake criteria** sit side by side, closed until you select **Open** (**Hide** closes them again), each with a one-line summary such as "3 sections · 412 words." or "Nothing written yet." When the patient agent is on, the summary card shows **Turns (7 days)**, **Pending drafts**, **Open escalations**, **Upcoming appointments** and **Waiting on a patient's choice**. |
| **Notifications** | Everyone | The service's tasks, patient messages and pings. See [Notifications](/cms/clinical-care/notifications#a-services-notifications). |
| **Roster** | Admins, doctors, nurses and receptionists | The service's roster. See [Rostering](/cms/front-desk/rostering). |
| **Board** | Admins, doctors and nurses | The cohort roster on a page of its own (**Cohort board**). |
| **Agents** | Admins | **Agent runs**, **Daily agent schedules**, the **Default agents** cards and the **Agents** table. |
| **AI content library** | Admins, doctors and nurses | The service's documents and pictures. See [Service Context & Content](/cms/services/context-and-content#content-library). |
| **Care team** | Admins, doctors, nurses and receptionists | **Care team**, **Coverage / rota** and **Availability**. |

Admins open the service's settings with the settings button beside the service's name. They open as a window over the page you are on (`/services/<id>/settings`), with sections such as **General**, **Queue and sessions**, **Patient agent**, **Education and counselling**, **Pre-visit history**, **Patient notifications**, **When a patient is added**, **What this service looks after** and **Configuration history**.

The owner can change the name, specialty, mode of care and setting with **Edit service details**.

An administrator previewing the CMS as another role sees that role's sidebar. See [Choosing & Switching](/cms/administration/switching).

### Role home pages

Every role's home page in a service is its **Workstation** (`/services/<id>/workstation`), whatever template the service was made from.

Choosing a service in the context switcher, or on **Where are you working today?** after signing in, opens this page. The top of the page carries a "&lt;Role&gt; home" badge and quick actions for adding people and booking:

- **Add patient**: "Register a patient, then add them to &lt;service&gt;" (roles that may register patients).
- **Add staff**: "Invite someone to the organisation" (roles that may open Staff).
- **Book appointment**: "Find a slot and book a patient in" (receptionists).

A quick action appears only when both your role and the role you are previewing may open its page.

## 5. Deleting a service

The owner can delete a service from **Settings → Danger zone**. This permanently removes it with its care team, agents, cohort membership, encounter notes and message history. It cannot be undone. Patients stay in the organisation's patient directory.

A service that still has demo patients cannot be deleted until you remove them first. See [Demo Services](/cms/services/demo-services#remove-or-reset-the-demo).

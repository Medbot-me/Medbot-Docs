# Encounter Recording

A clinician can **record a consultation for up to an hour** and have AI draft a structured clinical note from it, then review the draft section by section before anything goes into the note.

Recording is available wherever Medbot's notes editor is used: the web app's notes workspace, the [Workstation](/docs/services/workstation) consult screen, and the patient notes editor in Medbot CMS. On the Workstation the same recorder is used for ward rounds in an Inpatient service and remote reviews in a Remote service. Quick dictation is still available next to it.

## Patient consent

Before the recorder or dictation captures anything, Medbot asks you to confirm the patient's consent. The **Patient consent to record** dialog says: "Before recording, ask the patient whether they agree to this consultation being recorded and transcribed to draft the clinical note. Their consent is saved to the record."

1. Ask the patient.
2. Tick "The patient has given verbal consent to record this consultation."
3. Select **Confirm and record** (or **Confirm** for dictation).

Nothing records until the consent is saved. If it cannot be saved, the dialog says so and nothing records. **Cancel** stops without recording. One confirmation covers both the recorder and dictation on the same note for one clinic session; the recorder then shows "Verbal consent to record confirmed at" and the time.

Dictation that is not about a patient, such as teaching a protocol by voice, asks for your own confirmation instead: "I agree to this dictation being recorded and transcribed."

## Recording

1. In the editor, click **Record encounter** ("Record the consultation and draft a clinical note from it"). Allow microphone access if the browser asks.
2. An **AI is listening** pill shows the elapsed time and progress. Use **Pause** and **Resume** as needed.
3. Click **Stop** when the consultation ends. The pill shows **Finishing the recording…**, then **Writing the summary…**.

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Maximum length | 60 minutes. The timer warns in the last 5 minutes, and recording stops by itself at the limit. |
| Transcription | In segments as you go: every 5 minutes, or every 45 seconds on the Workstation consult screen for a near-live transcript. |

**No audio is kept.** Each segment is transcribed and then discarded; only the transcript is stored. If a segment fails to upload or transcribe, you can **Retry** it or **Continue without** it, and the gap is marked in the transcript.

Writing the summary runs in the background, so closing the tab does not lose the recording. Earlier recordings for the note are listed under **Previous sessions**.

## Reviewing the summary

When the summary is ready, **Encounter summary** opens with the **Recorded session** transcript on one side and the **Proposed clinical note** on the other.

The note is drafted under these headings:

- Presenting complaint
- History of presenting complaint
- Past medical / surgical history
- Medications
- Allergies
- Family history
- Social history
- Patient concerns
- Examination
- Assessment
- Plan
- To clarify: shown as **Confirm before signing**, for anything the AI was unsure of

Edit any section, then:

- **Add to note** on a section inserts just that section into the note, as headings and bullets.
- **Add all** (or **Add remaining N**) inserts everything not yet added.
- **Regenerate** drafts the summary again from the same transcript.
- **Discard** drops the draft.

A recording belongs to the note it was made in. If you switch to a different note, inserting is blocked ("This recording was made for a different note") until you switch back.

## For administrators

Transcription uses the **default audio model**, managed by an administrator under **Audio Models** (`/admin/audio-models`), where one model is marked with **Set as default**. The summary is written by the platform's default language model.

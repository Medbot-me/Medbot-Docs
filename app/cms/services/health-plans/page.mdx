# Health Plans

Patients keep a **health plan** in the patient portal: goals such as fewer sweet drinks a week or 150 minutes of exercise, tracked against what they log. See [Health Plan](/patient/health-plan) for the patient's side. A service meets these plans in two ways:

- **A patient sends you their plan for review.** It lands in the Workstation's **Health plans** tab. You reply with advice and suggested changes, and the patient accepts or keeps each one.
- **You propose a plan after a visit.** From the patient's **Health plan** tab you set medical targets and lifestyle goals. The patient starts the plan in the portal and chooses whether to share their progress with you.

Only a clinic can set a **medical target**: blood pressure, LDL cholesterol, HbA1c or fasting glucose. Once a patient accepts one, they can pause it but not change it.

## Reviewing a plan a patient sent

Patients can send their plan to services that take concerns from patients. Turn that on under **Settings → Concerns from patients not booked here**. See [Pre-visit History & Escalations](/docs/services/pre-visit-and-escalations).

1. Open **Workstation → Health plans**. It lists plans under **Waiting** (with a count) and **Replied** (the last 30 days). Each card shows the patient, how many goals, and whether they shared 30 days of their log.
2. Select a plan. The detail shows when it was sent, **The patient's message**, **Their goals now**, and **Their last 30 days, as shared** when the patient shared their log. **Open the patient's page** goes to the patient in this service.
3. Select **Take this one** so colleagues know you have it. The patient sees "A clinician is looking at it".
4. For each goal, change the **Suggested target**, or select **Suggest removal** (**Keep this goal** undoes it). Add **Why** if you like; the patient reads it.
5. **Add a goal** suggests a new one, including a medical target.
6. Write **Your advice** "in words they will read", and select **Send to patient**.

The patient's goals change only when they accept each suggestion. If the patient has ended the plan since sending it, you see their goals as sent and can only advise.

Under the plan, a line says whether a note was filed in the patient's chart. A note is filed only when the patient is linked to your clinic; otherwise the plan is only here.

## Proposing a plan after a visit

A service patient's page has a **Health plan** tab.

- If the patient has not connected their Medbot account to your clinic, it says **Not connected to Medbot**, and a plan cannot reach them.
- If they share a plan with this service, **Their plan, shared with this service** shows each active goal with its target and progress, "from what the patient logs and their results, as they see it".
- **Your latest plan for them** shows the last plan you sent, when, and what became of it.

To propose one, use **Start a health plan**:

1. Start from a template, **Cholesterol and weight**, **Blood pressure at home** or **Pre-diabetes**, or select **Suggest from results**. Medbot reads the patient's recent cholesterol, HbA1c, glucose, weight and blood pressure results in your chart and proposes targets, saying which results it used. Check every target before you send it.
2. Adjust the goals under **Medical targets** and **Lifestyle goals**, or **Add a goal**.
3. Write **Your note to the patient**, for example what you agreed at the visit.
4. Select **Send to patient**.

The patient gets a notification and starts the plan in the portal. If they already keep a plan, your goals reach them as suggestions on it instead. One proposed plan can wait for a patient at a time.

### From the end of a consult

When the patient is on the portal, the Workstation's **End consult** dialog has a box, **Propose a health plan next**. Tick it, and ending the consult opens the patient's **Health plan** tab with **Suggest from results** already run, ready for you to check and send. See [Workstation](/docs/services/workstation#ending-the-consult).

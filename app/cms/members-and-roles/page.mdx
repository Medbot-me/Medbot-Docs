# Members & Roles

Staff belong to an organisation through a **membership**. A membership is one role for one person: it carries the person's name, email, role, professional registration number where the role has one, their Practitioner record on that organisation's Medplum, and whether it is active. In Singapore organisations, a doctor's membership can also hold their NRIC or FIN.

A person can hold several roles in the same organisation, as several memberships under one sign-in. See [One person, several roles](#one-person-several-roles).

Members are managed from `/staff`, which lists the current organisation's members and, for admins, the [pending access requests](/cms/join-an-organisation#resolving-requests-admins) above them.

## Roles

An organisation uses four clinic roles. Each membership has exactly one; a person who needs two holds two memberships.

| Role | Shown as | Broadly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `admin` | Clinic Admin | Full clinic administration: staff, patients, appointments, locations and sessions, displays, queue, clinical records, formulary, documents, results and the review rota. |
| `receptionist` | Receptionist | Front desk: registering patients, appointments, today's session setup, the queue, patient documents, diagnostic result upload and viewing. |
| `doctor` | Doctor | Clinical work: patient and appointment reads, vitals, vaccinations, formulary, documents, referrals, medical certificates (only a Doctor issues them), and acknowledging, reassigning or withdrawing results. |
| `nurse` | Nurse | As `doctor`, minus referrals, medical certificates and results acknowledgement. |

The full matrix of what each role can do lives in [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

The same four roles are used across Medbot. In the web app, a person's role on a [Service](/docs/services) care team is their role in the organisation, not a separate service role.

A fifth role, `superuser`, exists for internal and testing accounts. It passes every role check. It is not a clinic role anyone should be given in normal use; on staging it can be created by [CSV seeding](/cms/seed-data) for test accounts.

Separately, a **platform superadmin** is a Medbot-wide role on the user account, not a membership, used to run [Medbot-hosted organisations](/cms/hosted-organisations#for-medbot-platform-administrators). It gives no access to any clinic's data by itself.

## Adding a member

An admin clicks **Add Staff** and, in the **Add staff member** dialog, enters:

- **Email**: the address they will sign in with
- **First name** and **Last name**
- **Role**
- **Registration number**, depending on the role:

| Role | Field | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `doctor` | **Medical License Number** (MCR number) | `M`, five digits and a letter, e.g. `M12345A` |
| `nurse` | **Nursing Registration Number** (SNB number) | 4 to 15 letters or digits, e.g. `N123456` |
| `admin`, `receptionist` | None | |

The number is checked as it is typed ("MCR number must look like M12345A."), and stored upper-case without spaces.

- **NRIC or FIN (optional)**: doctors in **Singapore** organisations only. It is needed before that doctor's SmartCMS claims can be submitted. It is checksum-validated, and only ever shown masked (`*****567D`) once saved.

Adding a member creates their Practitioner record on the organisation's own Medplum alongside their membership row, with the registration number as an identifier, so they can act clinically from their first sign-in. The NRIC or FIN stays on the membership only and is never written to the Practitioner record. If any step fails, the ones before it are undone rather than leaving a half-created member.

**Add Staff** is only for people the organisation does not have yet. Adding someone already on the staff, or already invited, is refused with a message pointing to their row: give an existing member a second role with **Add another role** (below), not by adding them again.

If the person already has a Medbot account under that email they are linked immediately; otherwise the membership waits for them to sign up. See [Join an Organisation](/cms/join-an-organisation). To add many people at once, use [Seeding Staff & Patients](/cms/seed-data).

## Changing a role, registration or status

From each member's row, an admin can change that role, give the person another role, correct their registration, or deactivate that role.

- **Edit role** changes the row's role into one the person does not already hold. If the change would drop a registration number (for example Doctor to Clinic Admin), the dialog warns first and suggests **Add another role** instead.
- **Edit registration** opens "Edit registration — &lt;name&gt;" to correct the MCR or SNB number and, for a Singapore doctor, add or replace the NRIC or FIN ("On file: *****567D. Enter a new one to replace it."). A new registration number is written to the member's Practitioner record. The NRIC or FIN is not.
- Changing someone's role **to doctor** requires a medical license number.

Two rules are always enforced:

- **An organisation always keeps at least one active admin.** Deactivating or demoting the last one is refused.
- **Nobody deactivates themselves**, in any of their roles.

Deactivating a membership ends that role's access. A person with another active role in the organisation keeps working in it. Their account is only locked out entirely if they have no active membership anywhere else: a doctor who covers two clinics and leaves one keeps working at the other.

The steps and messages are on [Staff](/cms/staff#changing-a-role-or-registration-number).

The details of the Singapore registration fields are on [Singapore Registration](/cms/smartcms/registration).

## One person, several roles

A person can hold more than one role in the same organisation, for example Doctor and Clinic Admin. Each role is its own membership: its own row on `/staff`, its own active or deactivated status, and its own registration number. All of a person's roles in an organisation share one sign-in and one Practitioner record.

- An admin gives an existing member a role with **Add another role** on their row. The person must have joined (not only been invited), and the row must be active.
- The same role cannot be held twice. A deactivated role is reactivated, not added again.
- A registration number belongs to its registry, not to the person. The Doctor role carries the MCR number and the Nurse role the SNB number; each is entered for that role, and a Clinic Admin or Receptionist role carries none.
- The person works as one role at a time and [switches between them](/cms/switching#switching-role) with the role menu at the top of the screen. Permissions are those of the active role only; they do not add up across roles.

For example, a doctor who also runs the clinic works as Clinic Admin to manage staff and as Doctor to issue a medical certificate.

## One person, several organisations

Memberships are independent. The same email can be a member of several organisations with different roles in each. Within an organisation, an email holds each role at most once. Every actor recorded on a clinical or operational write (who performed a stock movement, who acted on a queue ticket, who owns an order set) is the member: the person, in the role they were working as. It is resolved server-side from the caller's own session, and the role can only ever be one of their own active roles in that organisation.

# Members & Roles

Staff belong to an organisation through a **membership**. The membership carries the person's name, email, role, licence number where the role requires one, their Practitioner record on that organisation's Medplum, and whether they are active.

Members are managed from `/staff`, which lists the current organisation's members and — for admins — the [pending access requests](/cms/join-an-organisation#resolving-requests-admins) above them.

## Roles

An organisation uses four clinic roles. Each membership has exactly one.

| Role | Broadly |
| --- | --- |
| `admin` | Full clinic administration: staff, patients, appointments, locations and sessions, displays, queue, clinical records, formulary, documents, results and the review rota. |
| `receptionist` | Front desk: registering patients, appointments, today's session setup, the queue, patient documents, diagnostic result upload and viewing. |
| `doctor` | Clinical work: patient and appointment reads, vitals, vaccinations, formulary, documents, referrals, medical certificates, and acknowledging, reassigning or withdrawing results. |
| `nurse` | As `doctor`, minus referrals, medical certificates and results acknowledgement. |

The full matrix of what each role can do lives in [Roles & Access](/docs/medbot-cms/roles-and-access).

A fifth role, `superuser`, exists for internal and testing accounts. It passes every role check, and is not a clinic role anyone should be given in normal use.

## Adding a member

An admin adds a member with:

- **Email** — the address they will sign in with
- **First name** and **Last name**
- **Role**
- **Licence number**, where the chosen role requires one (a doctor does)

Adding a member creates their Practitioner record on the organisation's own Medplum alongside their membership row, so they can act clinically from their first sign-in. A duplicate email in the same organisation is rejected. If any step fails, the ones before it are undone rather than leaving a half-created member.

If the person already has a Medbot account under that email they are linked immediately; otherwise the membership waits for them to sign up — see [Join an Organisation](/cms/join-an-organisation).

## Changing a role or deactivating

An admin can change a member's role, update their profile, or deactivate them. Two rules are enforced:

- **An organisation always keeps at least one active admin.** Deactivating or demoting the last one is refused.
- **Nobody deactivates themselves.**

Deactivating a member ends their access to that organisation. Their account is only locked out entirely if they have no active membership anywhere else — a doctor who covers two clinics and leaves one keeps working at the other.

A licence number change is synced to the member's Practitioner record.

## One person, several organisations

Memberships are independent. The same email can be a member of several organisations with a different role in each, and an email is unique *within* an organisation rather than globally. Every actor recorded on a clinical or operational write — who performed a stock movement, who acted on a queue ticket, who owns an order set — is the member, resolved server-side from the caller's own session, never something a client supplies.

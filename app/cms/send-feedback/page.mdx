# Send Feedback

Staff can send the Medbot team feedback, a question, a complaint, a suggestion or a comment about Medbot CMS from any screen. A person on the Medbot team reads every message.

Use [Contact support](/docs/admin/billing-and-support) for help with your organisation's account or plan, and **Report a problem** in the web app for something that is broken.

## Where to find it

Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar (your name, then **…**) and select **Send feedback**. The form opens over the screen you are on.

## Send a message

1. Choose **Feedback**, **Enquiry**, **Complaint**, **Suggestion** or **Comment**.
2. Your name and email are filled in from your account.
3. Choose how you'd like a reply: **Email**, **Phone call**, **WhatsApp** or **Any**. A phone call or WhatsApp needs your phone number.
4. Write the message. Don't include patient details or NRIC numbers.
5. Select **Send message**. You'll get a reference like `FB-7K3Q9M`.

If you untick **Medbot may use these details to reply to me**, the form tells you the team won't be able to reply, and asks you to confirm with **Send without a reply**. Your contact details are then not sent.

## What is attached

The message carries the screen you were on (for example **Patients › Chart**), the organisation you were working in, the app version, your browser and window size, and any errors the app showed you just before. Record ids in the address are hidden as `:id`, so it never says which patient you had open. Select **See what's attached** before sending to see it all.

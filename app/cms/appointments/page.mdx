# Appointments

Appointments is the clinic's list of booked visits. Find it at **Scheduling → Appointments** (`/appointments`). Receptionists and Clinic Admins book, edit, and cancel appointments and answer booking requests from the patient portal. Doctors and nurses can look at the list but not change it.

## Who can do this

| Task | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See the appointment list | Yes | Yes | View only | View only |
| Book a new appointment | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Book with the agent | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Edit or cancel an appointment | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Answer a booking request | Yes | Yes | No | No |

A doctor or nurse who opens a page for booking or booking requests sees a message that says which roles are allowed.

## Finding appointments

The list shows every appointment with the patient, practitioner, date, time, duration, service, and status. Narrow it with the patient search box, the practitioner filter, and the date filter. All three apply together. Select **Clear date** to remove the date filter.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Booked** | Confirmed and upcoming. |
| **Arrived** | The patient has checked in. |
| **Fulfilled** | The visit is finished. |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled. |
| **No-show** | The patient did not come. |

Times are always shown in Singapore time, whatever the time zone of your computer.

## Booking an appointment

1. Select **New Appointment**.
2. Search for the patient and select them. Their details appear below the search box.
3. Choose the **Clinic location**, the **Date**, and the **Time**. Times run from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM in half hour steps.
4. Choose the **Duration**: 15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 minutes.
5. Choose the **Practitioner**. Only doctors and nurses who can work at the chosen location are listed, doctors first. A nurse is shown with **(nurse-led visit)** after their name. See [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup) and [Nurse-led visits](#nurse-led-visits).
6. Choose the **Service type**. You can also choose a **Reason** and add **Notes**.
7. Select **Book appointment**.

You return to the appointment list. The new appointment shows as **Booked**.

> **Note:** The patient search box in the booking form currently lists only the first 20 patients of your organization. A patient beyond that cannot be found by name there.

### Nurse-led visits

Book a nurse as the **Practitioner** for a visit that a nurse runs without a doctor. The nurse leads the visit:

- At check-in the visit stays with the nurse. No doctor is rotated onto it. See [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue#nurse-led-visits).
- The lead nurse starts the visit and closes it with **Complete visit**.
- A doctor can also close it. If a doctor sees the patient during the visit, it becomes a doctor-led visit.
- A Clinic Admin can close any visit, and can hand an open visit to another doctor or nurse with **Change lead** on the queue.

In Singapore organisations, a scheme claim for a nurse-led visit is held for an administrator. See [Claims Workflow](/cms/smartcms/claims).

### Two bookings at the same time

A booking is refused when the patient or the practitioner already has an appointment that overlaps the time. The form stays open and shows a message such as "Marcus Lim already has an appointment that overlaps this time". The message shows the times in UTC, which is eight hours behind Singapore time. Choose a different time. The same check applies when you edit an appointment and when you accept a booking request.

### Booking with the agent

Select **Use agent** to book by describing the visit in words. The agent looks up the patient and the doctor and suggests a booking. A staff member reviews the suggestion and approves it before anything is booked.

## Responding to portal booking requests

Patients can ask for a visit from the [patient portal](/patient/visits). A request is not a booking. It waits in **Booking requests** until staff answer it.

1. Select **Booking requests**. The page counts the requests waiting.
2. Read the **Requested date**, the **Time preference**, and the **Reason**.
3. Under **Exact appointment time**, set the **Date**, **Time**, and **Duration**, and choose the **Assigned clinician**. As when booking, a nurse is listed with **(nurse-led visit)**.
4. Choose one of the answers below.

| Answer | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept and book** | Books the appointment at the time you set. |
| **Counter-propose** | Offers the patient a different time. Fill in the time and the clinician first. A note is optional. |
| **Reject** | Declines the request. A **Review note** is required. |

What the patient sees: after **Counter-propose**, the patient's request shows **Clinic proposed a new time**, your note, and the buttons **Accept proposed time** and **Decline proposed time**. When the patient accepts, the appointment appears in your list as **Booked** and the request leaves **Booking requests**. The request card does not update by itself, so reload the page to see it go.

> **Note:** An appointment made from an accepted counter-proposal currently shows **Your doctor** as its practitioner, not the doctor's name.

## Editing and cancelling

Select the three dot button at the end of an appointment row, then choose **Edit** or **Cancel appointment**.

**Edit** opens a form with the **Date**, **Time**, **Duration**, **Practitioner**, **Service type**, **Reason**, and **Notes**. You cannot change the patient or the location. Select **Save changes**.

**Cancel appointment** asks you to confirm. The appointment shows as **Cancelled**. A cancelled appointment can be reopened from the [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue).

Cancelled, no-show, and fulfilled appointments have no menu, so they cannot be edited or cancelled.

# Appointments

`/appointments` is where `receptionist` and `admin` book, review, and manage appointments; `doctor` and `nurse` get read-only access to the same list. It's also where staff review and respond to booking requests submitted through the [patient portal](/cms/patient-portal).

## Finding appointments

Narrow the list with the patient search box, the practitioner filter, and the date filter — all three apply together, and **Clear date** removes the day filter.

## Booking directly

Select **New Appointment** and choose the patient, doctor, date, time, and duration. **Use agent** offers the same booking flow with guided, conversational assistance instead of filling the form by hand.

## Responding to portal booking requests

Open **Booking requests** to review requests submitted from the patient portal. For each one, set the exact date, time, duration, and assigned doctor, then:

- **Accept and book** — confirms the request as a real appointment.
- **Counter-propose** — suggests a different time with a note; the patient sees it in their portal and can accept or decline it.
- **Reject** — declines the request with a note.

## Editing and cancelling

Use an appointment's row menu to edit its details or cancel it. Cancelled and completed appointments have no row menu — a closed appointment can't be edited or cancelled after the fact.

## Slot-conflict checking

Booking or moving an appointment is refused, server-side, whenever the patient or the doctor already has an overlapping appointment — on the booking form, on an edit/reschedule, and on booking-request acceptance alike. This is a fail-closed check: if it can't be verified, the booking doesn't go through. The fix is always to pick a different time, not to work around the conflict.

## Eligibility

The doctor dropdown only lists doctors eligible for the appointment's clinic location — see [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup#how-eligibility-rules-apply-elsewhere) for how location eligibility is determined. Appointment times always display in the clinic's Singapore timezone regardless of browser or server timezone.

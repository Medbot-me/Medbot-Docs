# Reception Queue

The reception queue is today's list of booked and checked-in patients at one clinic location. Find it at **Scheduling → Queue** (`/queue`). Receptionists and Clinic Admins run it. Doctors and nurses see a shorter view of their own work.

## Who can do this

| Task | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See today's queue | Yes | Yes | View only | View only |
| Check a patient in | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Call a patient to a room | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Route a patient to a stage | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Take payment, void a payment, or mark the visit paid | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Assign or change the doctor | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Cancel an appointment, mark a no-show, or reopen | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Arrange a session for the patient | No | No | Yes | No |
| Complete the visit and send the outcome | No | No | Yes | No |
| See the CHAS badge and select **Verify now** (Singapore) | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Doctors see only the patients assigned to them. Nurses see every patient at the location and open the vitals form from their own view. Doctors and nurses do not see the **Bill** column or any payments. The **Arrange session** and **Complete visit** buttons appear on a doctor's row only after a consultation has been started for that appointment.

## Reading the queue

The page header shows the location, today's session with an **Open** or **Closed** badge, how many doctors are on duty, and a **Self check-in code**. If nobody has opened a session yet, it says **No session yet today**. Select **Create session** to open one, or see [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup).

The table lists patients in this order:

1. Patients reception has moved to a fixed place in the queue.
2. Patients who have arrived, earliest arrival first.
3. Patients who are booked but have not arrived, by appointment time.
4. Completed and cancelled visits, at the bottom.

Each row shows the ticket, the time, the patient, the NRIC or passport, the reason, the doctor, the workflow controls, the **Bill**, and the status. On a narrow screen some columns, including **Bill**, are hidden. Turn them on from the **View** menu.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Booked, not yet checked in. |
| **Waiting** | Checked in and waiting. |
| **Consultation** | A consultation has been started for this appointment. |
| **Done** | The visit is finished. |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled or marked as a no-show. |

The page updates itself about every four seconds, and the line under the title shows when it last updated. Nobody needs to reload it. Select **Refresh** to update it at once.

Four filters narrow the list: the location, the doctor (**All doctors**, **Unassigned**, or one doctor), the stage (**All stages**, **Not yet routed**, or one stage), and the status (**Active (hide Done)**, **Waiting only**, **Show cancelled**, or **All**).

## Checking a patient in

1. Choose the location at the top of the page.
2. Find the patient's row and select **Check in**.

A message such as "Kamala Perera checked in" appears, with the ticket number and the doctor, for example "Ticket #1 · Marcus Lim". The row now shows the ticket and the status **Waiting**.

Check-in records the arrival time, gives the patient a queue ticket from the location's open session, and assigns the next doctor marked active in [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup). Doctors take turns in the order they were marked active. If no doctor is marked active, the patient still gets a ticket but no doctor. Checking in a patient who already has a ticket keeps the same ticket and does not move the rotation on.

Check-in has no time window. A patient booked for later in the day can be checked in as soon as they arrive.

Patients can also check themselves in from the [patient portal](/patient/visits). They either share their location or type the six digit **Self check-in code** shown in the page header. A self check-in works like a staff check-in, and the row appears in the queue with a ticket.

### CHAS verification at check-in (Singapore)

For a Singapore organisation that subscribes to CHAS, checking a patient in also asks MHCP for their CHAS card. This happens on the first check-in of the appointment, from the desk or the portal, at a location connected for CHAS. Nobody has to press anything. Verification never blocks check-in. If it fails, the patient is still checked in.

The result shows on the CHAS badge under the patient's name in their chart. Every role sees it. The badge shows one of:

- the card's name
- **CHAS not verified**
- **No CHAS card**
- the card name followed by **expired**

For a valid card, the badge also shows when it is valid, the chronic tier, the chronic and acute balances for this year, and the visits left at this clinic. "checked" and a date and time, in Singapore time, show when MHCP was last asked.

Select **Verify now** on the badge to ask MHCP again. Any role can. Medbot asks at the clinic of the patient's visit in today's queue session. If the patient is not on today's queue, it asks at the organisation's default location. A successful check says, for example, "Verified at Main Clinic." A failed one says why and changes nothing else:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Not verified: the patient has no Singapore NRIC (S or T) recorded." | Record the patient's NRIC in their chart. See [Patient Records & Consent](/cms/patient-records). |
| "Not verified: … is not connected to SmartCMS for CHAS. It needs an HE code and CHAS switched on in the SmartCMS settings." | Ask a Clinic Admin to finish the location's [SmartCMS settings](/cms/smartcms/settings). |
| "Not verified: SmartCMS is switched off on this Medbot server." | Nothing is sent from this server. Contact Medbot. |
| "Not verified: the patient's details could not be sent to MHCP. Check the NRIC." | Correct the patient's NRIC, then select **Verify now**. |
| "Not verified: MHCP's answer could not be read. Try again later." | Select **Verify now** later. |
| "Not verified: the SmartCMS gateway did not accept the call from Main Clinic. Try again later." | Select **Verify now** later. |

When you bill the visit, the CHAS panel warns if the card is not verified, was verified more than 30 days ago or in an earlier year, or is not valid on the visit date. MHCP is asked again when the bill is issued. See [Claims Workflow](/cms/smartcms/claims#1-check-in-and-chas-verification).

## Calling a patient to a room

1. On a checked-in row, select **Call**.
2. Choose a room in **Choose a room**, then select **Call**.

A message such as "Called MAIN001 to Consult 1" appears, and the row shows **Called → Consult 1**. Only rooms set up for today's session are listed. The waiting room display shows the ticket and the room, and a room door screen shows the ticket for its own room. See [Patient-Facing Displays](/cms/display-boards). The room label stays until you route the patient to another stage.

## Assigning the doctor

Use the doctor menu on the row to change the doctor. A doctor chosen by hand shows **Manual**. A doctor chosen by the rotation shows **Auto-rotated**.

## Routing a patient

The **Route…** menu on a checked-in row moves the patient through the clinic: **Nurse**, **Doctor**, **Investigations**, **Dispensary**, **Completed**. Use it to show where the patient is. The nurse's own view shows a patient routed to the nurse as **Vitals now**, and a patient routed on to the doctor as **Ready for doctor**.

## Taking payment

Take payment from the **Bill** column. It has a button on rows that are **Waiting**, **Consultation**, or **Done**, so a patient can pay on the way out after the doctor has finished. **Done** rows are hidden by default; set the status filter to **All** to see them. The button reads **Take payment** until the visit is paid, then **✓ Paid**.

1. Select **Take payment** (or **✓ Paid**) on the patient's row. The **Payment from** dialog opens with the patient's name.
2. Check what is owed. See [What the patient owes](#what-the-patient-owes).
3. Check the **Amount**, which is in the bill's currency, for example **Amount (SGD)**. It starts at what is still owed. Change it if the patient pays only part of it now.
4. Choose the **Method**: **Cash**, **PayNow**, **NETS**, **Card**, or **Other**.
5. For any method other than cash, you can enter a **Reference (optional)**, such as the slip or transaction number (up to 64 characters).
6. Select **Record payment**.

If the payments now cover what is owed, the dialog closes and the row shows **✓ Paid**. Otherwise the dialog stays open, says "Payment recorded.", lists the payment, and shows what is **Still owed**. Take the rest the same way, now or later.

Enter what the patient pays towards the bill, not the note they hand over. If the amount is more than is owed, the dialog warns, for example, "$5.00 more than is owed. Enter what is paid towards the bill; change handed back is not part of the payment." You can still record it, and the dialog then shows the visit as **Overpaid**. An amount that is not a number, is zero, or is too large shows "Enter an amount such as 41.50, up to $1,000,000.00."

Each payment is listed with its amount, method, reference, the time, and who recorded it. Taking payment does not print a receipt. For a receipt, print the visit's invoice from [Billing](/cms/billing#finding-and-reprinting-invoices).

### What the patient owes

The top of the dialog sets out what the patient owes on the visit's bill:

| Line | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Cash due on the bill** | The bill's **Patient pays (cash)** amount. It reads **No bill yet** if the visit has not been billed, or **Could not be read** if the bill could not be read just now. |
| **Payers refused or paid short** | What a payer refused or paid less than the claim asked for. Only shown when there is some. |
| **Subsidy whose claim was discarded** | A subsidy the bill counted on, whose claim staff discarded. Only shown when there is some. |
| **Marked paid before amounts were recorded** | For a visit marked paid before payments were recorded with amounts, the cash due is counted as already paid. |
| **Paid so far** | The total of the payments taken, not counting voided ones. |
| **Still owed** or **Overpaid** | The difference. Not shown when it is zero. |

While a payer has not yet answered a claim on the bill, the subsidy is assumed to be paid and the dialog says "A payer has yet to answer a claim on this bill, so what is owed may still change."

In Singapore, **Claims today**, opened from the claims queue, shows the same figure for each checked-in patient at a location. See [SmartCMS Claims](/cms/smartcms/claims).

### When the visit is marked paid

Medbot marks the visit paid itself when the payments cover what is owed on an issued bill. Payments alone never mark a visit paid without a bill to compare them with.

- **No bill yet.** A payment can be taken before the visit is billed, for example a deposit. It is kept, but the visit is not marked paid: "Payment recorded. This visit has no bill yet, so it is not marked paid. It can be marked paid once the bill is issued and the payments cover it."
- **Bill could not be read.** The payment is kept and the paid status is left as it was. The dialog says so, for example "Payment recorded. The bill could not be read just now, so the visit is not marked paid: check it once the bill can be read." Open the dialog again later.
- **Payments already cover the bill.** When the bill is issued after the patient paid, or the bill leaves nothing for the patient to pay (for example, a scheme pays it in full), the dialog says "The payments taken already cover the bill." or "Nothing is due on this bill." Select **Mark visit paid**. It is refused if the payments do not cover the bill, for example "The payments taken do not cover the bill: SGD 12.00 is still owed."

The paid status is checked again only when someone records or voids a payment, or selects **Mark visit paid**. If a payer refuses a claim after the visit was marked paid, the row still shows **✓ Paid**. Open the dialog to see whether anything is still owed.

### Voiding a payment

A payment cannot be edited. If one was entered wrongly, void it and enter it again.

1. Open the dialog and select **Void** on the payment.
2. Optionally enter a **Reason (optional)** (up to 200 characters).
3. Select **Void payment**, or **Keep** to leave it.

The dialog says "Payment voided." The payment stays in the list, struck through, with who voided it and the reason. If the payments left no longer cover what is owed, the visit goes back to unpaid. Any Receptionist or Clinic Admin can void any payment.

### Payment messages

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "This payment was already recorded, so it was not entered a second time." | Nothing. Recording the same payment again, for example after a slow connection, does not enter it twice. |
| "This payment was already entered and has since been voided. Check the visit’s payments, then enter it again if it should stand." | Check the list, then enter the payment again if it is needed. |
| "This payment attempt was already used for a different payment. Close the window and enter the payment again." | Close the dialog, open it again, and enter the payment. |
| "This visit’s bill is in SGD; a payment cannot be in USD." | Payments must be in the bill's currency. Close the dialog and open it again. |
| "This payment was not found, or is already voided." | Close the dialog and open it again to see the current list. |
| "This visit has no bill yet, so there is nothing to settle the payments against." | Generate the invoice in [Billing](/cms/billing), then select **Mark visit paid**. |
| "The bill could not be read just now. Try again in a moment." | Try again later. |
| "No visit found for this appointment — it may not be in today’s queue yet." | Check the patient in first. |

## Cancelling, marking a no-show, and reopening

1. Select the ellipsis button on the row.
2. Choose **Cancel appointment** or **Mark no-show**, then confirm.

The patient leaves the active queue. To find the row again, set the status filter to **Show cancelled**. Select **Reopen** to put the patient back. A reopened patient gets a new ticket and a new place in the queue.

## What the doctor and nurse see

A doctor opens **Today's Patients**. Each row has **Start consultation**, which opens the patient's chart at **Encounters** for that appointment, or **Continue consultation** once one has started. When the doctor starts the encounter there, the **Start Encounter** dialog says "Create a new encounter for this patient's visit in today's queue." The encounter is linked to that exact queue appointment, which moves the row to **Consultation** and shows **Complete visit**. If the visit already has an open encounter, Medbot reuses it rather than starting a second. A finished visit's row shows **Review** instead. An encounter started later from that chart is not added to the finished visit. It goes to the patient's open visit in the queue, if they have one. See [Encounters & Clinical Notes](/cms/encounters). A nurse opens **Today's Queue**, and the **Vitals** button on each row opens the vitals form. See [Vitals](/cms/vitals). Neither role can check patients in, change the doctor, or call a room.

## Arranging a session and completing the visit

Once a doctor has started a consultation for an appointment, two buttons appear on that row.

**Arrange session** offers the patient an assistant session for this visit. Choose **Financial counselling** or **Health education**, add a **Reason (shown to the patient)**, and select **Arrange session**. The row then shows a badge with the session type. The patient starts the session from the portal, and does not need to be enrolled in a service. See [Patient Portal](/cms/patient-portal).

**Complete visit** finishes the visit and sends the outcome to the patient.

1. Select **Complete visit**.
2. Optionally select **Generate draft** to have the assistant propose the text from the visit. Check and edit everything it writes.
3. Write the **Findings** in words the patient can read.
4. Under **Medicines started**, select **Add** and enter the medicine name and the dose and instructions.
5. Under **Next steps**, select **Add**, then choose **Follow-up appointment**, **Advice**, or **Test result** and describe what the patient should do. Any session you arranged already appears as an **AI session** step.
6. Turn on **Also notify the patient by SMS** if you want a text message as well.
7. Select **Send to patient**.

The outcome goes to the patient's portal, and the row leaves the active list. Sending the outcome cannot be undone from the queue, so read it through first.

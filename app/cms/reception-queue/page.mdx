# Reception Queue

`/queue` projects today's Medplum appointments into a location-scoped operational queue. Use the location picker (or `?locationId=...`) to switch branches — appointments without a clinic-location extension stay on the default location so legacy bookings remain visible. Medplum stays authoritative for appointment status, patients, practitioners, and linked encounters; Postgres holds only the local operational fields Medplum doesn't model: the clinic session, public queue ticket, manual queue position, arrival timestamp, route stage, payment flag, assignment source, latest called room/timestamp, and the assignment audit trail.

## Checking a patient in

`receptionist` and `admin` can check in scheduled patients, cancel or mark no-show, reopen a cancelled/no-show row, assign doctors, route active patients through `nurse` → `doctor` → `dispensary` → `completed`, and toggle payment between `unpaid`/`paid`. They can also call a checked-in patient to an active room — the latest called room and timestamp drive the public display boards until the patient's route stage changes.

Check-in stamps the first physical arrival time without overwriting it on retries, issues a queue ticket from the location's open session, and assigns the next active session doctor by round-robin. A duplicate check-in reuses the existing ticket rather than advancing the rotation. Reopening a cancelled or no-show appointment runs the same location-aware check-in path: it stamps a fresh arrival time, clears any old manual queue position, issues a valid session ticket, and returns the patient to the active queue at the correct place.

The staff queue polls for updates every four seconds while its tab is visible, so changes made by other staff show up without a manual reload.

## Queue ordering

Ordering is explicit: manual `queue_position` pins sort first, then physically present patients sort ahead of booked patients, and completed/cancelled rows sort last. Present patients order by `arrived_at`; booked patients use their scheduled start time. An active encounter linked to an appointment surfaces as `in-progress` in the queue projection and sets `hasEncounter`, so doctor/nurse actions can continue (or disable) already-started clinical work.

## Doctor and nurse view

Doctors and nurses get a read-only **Today's Patients** view scoped to the patients assigned to them — it refreshes automatically as reception checks people in, and links straight into that patient's encounters. Reassigning a patient or changing their room stays reception's job, not something a doctor or nurse can do from this view.

## Self check-in and the patient's visit journey

Patients can check themselves in from the [patient portal](/patient/visits) instead of queuing at the counter: either by location, when their phone shows they are at the clinic, or by entering the **arrival code**, the 6-digit code shown on the queue page for today's location. Self check-in follows the same path as a staff check-in: it stamps the arrival time and issues a queue ticket. The patient then sees their queue number and where to go next, live, on the portal.

Patients can also be routed through an **Investigations** stage, alongside nurse, doctor and dispensary.

Two actions on the queue table carry the visit through to the patient's portal. Both are for `admin` and `doctor`:

- **Arrange session** offers the patient an AI session for this visit (**Financial counselling** or **Health education**), with a reason that gives the assistant a head start, e.g. "Newly diagnosed hypertension: walk through the medicine and what to watch for." The patient does not need to be enrolled in a Service; the session appears on their portal under the sessions their clinician arranged.
- **Complete visit** finishes the visit and sends the outcome to the patient: **Findings** (written for the patient to read), **Medicines started** (name, dose and instructions) and **Next steps** (such as a follow-up appointment, a test result, advice or an AI session). The form is pre-drafted by AI from the visit, for the doctor to check and edit.

A Service in the web app can follow the same queue from its [Workstation](/docs/services/workstation).

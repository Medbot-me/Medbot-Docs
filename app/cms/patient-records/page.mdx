# Patient Records & Consent

Patient records are the list of everyone registered at your clinic and each person's chart. Find them at **Patients → Patients** (`/patients`). All four roles can open a chart. Front desk staff register patients.

## Who can do this

| Task | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See the patient list and open a chart | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Register a patient | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Invite a patient to self-register | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Deactivate a patient | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Edit contact details | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Record or change communication consent | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Doctors and nurses who open `/patients/new` or `/patients/invite` see the message "This action requires one of the following roles: admin, receptionist."

## Finding a patient

The list shows each patient's name, contact details, birth date, gender, address and status. Search by name only. Phone, email and identifier numbers are not searched. Use the status filter to show **Active**, **Inactive** or **Deceased** patients. The newest updates appear first, 20 patients per page.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/patient-records/01-patients-list.png" alt="The Patients page with the name search box, the status filter and a table of patients" />
_The Patients page_

Patients with no phone or email on file carry a **No contact on file** badge.

## Registering a patient

1. Select **Add Patient**.
2. Choose the **Identifier type**: **NRIC**, **FIN** or **Passport**, then enter the number. The form says **Valid** when the number is acceptable, or explains why not.
3. Enter **Given name**, **Family name** and **Date of birth**. These are required. **Sex** defaults to **Unknown**.
4. Optionally add a phone number, an email address, the **Preferred contact channel** (**Phone** or **Email**), communication consent and an address.
5. Select **Register patient**. The new patient's chart opens.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/patient-records/02-register-patient-form.png" alt="The Register patient form with identifier type, names, date of birth, contact details, consent grid and address" />
_The Register patient form_

Rules the form enforces:

- The identifier must match the selected type. A wrong check digit shows "Identifier doesn't match the selected type (NRIC)." and keeps **Register patient** disabled.
- A patient with the same identifier can exist only once per clinic. Otherwise you see "A patient with this identifier already exists in this clinic," and the message notes that the patient may be archived and points you to the clinic's medical record system to view or restore them. Names are not compared. Search by name first (including the **Inactive** filter) to avoid a second record for the same person. If a matching patient is inactive, restore the existing record rather than registering a new one.
- A Singapore mobile number has 8 digits and starts with 8 or 9. Other numbers start with a plus sign and have 8 to 15 digits. A number with seven or more zeros in a row is refused.
- If consent could not be saved, the patient is still registered. A warning tells you to record consent again from the chart.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/patient-records/03-duplicate-identifier.png" alt="The Register patient form showing the message that a patient with this identifier already exists" />
_The duplicate identifier message_

## The patient chart

The chart opens on **Overview**. Cards on the overview:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Allergies** | Drug allergies and intolerances, or "No known drug allergies". Not shown to Receptionists. See [Allergies & Medication Safety](/cms/allergies-and-medication-safety) |
| **Patient-reported intake** | Answers from a self-registration, only for patients who registered themselves |
| **Demographics** | Birth date, gender, and marital status, preferred language and date of death when recorded |
| **Contact** | Phone and email with a **Preferred** badge, plus **Portal invite**, **Caregivers** and **Edit contact**. See [Caregiver access](/cms/patient-portal#caregiver-access) |
| **Address** | Addresses on file |
| **Identifiers** | Identifier type and number |
| **Communication consent** | Consent by purpose and channel, plus **Edit consent** |
| **Vitals** | See [Vitals](/cms/vitals) |
| **Clinical notes** | See [Encounters & Clinical Notes](/cms/encounters) |

The sidebar lists the other chart sections under the patient's name: **Accounts**, **Encounters**, **Notes**, **Results**, **Vaccinations**, **Documents** and **Messages**. **Accounts** is visible to Clinic Admins and Receptionists only. **Ping staff** at the top of every chart sends a staff ping.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/patient-records/04-chart-overview.png" alt="A patient chart overview with demographics, contact, address, identifiers, consent, vitals and clinical notes cards" />
_A patient chart overview_

## Editing contact details

1. On **Overview**, select **Edit contact**.
2. Change **Phone**, **Email** or **Preferred channel**. Clearing a field removes that entry.
3. Select **Save contact**.

Invalid numbers are refused with "Invalid phone number." or "Invalid email address." If a colleague saved the chart after you opened it, you see "This record changed since you opened it. Please refresh and try again." Refresh the chart and repeat the change.

## Communication consent

Consent controls how the clinic may contact a patient. It is deny by default: the clinic may contact a patient only for the purpose and channel combinations ticked. Purposes are **Appointment reminders**, **Clinical recalls**, **Clinical updates**, **Documents sent to the patient**, **Documents sent to a third party** and **Health promotion**. Channels are **Email**, **SMS** and **WhatsApp**. You can record SMS and WhatsApp consent even when the clinic does not send on those channels yet.

1. On **Overview**, select **Edit consent**.
2. Tick the purpose and channel combinations the patient agreed to.
3. Select **Save consent**. Saving replaces the whole set, so untick anything the patient withdrew.

A purpose with nothing ticked shows **No consent**. Treat any combination not shown as not permitted.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/patient-records/05-edit-consent-dialog.png" alt="The Communication consent dialog with a grid of purposes and channels" />
_The consent grid_

Saving needs your account to be linked to a clinical profile. Otherwise you see "Your account is not linked to a practitioner profile. Contact an administrator." Ask a Clinic Admin for help. If the consent changed after you opened the chart, you see the same "record changed" message as for contact details.

## Inviting a patient to register themselves

Use this for a person who asked for an appointment but is not registered yet.

1. Select **Invite to self-register** on the Patients page. The **Invite to self-register** page opens.
2. Select **Invite to self-register** again, optionally enter a given and family name, choose **SMS** or **Email**, and enter the phone number or email address.
3. Select **Send link**.

The patient receives a one-time link that expires after 7 days. The **Sent registration links** table shows each invite as pending, completed, expired or revoked, and a **View patient** link once completed. If the clinic's text message or email service is not set up, the dialog shows an error and the patient does not receive the link.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/patient-records/07-invite-to-self-register.png" alt="The Send a self-registration link dialog with name fields, SMS or email choice and a phone number field" />
_Sending a registration link_

What the patient sees: a registration form on the patient app. See [Patient Portal](/cms/patient-portal).

When the patient submits the form, their chart shows the **Patient-reported intake** card labeled **Unverified**. It lists drug allergies, reported health conditions, current treatment and medications, and hospitalization history. These answers are patient-reported and not checked against clinical records. Confirm allergies, conditions and medications with the patient before you rely on them. Until a drug allergy is recorded, the **Allergies** card also shows the reported allergies, and doctors and nurses can select **Record this allergy**. See [Allergies & Medication Safety](/cms/allergies-and-medication-safety#allergies-the-patient-reported-at-registration).

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/patient-records/08-patient-reported-intake.png" alt="The Patient-reported intake card on a chart with an Unverified badge, allergies and conditions" />
_The Patient-reported intake card_

## Deactivating a patient

1. On the Patients page, select the actions menu (three dots) on the patient's row.
2. Select **Deactivate patient**, then **Deactivate**.

The patient's history stays intact and the status becomes **Inactive**. An inactive patient can no longer be selected for care: **Start Encounter** is disabled. Use the **Inactive** filter to find them. The CMS has no button to reactivate a patient; if someone was deactivated in error, restore the record in the clinic's medical record system rather than registering them again (which the app refuses as a duplicate).

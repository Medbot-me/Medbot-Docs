# AI Policy

The AI policy decides which AI providers and regions the organisation's AI work may use: its agents, assistants such as the [Home assistant](/cms/home-assistant), and drafting tools. It also sets the organisation's default model. Find it in the **Home** tab at **Organisation → AI policy** (`/ai-policy`).

Until an administrator saves a policy, the organisation has none and its AI calls follow the platform defaults.

## Who can do this

| Task | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See and change the AI policy | Yes | No | No | No |

Only Clinic Admins have **AI policy** in the sidebar. Anyone else who opens `/ai-policy` sees "Only organisation admins can see or change the AI policy." The server refuses them too.

The page works on the organisation you are signed in to. To change another organisation's policy, [switch](/cms/switching) to it first.

## Setting the policy

The page is headed with the organisation's name: "which AI providers and regions this organisation's agents, assistants and drafting tools may use." Under **Providers and regions**:

1. Turn on **Restrict this organisation's AI to the choices below**. Off, "AI calls follow the platform defaults, as before." On, "AI calls made for this organisation use only the providers and regions below."
2. Tick the **Allowed providers**. Each says where it is called, for example "Called in ap-southeast-1.", or "The region it is called in cannot be stated from here."
3. Optionally, type the **Allowed regions**, cloud region codes separated by commas, such as `ap-southeast-1`. Buttons such as **Add ap-southeast-1** add a provider's region for you. Leave the field empty to allow any region of the providers you ticked. With regions listed, only a provider called in one of them can run, so a provider whose region is not known cannot run, and the page says so.
4. Choose a **Default model**, or **The platform default, when the policy allows it**. It is "Used when an agent asks for a model the policy does not allow." The Home assistant always runs on it.
5. Select **Save policy**. "Saved." confirms it, and the page shows when it was last saved (Singapore time) and by whom.

Saving a policy with the switch off stores your choices without applying them. Turn the switch on and save again to apply them.

## Models in the catalogue

**Models in the catalogue** lists every active model the platform offers, with its provider and region, and whether your choices would allow it: **Allowed**, **Provider not allowed** or **Region not allowed**. The list is "Shown as if the policy were on."

With the policy on, an agent that asks for a model the policy does not allow runs on the default model instead. If no model is allowed, the call fails rather than use one the policy forbids.

If something in your choices does not fit together, the page lists the problems in red and **Save policy** stays disabled until you fix them.

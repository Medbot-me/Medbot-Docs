# Getting Started

This page walks a new staff member through signing in, choosing an organization, and finding their way around Medbot CMS. It applies to every role.

## Before you start

You need a Medbot sign-in and a place in an organization. Your Clinic Admin adds you from [Staff](/cms/staff), which emails you an invitation if you have no Medbot sign-in yet, or shares a join code (see [Join an Organization](/cms/join-an-organisation)). If your clinic does not use Medbot yet, see [Create an Organization](/cms/create-organisation).

## 1. Sign in or create your account

Open the CMS. Its public pages (`/`, **Features** and **About**, whose **Contact** section `/contact` now opens) describe Medbot CMS and have **Sign in** and **Set up your clinic** at the top.

### New to Medbot

Select **Set up your clinic**, or **Create an account** under "New to Medbot?" on the sign-in page. **Create your Medbot CMS account** (`/sign-up`) says "Invited by a clinic? Use the email address the invitation was sent to."

1. Select **Continue with Google**, if your deployment offers it, or fill in **Full name**, **Email**, **Password** and **Confirm password** (at least 8 characters).
2. Select **Create account**. You are signed in at once.

If you were invited, the invitation email opens this form with your email address already filled in. A Medbot account made in the Medbot app or Medbot Health works here too: sign in with it instead.

### Signing in

The **Sign in to Medbot CMS** page (`/login`) offers these ways in:

- **Continue with Google**, as a button at the top of the page, if your deployment offers it. Below it, after "or", are the two tabs.
- **Password** (the default tab). Enter your **Email** and **Password** and select **Sign in**.
- **Email code**. Enter your **Email** and select **Email me a code**: "If an account uses this address, we sent it a code." Enter the 6-digit code from the email and select **Continue**. **Send a new code** sends another, and **Use a different address** goes back. A code signs in an existing account only; it does not create one.

If you see "This account has been disabled. Contact your administrator.", you no longer have an active role in any organization. Ask your Clinic Admin.

### Verify your email

When you register, Medbot emails you a 6-digit code. Until you enter it, an amber banner at the top of the CMS says "**Verify your email.** We sent a code to &lt;your email&gt;. Verifying it lets you join organisations that invited you." Select **Enter code**, type the code and select **Verify email**.

You can use the CMS before verifying, but clinics recognise staff by email, so a few steps wait for it:

- **Joining an organisation that invited you.** The invitation is claimed the moment your address is verified, in any Medbot app.
- **Requesting access with a join code**, and **creating an organisation**. These pages first show **Verify your email**: "Clinics recognise staff by email, so confirm that &lt;email&gt; is yours before joining or creating an organisation." Send yourself a code and enter it there.

## 2. Choose your organization

After you sign in, the welcome screen shows **Welcome back** and **Choose the organisation and role to continue as.** Each organization you belong to appears once, as a card with its name and the role you will enter it as.

1. Select **Enter as** followed by your role on the card, for example **Enter as Receptionist**.
2. The CMS opens on the **Queue** page.

The same screen has **Join with code**, **Create organisation**, **Manage organisations**, and **Sign out**. You see the welcome screen once each time you sign in. To change organization later, see [Choosing & Switching](/cms/switching).

> **Note:** You work as one role at a time. If you belong to two organizations, you can be a Doctor in one and a Nurse in the other. If you hold more than one role in the same organization, for example Doctor and Clinic Admin, you enter as one of them and switch with the role menu (see step 3).

## 3. Learn the sidebar

The sidebar on the left lists the pages your role can open. The groups are Patients, Scheduling, Billing, Pharmacy, SmartCMS (Singapore organisations only), and Administration. You only see items your role may use.

| Group | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Patients: Patients, Results, Inbox, Tasks | Yes | Patients, Results, Inbox, Tasks | Patients, Results, Tasks | Patients, Results, Tasks |
| Patients: Photo capture | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Patients: Explanation library | Yes | No | Yes | No |
| Scheduling: Appointments, Roster, Queue, Session | Yes | Yes | Appointments, Roster, Queue | Appointments, Roster, Queue |
| Billing: Billing, Catalog | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Billing: Services, Devices & substances | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Pharmacy: Formulary, Inventory, Stock | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Pharmacy: Order sets | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Pharmacy: Medication safety | Yes | No | No | No |
| SmartCMS: Claims | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Administration: Staff | Yes | No | No | No |
| Administration: **Organisations** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Platform: Hosted organisations, Caregiver access, Record services | Medbot platform superadmins only, whatever their clinic role | | | |

**SmartCMS → Reports** shows to the roles that work on at least one of the organisation's reports; see [Reports](/cms/smartcms/reports).

At the top of the sidebar you see your organization and your role. At the bottom you see your name, which opens your account menu.

If you hold more than one role in the organization, a rounded **Working as** button at the right of the top bar shows the role you are working as. Open it and choose another role to switch; the sidebar and every page then follow that role. See [Switching role](/cms/switching#switching-role). If your deployment is a test environment, a strip at the top of the screen reads **STAGING** and says the data is test data only.

For what each role can do on each page, see [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

## 4. Your name and appearance

Open **Settings** from your account menu. Both sections apply to you only and do not change anything for other staff. Settings for the whole clinic are in [Organization Settings](/cms/settings).

- **Profile**: your **Full name**, "How your name appears on organisations you create or join." Change it and select **Save name** ("Name saved"). If your account has no name yet, the section says "Your account has no name yet. Add one so organisations you create or join can show it."
- **Appearance**: how the CMS looks for you.

The account menu also has **Contact support**, which opens Medbot's [support page](/docs/plans-and-billing#contact-support) in a new tab.

## 5. If you cannot open a page

- If the sidebar does not show a page, your role cannot open it.
- If you open a page by address and see "This action requires one of the following roles", check the role menu: if you hold another role that can open the page, switch to it. Otherwise ask your Clinic Admin whether you should have another role.
- If you are asked to log in again, sign in and choose your organization again.

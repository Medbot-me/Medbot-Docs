# Staff App Overview

## What is Medbot CMS?

Medbot CMS is the clinic operations app. It is the day-to-day system a clinic's front desk, nurses, doctors, and administrators use to run a physical location. Staff register patients, book and run appointments, manage a walk-in queue, roster staff, record clinical care, file results, and bill for visits. It is also where the organisation runs its [care services](/cms/services) and its administration.

It sits alongside the patient portal, where a clinic's own patients sign in to request appointments, read visit summaries, message the clinic, and manage their contact details. See the [Patient](/patient) tab for the patient's own view.

Medbot web, the other staff-facing app, is for your personal work only: your own notes, agents, datasets and research. Everything that belongs to an organisation is in Medbot CMS. Old organisation links in Medbot web redirect here.

## Key concepts

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization** | A clinic's account: its staff, locations, and patients. Roles are set per organization, so someone can be a Clinic Admin at one clinic and a Nurse at another. See [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions). |
| **Role** | Clinic Admin, Receptionist, Doctor, or Nurse. A person can hold more than one role in the same organization, such as Doctor and Clinic Admin, and works as one at a time, switching from the [sidebar switcher](/cms/switching#the-sidebar-switcher) or the **Working as** menu. See [Switching role](/cms/switching#switching-role). |
| **Care service** | A programme of care the organisation runs, such as a chronic disease or IVF service, with its own referrals, workstation and care team. You work in one service at a time, chosen after sign-in and changed from the sidebar switcher. See [Services](/cms/services) and [Choosing a service](/cms/switching#choosing-a-service). |
| **Home page** | Where the CMS tab opens for your role in the current service: the **Workstation** for doctors and nurses, **Referrals** for Clinic Admins and receptionists. With no service, the queue. See [Where the CMS opens](/cms/switching#where-the-cms-opens). |
| **Home and CMS tabs** | The two halves of the sidebar: **Home** for the Home assistant, tasks and the organisation's administration, **CMS** for the care service and clinic operations. See [Home and CMS](#home-and-cms). |
| **Clinic location** | A physical site. Sessions, rooms, staff assignments, and the queue are all scoped to one location at a time. |
| **Clinic session** | A location's working day: which staff and rooms are active. Opened from [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup). |
| **Reception queue** | The live, location-scoped list of today's booked and checked-in patients. See [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue). |
| **Roster** | The staff schedule: draft shifts, recurring patterns, and a publish step that starts each day's session and attendance tracking. See [Rostering](/cms/rostering). |
| **Home assistant** | A chat on the Home tab that answers questions about the organisation's patients, queue, appointments and unpaid invoices from the records. See [Home Assistant](/cms/home-assistant). |
| **Patient portal** | A separate, patient-facing app where a clinic's patients sign in to manage their care. Staff connect a patient to it from the chart. See [Patient Portal](/cms/patient-portal). |

## Home and CMS

Under the switcher at the top of the sidebar are two tabs, **Home** and **CMS**. Each page belongs to one of them, and the tab follows the page you are on, so a link from one tab into a page of the other switches the tab too.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | The [Home assistant](/cms/home-assistant), your care-service [Tasks](/cms/tasks#the-task-inbox), and the organisation's administration: **Patients**, **Staff**, **Agents**, **Settings** and **AI policy**. Selecting **Home** opens a new chat (`/home`). |
| **CMS** | The pages of the care service you are working in, then clinic operations: results, notes and messages, the queue, sessions, appointments, the roster, billing, claims and pharmacy. A patient's chart opens here. Selecting **CMS** opens your home page in the current service, or the queue when no service is set. |

The switcher above the tabs changes the organisation, the care service and the role you work as. See [Choosing & Switching](/cms/switching).

## The sidebar

The sidebar shows the current tab's pages, and only those your role may open. Groups with an arrow open and close. A group opens by itself when it holds the page you are on. While a Clinic Admin [previews another role](/cms/switching#previewing-another-role), the sidebar, the phone's bar and search follow the previewed role.

### Home tab

| Item or group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | The search palette. See [Search](#search). |
| **New chat** | A new conversation with the [Home assistant](/cms/home-assistant). |
| **Tasks** | The care services' [Task Inbox](/cms/tasks#the-task-inbox) (`/services/inbox`), with the number of tasks waiting for you. |
| **Chats** | Your recent conversations with the Home assistant, under **Today**, **Previous 7 days** and **Older**. |
| **Organisation** | **Patients** (the [patient list](/cms/patient-records)), **Staff** ([Staff](/cms/staff), Clinic Admins), **Agents** (the organisation's agents, `/agents`), **Settings** ([Organisation Settings](/cms/settings), Clinic Admins), **AI policy** ([AI Policy](/cms/ai-policy), Clinic Admins), and for doctors, nurses and receptionists **Downtime recovery** ([Recovery After an Outage](/cms/downtime/recovery)). |

Medbot platform administrators also see a **Platform** group. See [Platform Administration](/cms/platform-administration).

### CMS tab

| Item or group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | The search palette. See [Search](#search). |
| The open patient | When a chart is open, a card with the patient's name and their chart sections. See [The open patient](#the-open-patient). |
| The care service | The service you are working in, by name, with its pages for your role, starting with **Home**. With no service set, **Choose a service**. See [Services](/cms/services). |
| **Clinical** | **Results**, **Templates**, **Photo capture**, **Explanation library**, **Approved answers**, **Answer reports**, **Patient concerns**, **Patient questions**, **Inbox** and **Tasks** (staff pings). |
| **Operations** | **Queue**, **Session** (with **Rooms**, **Displays** and **Create session**), **Appointments** and **Roster** (with **Attendance**). Open unless you close it. |
| **Admin** | Three groups: **Billing** (**Billing**, **Catalog**, **Billable services**, **Devices & substances**), **Claims** (**Claims**, **Messages**, **Reports**: Singapore organisations with a SmartCMS module only) and **Pharmacy** (**Formulary**, **Inventory**, **Stock**, **Order sets**, **Medication safety**). |

Downtime devices, downtime recovery and system status are not in the sidebar for Clinic Admins. Open them from the **Downtime and status** section of [Organisation Settings](/cms/settings#downtime-and-status).

### The open patient

Opening a patient's chart adds a card under **Search** with the patient's initials and name and the chart sections your role may open: **Overview**, **Accounts**, **Encounters**, **Notes**, **Results**, **Photos**, **Vaccinations**, **Documents**, **Messages**, **Shared by patient** and **Audit log**. Select the name to go to **Overview**, or the cross (**Close patient**) to leave the chart. See [The patient chart](/cms/patient-records#the-patient-chart).

## Search

**Search** at the top of the sidebar opens one search box over the organisation. Press ⌘K or Ctrl+K to open it from anywhere, or select **Search** on a phone's bottom bar. Type at least two characters.

What it finds depends on the tab you opened it from, and each source only for the roles whose page it comes from:

| Tab | Filter chips | What is searched |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Home** | **All**, **Patients**, **Services**, **Staff**, **Pages** | Patients, the Home tab's pages, and staff (Clinic Admins only) |
| **CMS** | **All**, **Patients**, **Appointments**, **Results**, **Formulary**, **Pages** | Patients, care services, the CMS tab's pages, appointments, results, and formulary medicines (Clinic Admins, Doctors and Nurses) |

A chip narrows the list to one kind. Patients are found the same way as on the [patient list](/cms/patient-records#finding-a-patient). Appointments come from the most recent 200, and results from the last 180 days of the results inbox, so older ones are not found here.

With nothing typed, the box offers your **Recent searches**. They are kept only until the browser tab closes, because a search can be a patient's name.

## On a phone

On a phone the sidebar becomes a sheet, the **Home** and **CMS** tabs sit under the header, and a bar along the bottom of the screen holds:

| Tab | Bar |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | **New chat**, **Tasks**, **Search**, **More** |
| **CMS** | **Queue**, **Appointments**, **Tasks**, **Search**, **More** |

The bar shows only the pages your role may open. **More** opens a sheet with the tab's other pages, grouped as in the sidebar, plus **Open Medbot web** and **Help and support**.

The bar is hidden where a page needs the bottom of the screen: the notes workspace, a care service's consult editor and the kiosk. On a chart's **Overview**, **Encounters** and **Results**, the section's own buttons take its place.

## What you can do with Medbot CMS

1. **Run the front desk** by registering patients, booking appointments, operating the [reception queue](/cms/reception-queue), and replying to portal messages.
2. **Roster and track staff** by planning shifts, publishing the week, and letting [attendance](/cms/attendance) derive from what staff actually do in the app.
3. **Deliver clinical care** by starting encounters, recording vitals and vaccinations, placing orders, reviewing results, and issuing [documents and certificates](/cms/documents-and-certificates).
4. **Run care services** with referrals, a workstation and a care team: [Services](/cms/services).
5. **Run the back office** by managing [staff accounts](/cms/staff), maintaining the [formulary](/cms/formulary) and [inventory](/cms/inventory-and-stock), and handling [billing](/cms/billing).
6. **Ask about the day** in the [Home assistant](/cms/home-assistant): who is waiting, who is booked, a patient's latest results, what is unpaid.
7. **Give patients self-service access** by inviting them to the [patient portal](/cms/patient-portal).

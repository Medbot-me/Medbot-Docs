# Staff App Overview

## What is Medbot CMS?

Medbot CMS is the clinic operations app. It is the day-to-day system a clinic's front desk, nurses, doctors, and administrators use to run a physical location. Staff register patients, book and run appointments, manage a walk-in queue, roster staff, record clinical care, file results, and bill for visits. It is also where the organisation runs its [care services](/cms/services) and its administration.

It sits alongside the patient portal, where a clinic's own patients sign in to request appointments, read visit summaries, message the clinic, and manage their contact details. See the [Patient](/patient) tab for the patient's own view.

Medbot web, the other staff-facing app, is for your personal work only: your own notes, agents, datasets and research. Everything that belongs to an organisation is in Medbot CMS. Old organisation links in Medbot web redirect here.

## Key concepts

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization** | A clinic's account: its staff, locations, and patients. Roles are set per organization, so someone can be a Clinic Admin at one clinic and a Nurse at another. See [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions). |
| **Role** | Clinic Admin, Receptionist, Doctor, Nurse, Patient service associate, or Allied health professional. A person can hold more than one role in the same organization, such as Doctor and Clinic Admin, and works as one at a time, switching from the [sidebar switcher](/cms/administration/switching#the-sidebar-switcher) or the **Working as** menu. See [Switching role](/cms/administration/switching#switching-role). |
| **Care service** | A programme of care the organisation runs, such as a chronic disease or IVF service, with its own referrals, workstation and care team. You work in one service at a time, chosen after sign-in and changed from the sidebar switcher. See [Services](/cms/services) and [Choosing a service](/cms/administration/switching#choosing-a-service). |
| **Home page** | Where the **Home** tab opens for your role: the current service's **Workstation**, for every role. With no service, the whole organisation's **Workstation** (`/workstation`). See [Role home pages](/cms/services/getting-started#role-home-pages). |
| **Home and AI tabs** | The two halves of the sidebar: **Home** for the care service and clinic operations, **AI** for the Home assistant and the organisation's administration. See [Home and AI](#home-and-cms). |
| **Clinic location** | A physical site. Sessions, rooms, staff assignments, and the queue are all scoped to one location at a time. |
| **Clinic session** | A location's working day: which staff and rooms are active. Opened from [Session Setup](/cms/front-desk/session-setup). |
| **Reception queue** | The live, location-scoped list of today's booked and checked-in patients. See [Reception Queue](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue). |
| **Roster** | The staff schedule: draft shifts, recurring patterns, and a publish step that starts each day's session and attendance tracking. See [Rostering](/cms/front-desk/rostering). |
| **Home assistant** | A chat on the AI tab that answers questions about the organisation's patients, queue, appointments and unpaid invoices from the records, and, where your deployment turns it on, helps a doctor or nurse run a clinic day. See [Home Assistant](/cms/home-assistant). |
| **Patient portal** | A separate, patient-facing app where a clinic's patients sign in to manage their care. Staff connect a patient to it from the chart. See [Patient Portal](/cms/clinical-care/patient-portal). |

## Home and AI [#home-and-cms]

Under the switcher at the top of the sidebar are two tabs, **Home** and **AI**. Each page belongs to one of them, and the tab follows the page you are on, so a link from one tab into a page of the other switches the tab too.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | The pages of the care service you are working in, or, with no service, the whole organisation's: [Notifications](/cms/clinical-care/notifications), the **Workstation**, **Patients**, the roster, results, the organisation's agents, pharmacy, billing and claims. A patient's chart opens here. Selecting **Home** opens your home page in the current service, or the whole organisation's **Workstation** when no service is set. |
| **AI** | The [Home assistant](/cms/home-assistant) and the organisation's administration: **Staff**, **Settings** and **AI policy**. Selecting **AI** opens a new chat (`/ai`). |

The switcher above the tabs changes the organisation, the care service and the role you work as. See [Choosing & Switching](/cms/administration/switching).

## The sidebar

The sidebar shows the current tab's pages, and only those your role may open. Groups with an arrow open and close. A group opens by itself when it holds the page you are on. While a Clinic Admin [previews another role](/cms/administration/switching#previewing-another-role), the sidebar, the phone's bar and search follow the previewed role.

### Home tab

| Item or group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | The search palette. See [Search](#search). |
| The open patient | When a chart is open, a card with the patient's name and their chart sections. See [The open patient](#the-open-patient). |
| The care service | When you are working inside a service: a link back to the whole organisation, then the service's name with its pages for your role, starting with **Home**. The whole organisation's pages below are not shown. See [Services](/cms/services). |
| The organisation's name | With no service set, the organisation's name heads the list, with a settings button for Clinic Admins, and **All services** sits directly under it. The services themselves are not listed in the sidebar: reach one from **All services**, from the switcher at the top, or from search. |
| The organisation's pages | One group: **Notifications** ([Notifications](/cms/clinical-care/notifications), `/notifications`, with the number waiting for you), **Workstation** (today's patients across every service, with **Appointments** as its second tab), **Patients** (the [patient list](/cms/clinical-care/patient-records)), **Roster** (with **Attendance**), **Results**, **Identity changes**, **AI content library** (with **Approved answers**), **Templates** and **Agents** (the organisation's agents, `/agents`). |
| **Ask Medbot** | A group that opens and closes: **Answer reports**, **Patient concerns** and **Patient questions** (with **Common questions**). |
| **Pharmacy** | **Formulary**, **Inventory**, **Stock**, **Order sets**, **Medication safety**, **Dosing rules** and **Dose overrides**. |
| **Billing** | **Billing**, **Catalog**, **Billable services** and **Devices & substances**. |
| **Claims** | **Claims**, **Messages** and **Reports**: Singapore organisations with a SmartCMS module only. |

### AI tab

| Item or group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | The search palette. See [Search](#search). |
| **New chat** | A new conversation with the [Home assistant](/cms/home-assistant). |
| **Chats** | Your recent conversations with the Home assistant, under **Today**, **Previous 7 days** and **Older**. |
| **Organisation** | **Staff** ([Staff](/cms/administration/staff), Clinic Admins), **Settings** ([Organisation Settings](/cms/administration/settings), Clinic Admins), **AI policy** ([AI Policy](/cms/administration/ai-policy), Clinic Admins), and for doctors, nurses and receptionists **Downtime recovery** ([Recovery After an Outage](/cms/downtime/recovery)). |

Medbot platform administrators also see a **Platform** group. See [Platform Administration](/cms/administration/platform-administration).

Downtime devices, downtime recovery and system status are not in the sidebar for Clinic Admins. Open them from the **Downtime and status** section of [Organisation Settings](/cms/administration/settings#downtime-and-status).

### The open patient

Opening a patient's chart adds a card under **Search** with the patient's initials and name and the chart sections your role may open: **Overview**, **Medications**, **Accounts**, **Encounters**, **AI encounters**, **Notes**, **Results**, **Photos**, **Vaccinations**, **Documents**, **Messages**, **Shared by patient** and **Audit log**. **Medications** is for clinical staff: see [Prescribing & Medications](/cms/clinical-care/prescribing). **AI encounters** lists every AI encounter the organisation started for the patient: see [AI encounters across services](/cms/clinical-care/encounters#ai-encounters-across-services). Select the name to go to **Overview**, or the cross (**Close patient**) to leave the chart. See [The patient chart](/cms/clinical-care/patient-records#the-patient-chart).

### Going back, and pages that are not found

**Back**, and the chart's **Back to …** link, return you to the page you opened the chart from: the queue, the patient list, appointments, tasks, search, a result and so on. Medbot remembers this for each browser tab, and for each patient you have open. With nothing remembered, for example after opening a pasted link in a new tab, Back goes to the chart's usual parent page. The patient's name on a queue row and on an appointment row opens their chart.

A page that does not exist shows **This page could not be found**, with **Go to Home**. A patient who is not found shows **Patient not found** ("This patient may have been removed, or the link may be wrong.") with **Go to Patients**. A part of a chart that is missing, that you may not open, or that was linked wrongly says "This part of the chart may have moved, you may not have access to it, or the link may be wrong." with **Go to the patient's overview**.

## Search

**Search** at the top of the sidebar opens one search box over the organisation. Press ⌘K or Ctrl+K to open it from anywhere, or select **Search** on a phone's bottom bar. Type at least two characters. The same box also runs commands for your role; see [Command Palette & Shortcuts](/cms/reference/keyboard-shortcuts).

What it finds depends on the tab you opened it from, and each source only for the roles whose page it comes from:

| Tab | Filter chips |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | **All**, **Patients**, **Appointments**, **Results**, **Formulary**, **Pages** |
| **AI** | **All**, **Patients**, **Services**, **Staff**, **Pages** |

A chip narrows the list to one kind. Patients are found the same way as on the [patient list](/cms/clinical-care/patient-records#finding-a-patient). Appointments come from the most recent 200, and results from the last 180 days of the results inbox, so older ones are not found here.

With nothing typed, the box offers **Find patient** first, then your **Recent patients** and **Recent searches**. They are kept only until the browser tab closes or you sign out, because a search can be a patient's name.

## On a phone

On a phone the sidebar becomes a sheet, and a bar along the bottom of the screen holds:

| Tab | Bar |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | **Workstation**, **Notifications**, **Search**, **More** |
| **AI** | **New chat**, **Search**, **More** |

The bar shows only the pages your role may open. **More** opens a sheet with the tab's other pages, grouped as in the sidebar, plus **Open Medbot web** and **Help and support**.

The bar is hidden where a page needs the bottom of the screen: the notes workspace, a care service's consult editor and the kiosk. On a chart's **Overview**, **Encounters** and **Results**, the section's own buttons take its place.

## What you can do with Medbot CMS

1. **Run the front desk** by registering patients, booking appointments, operating the [reception queue](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue), and replying to portal messages.
2. **Roster and track staff** by planning shifts, publishing the week, and letting [attendance](/cms/front-desk/attendance) derive from what staff actually do in the app.
3. **Deliver clinical care** by starting encounters, recording vitals and vaccinations, placing orders, reviewing results, and issuing [documents and certificates](/cms/clinical-care/documents-and-certificates).
4. **Run care services** with referrals, a workstation and a care team: [Services](/cms/services).
5. **Run the back office** by managing [staff accounts](/cms/administration/staff), maintaining the [formulary](/cms/pharmacy/formulary) and [inventory](/cms/pharmacy/inventory-and-stock), and handling [billing](/cms/billing).
6. **Ask about the day** in the [Home assistant](/cms/home-assistant): who is waiting, who is booked, a patient's latest results, what is unpaid.
7. **Give patients self-service access** by inviting them to the [patient portal](/cms/clinical-care/patient-portal).

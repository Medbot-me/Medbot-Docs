# Staff App Overview

## What is Medbot CMS?

Medbot CMS is the clinic operations app. It is the day-to-day system a clinic's front desk, nurses, doctors, and administrators use to run a physical location. Staff register patients, book and run appointments, manage a walk-in queue, roster staff, record clinical care, file results, and bill for visits. It is also where the organisation runs its [care services](/cms/services) and its administration.

It sits alongside the patient portal, where a clinic's own patients sign in to request appointments, read visit summaries, message the clinic, and manage their contact details. See the [Patient](/patient) tab for the patient's own view.

Medbot web, the other staff-facing app, is for your personal work only: your own notes, agents, datasets and research. Everything that belongs to an organisation is in Medbot CMS. Old organisation links in Medbot web redirect here.

## Key concepts

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization** | A clinic's account: its staff, locations, and patients. Roles are set per organization, so someone can be a Clinic Admin at one clinic and a Nurse at another. See [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions). |
| **Role** | Clinic Admin, Receptionist, Doctor, Nurse, Patient service associate, or Allied health professional. A person can hold more than one role in the same organization, such as Doctor and Clinic Admin, and works as one at a time, switching from the [sidebar switcher](/cms/administration/switching#the-sidebar-switcher) or the **Working as** menu. See [Switching role](/cms/administration/switching#switching-role). |
| **Care service** | A programme of care the organisation runs, such as a general practice clinic, a chronic disease or an IVF service, with its own patients, queue, staff and settings. You work in one service at a time, or at **Org Level** across all of them, chosen after sign-in and changed from the sidebar switcher. See [Services](/cms/services) and [Choosing a service](/cms/administration/switching#choosing-a-service). |
| **Home page** | Where the CMS opens: in a service, its **Patients** page (the service's [Workstation](/cms/services/workstation)), for every role. At Org Level, the organisation's **Patients** page (`/patients/queue`). See [Where the CMS opens](/cms/administration/switching#where-the-cms-opens). |
| **Home and AI tabs** | The two halves of the sidebar: **Home** for patients, care services and clinic operations, **AI** for the assistant. See [Home and AI](#home-and-ai). |
| **Clinic location** | A physical site with its rooms. Each care service has its own clinic location, and one service is the organisation's **default clinic**. Locations and rooms are kept in [Organisation Settings](/cms/administration/settings). |
| **Clinic session** | A service's working day: who is on duty, their rooms, and whether check-in is open. Each service that runs a queue has one session a day, on its Workstation's **Session** tab. See [The Session tab](/cms/services/workstation#the-session-tab). |
| **Reception queue** | The live list of today's booked and checked-in patients of a service, run by the front desk on its Workstation's **Queue** tab. See [Reception Queue](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue). |
| **Roster** | The staff schedule: draft shifts, recurring patterns, and a publish step that starts each day's session and attendance tracking. See [Rostering](/cms/front-desk/rostering). |
| **Home assistant** | A chat on the AI tab that answers questions about the organisation's patients, queue, appointments and unpaid invoices from the records, and, where your deployment turns it on, helps a doctor or nurse run a clinic day. See [Home Assistant](/cms/home-assistant). |
| **Patient portal** | A separate, patient-facing app where a clinic's patients sign in to manage their care. Staff connect a patient to it from the chart. See [Patient Portal](/cms/clinical-care/patient-portal). |

## Home and AI

Under the switcher at the top of the sidebar are two tabs, **Home** and **AI**. Each page belongs to one of them, and the tab follows the page you are on, so a link from one tab into a page of the other switches the tab too.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Where the CMS opens (`/`). The care service you are working in, the open patient, and the organisation's pages: patients, staff, the roster, results, agents, the AI content library, templates, pharmacy, billing and claims. A patient's chart and every care service page open here. |
| **AI** | The [Home assistant](/cms/home-assistant) (`/ai`), your saved chats, and for Medbot platform administrators the **Platform admin** panel. Selecting **AI** opens a new chat. |

The switcher above the tabs changes the organisation, the care service (or **Org Level**, for no service) and the role you work as. See [Choosing & Switching](/cms/administration/switching).

Older addresses still work: `/home` and `/home/chats/<id>` open the assistant at `/ai`, `/queue` and `/workstation` open the organisation's **Patients** page, `/patients` opens its **Patient list**, and `/appointments` opens its **Appointments** tab.

## The sidebar

The sidebar shows the current tab's pages, and only those your role may open. Groups with an arrow open and close. A group opens by itself when it holds the page you are on. While a Clinic Admin [previews another role](/cms/administration/switching#previewing-another-role), the sidebar, the phone's bar and search follow the previewed role.

### Home tab

| Item or group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | The search palette. See [Search](#search). |
| The open patient | When a chart is open, a card with the patient's name and their chart sections. See [The open patient](#the-open-patient). |
| **Back to Org Level** | Inside a service: leaves the service for the organisation's own pages ("Workstation, billing, claims, pharmacy"). |
| The care service | Inside a service: its name (with **Draft** while it is a draft, and a settings button for Clinic Admins), then its pages for your role: **Patients** (its [Workstation](/cms/services/workstation)), **Overview**, **Staff**, **Roster**, **Agents** and **AI content library**. See [The service's pages](/cms/services/getting-started#4-the-service-page). |
| The organisation | At Org Level: the organisation's name, with a settings button for Clinic Admins that opens [Organisation Settings](/cms/administration/settings) over the page, then **Patients** (today's queue across every service, the **Patient list** and **Appointments**), **Staff** (Clinic Admins), **Roster**, **Results**, **Agents** (the organisation's agent library, `/agents`), **AI content library** and **Templates**. |
| **Pharmacy** | **Formulary**, **Inventory**, **Stock**, **Order sets**, **Medication safety**, **Dosing rules** and **Dose overrides**. |
| **Billing** | **Billing**, **Catalog**, **Billable services** and **Devices & substances**. |
| **Claims** | **Claims**, **Messages** and **Reports**: Singapore organisations with a SmartCMS module only. |

Notifications, including care-service tasks and staff pings, are under the bell at the top of the page.

Downtime devices, downtime recovery, system status and the [AI policy](/cms/administration/ai-policy) are not in the sidebar. Clinic Admins open them from [Organisation Settings](/cms/administration/settings#downtime-and-status).

### AI tab

| Item or group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | The search palette. |
| **New chat** | A new conversation with the [Home assistant](/cms/home-assistant). |
| **Chats** | Your recent conversations with the assistant, under **Today**, **Previous 7 days** and **Older**. |
| **Platform** | **Platform admin**, for Medbot platform administrators only. See [Platform Administration](/cms/administration/platform-administration). |

### The open patient

Opening a patient adds a card under **Search** with the patient's initials and name and the chart sections your role may open: **Overview**, **Problem list**, **Medications**, **Accounts**, **Encounters**, **AI encounters**, **Notes**, **Results**, **Photos**, **Vaccinations**, **Documents**, **Messages**, **Shared by patient** and **Audit log**. **Overview** is the [patient workspace](/cms/clinical-care/patient-records#the-patient-workspace). **Problem list** and **Medications** are for clinical staff: see [The problem list](/cms/clinical-care/patient-records#the-problem-list) and [Prescribing & Medications](/cms/clinical-care/prescribing). **AI encounters** lists every AI encounter the organisation started for the patient: see [AI encounters across services](/cms/clinical-care/encounters#ai-encounters-across-services). Select the name to go to **Overview**, or the cross (**Close patient**) to leave the chart. See [The patient chart](/cms/clinical-care/patient-records#the-patient-chart).

### Going back, and pages that are not found

**Back**, and the chart's **Back to …** link, return you to the page you opened the chart from: the queue, the patient list, appointments, notifications, search, a result and so on. Medbot remembers this for each browser tab, and for each patient you have open. With nothing remembered, for example after opening a pasted link in a new tab, Back goes to the chart's usual parent page. The patient's name on a queue row and on an appointment row opens their chart.

A page that does not exist shows **This page could not be found**, with **Go to Home**. A patient who is not found shows **Patient not found** ("This patient may have been removed, or the link may be wrong.") with **Go to Patients**. A part of a chart that is missing, that you may not open, or that was linked wrongly says "This part of the chart may have moved, you may not have access to it, or the link may be wrong." with **Go to the patient's overview**.

## Search

**Search** at the top of the sidebar opens one box for finding anything and doing anything. Press ⌘K (Ctrl+K off a Mac) to open it from anywhere, or select **Search** on a phone's bottom bar. Type at least two characters. The same box has three more modes, each with its own shortcut: **Find patient**, **Commands** and the open chart's sections.

### Searching

What it finds depends on the tab you opened it from, and each source only for the roles whose page it comes from:

| Tab | Filter chips | What is searched |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Home** | **All**, **Patients**, **Appointments**, **Results**, **Formulary**, **Pages** | Patients, care services, the Home tab's pages, appointments, results, formulary medicines (Clinic Admins, Doctors and Nurses), and your commands |
| **AI** | **All**, **Patients**, **Services**, **Staff**, **Pages** | Patients, the AI tab's pages, and staff (Clinic Admins only) |

A chip narrows the list to one kind. Patients are found by name, NRIC or FIN, phone number or date of birth, as on the [patient list](/cms/clinical-care/patient-records#finding-a-patient). A patient row shows their sex and date of birth, never their identification number, even when you searched by it. Appointments come from the most recent 200, and results from the last 180 days of the results inbox, so older ones are not found here.

Under **All**, up to five of your own commands that match follow the patients, records and pages, with **All commands matching "&lt;text&gt;"** to see the rest. A command is listed when each word you typed starts a word of its name or keywords, so a surname does not bring up commands. **Search patients only for &lt;text&gt;** hands what you typed to Find patient.

With nothing typed, the box offers **Find patient** first, then your **Recent patients** and **Recent searches**. They are kept only until the browser tab closes, only for you and this organisation, because a search can be a patient's name.

### Find patient

**Find patient** (⌘P, Ctrl+P) lists the patients **In today's queue** and those **Recently opened** first, then searches as you type a name, phone number or date of birth. Choose a patient to open their chart.

### Commands

Type `>` in the box, or press ⌘⇧P (Ctrl+Shift+P), to see **Commands**: the tasks your role can do, such as **Check in patient**, **Take payment**, **Call patient**, **Start consult**, **Prescribe medication**, **Issue MC**, **Record vitals**, **Book appointment**, **Scan clinic card**, **Register new patient** or **Open patient list**. A command your role cannot use is never listed.

- **Tab** switches between **Suggested** (common for your role, and the ones you use most in this organisation), **A–Z** and **By category**.
- A command that needs a patient or a visit asks for it next: **Choose patient**, or **Choose visit** from your own queue. With a patient's chart open, an **Acting on** strip names them, and the palette suggests starting or resuming that patient's consult.
- Commands do what the page's own buttons do. **Take payment** opens the payment dialog on the board you work from, **Issue MC** opens the certificate dialog, and **Record vitals** with no encounter open asks to start one.

### The open chart

Type `@` to jump to a section of the open chart: medications, allergies, results, documents and the rest. Type `?` for the palette's help: the prefixes and the keys (↑ ↓ to move, ↵ to run, **Tab** to switch views, ⌫ to go back a step).

### Keyboard shortcuts

Change the shortcuts for search, Commands and Find patient under **Preferences → Keyboard shortcuts** in your account menu (`/preferences/shortcuts`), or the **Keyboard shortcuts** command. They are "Saved to your account, so they follow you to any computer you sign in on." Select a shortcut's change button and press the new keys. A shortcut that would break typing, or one the browser keeps, is refused; ⌘P replaces the browser's Print shortcut, and the page says so. **Reset to defaults** restores ⌘K, ⌘⇧P and ⌘P.

## On a phone

On a phone the sidebar becomes a sheet, the **Home** and **AI** tabs sit under the header, and a bar along the bottom of the screen holds:

| Tab | Bar |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | **Patients**, **Search**, **More** |
| **AI** | **New chat**, **Search**, **More** |

The bar shows only the pages your role may open. **More** opens a sheet with the tab's other pages, grouped as in the sidebar, plus **Open Medbot web** and **Help and support**.

The bar is hidden where a page needs the bottom of the screen: the notes workspace, a care service's consult editor and the kiosk. On a chart's **Encounters** and **Results**, the section's own buttons take its place. Pages are laid out for phone widths: headers stack their buttons under the title, and wide tables keep their key columns and move the rest into the row.

## What you can do with Medbot CMS

1. **Run the front desk** by registering patients, booking appointments, operating the [reception queue](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue), and replying to portal messages.
2. **Roster and track staff** by planning shifts, publishing the week, and letting [attendance](/cms/front-desk/attendance) derive from what staff actually do in the app.
3. **Deliver clinical care** by starting encounters, recording vitals and vaccinations, placing orders, reviewing results, and issuing [documents and certificates](/cms/clinical-care/documents-and-certificates).
4. **Run care services**, each with its own patients, queue, session, staff and referrals: [Services](/cms/services).
5. **Run the back office** by managing [staff accounts](/cms/administration/staff), maintaining the [formulary](/cms/pharmacy/formulary) and [inventory](/cms/pharmacy/inventory-and-stock), and handling [billing](/cms/billing).
6. **Ask about the day** in the [Home assistant](/cms/home-assistant): who is waiting, who is booked, a patient's latest results, what is unpaid.
7. **Give patients self-service access** by inviting them to the [patient portal](/cms/clinical-care/patient-portal).

# Staff App Overview

## What is Medbot CMS?

Medbot CMS is the clinic operations app. It is the day-to-day system a clinic's front desk, nurses, doctors, and administrators use to run a physical location. Staff register patients, book and run appointments, manage a walk-in queue, roster staff, record clinical care, file results, and bill for visits.

It sits alongside the patient portal, where a clinic's own patients sign in to request appointments, read visit summaries, message the clinic, and manage their contact details. See the [Patient](/patient) tab for the patient's own view.

## Key concepts

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization** | A clinic's account: its staff, locations, and patients. Roles are set per organization, so someone can be a Clinic Admin at one clinic and a Nurse at another. See [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions). |
| **Role** | Clinic Admin, Receptionist, Doctor, or Nurse. A person can hold more than one role in the same organization, such as Doctor and Clinic Admin, and works as one at a time, switching from the role menu. See [Choosing & Switching](/cms/switching#switching-role). |
| **Clinic location** | A physical site. Sessions, rooms, staff assignments, and the queue are all scoped to one location at a time. |
| **Clinic session** | A location's working day: which staff and rooms are active. Opened from [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup). |
| **Reception queue** | The live, location-scoped list of today's booked and checked-in patients. See [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue). |
| **Roster** | The staff schedule: draft shifts, recurring patterns, and a publish step that starts each day's session and attendance tracking. See [Rostering](/cms/rostering). |
| **Patient portal** | A separate, patient-facing app where a clinic's patients sign in to manage their care. Staff connect a patient to it from the chart. See [Patient Portal](/cms/patient-portal). |

## What you can do with Medbot CMS

1. **Run the front desk** by registering patients, booking appointments, operating the [reception queue](/cms/reception-queue), and replying to portal messages.
2. **Roster and track staff** by planning shifts, publishing the week, and letting [attendance](/cms/attendance) derive from what staff actually do in the app.
3. **Deliver clinical care** by starting encounters, recording vitals and vaccinations, placing orders, reviewing results, and issuing [documents and certificates](/cms/documents-and-certificates).
4. **Run the back office** by managing [staff accounts](/cms/staff), maintaining the [formulary](/cms/formulary) and [inventory](/cms/inventory-and-stock), and handling [billing](/cms/billing).
5. **Give patients self-service access** by inviting them to the [patient portal](/cms/patient-portal).

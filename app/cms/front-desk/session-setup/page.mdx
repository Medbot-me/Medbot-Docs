# Session Setup

Each care service that runs a queue has its own session for the day: when check-in opens, who is on duty, which room each clinician works in, and the screens that show the service's numbers. Find it on the **Session** tab of the service's **Workstation** (`/services/<service>/workstation/session`). A session is one service's working day at its clinic location.

The organisation no longer has one session page for a whole location. `/session` opens the Session tab of the service the CMS is set to, or the organisation's **Patients** page when no service is set. Clinic locations and their rooms are set up in [Organisation Settings](/cms/administration/settings#clinic-locations).

## Who can do this

| Task | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See the Session tab | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Start, close, or reopen today's session | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Put staff on or off duty, and choose who takes check-ins | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Set a clinician's room, and rooms' availability | Yes | Yes | Own room | Own room |
| Add, change, or revoke displays | Yes | No | No | No |

A Patient service associate runs the session as a Receptionist does. An Allied health professional sees it without controls.

## Starting today's session

The **Today's session** card shows the service's session with **Not started**, **Open** or **Closed**, and what starting it does: "Starting opens check-in for &lt;service&gt; and turns on its displays. If no one starts it, the first check-in will." A service set not to start by itself says instead that check-in stays closed until the session is started.

Select **Start today's session**. Starting puts the staff rostered today for the service on duty (see [Rostering](/cms/front-desk/rostering)). Once open, the card says who opened it, or that it opened at the first check-in, and when it closes by itself, for example "Closes by itself at midnight."

**At a glance** counts the day so far: **Booked, not arrived**, **Waiting**, **With a clinician**, **Seen** and **Missed Queue**.

## Closing and reopening

Select **Close today's session** at the end of the day. **Close today's session** shows how many were seen and lists each patient still in the queue, with a choice for each: **Keep the visit open**, **Mark as left** or **Book for tomorrow**. A patient in the Missed Queue starts on **Mark as left**, everyone else on **Keep the visit open**. Select **Close session**, or **Keep session open**.

A closed session says "Closed at &lt;time&gt;. Patients still in the queue keep their visits." **Reopen session** opens it again.

## Who is on duty

The **Who is on duty** card (**On duty now** once the session is open) lists the service's staff for today: their **Name**, **Room**, what they are doing **Now** (for example "With DIA014", or **Off duty**), how many they have **Seen today**, an **On duty** switch, and, for a doctor, **Takes check-ins**.

- "Changes apply straight away: the next check-in skips anyone off duty or not taking check-ins. Each clinician's room is where their Call sends patients."
- Doctors who take check-ins share new patients in turn. The one the next check-in goes to is marked **Next check-in**. See [Checking a patient in](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue#checking-a-patient-in).
- To add someone who is not rostered, choose them in **Someone not on the roster** and select **Add to today**.
- With nobody rostered, the card says "No one is rostered for &lt;service&gt; today. Add the roster first, or add staff after starting."

Choose a clinician's **Room** from the location's rooms, or **No room**. If another service at the location uses the same room today, the room is flagged with "&lt;room&gt; is also used by &lt;service&gt; today". A doctor or nurse can change their own room.

## Rooms

The **Rooms** card lists the location's rooms. Untick **Available** for a room that cannot be used today, and add a note, for example "Doppler out for repair". Rooms are added in [Organisation Settings → Clinic locations](/cms/administration/settings#clinic-locations). With none, the card says "This location has no rooms yet. An administrator adds them in Organisation settings."

## Displays

The **Displays** card lists the screens at the location that show the service's numbers, each with its type (**Waiting room**, **Room door** with its room, or **Check-in QR code**) and whether it is online. A Clinic Admin adds and manages them here. See [Patient-Facing Displays](/cms/front-desk/display-boards).

## Self check-in

The **Self check-in** card shows the location's check-in code: "One code for the location. Patients scan the counter QR or enter the code, then choose &lt;service&gt; from the services open now." See [Checking in with the Medbot app](/cms/front-desk/clinic-card-check-in#checking-in-with-the-medbot-app).

## Patient steps

For a service whose visits carry steps, the tab ends with the service's **Patient steps**. See [Workstation](/cms/services/workstation).

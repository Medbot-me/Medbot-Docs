# Appointments

Appointments is the organisation's list of booked visits. Find it on the **Appointments** tab of **Patients** (`/patients/appointments`) in the **Home** tab, beside the [Queue](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue) and **Patient list**. The old addresses `/appointments` and `/workstation/appointments` open it. Receptionists and Clinic Admins book, edit, and cancel appointments and answer booking requests from the patient portal and Find care. Every appointment is for one [service](/cms/services/appointments): the booking form and booking requests ask which. Doctors and nurses can look at the list but not change it. A service's own appointments are on its [Workstation](/cms/services/workstation).

## Who can do this

| Task | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See the appointment list | Yes | Yes | View only | View only |
| Book a new appointment | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Book with the agent | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Edit or cancel an appointment | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Answer a booking request | Yes | Yes | Find care requests sent to doctors | Find care requests sent to nurses |
| Place a request that needs a service | Yes | No | No | No |

A doctor or nurse who opens the booking page sees a message that says which roles are allowed. A doctor or nurse sees **Booking requests** only when the organisation sends Find care appointment requests to their role (see [Requests from Find care](#requests-from-find-care)).

## Finding appointments

The list shows every appointment with the patient, practitioner, date, time, duration, service, and status. Narrow it with the patient search box, the practitioner filter, and the date filter. All three apply together. Select **Clear date** to remove the date filter.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Booked** | Confirmed and upcoming. |
| **Arrived** | The patient has checked in. |
| **Fulfilled** | The visit is finished. |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled. |
| **No-show** | The patient did not come. |

Times are always shown in Singapore time, whatever the time zone of your computer.

## Booking an appointment

1. Select **New Appointment**.
2. Search for the patient and select them. Their details appear below the search box.
3. Choose the **Service**: one of your organisation's services that takes appointments (an inpatient service does not). With only one, it is chosen for you; with none, the form says "No service takes appointments yet".
4. Choose the **Clinic location**, the **Date**, and the **Time**. Times run from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM in half hour steps.
5. Choose the **Duration**: 15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 minutes.
6. Choose the **Practitioner**. Only doctors and nurses who can work at the chosen location are listed, doctors first. A nurse is shown with **(nurse-led visit)** after their name. See [Session Setup](/cms/front-desk/session-setup) and [Nurse-led visits](#nurse-led-visits).
7. Choose the **Service type**. You can also choose a **Reason** and add **Notes**.
8. Select **Book appointment**.

You return to the appointment list. The new appointment shows as **Booked**.

### Nurse-led visits

Book a nurse as the **Practitioner** for a visit that a nurse runs without a doctor. The nurse leads the visit:

- At check-in the visit stays with the nurse. No doctor is rotated onto it. See [Reception Queue](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue#nurse-led-visits).
- The lead nurse starts the visit and closes it with **Complete visit**.
- A doctor can also close it. If a doctor sees the patient during the visit, it becomes a doctor-led visit.
- A Clinic Admin can close any visit, and can hand an open visit to another doctor or nurse with **Change lead** on the queue.

In Singapore organisations, a scheme claim for a nurse-led visit is held for an administrator. See [Claims Workflow](/cms/smartcms/claims).

### Two bookings at the same time

A booking is refused when the patient or the practitioner already has an appointment that overlaps the time. The form stays open and shows a message such as "Marcus Lim already has an appointment that overlaps this time". The message shows the times in UTC, which is eight hours behind Singapore time. Choose a different time. The same check applies when you edit an appointment and when you accept a booking request.

### Booking with the agent

Select **Use agent** to book by describing the visit in words. The agent looks up the patient and the doctor and suggests a booking. A staff member reviews the suggestion, chooses the **Service** the appointment is for, and approves it before anything is booked.

## Responding to portal booking requests

Patients can ask for a visit from the [patient portal](/patient/visits) and from a clinic's page in [Find care](/patient/find-care). A request is not a booking. It waits in **Booking requests** (`/patients/appointments/requests`) until staff answer it. A request to change an existing appointment is marked **Change of time**, and one to book a past visit again **Book again**.

1. Select **Booking requests**. The page counts the requests waiting.
2. Read the **Requested date**, the **Time preference**, and the **Reason**.
3. Under **Exact appointment time**, set the **Date**, **Time**, and **Duration**, and choose the **Assigned clinician**. Choose the **Service** too, unless the patient already asked about one (it is chosen for you) or your organisation has only one. A request to change the time of an existing appointment keeps that appointment's service. As when booking, a nurse is listed with **(nurse-led visit)**.
4. Choose one of the answers below.

| Answer | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept and book** | Books the appointment at the time you set. |
| **Counter-propose** | Offers the patient a different time. Fill in the time and the clinician first. A note is optional. |
| **Reject** | Declines the request. A **Review note** is required. |

What the patient sees: after **Counter-propose**, the patient's request shows **Clinic proposed a new time**, your note, and the buttons **Accept proposed time** and **Decline proposed time**. When the patient accepts, the appointment appears in your list as **Booked** and the request leaves **Booking requests**. The request card does not update by itself, so reload the page to see it go.

> **Note:** An appointment made from an accepted counter-proposal currently shows **Your doctor** as its practitioner, not the doctor's name.

### Requests that need a service

When booking, a patient chooses what they need: one of the services the clinic offers, or **I'm not sure**. A request sent with **I'm not sure** reaches the organisation without a service, and an administrator places it.

- A Clinic Admin's **Booking requests** has two tabs: **By service** and **Needs a service** (`?queue=needs-service`). Other roles see one list.
- Each active Clinic Admin is emailed once when such a request arrives. The email names the clinic and links to the tab; it never names the patient or repeats their note. The request also shows in [Notifications](/cms/notifications) as **Task · Request needs a service**, with **Assign a service**.
- To place it, choose the **Service** and the time, and select **Counter-propose**. The request then carries the service and moves to **By service**.

### Requests from Find care

An organisation listed in Find care can take bookings there (see [Public profile](/cms/administration/settings#public-profile)). A patient the clinic already knows books as from the portal. A patient the clinic does not know sends an appointment request with the service, a preferred date and time of day, and a reason, and agrees to share their details.

Such a request is marked **New patient, from Find care**, with what the patient told Medbot. Before you can confirm a time, **Find or add the patient record**: "This patient asked from Find care before your clinic knew them. Link their record to confirm a time; it connects their Medbot account to it, with the consent they gave when asking." The patient's **Name**, **Date of birth**, **Phone** and **Email** are shown. Find their record, or add one, and select **Link record**. Then accept or propose a time as for any request. You can reject the request before linking.

Who answers these requests is set in **Appointment requests go to**, under the Public profile: Receptionists, Nurses or Doctors. Admins and receptionists can always act on them. Self-referrals with a history from Ask Medbot still arrive in Referrals, not here.

## Editing and cancelling

Select the three dot button at the end of an appointment row, then choose **Edit** or **Cancel appointment**.

**Edit** opens a form with the **Date**, **Time**, **Duration**, **Practitioner**, **Service type**, **Reason**, and **Notes**. You cannot change the patient, the location or the service: to move an appointment to another service, cancel it and book a new one. Select **Save changes**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/appointments/04-edit-appointment.png" alt="The Edit appointment form over the appointment list, showing date, time, duration, practitioner, service type, reason, and notes" />

**Cancel appointment** asks you to confirm. The appointment shows as **Cancelled**. A cancelled appointment can be reopened from the [Reception Queue](/cms/front-desk/reception-queue).

Cancelled, no-show, and fulfilled appointments have no menu, so they cannot be edited or cancelled.

# Reception Queue

The reception queue is today's list of booked and checked-in patients at one clinic location. Find it on the **Workstation**, in the **Home** tab. Old `/queue` links open the whole organisation's **Workstation**. Receptionists, Patient service associates and Clinic Admins run it. Doctors and nurses see a shorter view of their own work, and an Allied health professional sees the whole queue read-only. Each [service](/cms/services) keeps its own queue and numbers its own tickets. This page shows every service's queue at the location, and you can narrow it to one.

## Who can do this

| Task | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See today's queue | Yes | Yes | Own patients | Yes |
| Check a patient in | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Add a walk-in to the queue | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Call a patient to a room | Yes | Yes | Own patients | Nurse-led visits they lead |
| Move a called patient who did not come to the Missed Queue, requeue them, or return them | Yes | Yes | Own patients | Nurse-led visits they lead |
| Record that a patient left before finishing | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Add or edit a remark on a row | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Route a patient to a stage | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Mark a visit **Completed** on the board | Yes | No | No | No |
| Take payment, void a payment, or mark the visit paid | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Assign or change the doctor | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Change who leads a visit | Yes | No | No | No |
| Cancel an appointment, mark a no-show, or reopen | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Start an AI encounter for the patient | No | No | Yes | Nurse-led visits they lead |
| Complete the visit and send the outcome | No | No | Yes | Nurse-led visits they lead |
| See the CHAS badge and select **Verify now** (Singapore) | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

A Patient service associate does the front desk's work on this page, as a Receptionist does, except that they cannot open [Session Setup](/cms/front-desk/session-setup). An Allied health professional sees every patient at the location, with sex, age, status and the Missed Queue, but has no check-in, calling, routing or payment controls. They can add remarks.

Doctors see only the patients assigned to them. Nurses see every patient at the location and open the vitals form from their own view. Doctors and nurses do not see the **Bill** column or any payments. The **Start AI encounter** and **Complete visit** buttons appear on a doctor's row only after a consultation has been started for that appointment. A nurse sees them only on a [nurse-led visit](#nurse-led-visits) that has been started.

## Reading the queue

The page header shows the location, today's session with an **Open** or **Closed** badge, how many doctors are on duty, and sometimes a **Self check-in code**. When the location has more than one service with a queue, a service list beside the location list narrows the board to one service. Patients no longer need that code: they register from the portal with one button. If nobody has opened a session yet, it says **No session yet today**. Select **Create session** to open one, or see [Session Setup](/cms/front-desk/session-setup).

The table lists patients in this order:

1. Patients reception has moved to a fixed place in the queue.
2. Patients who have arrived, earliest arrival first.
3. Patients who are booked but have not arrived, by appointment time.
4. Completed and cancelled visits, at the bottom.

Each row shows the **Ticket**, the time, the **Patient**, **Sex**, **Age**, the NRIC or passport, the reason, **Remarks**, the **Clinician** (the doctor, or the nurse who leads a nurse-led visit), the workflow controls, the **Bill**, and the status. Select the patient's name to open their chart. Every role sees the **Ticket** column.

- **Ticket.** A ticket carries its service's prefix and a number, for example `DIA014`. Each service numbers its own patients within the day's session, so two services can each have a patient number 14. When more than one service shows, the service's name sits under the number.
- **Sex and Age.** Age is the patient's age on the day of the visit: whole years from 18, and years and months below that, for example `81y` or `2y5m`. Below the widest screens these two columns are hidden and appear on one line under the patient's name, for example `M · 45y`.
- **Remarks.** See [Remarks](#remarks).

On a narrow screen some columns, including **Bill**, are hidden. Turn columns on or off from the **View** menu. On the reception board **NRIC / Passport** and **Reason** start hidden, and your **View** choices are remembered in this browser.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Booked, not yet checked in. |
| **Waiting** | Checked in and waiting. |
| **Consultation** | A consultation has been started for this appointment. |
| **Done** | The visit is finished. |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled or marked as a no-show. |

The page updates itself about every four seconds, and the line under the title shows when it last updated. Nobody needs to reload it. Select **Refresh** to update it at once.

Four filters narrow the list: the location, the doctor (**All doctors**, **Unassigned**, or one doctor), the stage (**All stages**, **Not yet routed**, or one stage), and the status (**Active (hide Done)**, **Waiting only**, **Show cancelled**, or **All**).

## Checking a patient in

1. Choose the location at the top of the page.
2. Find the patient's row and select **Check in**.

A message such as "Kamala Perera checked in" appears, with the ticket number and the doctor, for example "Ticket DIA001 · Marcus Lim". The row now shows the ticket and the status **Waiting**, and a **Give the patient their number** window opens (see [Giving the patient their number](#giving-the-patient-their-number)).

Check-in records the arrival time and gives the patient a queue ticket from the location's open session, in the queue of the patient's service. The service is the one the appointment was booked into, or the patient's only clinic service. If check-in cannot tell, a window asks **Which service is &lt;patient&gt; here for?** Choose one. Check-in then assigns the next doctor of that service marked active in [Session Setup](/cms/front-desk/session-setup). Doctors take turns in the order they were marked active, and each service rotates among its own doctors. If no doctor is marked active, the patient still gets a ticket but no doctor. Checking in a patient who already has a ticket keeps the same ticket and does not move the rotation on. A visit booked to a nurse is [nurse-led](#nurse-led-visits): it keeps that nurse, no doctor is assigned, and the rotation does not move on.

Check-in has no time window. A patient booked for later in the day can be checked in as soon as they arrive.

Patients can also register themselves from the [patient portal](/patient/visits). On the day of the appointment they choose how they expect to pay under **Paying with** and select **Register and get my queue number**. They do not share their location or type a code. A self check-in works like a staff check-in, and the row appears in the queue with a ticket.

### Giving the patient their number

Right after a check-in, and from **Show ticket** on any row with a ticket, a window titled "&lt;patient&gt; is checked in" shows the queue number in large type, and the clinician. Give the patient their number in one of two ways, and Medbot records which one you used:

- **Print queue ticket** prints an 80 mm slip with the number, the clinic and the service, the check-in time, and the appointment time or **Walk-in**.
- **I told the patient the number** records that you said it. Use it when the ticket cannot print. If printing fails, the window says "The ticket could not be printed" and asks you to tell the patient their number instead. Say the number one letter or digit at a time, for example "D, I, A, zero, one, five", and ask the patient to repeat it.

The window reminds you that patients can also see their number in the patient portal under **Today's visit**.

### Adding a walk-in

Select **Add to queue** above the table for a patient who arrived without an appointment.

1. Search for the patient by name or NRIC. A patient already in today's queue is greyed out. If they are not registered yet, choose **Register new patient**.
2. Choose the **Visit type**: **Walk-in** (joins the end of the queue) or **Urgent** (seen next, flagged **Urgent**, for patients who cannot wait). **Send to** starts on **Nurse** for a walk-in and **Doctor** for an urgent case.
3. When the board shows every service, choose the **Service** the patient is here for. The walk-in joins that service's queue.
4. Optionally enter a **Reason for visit**, choose the **Clinician** (**Next in rotation** or a doctor) and **Send to** a stage: **Nurse**, **Doctor**, **Investigations** or **Dispensary**.
5. Select **Add to queue** (**Add as urgent** for an urgent case).

The patient is checked in with a ticket straight away, and the ticket window opens. A patient who is already in today's queue is not added a second time.

### CHAS verification at check-in (Singapore)

For a Singapore organisation that subscribes to CHAS, checking a patient in also asks MHCP for their CHAS card. This happens on the first check-in of the appointment, from the desk or the portal, at a location connected for CHAS. Nobody has to press anything. Verification never blocks check-in. If it fails, the patient is still checked in.

The result shows on the CHAS badge under the patient's name in their chart. Every role sees it. The badge shows one of:

- the card's name
- **CHAS not verified**
- **No CHAS card**
- the card name followed by **expired**

For a valid card, the badge also shows when it is valid, the chronic tier, the chronic and acute balances for this year, and the visits left at this clinic. "checked" and a date and time, in Singapore time, show when MHCP was last asked.

Select **Verify now** on the badge to ask MHCP again. Any role can. Medbot asks at the clinic of the patient's visit in today's queue session. If the patient is not on today's queue, it asks at the organisation's default location. A successful check says, for example, "Verified at Main Clinic." A failed one says why and changes nothing else:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Not verified: the patient has no Singapore NRIC (S or T) recorded." | Record the patient's NRIC in their chart. See [Patient Records & Consent](/cms/clinical-care/patient-records). |
| "Not verified: … is not connected to SmartCMS for CHAS. It needs an HE code and CHAS switched on in the SmartCMS settings." | Ask a Clinic Admin to finish the location's [SmartCMS settings](/cms/smartcms/settings). |
| "Not verified: SmartCMS is switched off on this Medbot server." | Nothing is sent from this server. Contact Medbot. |
| "Not verified: the patient's details could not be sent to MHCP. Check the NRIC." | Correct the patient's NRIC, then select **Verify now**. |
| "Not verified: MHCP's answer could not be read. Try again later." | Select **Verify now** later. |
| "Not verified: the SmartCMS gateway did not accept the call from Main Clinic. Try again later." | Select **Verify now** later. |

When you bill the visit, the CHAS panel warns if the card is not verified, was verified more than 30 days ago or in an earlier year, or is not valid on the visit date. MHCP is asked again when the bill is issued. See [Claims Workflow](/cms/smartcms/claims#1-check-in-and-chas-verification).

## Calling a patient to a room

1. On a checked-in row, select **Call**.
2. Choose a room in **Choose a room**, then select **Call**.

A message such as "Called MAIN001 to Consult 1" appears, and the row shows **Called → Consult 1**. Only rooms set up for today's session are listed. The waiting room display shows the ticket and the room, and a room door screen shows the ticket for its own room. See [Patient-Facing Displays](/cms/front-desk/display-boards). The room label stays until you route the patient to another stage. A called patient's row offers **Call again**, to announce them again or to send them to another room. A doctor or a lead nurse can call their own patients from their own view in the same way.

## When a called patient does not come

Once a patient has been called and has not come to the room, their row offers **Did not come**. It has two choices:

| Choice | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Requeue** | Puts the patient back behind the next few patients who are ready to be seen. The menu says how many, for example "Back 3 places, after the next 3 patients ready to be seen". The call ends and the patient keeps their ticket and arrival time. |
| **Move to Missed Queue** | Takes the patient out of the active queue until they come back. |

Only patients ready to be seen count when places are counted: those at the nurse, away for investigations or at the dispensary are skipped. Places are counted among the patients of the same service who wait for the same doctor, or the whole service queue when the visit has no doctor.

How many places a requeue moves a patient back is set in this order: the doctor's own number, then the service's number, then the clinic's. The clinic's number is 3 until a Clinic Admin changes it under **Queue** in [Organisation Settings](/cms/administration/settings#queue). A service can set its own in its Workstation settings, and a doctor or nurse sets their own under **Settings** in their account menu: see [Getting Started](/cms/getting-started#4-your-name-and-appearance).

### The Missed Queue

The **Missed Queue** panel under the table is always shown, with a count. It lists each patient who was called and did not come, with their ticket, doctor, the time they were missed and any remark. If nobody has missed their turn it says "No one has missed their turn."

- **Return to queue** opens **Return &lt;patient&gt; to the queue**. Choose where they wait: **Next in line** (the default), **After the next N patients**, or **Back of the queue**. The front desk can return any patient. A doctor, or a lead nurse, can return their own.
- Calling a patient who is in the Missed Queue also returns them.
- **Patient left** (front desk only) records that the patient went home. See [When a patient leaves](#when-a-patient-leaves).

### Remarks

The **Remarks** column holds a short note about a patient's visit for the other staff, such as "Went to the toilet, back in 5 min" or "Needs wheelchair". Every role that sees the queue can add one, including an Allied health professional.

1. Select the remark, or the add icon on an empty one. The **Remark for &lt;patient&gt;** editor opens.
2. Type the remark, up to 280 characters on one line. Select **Save**, or press Enter.
3. To remove it, select **Clear**. **Discard** drops an unsaved draft.

The editor shows who last changed the remark and when, and **History** lists the earlier versions. If someone else changed the remark while you were typing, your draft is kept, the editor shows theirs, and you choose what to save. A remark is an operational note for staff. It is not part of the patient's record. Medbot deletes remarks and their history after 12 months by default. A Clinic Admin can set between 1 and 48 months under **Queue** in [Organisation Settings](/cms/administration/settings#queue).

## Assigning the doctor

Use the doctor menu on the row to change the doctor. A doctor chosen by hand shows **Manual**. A doctor chosen by the rotation shows **Auto-rotated**.

## Routing a patient

The **Route…** menu on a checked-in row moves the patient through the clinic: **Nurse**, **Doctor**, **Investigations**, **Dispensary**, **Completed**. Use it to show where the patient is.

Marking a visit **Completed** closes it, so only a Clinic Admin sees **Completed** in the menu. A receptionist routes the patient between the other stages, and the doctor, or the lead nurse of a nurse-led visit, closes the visit with **Complete visit**. The nurse's own view shows a patient routed to the nurse as **Vitals now**, and a patient routed on to the doctor as **Ready for doctor**.

## Taking payment

Take payment from the **Bill** column. It has a button on rows that are **Waiting**, **Consultation**, or **Done**, so a patient can pay on the way out after the doctor has finished. **Done** rows are hidden by default; set the status filter to **All** to see them. The button reads **Take payment** until the visit is paid, then **✓ Paid**. Reception and Clinic Admins see an amber **Owes $X** instead if the visit was marked paid but now owes money (see [When the visit is marked paid](#when-the-visit-is-marked-paid)).

1. Select **Take payment** (or **✓ Paid**) on the patient's row. The **Payment from** dialog opens with the patient's name.
2. Check what is owed. See [What the patient owes](#what-the-patient-owes).
3. Check the **Amount**, which is in the bill's currency, for example **Amount (SGD)**. It starts at what is still owed. Change it if the patient pays only part of it now.
4. Choose the **Method**: **Cash**, **PayNow**, **NETS**, **Card**, or **Other**.
5. For any method other than cash, you can enter a **Reference (optional)**, such as the slip or transaction number (up to 64 characters).
6. Select **Record payment**.

If the payments now cover what is owed, the dialog closes and the row shows **✓ Paid**. Otherwise the dialog stays open, says "Payment recorded.", lists the payment, and shows what is **Still owed**. Take the rest the same way, now or later.

Enter what the patient pays towards the bill, not the note they hand over. If the amount is more than is owed, the dialog warns, for example, "$5.00 more than is owed. Enter what is paid towards the bill; change handed back is not part of the payment." You can still record it, and the dialog then shows the visit as **Overpaid**. An amount that is not a number, is zero, or is too large shows "Enter an amount such as 41.50, up to $1,000,000.00."

Each payment is listed with its amount, method, reference, the time, and who recorded it. Taking payment does not print a receipt. For a receipt, print the visit's invoice from [Billing](/cms/billing#finding-and-reprinting-invoices).

### What the patient owes

The top of the dialog sets out what the patient owes on the visit's bill:

| Line | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Cash due on the bill** | The bill's **Patient pays (cash)** amount. It reads **No bill yet** if the visit has not been billed, or **Could not be read** if the bill could not be read just now. |
| **Payers refused or paid short** | What a payer refused or paid less than the claim asked for. Only shown when there is some. |
| **Subsidy whose claim was discarded** | A subsidy the bill counted on, whose claim staff discarded. Only shown when there is some. |
| **Marked paid before amounts were recorded** | For a visit marked paid before payments were recorded with amounts, the cash due is counted as already paid. |
| **Paid so far** | The total of the payments taken, not counting voided ones. |
| **Still owed** or **Overpaid** | The difference. Not shown when it is zero. |

While a payer has not yet answered a claim on the bill, the subsidy is assumed to be paid and the dialog says "A payer has yet to answer a claim on this bill, so what is owed may still change."

In Singapore, **Claims today**, opened from the claims queue, shows the same figure for each checked-in patient at a location. See [SmartCMS Claims](/cms/smartcms/claims).

### When the visit is marked paid

Medbot marks the visit paid itself when the payments cover what is owed on an issued bill. Payments alone never mark a visit paid without a bill to compare them with.

- **No bill yet.** A payment can be taken before the visit is billed, for example a deposit. It is kept, but the visit is not marked paid: "Payment recorded. This visit has no bill yet, so it is not marked paid. It can be marked paid once the bill is issued and the payments cover it."
- **Bill could not be read.** The payment is kept and the paid status is left as it was. The dialog says so, for example "Payment recorded. The bill could not be read just now, so the visit is not marked paid: check it once the bill can be read." Open the dialog again later.
- **Payments already cover the bill.** When the bill is issued after the patient paid, or the bill leaves nothing for the patient to pay (for example, a scheme pays it in full), the dialog says "The payments taken already cover the bill." or "Nothing is due on this bill." Select **Mark visit paid**. It is refused if the payments do not cover the bill, for example "The payments taken do not cover the bill: SGD 12.00 is still owed."

Medbot marks a visit paid when someone records or voids a payment, or selects **Mark visit paid**. If a payer then refuses a claim or pays short, the visit stays marked paid, and the patient still sees it as paid. Reception and Clinic Admins, however, see an amber **Owes $X** button in the **Bill** column instead of **✓ Paid**. On a narrow window the **Bill** column is hidden, and the same warning appears under the patient's name as **Marked paid, owes $X**.

Select it to open the **Payment from** dialog. A notice at the top says "This visit is marked paid, but a payer refused or paid short on a claim. Take the balance below, or correct the claim in Claims." If no payer fell short, it says "This visit is marked paid, but its bill now asks for more than was paid. Take the balance as usual. The button goes back to **✓ Paid** at the next refresh once the payments cover what is owed.

The amount may still change while another payer has yet to answer a claim. The button's tooltip then adds "It may still change." It can take about a minute for the button to follow a payer's answer. Doctors and nurses do not see it, because they do not see the **Bill** column.

### Voiding a payment

A payment cannot be edited. If one was entered wrongly, void it and enter it again.

1. Open the dialog and select **Void** on the payment.
2. Optionally enter a **Reason (optional)** (up to 200 characters).
3. Select **Void payment**, or **Keep** to leave it.

The dialog says "Payment voided." The payment stays in the list, struck through, with who voided it and the reason. If the payments left no longer cover what is owed, the visit goes back to unpaid. Any Receptionist or Clinic Admin can void any payment.

### Payment messages

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "This payment was already recorded, so it was not entered a second time." | Nothing. Recording the same payment again, for example after a slow connection, does not enter it twice. |
| "This payment was already entered and has since been voided. Check the visit’s payments, then enter it again if it should stand." | Check the list, then enter the payment again if it is needed. |
| "This payment attempt was already used for a different payment. Close the window and enter the payment again." | Close the dialog, open it again, and enter the payment. |
| "This visit’s bill is in SGD; a payment cannot be in USD." | Payments must be in the bill's currency. Close the dialog and open it again. |
| "This payment was not found, or is already voided." | Close the dialog and open it again to see the current list. |
| "This visit has no bill yet, so there is nothing to settle the payments against." | Generate the invoice in [Billing](/cms/billing), then select **Mark visit paid**. |
| "The bill could not be read just now. Try again in a moment." | Try again later. |
| "No visit found for this appointment — it may not be in today’s queue yet." | Check the patient in first. |

## Cancelling, marking a no-show, and reopening

1. Select the ellipsis button on the row.
2. Choose **Cancel appointment** or **Mark no-show**, then confirm.

The patient leaves the active queue. To find the row again, set the status filter to **Show cancelled**. Select **Reopen** to put the patient back. A reopened patient gets a new ticket and a new place in the queue.

## When a patient leaves

If a patient who has arrived goes home before the visit is finished, record it so their number leaves the queue and the display boards. Select **Patient left** on their row in the [Missed Queue](#the-missed-queue), or use **Patient left before finishing** on the patient's visit in the service's [Workstation](/cms/services/workstation). Choose a reason: **Could not wait any longer**, **Felt better and chose to leave**, **Will come back another day**, **Sent elsewhere for care**, or **Another reason** with your own words. Then select **Record that the patient left**. The steps the patient did not reach stay on the record as they were.

Finishing a patient's last step in the Workstation finishes the visit: it takes their number off the queue and the waiting-room screen, and shows the patient their visit summary.

## What the doctor and nurse see

A doctor opens **Today's Patients**. Each row has **Start consultation**, which opens the patient's chart at **Encounters** for that appointment, or **Continue consultation** once one has started. For a patient who has checked in, **Start consultation** starts the encounter itself, once per visit, and shows a spinner while it works. The encounter is linked to that exact queue appointment, which moves the row to **Consultation** and shows **Complete visit**. A patient who has not checked in yet, or an organisation that asks for a care category, opens the **Start Encounter** dialog instead, which says "Create a new encounter for this patient's visit in today's queue." If the visit already has an open encounter, Medbot reuses it rather than starting a second. A finished visit's row shows **Review** instead. An encounter started later from that chart is not added to the finished visit. It goes to the patient's open visit in the queue, if they have one. See [Encounters & Clinical Notes](/cms/clinical-care/encounters). A nurse opens **Today's Queue**, and the **Vitals** button on each row opens the vitals form. See [Vitals](/cms/clinical-care/vitals). On a nurse-led visit the button reads **Start nurse visit**, then **Continue nurse visit**, and **Review** once the visit is finished. It opens the patient's chart at **Encounters** for that appointment, in the same way as a doctor's **Start consultation**. Neither role can check patients in or change the doctor. Each can call a room for the patients they lead, and use **Did not come**, the [Missed Queue](#the-missed-queue) and **Remarks** on them.

## Nurse-led visits

A visit booked to a nurse is nurse-led. See [Appointments](/cms/front-desk/appointments#nurse-led-visits). It stays in the queue with that nurse as its clinician, and no doctor is rotated onto it.

- **The lead nurse starts it** with **Start nurse visit**, and closes it with **Complete visit**, as a doctor would.
- **Whether a visit is nurse-led follows who took part.** If a doctor sees the patient, for example by opening the visit's encounter, signing an order or writing a note, the visit becomes doctor-led, and the nurse's row no longer offers **Complete visit**.
- **Who can close it.** The lead nurse or any doctor closes a nurse-led visit, and a Clinic Admin can close any visit. Other nurses cannot. On another nurse's visit, **Complete visit** is greyed out with "Led by &lt;name&gt;. Only the lead nurse or a doctor can close this visit." A nurse who tries to close a doctor-led visit is refused with "This visit is led by a doctor. A nurse closes only a visit that she leads; ask the doctor or an administrator to close it."
- **Marking it Completed on the board** follows the same rule. Only a Clinic Admin sees **Completed** in the **Route…** menu, and a receptionist cannot mark a visit completed.

In Singapore organisations, a scheme claim for a nurse-led visit is held for an administrator to review. See [Claims Workflow](/cms/smartcms/claims).

### Changing who leads a visit

A Clinic Admin can hand an open visit to another doctor or nurse, for example when the lead nurse goes off sick. This works for a visit a nurse has already started.

1. On the queue, select **Change lead** on the patient's row. It appears on rows that are not finished or cancelled.
2. The **Change who leads &lt;patient&gt;'s visit** dialog says who leads the visit now. Choose the **New lead** from the doctors and nurses who work at this location.
3. Optionally enter a **Reason (kept in the audit log)**.
4. Select **Change lead**.

The new lead is the one who can close the visit. **Change lead** is greyed out when no other doctor or nurse works at the location.

## Starting an AI encounter and completing the visit

Once a doctor has started a consultation for an appointment, two buttons appear on that row. On a nurse-led visit, they appear for the lead nurse once the nurse visit has started.

**Start AI encounter** offers the patient an AI encounter for this visit, one the assistant leads. Choose **Health education** or **Financial counselling**, and the **Start AI encounter** window opens. Add a **Reason, shown to the patient** and send the link. The row then shows a badge with the encounter type. The patient starts it from the portal, and does not need to be enrolled in a service. This is the same window as everywhere else: see [Starting an AI encounter](/cms/clinical-care/encounters#starting-an-ai-encounter). See also [Patient Portal](/cms/clinical-care/patient-portal).

**Complete visit** finishes the visit and sends the outcome to the patient.

1. Select **Complete visit**.
2. Optionally select **Generate draft** to have the assistant propose the text from the visit. Check and edit everything it writes.
3. Write the **Findings** in words the patient can read.
4. Under **Medicines started**, select **Add** and enter the medicine name and the dose and instructions.
5. Under **Next steps**, select **Add**, then choose **Follow-up appointment**, **Advice**, or **Test result** and describe what the patient should do. Any AI encounter you started already appears as an **AI session** step.
6. Turn on **Also notify the patient by SMS** if you want a text message as well.
7. Select **Send to patient**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/reception-queue/06-complete-visit.png" alt="The Complete visit panel with findings, one medicine, and two next steps filled in, and the Send to patient button at the bottom" />

The outcome goes to the patient's portal, and the row leaves the active list. Sending the outcome cannot be undone from the queue, so read it through first.

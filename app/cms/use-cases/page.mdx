# Use Cases

A use case is a walkthrough of one clinical area from end to end: how to set it up in Medbot CMS, what the care team does each day, and what the patient sees in the patient app. Each one runs in a [demo organisation](/cms/services/demo-services#your-demo-organisation) with synthetic patients, so you can follow it, or show it to a clinic, without touching a real record.

| Use case | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [IVF & Assisted Reproduction](/cms/use-cases/ivf) | A fertility centre with fourteen treatment cycles at every stage: the cycles board, protocols, starting a cycle, monitoring scans, plan changes approved by a doctor, doses, red flags, the patient's **My cycle**, and the cycle summary. |

# IVF & Assisted Reproduction

This guide sets up a fertility centre in Medbot CMS from nothing and then walks through every IVF feature, from the cycles board to the patient's **My cycle** in the patient app. It runs in a **demo organisation** with synthetic patients, so you can follow it on your own or use it to show Medbot to a clinic.

By the end you will have:

- a **demo organisation**, with you as its administrator
- an **IVF and assisted reproduction** demo service
- the **Orchard Fertility Centre** demo set: fourteen treatment cycles at every stage, a care team of four, and a sign-in for every staff member and patient

> **Preview.** IVF cycle tracking is still a preview. Its clinical content, including doses, thresholds and red-flag wording, has not yet been reviewed by a clinician. The demo protocols say so ("DEMO ONLY: ... No clinician has reviewed these values"). Do not use them for real patients.

> **Synthetic data only.** Demo patients have no real phone, email or ID number, and their addresses say "(demo address)". Nothing is ever sent to them. A demo organisation cannot hold real patients or staff. See [A demo organisation is sealed](/cms/services/demo-services#a-demo-organisation-is-sealed).

## Before you start

- **Who can do it.** Anyone signed in to Medbot CMS. You do not need to belong to an organisation, and the demo organisation does not count towards the one organisation you may create.
- **What you need.** A browser for Medbot CMS, and a second browser profile or private window for the patient app, so you can be signed in as a staff member and a patient at the same time.
- **How long it takes.** About five minutes to set up. Loading the fourteen patients takes a few minutes more and runs in the background.
- **The demo day.** The patients are dated relative to the day you load them, so their story is always current. "Today" in this guide is the day you load the set. Load it on the morning of a demonstration so that "tonight", "tomorrow" and "this week" mean what they say.

### What the load sets up

Besides the patients' charts, appointments, notes and portal records, loading the Orchard set:

- **Turns on IVF cycle tracking for this service.** The service gains a **Protocols** page, a **Cycles** tab on its Workstation and a **Cycle** part on each patient's page. A reset turns it off again.
- **Adds the care team**, with sign-ins (below).
- **Creates Dr Lim's protocols**: **Antagonist protocol (Dr Lim)** version 2, which superseded version 1, plus derived **IUI**, **Frozen embryo transfer** and **Stimulation only** protocols. All are shared with the nurse.
- **Fills in the clinic calendar**, if the organisation has none: opening hours and capacity (6 egg retrievals and 12 scans a session), and a day of leave for Dr Lim on the first Saturday at least three days ahead.
- **Adds fertility medicines to the formulary**, such as follitropin alfa and cetrorelix, so dictated doses can be checked against it.
- **Sets the service's message approval policy**, if it has none, so a nurse approves each plan-change message before the patient sees it.
- **Links a partner to every cycle**, so the partners' screening counts on the readiness checklist.
- **Runs the rule engine**, so every flag you see was raised by Medbot's rules, not typed in.
- **Writes the first weekly review pack**.

## The cycles board

The **Cycles** tab of the service's Workstation puts every running cycle on one board. Open it as **Dr Lim**: **Workstation** → **Cycles**. It has three views: **Board**, **Tomorrow** and **Weekly review**. The header links to **How the Cycles tab works**, this section.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/use-cases/ivf/06-cycles-board.png" alt="The Cycles board with its tiles, filter chips and fourteen cycles" />

**The tiles** count the day at a glance: **Active cycles** (14), **Scans today**, **Triggers this week** (5), **Needs attention** (2), **Open escalations** (0) and **Unconfirmed doses** (1). Click **Active cycles**, **Scans today**, **Triggers this week** or **Needs attention** to filter the board by it. A count that needs action shows in red. A note above the tiles says when the clinic is closed, for example "Clinic closed Sun 22 Nov (weekly hours)".

**The filter chips** narrow the board: **All cycles**, **Trigger likely this week**, **Scans today**, **Scans tomorrow**, **Needs attention**, **Needs decision**, **OHSS watch** and **Missing records**. **Save a filter** keeps your own combination, such as "trigger within 3 days and missing records", for everyone in the clinic.

Try these with the Orchard set:

- **Needs attention** shows **Mei Lin Tan** (**Antagonist start due**: her lead follicle reached 13 mm on day 6 and no antagonist dose is planned for tonight) and **Sarah Goh** (**Dose unconfirmed**). A cycle needs attention when it has an unacknowledged warning or an open escalation.
- **OHSS watch** shows **Hui Min Koh**. Dr Lim has already acknowledged her watch, so it stays on her row but does not count as needing attention.
- **Missing records** shows Mei Lin and Rachel Lim (varicella immunity) and Amanda Wong (treatment consent).
- **Trigger likely this week** shows the five patients due to trigger in the next seven days.

Each row shows the patient, the **Kind** (IVF, IUI, FET or Stim only), the cycle and stimulation **Day** ("CD6 · S5"), the **Last monitoring** (follicles, lead follicle, E2 and lining), the **Next action**, the **Trigger** date (an estimate is marked "(est.)") and the **Attention** badges, most urgent first. **Board settings** chooses the columns and the weekly review schedule.

**Click a row** to open its side sheet. For Mei Lin it explains, in full, why the antagonist is due, lists what is next (scans, the estimated trigger and egg retrieval), her last monitoring, the plan in force ("Version 1, approved ... ### Ask about the clinic

Below the board, **Ask about the clinic** answers questions from the board and nothing else, with the rows each answer rests on. Try the suggestions: "Who is likely to trigger this week, and is anyone missing something?", "Who is coming in tomorrow?", "Which cycles need a decision?" or "Is anyone on leave this week?". It cannot change anything, and it declines questions the board cannot answer. It uses the organisation's [AI policy](/cms/administration/ai-policy).

## Protocols

A protocol is its doctor's own. Open the service's **Protocols** page (the **Protocols** button in the service's header) as **Dr Lim**. The library lists **Antagonist protocol (Dr Lim)** at **v2 approved**, and the derived **Frozen embryo transfer**, **IUI** and **Stimulation only** protocols. Each card shows its owner and who it is shared with ("shared with Siti Nurhaliza (use only)"), how many **Prerequisites**, **Activities**, **Parameters adopted** and **Rules adopted** it has, and its **Versions**: version 1 was the conservative baseline, superseded by version 2, on which nine cycles are running.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/use-cases/ivf/10-protocol-library.png" alt="The protocol library with Dr Lim's antagonist, frozen embryo transfer, IUI and stimulation-only protocols" />

Open **Antagonist protocol (Dr Lim)** to see:

- **Prerequisites**, checked against the clinic's own results for each cycle: HIV, hepatitis B and C and syphilis screening for both partners within six months of egg retrieval, rubella and varicella immunity, AMH, semen analysis, counselling and signed treatment consent. Each says what it blocks, such as "blocks egg retrieval".
- **Activities**, placed on the clinic's calendar when the period starts: the baseline scan, follitropin alfa from stimulation day 1, cetrorelix from stimulation day 6, monitoring scans, the trigger, egg retrieval two days later, progesterone and the pregnancy blood test.
- **Parameters**, each with its source and who adopted it: for example **Antagonist flexible start, lead follicle** 12 mm, adopted by Dr Jonathan Lim. "A default is only in force once the owning doctor has adopted it."
- **Rules**, which remind and flag against those values: **Antagonist start due**, **Dose not confirmed**, **Checklist item missing**, **Trigger criteria met**, **OHSS watch** and **Luteal support missing**. "A rule that is not adopted is shown as not evaluated, never as passing."

## Change the plan

Mei Lin's scan today shows a 13 mm lead follicle, so the antagonist is due. Dr Lim changes her plan by dictation.

1. In **Change the plan** on her **Cycle**, type or say: "Reduce Gonal-f to 150 units from tonight, start cetrorelix 0.25 mg tonight, rescan with an estradiol on cycle day 8".
2. Click **Read it**. Medbot reads it into proposals and checks each against the formulary and her plan: "3 of 3 instructions can be proposed, checked against plan version 1."
   - **Dose change**: Follitropin alfa 225 IU to 150 IU, from today. Medbot knows Gonal-f is follitropin alfa.
   - **Start**: Cetrorelix 0.25 mg from today, with the note that starting it now replaces the plan's start rule.
   - **Visit**: a scan with oestradiol on cycle day 8, with a warning that a monitoring scan is already booked that day.
3. **Protocol rules** shows the effect on the rules: **Antagonist start due** now passes; **Checklist item missing** still does not (varicella).
4. Untick any proposal you do not want, then click **Confirm plan version 2**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/use-cases/ivf/15-change-the-plan.png" alt="Change the plan with three proposals read from a dictation, and the protocol rules check" />

The card confirms "Plan version 2 is in force" with what changed. From here, Medbot:

- reschedules the rest of the cycle from today and books the new visit
- drafts the medication orders, **unsigned**, for Dr Lim to sign
- drafts a message to the patient, which is sent according to the service's message approval policy

Only a doctor can confirm a plan. AI reads the dictation; nothing changes until the doctor confirms, and orders change only when the doctor signs.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/use-cases/ivf/16-task-inbox-doctor.png" alt="Dr Lim's Task Inbox with the message to the patient, the medication orders to sign, and Hui Min Koh's OHSS watch" />

2. Click **Review and sign**. **Sign 2 orders?** shows each order with its safety checks, for example "Already taking Follitropin alfa 300 IU pre-filled pen" and the allergy status not being recorded.
3. Choose what happens **After signing** (**Finish the encounter** or **Keep the encounter open**) and click **Sign and finish encounter**, or **Keep as drafts**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/use-cases/ivf/16b-sign-orders.png" alt="The Sign 2 orders dialog for cetrorelix and follitropin alfa, with its safety warnings and the After signing choice" />

### Approve the message to the patient

The same change drafts a message to Mei Lin. With the demo's approval policy, a nurse approves it first. As **Siti**, open **Notifications** and select **Review** on the task. **Treatment plan: Message the patient in the patient portal** shows the draft:

> Hello Mei, Dr Lim has updated your treatment plan (version 2). What changed: Cetrorelix starts on Sat 21 Nov; Follitropin alfa dose: 225 IU to 150 IU; Scan on cycle day 8. Your reminders and visits in My cycle are updated. If anything does not look right, reply here and the clinic will help.

## A new cycle starts

## Red flags and escalations

1. In the patient app, as **Hui Min Koh**, open **Log** → **How you feel** (or **My cycle** → **Your diary** → **How you feel**). **Bloating** is the first choice for an IVF patient.
2. Choose **Something else** and describe it: "Very swollen, 2 kg heavier since yesterday, hard to breathe lying down". Tap **Save symptom**.
3. The service's red-flag rules match it at once, before any AI is involved. **Please read this**: "This may need urgent medical attention. Please call 995 or go to your nearest emergency department now. I've alerted your care team." with **Call 995** and **I understand, continue**.

## Reset or remove the demo

On the service's **Demo patients** panel, as its owner or an organisation admin:

- **Reset and reload** deletes everything the demo patients have, including what you did while demoing, and loads them again dated from today. Do this before each demonstration to start from the numbers in this guide. It also ends the demo sign-ins; the next load gives them a new password.
- **Remove demo patients** deletes them and everything they touched, and turns IVF cycle tracking off again for the service.

To remove everything, delete the demo organisation from its card on **Organisations**. See [Remove or reset the demo](/cms/services/demo-services#remove-or-reset-the-demo) and [Deleting your demo organisation](/cms/services/demo-services#deleting-your-demo-organisation).

## In a real service

Outside a demo organisation, IVF cycle tracking is turned on per service by Medbot; contact Medbot to try it. Once it is on, doctors, nurses and admins start each cycle from the patient's page in the service with the **Start a cycle** card: choose the **Protocol** and the **Responsible doctor**, enter **Cycle day 1** if known, and click **Start cycle**. The cycle opens as planned on the protocol's approved version, and everything in this guide follows from there. A doctor first needs a protocol: **Start from the conservative baseline** on the **Protocols** page, then teach and adopt it. See [IVF Cycles](/cms/services/ivf-cycles).

## Troubleshooting

- **No Cycles tab, Protocols page or Cycle part.** IVF cycle tracking turns on when the Orchard set loads. Check the **Demo patients** panel says "14 demo patients loaded". If loading failed, use the panel's retry.
- **The numbers differ from this guide.** The story is dated from the day you loaded it, and the board reads the real clock. Use **Reset and reload** on the morning of a demonstration.
- **A demo sign-in does not work.** A reset ends the sign-ins and the next load gives a new password. Copy it again from **Demo sign-ins**.
- **"Read it" fails.** Dictation, **Ask about the clinic** and the review pack's summary need an AI model that the organisation's [AI policy](/cms/administration/ai-policy) allows.

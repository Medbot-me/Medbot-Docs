# Introduction

## What is an organisation?

An **organisation** is a clinic, hospital or care group as a tenant of Medbot CMS: its own staff, its own locations, its own patients, and, crucially, **its own patient records**, kept apart from every other organisation's. It owns one or more [services](/cms/services): a clinic is one kind of service, and an inpatient ward or a remote care service are others. Everything the CMS does happens inside exactly one organisation at a time.

Medbot CMS is multi-organisation, and it is where everything an organisation does lives: its clinic operations, its [care services](/cms/services) and its administration. Medbot web is for each person's own work. Any signed-in user can create an organisation, add staff to it with roles, and run every clinic workflow (patients, queue, sessions, results, documents, rostering, inventory, billing) scoped to the organisation they have chosen for this sign-in. A user who belongs to more than one switches between them.

> This is a change from the single-tenant CMS. There is no "first admin" bootstrap step: the first admin is whoever creates the organisation. Medbot makes the organisation's patient records store as part of creating it, so there is nothing to connect or configure.

## Key concepts

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Organisation** | The tenant. Carries a name, a slug, a **country** and **time zone**, an active flag, a join code, and its own patient records store. |
| **Service** | What an organisation delivers care through. Only a service has a queue, takes appointments and has a roster; the organisation and its locations hold what services share, such as the day's session, rooms and display boards. See [Services](/cms/services) and [Modes of Care](/cms/services/modes-of-care). |
| **Membership** | A person's place in one organisation, with exactly one [role](/cms/administration/members-and-roles) there. The same person can be a member of several organisations, with a different role in each. |
| **Current organisation** | The one the CMS is rendering right now. Chosen on the [welcome screen](/cms/administration/switching#the-welcome-screen) at every sign-in and changed from the sidebar switcher. See [Choosing & Switching](/cms/administration/switching). |
| **Join code** | A short code an admin hands to a new hire so they can request access from the app. See [Join an Organisation](/cms/administration/join-an-organisation). |
| **Access request** | A pending request to join, filed by join code and resolved by an admin from the staff screen. |
| **Country-specific integrations** | National services available to organisations in one country. Today that means [SmartCMS](/cms/smartcms) for organisations in Singapore. |

## What you can do

1. **Create an organisation** and become its first administrator: [Create an Organisation](/cms/administration/create-organisation).
2. **Join an existing one** with a join code, or have an admin invite you by email: [Join an Organisation](/cms/administration/join-an-organisation).
   Doctors can also have a small organisation of their own for themselves and their family: [Physician Family Organisation](/cms/administration/physician-family-organisation).
3. **Belong to several** and choose which one to work in: [Choosing & Switching](/cms/administration/switching).
4. **Bring in staff and patients**, one at a time or in bulk from a CSV file: [Members & Roles](/cms/administration/members-and-roles) and [Seeding Staff & Patients](/cms/administration/seed-data).
5. **Administer the organisation**: rename it, set its country and time zone, choose its integrations, rotate its join code: [Organisation Settings](/cms/administration/settings). Decide which AI providers and regions its AI may use: [AI Policy](/cms/administration/ai-policy).
6. **Connect to Singapore's national systems** through SmartCMS: verify CHAS at check-in, claim CHAS, Medisave and other subsidies from the bill, and send registry and disease reports: [SmartCMS](/cms/smartcms).

## The organisations screen

`/organisations` lists every organisation the signed-in user belongs to, with their role in each and a **Current** badge on the active one. From it they can switch to another, open **Settings** on any they administer, **Create organisation**, or **Claim an existing organisation** that Medbot already lists. The same screen shows:

- a banner when the user has just claimed an organisation: "&lt;name&gt; is on Medbot, with you as its first admin.", with **Open it**
- the demo organisation, and a doctor's [physician family organisation](/cms/administration/physician-family-organisation)
- **Your claims**, each with its status. "A Medbot administrator reviews every claim."
- **Join with a code**, to [request to join](/cms/administration/join-an-organisation) with a join code from an admin

Unlike every other staff page, this one does not require an organisation to be chosen. A brand-new user with no memberships is sent to onboarding instead, to join or set up an organisation (see [Getting Started](/cms/getting-started)). Opened with no memberships at all, the empty state says "You don't belong to any organisation yet. Create one or ask an administrator for a join code."

## Inside an organisation

Once an organisation is chosen, the sidebar has two tabs. **Home** holds the care service you are working in, [Notifications](/cms/clinical-care/notifications), the Workstation, patients and the clinic's operations. **AI** holds the [Home assistant](/cms/home-assistant) and the organisation's administration (staff, settings and the [AI policy](/cms/administration/ai-policy)). See [Staff App Overview](/cms/overview#home-and-cms).

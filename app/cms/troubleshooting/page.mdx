# Troubleshooting

Common questions and what to do next. If a control is missing or disabled, it is usually a role limit; check [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

## I signed in but landed on a "choose your organization" screen

You choose an organization once per sign-in session, even if you belong to only one. Select **Enter as** on the one you want to work in. See [Choosing & Switching](/cms/switching).

## I have two roles but can only do one role's work

You work as one role at a time, and permissions do not add up across roles. Use the **Working as** button at the right of the top bar to switch to the role that can do the task. If you do not see that button, you hold only one active role in this organization; ask your Clinic Admin to add another role. See [Switching role](/cms/switching#switching-role).

## A button I expected is missing or greyed out

What you can do depends on your role in the current organization. For example, only a Clinic Admin and a Receptionist can check a patient in, and only a Doctor can sign off a result. Check the **Who can do this** table on the feature page, or the full [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions) matrix.

## The reception queue is empty or says "No session yet today"

Nobody has opened a session for the location yet. Select **Create session**, or open one from [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup). Also confirm you have chosen the right location at the top of the [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue).

## A patient checked in but has no doctor

Check-in assigns the next doctor marked active in the session. If no doctor is marked active, the patient still gets a ticket but no doctor. Mark a doctor active in [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup), then assign the doctor on the row.

## I cannot call a patient to a room

Only rooms set up for today's session are listed. Add or assign rooms in [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup) first.

## I cannot publish the roster

Only a Clinic Admin can publish a week. A Receptionist can build and adjust a draft. See [Rostering](/cms/rostering#publishing-a-week-clinic-admin-only).

## A staff member reads as absent for a shift they worked

Attendance is derived from activity in the app during a published shift, not from a clock-in button. A shift with no recorded activity reads as **Absent**. A Clinic Admin can [correct the record](/cms/attendance#finalizing-and-correcting) with a reason.

## I cannot correct my own attendance

A Clinic Admin cannot reopen or correct their own record. Another Clinic Admin has to do it. See [Attendance](/cms/attendance).

## A voided document or record is still in the list

Voiding keeps the record for the history and marks it as voided, rather than removing it. This is deliberate. See [Documents & Certificates](/cms/documents-and-certificates#voiding-a-document).

## A page says "Medbot cannot reach" a service

A service Medbot depends on is down. Your record is not missing and nothing has been deleted. Select **Try again** in a moment. If it continues, switch to your clinic's downtime laptop, or follow your clinic's downtime procedure on paper. See [System Status](/cms/system-status#when-a-page-cannot-load) and [Working During an Outage](/cms/downtime/working-offline).

## A form says "We could not confirm this was saved"

The save may have reached Medbot before the answer was lost. What you typed is still in the form. Check the record before trying again, so nothing is entered twice. See [When a form cannot save](/cms/system-status#when-a-form-cannot-save).

## An amber or red banner is at the top of every page

Medbot is degraded (amber) or has a major outage (red). The banner says what is affected and what still works. See [The status banner](/cms/system-status#the-status-banner).

## A claim or report shows "Waiting for SmartCMS to recover"

The SmartCMS gateway is down. The submission is held, not lost, and is sent by itself when the gateway answers again. You do not need to resubmit it. See [Gateway Outages & Deadlines](/cms/smartcms/outages).

## A claim is held because the visit is nurse-led

The scheme needs the attending doctor's MCR number, which a nurse-led visit does not have. If a doctor has since taken part in the visit, select **Submit (check visit)**. Otherwise a Clinic Admin decides whether to **Submit as nurse-led**. See [Claims on a nurse-led visit](/cms/smartcms/claims#claims-on-a-nurse-led-visit).

## A downtime laptop is read-only or locked

A laptop that has not reached Medbot for more than 72 hours turns read-only, and after 7 days it locks. Connect it to the network and select **Connect to Medbot now**. A laptop whose clock looks wrong keeps everyone out until its clock agrees with Medbot's. See [Safety locks](/cms/downtime/working-offline#safety-locks).

## I typed my offline passphrase wrong too many times

After the 10th wrong passphrase in a row you are locked out of that laptop until a Clinic Admin re-enrolls you from its **Session** tab. Nothing is erased. See [Unlocking the laptop](/cms/downtime/working-offline#unlocking-the-laptop).

## The downtime laptop is still in downtime although Medbot is back

Downtime never ends by itself. A doctor or Clinic Admin selects **End downtime** on the laptop, and ends the outage in the CMS once the laptops have uploaded. See [Ending downtime](/cms/downtime/working-offline#ending-downtime).

## A reminder says downtime entries are overdue

Entries you wrote on a downtime laptop are waiting for you to verify them, past the 72-hour target. Select **Review them**, or open **Downtime recovery**. See [Recovery After an Outage](/cms/downtime/recovery).

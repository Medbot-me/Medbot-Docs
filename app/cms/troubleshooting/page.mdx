# Troubleshooting

Common questions and what to do next. If a control is missing or disabled, it is usually a role limit; check [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions).

## I signed in but landed on a "choose your organization" screen

You choose an organization once per sign-in session. Select the one you want to work in. If you belong to only one, it opens straight away. See [Choosing & Switching](/cms/switching).

## A button I expected is missing or greyed out

What you can do depends on your role in the current organization. For example, only a Clinic Admin and a Receptionist can check a patient in, and only a Doctor can sign off a result. Check the **Who can do this** table on the feature page, or the full [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions) matrix.

## The reception queue is empty or says "No session yet today"

Nobody has opened a session for the location yet. Select **Create session**, or open one from [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup). Also confirm you have chosen the right location at the top of the [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue).

## A patient checked in but has no doctor

Check-in assigns the next doctor marked active in the session. If no doctor is marked active, the patient still gets a ticket but no doctor. Mark a doctor active in [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup), then assign the doctor on the row.

## I cannot call a patient to a room

Only rooms set up for today's session are listed. Add or assign rooms in [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup) first.

## I cannot publish the roster

Only a Clinic Admin can publish a week. A Receptionist can build and adjust a draft. See [Rostering](/cms/rostering#publishing-a-week-clinic-admin-only).

## A staff member reads as absent for a shift they worked

Attendance is derived from activity in the app during a published shift, not from a clock-in button. A shift with no recorded activity reads as **Absent**. A Clinic Admin can [correct the record](/cms/attendance#finalizing-and-correcting) with a reason.

## I cannot correct my own attendance

A Clinic Admin cannot reopen or correct their own record. Another Clinic Admin has to do it. See [Attendance](/cms/attendance).

## A voided document or record is still in the list

Voiding keeps the record for the history and marks it as voided, rather than removing it. This is deliberate. See [Documents & Certificates](/cms/documents-and-certificates#voiding-a-document).

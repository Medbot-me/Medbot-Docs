# Documents & Certificates

A patient's **Documents** section lists their letters, memos, referral letters, and medical certificates. Open it from the patient's chart, in the sidebar under the patient's name. Voided documents stay in the list, marked **Voided**, so the history is kept.

## Who can do this

| Task | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See documents, and download, print, or email them | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Compose a letter or memo | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Compose a referral letter | Yes | No | Yes | No |
| Issue a medical certificate | No | No | Yes | No |
| Void a letter or memo | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Void a referral letter or medical certificate | Yes | No | Yes | No |

You can void only the kinds of document you are allowed to compose. A nurse can void a memo but not a referral.

A medical certificate prints the issuing doctor's MCR number, so it can only be issued while working as a Doctor. A Clinic Admin does not see **Issue medical certificate**. Someone who is both a Clinic Admin and a Doctor switches to their Doctor role first (see [Switching role](/cms/switching#switching-role)). Otherwise the request is refused with "A medical certificate carries the issuing doctor's MCR number, so it can only be issued while working as a doctor. Switch to your Doctor role to issue one." A Clinic Admin can still void a medical certificate.

## Composing a letter, memo, or referral

1. Select **Compose letter/memo**.
2. Choose the **Type**: **Letter** (for the patient or a third party), **Memo** (an internal note filed against the record), or **Referral Letter** (to another physician or specialist). Nurses see only **Letter** and **Memo**.
3. For a referral, enter the **Specialty**, for example "Hematology". It is required. You can also enter who it is **Referred to (optional)**.
4. For a letter, you can enter **To (optional)** and **Recipient email (optional)**.
5. Enter the **Subject** and the **Body**.
6. Select **Compose letter**, or **Compose referral letter**.

The button stays disabled until the required fields are filled. Medbot confirms with a message such as "Letter composed." The document then appears in the list with its author and date.

## Issuing a medical certificate

1. Select **Issue medical certificate**.
2. Choose the **Leave type**: **Outpatient Medical Leave** (not fit for work or school), **Light Duty** (fit for light duties only), or **Hospitalization Leave** (admitted or hospitalized). No other types exist.
3. Enter the **Start date** and **End date**. The end date must be on or after the start date.
4. Check **Number of days**. Medbot counts both the first and last day, so 25 to 27 September is 3 days. You do not enter it.
5. Optionally, add **Comments (optional)**.
6. Select **Issue certificate**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/documents-and-certificates/03-issue-certificate.png" alt="The Issue medical certificate window with Outpatient Medical Leave chosen, dates 25 to 27 September, and 3 days shown" />
_Issuing a certificate_

Each certificate gets a serial number for its clinic location and year, such as `MAIN-MC-2026-000001`. The count for each location and year starts at 000001 and goes up by one. A serial is never reused, even if the certificate is voided.

If you see "Your account is missing a linked practitioner profile or medical license number; contact an admin before issuing certificates.", ask a Clinic Admin to complete your staff record.

## Downloading, printing, and emailing

Select a row to open the document. From the keyboard, move to the document's summary with Tab and press Enter. Everyone who can see documents can use **Download**, **Print**, and **Email**.

Dates on a medical certificate or letter PDF are printed in the clinic's format, for example "4 Sept 1979", and as the day in Singapore, whatever the computer's own time zone or language.

For **Email**, the address starts as the patient's email. Medbot sends the document as a PDF attachment, but only if the patient has consented to it. It checks two consents in the patient's communication consent: **Documents sent to the patient** for the patient's own address, and **Documents sent to a third party** for any other address. See [Patient Records & Consent](/cms/patient-records).

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Enter a valid email address. | The address is not written correctly. |
| The patient has not consented to document delivery to their email address. | Ask the patient, then record consent on their chart. |
| The patient has not consented to document delivery to a third party by email. | The address is not the patient's. Record that consent first. |

Sending email needs an email sender to be set up for your Medbot deployment. If it is not, the send fails and you see a message that email is not configured. Ask whoever runs your deployment.

## Voiding a document

Voiding marks a document as made in error. Use it to fix a mistake: void the original, then compose or issue a new one.

1. Open the document and select **Void**.
2. Type a **Reason for voiding**.
3. Select **Confirm void**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/documents-and-certificates/04-void-document.png" alt="A referral letter open with a reason for voiding typed and the Confirm void button" />
_Voiding a referral letter_

Voiding cannot be undone. The document stays in the list with a **Voided** label, the reason, who voided it, and when. A voided document can no longer be downloaded, printed, or emailed. Its serial number is not reused.

## Checking a document

Each document's PDF carries a QR code and a link. Anyone who scans it, such as an employer or a school, opens a public page with no sign-in. The page says **Document is valid** or **Document has been voided**. It shows only who the document is for, its type, who issued it, and its date. For a medical certificate it also shows the leave type, dates, and serial.

It never shows a letter's text or a certificate's comments, because these can carry a diagnosis. A voided document's link still opens, showing that it was voided, so a replaced certificate visibly fails the check. An unknown or expired link shows **Document not found**.

## Result explanations

When a doctor approves a [result explanation](/cms/diagnostic-results#explaining-results-to-the-patient), its PDF is added to the patient's **Documents** as a **Result explanation**. Any staff member can select it, then **Open PDF** or **Print**. Doctors and nurses also see **View the explanation**. A result explanation cannot be voided from Documents. A doctor withdraws it from the explanation page, and it then shows as **Withdrawn** and can no longer be opened or printed. A version replaced by a revision is hidden.

## What the patient sees

The patient can read documents and letters in the patient app. See [Patient](/patient).

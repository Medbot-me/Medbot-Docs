# Patient-Facing Displays

A display is a screen in the waiting room or on a room door that shows which queue ticket is being called. It needs no sign-in. A Clinic Admin sets displays up in the **Displays** section of [Session Setup](/cms/session-setup) (`/session`).

## Who can do this

| Task | Clinic Admin | Receptionist | Doctor | Nurse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Add a display | Yes | No | No | No |
| See the list of displays | Yes | No | No | No |
| Revoke a display | Yes | No | No | No |

Reception controls what a display shows by calling patients to rooms. See [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue).

## Adding a display

1. Open **Scheduling → Session** and choose the clinic location.
2. In **Displays**, select **Add display**.
3. Enter a **Name**, for example "Lobby TV".
4. Choose the **Type**: **Waiting room (full board)** or **Room door (single room)**. For a room door screen, also choose the **Room**.
5. Select **Create**.

A window says the display is ready and shows its web address. Open that address on the display device, or select the copy button. **The address is shown only once.** If you lose it, revoke the display and add a new one.

The **Displays** list shows each display with its **Name**, **Type**, **Room**, **Last seen**, and **Status**. **Last seen** tells you whether the device is still open.

## What each display shows

A display belongs to one location. It updates itself about every four seconds, so nobody needs to reload it. It never shows a patient's name.

The waiting room board shows the location name and the time of the last update. Under **NOW SERVING** it lists the ticket last called for each room, with the room name. Under **WAITING** it lists the tickets of patients who have checked in and not been called. Before anyone is called it says **No patients called yet**. At the bottom it shows the **Self check-in code**, so patients can check in from their phones. See [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue).

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/display-boards/03-waiting-room-board.png" alt="A black waiting room board titled Main Clinic, with one ticket in Now serving beside Consult 1, one ticket under Waiting, and the check-in code at the bottom" />
_A waiting room board_

A room door screen shows the room name and only the ticket called to that room, with the words **Now serving**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/cms/display-boards/04-room-door-screen.png" alt="A black room door screen showing the room name Consult 1 and the ticket now being served" />

A ticket stays on a display until the patient is routed to another stage or called again.

## Revoking a display

In the **Displays** list, select **Revoke** on the display. There is no confirmation, and the status changes to **Revoked** at once. A revoked display, or a wrong address, shows a message that starts "Display deactivated" and tells people to contact reception. A revoked display cannot be turned on again. Add a new one instead.

> **Note:** Treat the display address like a key. Anyone who has it can open the board.

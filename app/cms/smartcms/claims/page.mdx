# Claims Workflow

This page follows a Singapore subsidy or Medisave claim from check-in to payment: verifying the patient's CHAS card, coding the diagnoses, billing the visit, and then preparing, sending, tracking and correcting the claim from the **Claims** queue.

The claim types Medbot handles are:

| Claim | Module | Sent to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CHAS** acute and chronic subsidies | CHAS | MOH's MHCP |
| **PHPC** (Haze, Flu Subsidy Scheme) | CHAS | MHCP |
| **Vaccination (VSS)** and **childhood developmental screening (CDS)** subsidies | CHAS | MHCP |
| **Screen for Life** screenings and follow-up consults | SFL | MHCP |
| **Medisave** for the Chronic Disease Management Programme | CDMP | CPF, through MediClaim |

> **Before go-live.** These workflows are built and tested against Synapxe's specifications and a mock gateway. A clinic location sends nothing until Synapxe approves it and an admin switches it on. See [SmartCMS Settings](/cms/smartcms/settings).

## The workflow at a glance

| Step | What happens | Who does it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. **Check-in** | The patient is checked in at the reception queue. Medbot verifies their CHAS card on the first check-in of the day. | Clinic Admin or Receptionist checks the patient in. Every clinic role sees the CHAS badge and can select **Verify now**. |
| 2. **Start the consultation** | The doctor selects **Start consultation** on **Today's Patients**, which creates the encounter for the visit. A claim needs it: the diagnoses, the bill and every claim belong to this encounter. | Doctor. A Clinic Admin or Nurse can also start an encounter from the patient chart. |
| 3. **Code the diagnoses** | The visit's diagnoses are coded in ICD-10-AM on the encounter and saved. | Doctor or Clinic Admin. |
| 4. **Order screenings and record vaccinations** | Only for a visit that claims them: a Screen for Life screening is ordered, a developmental screening (CDS) is recorded, or a vaccination is recorded with its SDD product. | Screen for Life: Clinic Admin, Doctor or Nurse. CDS: Clinic Admin or Doctor. |
| 5. **Bill the visit** | The charges are added and the invoice is generated for the visit, with what to claim: CHAS, subsidies and Medisave. For Medisave, the payer and their consent are checked first. | Clinic Admin or Receptionist. |
| 6. **Claims are prepared** | Issuing the bill prepares its claims. A complete claim is queued to send. A claim with gaps is held as **Waiting for review**. | Automatic. |
| 7. **Review and submit** | Only for a claim waiting for review: the cause is fixed, then the claim is re-prepared and submitted from the Claims queue. | Clinic Admin or Receptionist. |
| 8. **Sending** | Medbot sends each queued claim to MHCP, or to CPF for Medisave. | Automatic. |
| 9. **Tracking** | Medbot follows MHCP's or CPF's status until the claim is paid or closed. Staff can ask for the status at any time. | Automatic. Clinic Admin, Receptionist and Doctor can see the queue and refresh a CHAS claim. Only a Clinic Admin or Receptionist can check CPF. |
| 10. **Following up with CPF** | Medbot flags a Medisave claim CPF has not answered for 20 days. Staff match or dismiss messages from CPF that match no claim, and check that CPF's payment files tie out. | Clinic Admin or Receptionist. |
| 11. **Corrections** | Only when something was wrong: a rejected CHAS claim is appealed, a claim MHCP rejected is resubmitted, a Medisave claim is amended or cancelled, or the bill is voided and corrected. | Clinic Admin or Doctor appeals. Clinic Admin resubmits. Clinic Admin or Receptionist amends, cancels and voids. |

The [claims agent](/cms/smartcms/claims-agent) can also prepare claims every evening, and staff can ask it to prepare today's claims on demand. **Claims today** shows each patient's bill, claims and what they owe, one clinic day at a time.

## 1. Check-in and CHAS verification

When a patient checks in at the [reception queue](/cms/reception-queue) for the first time that day, Medbot asks MHCP for their CHAS card, chronic balance and acute balance. Verification never blocks check-in. If it fails, the patient is still checked in.

The patient header then shows a **CHAS badge** to every clinic role:

- the card name, or **CHAS not verified**, **No CHAS card**, or the card name followed by **expired**
- the card's validity, the chronic tier, the chronic and acute balances, and the number of visits at this clinic
- when the card was last **checked**, in Singapore time

**Verify now** asks MHCP again, and says "Verified at" the location when it succeeds. It checks at the clinic of the patient's visit in today's queue session, or at the organisation's default location if there is none.

If verification fails, the badge says why, for example:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Not verified: the patient has no Singapore NRIC (S or T) recorded." | Record the patient's NRIC on their [identifiers](/cms/patient-records). |
| "Not verified: … is not connected to SmartCMS for CHAS. It needs an HE code and CHAS switched on in the SmartCMS settings." | An admin finishes the location's [SmartCMS settings](/cms/smartcms/settings). |
| "Not verified: SmartCMS is switched off on this Medbot server." | Nothing is sent from this server. Contact Medbot. |
| "Not verified: MHCP's answer could not be read. Try again later." | Select **Verify now** later. |

A card verified more than 30 days ago, or in an earlier year than the visit, is flagged as stale. MHCP is asked again when the bill is issued, and **Verify now** checks it straight away.

## 2. Code the diagnoses

CHAS, Medisave, Screen for Life and the national reports all need the visit's diagnoses coded in **ICD-10-AM**. On the patient chart's **Overview**, the **Diagnoses (ICD-10-AM)** card covers the current visit. See [Encounters & Clinical Notes](/cms/encounters).

- Search by code or name, for example `J06.9` or "diabetes", and select **Add a diagnosis**.
- Add complications, and pick from the suggested CDMP conditions for a chronic visit.
- Select **Save diagnoses**. A code that is not on MOH's lists is marked **Not on MOH's lists**, because MHCP and CPF will not accept it.

Only Clinic Admins and doctors can edit diagnoses. Everyone else sees them read-only.

Coding a notifiable disease prepares a disease notification. A visit with a chronic diagnosis gets a CIDC report once it is completed or billed. Both wait for a doctor in [Reports](/cms/smartcms/reports).

### Screenings and vaccinations

- **Screen for Life.** The **Screen for Life** panel on the encounter checks eligibility with HPB and the patient's CHAS history (**Check eligibility**), then **Order screening**.
- **Developmental screening.** The **Childhood developmental screening (CDS)** panel shows the child's previous claims (**Check CDS history**) and records a screening (**Record screening**). It warns when MHCP already has a claim for that milestone, because a second one is rejected unless appealed.
- **Vaccinations.** Choosing the **SDD product** when you record a vaccination lets Medbot claim the vaccination subsidy and notify the National Immunisation Registry. The **Vaccination subsidy (VSS)** panel checks eligibility. See [Vaccinations](/cms/vaccinations).

## 3. Bill the visit

Claims start from the bill. In Singapore, a visit has **one bill**, and each bill belongs to one visit. On [Billing](/cms/billing), select **Generate invoice** and choose the **Visit** the bill is for.

Below the charges, the dialog shows a panel for each claim the visit can carry. A panel appears only if the organisation subscribes to its module **and** the visit's clinic location is connected. Otherwise the dialog says "SmartCMS is not connected for … at this location yet."

### CHAS claim

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheme** | **No CHAS claim**, **CHAS Acute**, **CHAS Chronic**, **PHPC Haze** or **PHPC Flu Subsidy Scheme**. |
| **Tier** (chronic only) | **Tier 1: less severe** or **Tier 2: more severe, with complications**. |
| **Amount to claim (SGD)** | The subsidy to claim. |
| **Diagnoses (ICD-10-AM)** | Filled in from the diagnoses coded at the visit. |
| **Referred to (optional)** | Where the patient was referred, if anywhere. |

PHPC also asks for the **Patient type** and any justification MHCP requires. The charges are grouped by their **Bill category** from the [Billable Catalog](/cms/billable-catalog).

### Screen for Life, vaccination and CDS subsidies

- **Screen for Life** lists the screenings and the follow-up consult to claim. A patient without a CHAS card is claimed as **No CHAS card (NONCHAS)**.
- **Vaccination and CDS subsidy claims** lists one line per claimable vaccination or screening, each with a **Check** against MHCP. A Singapore citizen without a CHAS card is claimed as **Citizen without a CHAS card (NC-SC)**.

### Medisave

1. Tick **Pay part with Medisave**.
2. Under **Check a payer**, enter the payer's NRIC and who the payer is (the patient or a family member).
3. Confirm that the patient and payer signed the once-off Medisave consent form (MCAF(S)). The consent must be dated on the visit day.
4. Select **Check Medisave**. Medbot shows the payer's balance.
5. Choose the **Chronic condition** and enter how much **Medisave pays**. Medbot caps it: "Medisave can pay at most $X of this bill: the payer can claim $Y, and the patient pays $Z after the bill's subsidies."

### After you generate the bill

The invoice gets a receipt number of the form `{LOCATION}-RC-{YYYY}-{NNNNNN}`. Its summary shows the category totals, **Net**, the GST included, and the **Total**. Below that are the estimated subsidies (CHAS, vaccination, CDS, Screen for Life), Medisave, and **Patient pays (cash)**.

If the claims could not be queued at that moment, the invoice is still issued and the dialog says: "The invoice is issued, but its SmartCMS claims could not be queued just now. They will be queued by the nightly check; see the claims queue tomorrow."

## 4. Claims are prepared

Issuing the bill prepares its claims automatically. Issuing the bill is the human decision, so a claim with nothing missing goes straight to **Queued to send**.

A claim with gaps is **held** as **Waiting for review**, with each problem listed in red on its row. Typical problems:

- "No CHAS card is verified for the patient; verify it, or choose a non-card patient type for PHPC."
- "The visit has no attending doctor with an MCR number."
- "The attending doctor's NRIC is not recorded."
- "The clinic location has no HE code."
- "The payer's once-off Medisave consent was taken on …, not on the visit day…"
- "Medisave cannot pay more than the $… the patient pays after the government subsidies on the bill (RC103, RC104)."
- "The patient's race is not recorded…"

Fix the cause, then select **Re-prepare** on the claim. If any one Screen for Life claim on a visit is held, the visit's whole Screen for Life batch waits.

A daily check at 02:30 (Singapore time) picks up any issued bill from the past week whose claims were not prepared. Where the agent policy is on, the nightly [claims agent](/cms/smartcms/claims-agent) run also prepares any vaccination or CDS subsidy claim that was missed, including on bills already paid.

## 5. The Claims queue

**SmartCMS → Claims** (`/claims`) lists every claim for the organisation, newest activity first. The page says which modules it covers and that "MHCP status is checked every four hours during the day." Below that, **Claims today** opens the [day view](#claims-today). With Medisave, Clinic Admins and receptionists also see a link to **Medisave payments from CPF**.

From top to bottom, the page shows:

1. **Claims agent.** The agent's recent runs and the buttons to start one. See [Claims Agent](/cms/smartcms/claims-agent).
2. **Notes for staff.** "What an agent or Medbot could not prepare, and why. Take each up by hand, then mark it resolved." Select **Resolve** once you have dealt with a note.
3. **The queue.** Filter by **All**, **Prepared** or **Needs attention**.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Claim** | The module, location, claim number, a summary and any problems. |
| **Prepared** | **Prepared by staff**, **Prepared automatically** or **Prepared by agent** and the agent's name. |
| **Status** | Where the claim stands. See below. |
| **Paid** | The amount paid and the date. |

**Needs attention** collects claims that are waiting for review, were refused or not confirmed, are parked because a location was switched off, were paid short by Medisave, have had no answer from CPF for 20 days, or that MHCP or CPF rejected, recovered or returned with an error. A rejected claim leaves the list once it is resubmitted, and comes back if its resubmission is discarded.

### Actions on a claim

| Action | When it is offered |
| --- | --- |
| **Submit** | A prepared claim with no problems. |
| **Submit all prepared: … at …** | Every prepared claim for one module at one location. Held claims are left for review: "… claim(s) held with issues were left for review." |
| **Re-prepare** | A held CHAS claim, after you fix the cause. The result says it is ready to submit, or still held and why. Not offered on a resubmission. |
| **Discard** | Any claim not yet sent. You are asked "Why is this claim being discarded?" |
| **Refresh status** | A sent CHAS claim. Asks MHCP for its status now. |
| **Check CPF now** | A sent Medisave claim. If nothing is back yet: "CPF has not answered this claim yet. Medbot checks again on its own." |
| **Appeal** | A CHAS claim MHCP rejected. See [Appealing a rejected CHAS claim](#appealing-a-rejected-chas-claim). |
| **Resubmit** | A CHAS, PHPC, Screen for Life, vaccination or CDS claim MHCP rejected. Clinic Admins only. See [Resubmitting a rejected claim](#resubmitting-a-rejected-claim). |
| **Amend** and **Cancel with CPF** | A Medisave claim. See [Amending or cancelling a Medisave claim](#amending-or-cancelling-a-medisave-claim). |

Claims the agent prepared but nobody submitted within 7 days are discarded as "Not submitted within 7 days".

### Claim statuses

Before MHCP or CPF decides on it, a claim shows where it is in sending:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting for review** | Prepared, waiting for a person to submit it, or held with problems. |
| **Queued to send** | Ready. Medbot sends it shortly. |
| **Sending** / **Sending (retrying)** | Being sent. Medbot retries after 1, 5 and 15 minutes. |
| **Sent** | The gateway received it. For Medisave this reads **Queued with CPF**. |
| **Accepted by MHCP** | MHCP accepted it. For Medisave this reads **Approved by CPF**. |
| **Not sent** | The gateway refused it. The row says why. |
| **Not confirmed** | Medbot cannot tell whether it arrived. It is never resent automatically. Check before sending again. |
| **Discarded** | Withdrawn by staff, or not submitted within 7 days. |

After that, the status is MHCP's or CPF's own, with its code and description. MHCP statuses include **Submitted**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Appealed**, **Paid**, **Recovered** and **Cancelled**. A claim rejected again after an appeal shows **Rejected after appeal**. A Medisave claim that CPF paid less than claimed shows **Approved with shortfall**, and the patient owes the difference. A Medisave claim CPF has not answered shows **No answer from CPF** after 20 days and **No answer from CPF: urgent** after 25. See [When CPF does not answer](#when-cpf-does-not-answer).

A few more statuses come from Medbot itself:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Rejected, resubmitted** | MHCP rejected the claim and an admin resubmitted it. The resubmission is its own row, marked **Resubmission 1** (then 2, and so on), and carries the live status. |
| **No answer from CPF** | CPF has not answered a Medisave claim 20 days after it was sent. See [When CPF does not answer](#when-cpf-does-not-answer). |
| **No answer from CPF: urgent** | Still no answer 25 days after it was sent. |

### Claims today

**Claims today** (`/claims/today`) shows every patient checked in at one clinic location on one day: their bill, the status of each claim on it, and what they still owe. Open it with **Claims today** on the **Claims** page. It is read only: work claims in the queue, bills in [Billing](/cms/billing), and payments at the [reception queue](/cms/reception-queue).

Choose the **Clinic** and the **Date**. It opens on today at the organisation's default location. For an earlier day the heading reads **Claims by day**. The page refreshes itself every 20 seconds.

Tiles at the top total the day: **Patients billed**, **Claimed**, **Approved so far**, **Cash due**, **Cash collected**, **Need attention** and **Paid, still owing**. **Paid, still owing** counts the patients whose visit reception marked paid but who now owe money again because a payer refused or paid short. It turns amber when it is above zero. Filter the rows by **All**, **Needs attention**, **Awaiting payer**, **Settled**, **No claim** or **Owes after paid**. **Owes after paid** shows just those patients.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **#** | The queue ticket. |
| **Patient** | The patient's name. |
| **Bill** | The bill's total. |
| **Claims** | Each claim on the bill, such as **CHAS ACT**, **VSS**, **CDS**, **Screen for Life**, **Medisave** or **Medisave amendment**, with its amount and status. Select one to open the queue. |
| **Patient pays** | What the patient owes. "est." marks a figure that can still change. |
| **Paid** | What the patient paid and how, for example "$20.00 · PayNow", or **Unpaid**, "owes …", "overpaid …", **Nothing to pay** or **Paid (amount not recorded)**. |
| **Status** | **Needs attention**, **Awaiting payer**, **Settled**, **No claim** or **Not billed yet**. A visit marked paid that now owes money also shows **Marked paid, owes $X** under it. |

Doctors see the page without the **Paid** column, the **Cash collected** and **Paid, still owing** tiles, and the **Owes after paid** filter.

When you open the row of such a patient, a note says "The queue shows this visit as paid, but $X is still owed. Take it from the Bill column of the queue." The queue shows the same figure as an amber **Owes $X** button; see [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue#when-the-visit-is-marked-paid).

To take a balance for one of today's patients, use **Take payment** in the [Reception Queue](/cms/reception-queue#taking-payment). Its dialog starts from the same owed figure as **Patient pays**.

**What the patient owes.** The bill's cash due assumes each scheme pays its share. **Patient pays** adds back whatever a payer refused or paid short, and the share of any claim staff discarded, because no payer will pay it. A claim that can no longer be sent counts as refused. A claim MHCP is still deciding, or that is under appeal, being cancelled or recovered, is not counted against the patient yet: the figure is marked "est.". A claim whose answer was lost (**Not confirmed**) is never charged to the patient. It shows as needing attention until staff find out what happened. For Medisave, only the submission in force counts, so an amendment or cancellation CPF accepted replaces the first claim's amount. The note under the table says: "“est.” marks an amount a payer has not answered yet: it changes if the payer approves less than was claimed."

A visit **Needs attention** when one of its claims needs attention in the queue, a payer refused or paid short, or a claimed subsidy was discarded. It is **Awaiting payer** while any payer has yet to answer, and **Settled** once they all have.

Select the arrow on a row to see its bill: the **Bill total** and receipt number, each claim taken off it with its status and reference ("payer approved …" when that differs from the claim), and **Patient pays in cash**. Lines below explain any difference, for example "Includes $… the payer did not approve." or "Includes $… of a subsidy whose claim was discarded: no payer will pay it." Then each payment, with its method, reference and time. A visit with no bill says "This visit has no bill yet."

If nobody checked in: "No patients were checked in here on this day." If the day cannot be read: "Could not load the day’s claims. Try again in a moment."

## 6. Sending and tracking

- **CHAS.** Medbot checks MHCP for each CHAS claim's status every four hours between 08:00 and 20:00 (Singapore time). It checks every run for the first 14 days, then daily, then weekly after 60 days. It stops once a claim is paid, finally rejected or cancelled, and keeps watching paid claims for audit recovery.
- **Medisave.** CPF answers through files rather than at once. Medbot collects CPF's advice at 09:00, 13:00 and 18:00 and its payment files at 10:00, Monday to Friday. Each answer is matched to its claim by the claim's HRN, submission type and the date and time it was sent.
- **Medisave claims CPF has not answered.** CPF keeps its answer for 30 days only, so Medbot flags a Medisave claim still waiting 20 days after it was sent. See [When CPF does not answer](#when-cpf-does-not-answer).
- **A location switched off.** If an admin switches a module off at a location, its claims are parked and nothing is sent.

### Medisave payments

**Medisave payments** (`/claims/medisave-payments`) shows the payment files CPF sent in the last 60 days: payment detail, summary and GIRO letter. For each file you see whether it was applied, how many records it held, its total, the claims it paid, and whether the totals tie out. **Check CPF payments now** collects new files immediately. If none arrived: "No payment files from CPF in the last 60 days."

Each day's tie-outs compare CPF's summary with its payment detail for every kind of submission the files name: first submissions (`FS`), then amendments (`AM`) and cancellations (`CA`), whose refunds are negative. Each line shows ✓ when the figures agree, or ✗ and "off by" the difference. A submission type that appears in only one of the two files shows as a difference.

### When CPF does not answer

CPF keeps its answer to a Medisave claim for 30 days only. Medbot checks every hour for claims still **Queued with CPF** with no answer, counting from when the claim was last sent:

| After | The claim shows | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 days | **No answer from CPF** | Under **Needs attention** in the Claims queue. |
| 25 days | **No answer from CPF: urgent** | Under **Needs attention** in the Claims queue. |

The claim reads "CPF has not answered this Medisave claim 20 days after it was sent, and keeps its answer for 30 days only: check CPF now." At 25 days it adds "and ask CPF about the claim today". Each stage raises one alert, not one every hour. Select **Check CPF now** on the claim to collect the answer, and look for it under [Messages from CPF that match no claim](#messages-from-cpf-that-match-no-claim). The flag clears as soon as CPF answers, whatever the answer. Nothing more happens after 30 days: the claim stays flagged as urgent until someone resolves it with CPF.

### Messages from CPF that match no claim

Sometimes a message from CPF matches none of Medbot's claims, for example an answer to a claim sent through another system, or one where CPF echoed a different submission date. These appear at the top of **Medisave payments** under **Messages from CPF that match no claim**: "Match each to the claim it answers, or dismiss it if the claim was not sent from Medbot. CPF keeps a message for 30 days." The section is hidden when there are none.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Message** | **Claim advice (CPFADV)** or **Error notice (ERRMSG)**, the clinic location and the day it was received. |
| **Claim reference** | For an advice, the HRN, submission type and when the claim was sent. For an error notice, the reference of the message CPF refused. |
| **CPF's answer** | CPF's status, for example **Approved**, **Pending with CPF** or **Rejected by CPF** (an error notice reads **Refused by MediClaim**), and the amount paid. |
| **Action** | Match or dismiss. |

1. Under **Choose the claim it answers**, pick the claim. Each choice shows the HRN, submission type, when it was sent and the amount. An advice offers only the claims sent from that location under its HRN and of the same submission type. If none fits: "No claim sent from here could be its claim."
2. Select **Match**. CPF's answer is applied to the claim as if it had matched when collected, and the claim's status in the queue changes.
3. Or select **Dismiss** if the message is not for a claim sent from Medbot. The message is kept, but leaves the list.

Medbot refuses a match that cannot be right, for example "The claim was sent from another clinic location." or "Choose a Medisave claim that has been sent to CPF." If the answer cannot be applied, the message stays on the list: "CPF’s answer could not be applied to the claim, so the message is still unmatched. Try again shortly." Every match and dismissal is audited.

## 7. Corrections

### Appealing a rejected CHAS claim

Clinic Admins and doctors can appeal a CHAS claim MHCP rejected. Receptionists cannot, and the claims agent never appeals.

1. Select **Appeal** on the rejected claim. The dialog **Appeal the CHAS claim** notes that "MHCP reviews appeals by hand; it may take a day or two to hear back."
2. Choose the **Reason for the appeal**:
   - A1 Claim overlooked or missed
   - A2 Administrative or manpower issues
   - A3 IT issues
   - A4 Re-submission after audit recovery
   - A5 Re-submission after claim cancellation
   - OT Clinical or other reasons
3. For **OT**, explain under **Reason for MHCP** (required, up to 1,000 characters).
4. Select **Send appeal**.

The appeal appears as its own row: **Appeal queued**, **Appeal submitted**, **Appeal refused**, **Appeal not confirmed** or **Appeal replaced**. Once MHCP accepts it, the claim shows as appealed and Medbot follows its status again.

A claim can be appealed once. Medbot explains when it cannot: "This claim has already been appealed.", "MHCP does not accept an appeal for this rejection.", or "An appeal for this claim is already in progress." Screen for Life claims cannot be appealed. A claim that has been resubmitted cannot be appealed either.

### Resubmitting a rejected claim

When MHCP rejects a claim because something on it was wrong, a Clinic Admin can send a corrected claim instead of appealing. This works for any claim sent to MHCP: CHAS, PHPC, Screen for Life, vaccination (VSS) and CDS. It does not apply to Medisave, which is amended with CPF instead. Receptionists and doctors cannot resubmit, and the claims agent never does.

1. If the rejection was about the bill, correct the bill first: void it with **Correct the bill** and bill the visit again. See [Voiding and correcting a bill](#voiding-and-correcting-a-bill).
2. Select **Resubmit** on the rejected claim. Confirm "Resubmit this claim to MHCP from the visit’s current bill? Correct the bill first if the rejection was about it."
3. Medbot prepares the claim again from the visit's current bill (the corrected one, if the bill was voided) as a new claim. You see "The corrected claim is queued for MHCP.", or, if something is still missing, "The corrected claim still has something to fix; it is waiting for review."

The new claim appears as its own row marked **Resubmission 1**. The rejected claim stays in the queue as **Rejected, resubmitted**, keeping MHCP's rejection.

- A held resubmission cannot be re-prepared. Fix the cause, **Discard** the resubmission, then select **Resubmit** on the rejected claim again.
- Each rejection is either appealed or resubmitted, not both. **Resubmit** is not offered while an appeal is under way: "An appeal for this claim is in progress; wait for MHCP’s answer."
- Only a rejected claim can be resubmitted: "Only a claim MHCP rejected can be resubmitted; MHCP has no cancellation for any other."
- If the visit's bill no longer carries the claim: "The visit’s bill no longer carries this claim. Correct the bill with the claim on it, then resubmit." If the visit has no issued bill: "The visit has no issued bill. Bill the visit again with the correction, then resubmit."

### Amending or cancelling a Medisave claim

- **Cancel with CPF.** Once a Medisave claim is queued, CPF is sent a cancellation under the same reference and refunds what it deducted. A claim not yet sent is simply withdrawn.
- **Amend.** Once CPF approves a claim, **Amend the Medisave claim** lets you change how much **Medisave pays** and, optionally, the **Chronic condition**. Select **Send amendment**. Only one amendment can be in progress at a time.

Both ask whose error it was: **The clinic's error** or **The patient's error**. The queue labels these rows **Amendment** and **Cancellation**.

### Voiding and correcting a bill

To fix a bill that has already been issued, void it from **Billing → Invoices** with **Void invoice** (or **Void draft** for a draft). Enter a **Reason**. If the bill carries a claim, you are asked **Whose error was the bill?**

Choose **Correct the bill** to bill the visit again. The new invoice replaces the voided one and its claims are prepared again. What happens to the old claims:

- A Medisave claim is cancelled with CPF, or withdrawn if it was not yet sent.
- A CHAS, Screen for Life, vaccination or CDS claim not yet sent is withdrawn.
- A claim already sent to MHCP cannot be withdrawn by Medbot. A note for staff says to cancel it in the MHCP portal, then resubmit the corrected claim. Once MHCP reports it cancelled, the corrected bill can be claimed.
- A claim MHCP rejected needs no cancellation. Once the corrected bill is issued, a Clinic Admin selects **Resubmit** on the rejected claim (see [Resubmitting a rejected claim](#resubmitting-a-rejected-claim)), then resolves the note.
- Billing a visit again never changes a claim that has already been sent. The corrected bill reaches MHCP only through a new claim.

The Invoices table marks the new invoice "Corrects …" and the old one "Voided for correction". An invoice marked as paid cannot be voided. See [Billing](/cms/billing#voiding-and-correcting-a-bill).

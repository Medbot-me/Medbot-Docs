# Service Registry (Developers)

`@repo/smartcms-contract` (`packages/smartcms-contract/src/service-types.ts`) lists all **56** SmartCMS service types from section 4 of the SmartCMS Interface Specification v1.8.8. Every other part of the integration looks services up here.

```ts
import { serviceBySn, serviceByRoute, serviceByWireType } from '@repo/smartcms-contract';

const balance = serviceBySn(1); // chasAnnualBalanceEnquiry
```

## What each entry carries

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `sn` | The specification's serial number, 1 to 56. |
| `serviceType` | The catalog name, exactly as the specification spells it. |
| `wireServiceType` | The value actually sent in the control header and the `ServiceType` header. Equal to `serviceType` except for S/N 48 (`getPaginatedReferralByInstitution`, as its workbook spells it) and S/N 54 and 55 (kebab-case). |
| `route` | The path under the gateway base URL. `null` only for S/N 25, whose route the specification leaves blank; the client refuses to call it. |
| `format` | `xml` or `json`. |
| `envelope` | The default wrapper: `scg-json` for JSON services, `scg-xml` for XML ones. It can be overridden per route with `SMARTCMS_ENVELOPE_OVERRIDES`. |
| `legacyEnvelope` | The older AP481 request shape the service's workbook documents (wrapping elements, fields, fixed values), or `null`. |
| `legacyEnvelopeCandidate` | True exactly when `legacyEnvelope` is set: S/N 1 to 3 and 12 to 16. |
| `group` | The entitlement group. Where a module exists, the group has the module's name. |
| `phase` | Delivery phase: **P0** platform, **P1** CHAS and MHCP subsidies, **P2** CDMP, **P3** registries and NEHR contribution, **P4** Healthier SG, **PM** an opt-in module outside the CHAS Badge, **X** out of scope, **L** legacy or retired. |
| `kind` | `enquiry` when the call changes no remote state, otherwise `submission`. |
| `safeToRetry` | True exactly for enquiries. |

> **Do not correct the spelling.** Names and routes are copied exactly, misspellings included (`submisssion/hsg/iquitjourney`, `putReconcillationFile`, `getPaginatedReferralByInstituion`). The gateway routes on these strings, and a test compares the list against a transcription of the specification.

A service can only be called when its group belongs to a module the organisation subscribes to and the location is entitled to it (see [Gateway](/cms/smartcms/gateway#entitlements)). Groups with no module (CHAS_DENTAL, ALPS, PSS, LEGACY) cannot be called at all. The planned modules, CMIS and HSG_BUNDLE, can be subscribed to, but Medbot has no code that calls their services yet. S/N 7, the clinic accreditation status query, is what a location's connection test calls.

Each group below says what Medbot does with it today. Everything has been tested against the mock gateway only; nothing has been sent to Synapxe UAT yet.

## Services by group

### CHAS: CHAS and MOH subsidy claims

Module: `CHAS`

In Medbot: all seven are used. S/N 1 verifies the CHAS card at check-in; S/N 2 sends CHAS, PHPC, vaccination (VSS), developmental screening (CDS) and Screen for Life claims from the issued bill, and an admin's resubmission of a rejected one; S/N 3 polls claim status on a schedule; S/N 4 appeals a rejected claim; S/N 7 checks accreditation and is the connection test; S/N 8 and 9 read a patient's vaccination and CDS claim history, which the bill's subsidy claims are built from. See [Claims Workflow](/cms/smartcms/claims).

| S/N | Service type | Route | Format | Kind | Phase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `chasAnnualBalanceEnquiry` | `enquiry/chasannualbalance` | XML | Enquiry | P1 |
| 2 | `chasBillClaimSubmission` | `claims/submission/chas` | XML | Submission | P1 |
| 3 | `chasBillClaimSubmissionStatusEnquiry` | `claims/enquiry/chas` | XML | Enquiry | P1 |
| 4 | `CHASAppealSubmission` | `submission/appeal` | XML | Submission | P1 |
| 7 | `ClinicAccreditationStatusquery` | `accreditation/clinicstatus` | XML | Enquiry | P0 |
| 8 | `VaccinationClaimHistory` | `enquiry/vaccinationclaim` | XML | Enquiry | P1 |
| 9 | `CDSClaimHistory` | `enquiry/cdsclaim` | XML | Enquiry | P1 |

### SFL: Screen for Life

Module: `SFL`. Screen for Life claims are submitted with S/N 2, so subscribing to SFL also creates CHAS entitlements.

In Medbot: both are used, to ask whether a patient is due for screening (S/N 11, which fans out to EISS and eCHAS) and to read their screening claims (S/N 10).

| S/N | Service type | Route | Format | Kind | Phase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | `CHASClaimHistoryQuery` | `claims/enquiry/claimhistory` | XML | Enquiry | P1 |
| 11 | `SFLEligibilityQuery` | `claims/enquiry/sfl` | XML | Enquiry | P1 |

### CDMP: Medisave (Chronic Disease Management Programme)

Module: `CDMP`

In Medbot: all four are used. S/N 15 checks the payer's Medisave balance (with the once-off consent); S/N 12 submits, amends and cancels Medisave claims; S/N 13 pulls CPF's advice from the CDMP mailbox, and staff match or dismiss what matches no claim; S/N 14 pulls CPF's payment files for reconciliation.

### NEHR_REFERRAL: Referral contribution to NEHR

Module: `NEHR_REFERRAL`

In Medbot: both are used. S/N 38 sends each issued referral letter to the National Electronic Health Record, and a `Cancelled` composition with the same identifier when a letter NEHR has, or may have, is voided. S/N 39 sends the daily reconciliation file at 00:15 Singapore time. A letter that may carry termination-of-pregnancy, voluntary sterilisation or transplant information is held until a doctor confirms the patient's consent. See [Reports](/cms/smartcms/reports#nehr-referral-letters).

| S/N | Service type | Route | Format | Kind | Phase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 38 | `putComposition` | `submission/hsg/composition` | JSON | Submission | P3 |
| 39 | `putReconcillationFile` | `submission/hsg/nehrreconcillation` | JSON | Submission | P3 |

## Other contract exports

| Module | What it provides |
| --- | --- |
| `envelope.ts` | Zod schemas for the control header, `SCG_RequestInfo` and `SCG_ResponseInfo` (strict on documented fields, passing unknown keys through); `SCG_STATUS_CODES` with HTTP status, error type and retry flag; `formatMsgDateTime` / `parseMsgDateTime` for Singapore-time timestamps. |
| `modules.ts` | `SMARTCMS_MODULES` (label, description, badge, status, service groups, "needs" chips, presets), `modulesForPreset('gp-starter')` and `SMARTCMS_BADGE_LABELS`. See [Modules & Gating](/cms/smartcms/modules). |
| `vocab.ts` | Submission states (including `superseded`, a rejected claim replaced by its resubmission), actor kinds (member, agent, job), gateway outcome kinds, business-error categories and entitlement environments (`off`, `uat`, `prod`). |
| `codesets/` | Code tables for MHCP (CHAS, Screen for Life, VSS and CDS), MCAF and Mediclaim (Medisave), HSAR ValueSet 7.3, NIR, MW Health, CIDC (including its ICD-10-AM mapping), DEROM (including the notifiable diseases) and NEHR, with crosswalks between them. Date-aware `lookup(setId, code, onDate)` and `activeEntries(setId, onDate)`. |
| `fhir-urls.ts` | `urn:gov:sg:nric` and Medbot's `http://medbot.me/fhir/.../smartcms-*` StructureDefinition, CodeSystem and NamingSystem URLs. |
| `schema-version.ts` | `SCHEMA_VERSION`, the version of the knowledge base the contract was transcribed from (`kb-2026-09`). |
| `claims-owed.ts` | `visitOwedOf`: what a patient owes on a visit once the payers have answered, shared by the counter's Take payment (through `apps/api`) and the claims day view. Also `claimStatusMeaning` (MHCP and CPF statuses as approved, refused or pending), `claimSharesOf`, `claimSchemeOf`, `claimCategoryOf` and `medisaveSubmissionType`. |
| `identifiers.ts` | `isValidNric`, `isValidFin` and `isValidNricOrFin`: the one NRIC and FIN check-letter implementation every Medbot surface uses, including M-series FINs. |
| `fhir/` | Helpers that build and read Medbot's SmartCMS FHIR shapes: national demographics on `Patient`, coded ICD-10-AM encounter diagnoses (with search), CDS screening `Procedure`s, and the CHAS, Screen for Life, VSS/CDS and Medisave choices on an `Invoice`. |
| `dto/` | The wire shapes `apps/api` returns and the CMS parses, with their validation rules where the back-end has them: `coverage.ts` (CHAS card verification, S/N 1), `claims.ts` (the claims queue and review, appeal and resubmission blockers), `claims-day.ts` (the claims day view), `sfl.ts` (Screen for Life), `vcdss.ts` (VSS and CDS), `cdmp.ts` (Medisave balance, consent and claims, unmatched CPF messages and the 30-day advice codes), `registry.ts` (CIDC reports), `derom.ts` (DEROM notifications, with DROM's error codes), `mwh.ts` (MW Health records, with MOM's first-error check), `demographics.ts` (national demographics) and `settings.ts` (the SmartCMS settings page). `hsg.ts` is an empty placeholder for Healthier SG. |

# Deep Research

## Introduction

The DeepResearch feature is an end-to-end research automation system designed to help users generate high-quality, structured, and verifiable research outputs. Instead of manually searching, filtering, reading, and organizing information, this feature guides users through a complete research workflow, from defining a research question to reviewing final evaluated results.

## Create a Research question

A research question is the foundation of your DeepResearch workflow. It defines the scope, purpose, and direction of the research process.
It guides the generation of inclusion exclusion criteria and hence helps to filter out irrelevant data. Make sure to be specific and avoid broad topics while
creating a research question.

To Create a research question

1. Navigate to home and click on "Deep Research" from the sidebar.

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/image1.png)


2. Click "Add new research Question"

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/image2.png)

3. Enter your primary research question in the input field.

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/image3.png)

4. Click Save.

Optional: Add additional context (e.g. sub-questions )

## Create inclusion-exclusion criteria

The next step in the workflow is the creation of inclusion exclusion criteria. Inclusion and exclusion criteria helps the system determine which information is relavant to your research question and which information should be filtered out.
This step ensures that only high-quality and relevant results move forward in the deep research pipeline.You can define your own inclusion exclusion criteria or generate 
one based on the research question.

To generate the criteria


![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/image10.png)
1. Click "Edit criteria"
2. Click "Generate Inclusion Exclusion Criteria"
3. Edit if you want to make any changes and click "Save"

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/image5.png)

## Create search strategies

Search strategies are search strings used to gather research papers for a specific research question. Each strategy has a strategy name, a search database (currently we only support pubmed), and a search query string
which is generated based on the research question, search database, search start date and end date.

In order to generate a search strategy:

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/image6.png)

1. Click "New Search Strategy"

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/image7.png)

2. Enter a name
3. Select search database
4. Select search start and end date
5. Click Generate Search Strategy
6. Click Update Strategy


## Run search strategies

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/image8.png)

After you have created the search strategies, simply run them using "Run Search Strategies" button. 

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/image11.png)

You are asked to enter a limit for the number of articles you want, enter you limit and click "Run Search".

## Use Inclusion Exclusion agents

Inclusion exclusion agents are the AI agents which help you filter your search results based on the inclusion and exclusion criteria you generated.
This agent analyses each search result, checks if it fits the inclusion criteria, and takes a decision (include or exclude) along with a justification for its decision.
You can define your own inclusion exclusion agents from the "Agents" tab or use a default one. You can select multiple inclusion exclusion agents and use them to filter your search results.
You will then be navigated to evaluation page where you can evaluate the results.To revisit your results, you can click on "View Agents Results" button.

To use inclusion exclusion agents:

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/image12.png)

1. Click on the "Select Agents" button

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/image13.png)

2. Select one or multiple agents to filter your results and click "Confirm".

You will be navigated to the agents' evaluation page.

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/image14.png)



## Evaluate agents' results

The agent evaluation page allows you to review each article independently.
You can see the title of the research paper, the decision of the agent (which is found using majority voting if you used multiple agents to process your articles),
along with the justifications for the decision. This step allows the results to be verified by the user.
Users can search the results by the title and can also download the results if they prefer using the "Download Results" button. In addition you can define more agents and process the final results further based on what u need.



![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/image15.png)
If you donot agree with the agents' decision, you can toggle the decision and enter your comments on it.

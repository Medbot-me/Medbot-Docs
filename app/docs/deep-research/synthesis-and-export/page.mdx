# Synthesis and Export

## Stage 7: Synthesis

The **Synthesis** stage answers the question from the included studies: pooled where their results can be combined, narrative where they cannot, with the certainty of each finding.

Choose how to synthesise, then click **Synthesise** (or **Synthesise again**):

| Choice | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Decide from the data** | Meta-analysis when two or more studies report the same effect with a confidence interval; otherwise narrative. |
| **Meta-analysis** | Pools effect estimates. Falls back to narrative, and says why, if nothing can be pooled. |
| **Narrative** | A structured narrative synthesis, without pooling. |

The result is badged **Meta-analysis** or **Narrative synthesis**. Up to five syntheses are kept; choose an earlier one from the history list.

### Findings by outcome

Each outcome has a card with its effect measure, how many studies reported an estimate, and a certainty badge. Where studies were pooled, it shows the pooled estimate with its 95% confidence interval and p-value, the number of studies, the model (random effects, DerSimonian–Laird, or fixed effect) and I² heterogeneity, with a forest plot. Marker size reflects each study's weight, and the vertical line marks no effect. Open **Why &lt;certainty&gt; certainty** to see all five GRADE domains for the outcome.

### The report

The written report has these sections: **Summary**, **Methods**, **Results** (**Study selection**, **Included studies**, **Findings by outcome**), **Certainty of evidence**, **Limitations** and **Conclusion**.

To change a section's wording, click its **Refine** button and tell Medbot what to change. Its rewrite appears next to the **Current wording**; keeping it saves a revised synthesis (badged **Revised wording**, with the section marked **Revised** and its **Previous wording** available). Rewrites never change the pooled estimates or certainty: for different numbers, change the extracted data or the grades and synthesise again.

### PRISMA flow

The synthesis includes a PRISMA 2020 flow of records: **Records identified in PubMed**, **Duplicates removed**, **Records screened**, **Excluded on title and abstract**, **Assessed for eligibility**, **Excluded after assessment** (broken down by reason) and **Studies included**, noting any records still awaiting a decision.

## Export

### Export the report

**Export report (Markdown)** on the Synthesis stage downloads the synthesis as a Markdown file, headed with the review's title, question and the date of the synthesis. The report is generated by Medbot; check it before relying on it.

### Export articles

**More actions → Export articles (CSV)** downloads every article in the review with these columns: PMID, Title, Authors, Journal, Publication date, DOI, URL, Screening decision, Screening decided by, Eligibility decision, Eligibility decided by, Exclusion reason, Text assessed, Study design, Sample size, Outcomes and GRADE certainty.

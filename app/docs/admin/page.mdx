# Platform Administration

> **For Medbot platform administrators only.** These pages describe the Admin Panel in the Medbot web app, used by the Medbot team to run accounts, research teams, billing, AI and support. Everything about clinic organisations and patients (organisations, services, the directory, claims, requests, support access, caregiver access and the audit log) is in the [platform admin panel in Medbot CMS](/cms/administration/platform-administration). If you are a clinician or clinic staff member, you will not see it and can skip this section. Organisation admins manage their own organisation in [Medbot CMS](/docs/getting-started/organisations), under [Settings](/cms/administration/settings) and [Staff](/cms/administration/staff), instead.

## Opening the Admin Panel

Platform administrators have a platform role of **admin** or **superadmin**. This is separate from any role you hold in an organisation.

1. Open **Settings** from the account menu.
2. Select **Admin Panel** at the top right.

The panel's sidebar shows **Platform admin** or **Platform superadmin** under its title, and a link to the platform admin panel in Medbot CMS. Select **Back to Settings** to leave. On a phone, the sections appear as a scrolling tab strip. If you are not an administrator, the panel redirects you to Home.

## Teams

**Teams** (`/admin/teams`) lists every research team, for support. A team has no clinic and no patient records, so nothing here reaches a chart. The cards show **Teams**, **People in teams**, **Pending invitations** and **Need attention**. Filter by **All**, **Active**, **Needs attention** or **Archived**, or by plan, and search with "Search by team name or email".

Open a team to see its plan and who it is billed to, its **Members** with their team role and seat, its **Seats and billing** against the billing account's contract, and counts of its **Work** (counts only: review titles and content stay with the team). A team with no admin shows **This team has no admin**.

| | Admin | Superadmin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See teams and their people | Yes | Yes |
| Resend an invitation | Yes | Yes |
| Change a member's role, withdraw an invitation, **Remove from team**, rename (**Rename team**), **Archive this team** and **Restore this team** | No | Yes |

The role rules are the ones people in the team meet: a team always keeps at least one admin. See [Research Teams](/docs/teams).

## Checking model prices

A model is billed at a price an admin sets by hand, in US dollars per million tokens (per minute for audio and realtime models). To catch a price that has drifted, the **Models**, **Audio Models** and **Realtime Models** pages compare each model's price with the provider's public price list. Medbot reads Vercel AI Gateway's list for models billed through the gateway and for OpenAI models (OpenAI publishes no price feed of its own), and AWS's price list for models billed through Bedrock.

- A **Published prices** summary sits above the list. Its headline says how many models are billed at a price that differs from the published one, or that all of them match. **Check again** reads the lists again. Each differing model has a **Review** button, and a fold-out lists models that have no published price to compare.
- In **Change price** (or **Set price**) the **Published price** panel shows what the provider lists, where it was read from, a link to the provider's pricing page and any caveats. **Use published price** copies the numbers into the form.
- The **add model** form shows the published price for the model you are adding, before you add it.

Nothing is saved on its own: a price changes only when you select **Save price**, which records it as a new price from then on.

## Confirmations

Actions that cannot be undone ask first, naming what goes and what happens next. Select **Cancel** or **Go back** to keep things as they are.

- **Deleting** a shared template, tool or agent, a sample note, an audio or realtime model, or a bug report, for example "Delete &lt;name&gt;?": "The tool is removed for good. This cannot be undone." A deleted bug report goes with its screenshot; to keep it on record, mark it resolved instead.
- **Resetting a care context prompt**: "Reset this prompt to the default?" The custom prompt and model choice for the slot are discarded and the shipped prompt is used.
- **Billing and organisation features**: starting an invoiced subscription, changing its seats, attaching a clinic and switching an organisation feature off. See [Billing & Support](/docs/admin/billing-and-support).
- **Taking over** a referral request another admin holds. See [Referral Requests](/docs/admin/referral-requests).

### Changing a platform role

In **User Management**, choosing a new role for someone asks "Change role?", saying who goes from which role to which. Granting superadmin adds "A superadmin can assign roles, delete users and wipe identities across the whole platform."; removing it adds "They lose superadmin access straight away." Select **Change role** to go ahead. You cannot change your own role.

# Platform Administration

> **For Medbot platform administrators only.** These pages describe the Admin Panel used by the Medbot team to run the platform. If you are a clinician or clinic staff member, you will not see it and can skip this section. Organisation admins manage their own organisation in [Medbot CMS](/docs/getting-started/organisations), under [Settings](/cms/settings) and [Staff](/cms/staff), instead.

## Opening the Admin Panel

Platform administrators have a platform role of **admin** or **superadmin**. This is separate from any role you hold in an organisation.

1. Open **Settings** from the account menu.
2. Select **Admin Panel** at the top right.

The panel's sidebar shows **Platform admin** or **Platform superadmin** under its title. Select **Back to Settings** to leave. On a phone, the sections appear as a scrolling tab strip. If you are not an administrator, the panel redirects you to Home.

## Confirmations

Actions that cannot be undone ask first, naming what goes and what happens next. Select **Cancel** or **Go back** to keep things as they are.

- **Deleting** a shared template, tool or agent, a sample note, an audio or realtime model, or a bug report, for example "Delete &lt;name&gt;?": "The tool is removed for good. This cannot be undone." A deleted bug report goes with its screenshot; to keep it on record, mark it resolved instead.
- **Resetting a care context prompt**: "Reset this prompt to the default?" The custom prompt and model choice for the slot are discarded and the shipped prompt is used.
- **Billing and organisation features**: starting an invoiced subscription, changing its seats, attaching a clinic and switching an organisation feature off. See [Billing & Support](/docs/admin/billing-and-support).
- **Taking over** a referral request another admin holds. See [Referral Requests](/docs/admin/referral-requests).

### Changing a platform role

In **User Management**, choosing a new role for someone asks "Change role?", saying who goes from which role to which. Granting superadmin adds "A superadmin can assign roles, delete users and wipe identities across the whole platform."; removing it adds "They lose superadmin access straight away." Select **Change role** to go ahead. You cannot change your own role.

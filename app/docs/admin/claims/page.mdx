# Claims Review

> **For Medbot platform administrators.** Admins can read claims and prepare the checklist; only superadmins decide them.

When someone [claims an organisation](/docs/getting-started/claim-an-organisation), a superadmin checks the claim. Open **Requests → Organisation claims** in the [platform admin panel in Medbot CMS](/cms/administration/platform-administration) (`/admin/requests/claims`; the old address in the Medbot app redirects there). Admins can read claims and prepare the checklist; a superadmin verifies, rejects and creates the organisation.

## The list

The tabs are **Open**, **Submitted**, **Being reviewed**, **Verified**, **On Medbot**, **Rejected**, **Cancelled** and **All**. The table shows **Organisation**, **Status**, **Checks** and **Submitted**. Open a claim to review it.

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/admin/01-claims-review.png" alt="A claim under review with the Verification checklist and Decision card" />

## Review a claim

1. Select **Take this claim** (or **Take over** if a colleague has it). **Release** gives it back.
2. In **Verification checklist**, mark each check **Confirmed**, **No match** or **Clear**:
   - Work email belongs to the organisation (pre-filled from the listing's website or referral email).
   - UEN matches the registered business.
   - Confirmed by phone with the organisation.
   - Clinic or practitioner licence checked.
3. Write what you did under **How you checked** and select **Save**.
4. Decide, under **Decision**:
   - **Verify** (confirm "Verify this claim?"). The claimant is emailed and can finish setup themselves from their claim. Or, once the claim is **Verified**, select **Create organisation** and confirm "Create &lt;name&gt;?": "Medbot creates the organisation and its patient records store. The claimant becomes the organisation's first admin."
   - **Reject**, with a **Reason** that the claimant will see.
5. After setup, **Resend ready email** sends the claimant the "ready" email again.

**Admin notes** are never shown to the claimant.

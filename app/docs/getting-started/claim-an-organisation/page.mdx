# Claim an Organisation

Medbot keeps a directory of clinics and services that are not on Medbot yet. If your organisation is listed, you can **claim** it. A Medbot administrator checks your claim, and once it is verified you become the organisation's first admin.

Claims are made in **Medbot CMS**, on its **Organisations** page (`/organisations`), where your memberships, the demo organisation and joining with a code are too. Sign in to Medbot CMS with your Medbot account; from the Medbot app, use **Open Medbot CMS** (see [Getting to Medbot CMS](/docs/getting-started/organisations#getting-to-medbot-cms)). Inside Medbot CMS, **Manage organisations** in the organisation switcher opens the page. Old links to `/organisations/claim` in the Medbot app redirect there.

Anyone signed in can make a claim. If your organisation is not listed, [create it](/cms/create-organisation) instead.

## Make a claim

1. In Medbot CMS, open **Organisations** and select **Claim an existing organisation**.
2. On **Claim an organisation**, type a name, postal code or specialty into the **Search by name, postal code or specialty** box and select **Search**.
3. Find your organisation in the results and select **Claim**. If someone else's claim is already open, the result shows **Claim under review** instead.
4. Under **Show that it is yours**, fill in the form:
   - **Your role at the organisation**, for example Clinic manager or Medical director.
   - **Your work email**. An address at the organisation's own domain is the quickest to check.
   - **Phone**. Medbot may call the organisation to confirm.
   - **UEN**, the business registration number.
   - **Clinic or practitioner licence number**.
   - **Links that show your connection** (optional, up to five, one per line).
   - **Anything else we should know**.
5. Select **Submit claim**.

## Follow your claim

Open **Organisations** in Medbot CMS, where **Your claims** lists your claims with their status, or open the claim directly. The **Your claim** page shows a status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Submitted** | Waiting for a Medbot administrator. You are emailed when it is verified. |
| **Being reviewed** | An administrator is checking it. |
| **Verified** | Your claim is accepted. A setup step may be waiting for you. |
| **On Medbot** | The organisation is set up. |
| **Not approved** | The claim was rejected. The reason is shown. |
| **Cancelled** | You or an administrator cancelled it. |

While a claim is open you can select **Cancel claim**.

## Finish setup

Once the claim is **Verified**, you are emailed and a **Finish setup** card appears on your claim: "Your claim is verified. Finish setup to bring your organisation onto Medbot with you as its first admin. Medbot keeps its health records for you; there is nothing else to enter." Select **Finish setup**. Medbot creates the organisation and its patient records store, and you become its first admin. A Medbot administrator can also create the organisation for you from the claim.

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/organisations/03-claim-status.png" alt="A verified claim, with the Finish setup card" />

When setup is complete, the page says the organisation is on Medbot. Select **Open it**: the **Organisations** page says "&lt;organisation&gt; is on Medbot, with you as its first admin." Select **Open it** there to start working in it in Medbot CMS.

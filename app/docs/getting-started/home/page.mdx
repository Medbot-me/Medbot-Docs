# Home

**Home** is your dashboard in the personal space. It is where you land after [onboarding](/docs/getting-started), and a quick way into the tools you use most.

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/getting-started/06-home.png" alt="The Home dashboard with credits, agents, notes, deep research and clinical resources cards" />

Choose **Personal space** in the switcher at the top of the sidebar to come back here from a [research team](/docs/teams). Organisation work is in Medbot CMS; see [Teams and Organisations](/docs/getting-started/organisations).

## What is on Home

| Card | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Welcome to Medbot** | A greeting with a link to this documentation. |
| **Organisation work** | Shown beside the welcome card if you belong to an organisation: "Services, patients and clinic operations live in Medbot CMS." **Open Medbot CMS** opens it. See [below](#organisations-and-medbot-cms). |
| **Run a clinic or service?** | Shown instead if you belong to none: "Set up your organisation, its services and its clinic in Medbot CMS." with **Open Medbot CMS**. |
| **Credits Remaining** | Your balance, counting what is left of your plan's [monthly allowance](/docs/ai-usage#monthly-allowance). Select **View Usage** to open the [Usage Dashboard](/docs/ai-usage). |
| **Recent Agent Runs** | A table of recent runs (**Agent Name**, status). Select **Create Agent** to build one, or **View All**. |
| **My Agents** | Your agents. Select **Manage Agents**. |
| **Notes** | **Create Notes** opens a new note; **View All Notes** opens your notes. |
| **Deep Research** | **New review** starts a [review](/docs/deep-research); **View All** lists them. |
| **Clinical Resources** | **Add New** uploads a [document](/docs/clinical_resources); **Manage Resources** lists them. |

## Organisations and Medbot CMS

Organisations, with their services, tasks, patients and staff, are in Medbot CMS. From Home, the **Organisation work** or **Run a clinic or service?** card is the way there: select **Open Medbot CMS**. The card is shown only where your Medbot has Medbot CMS set up.

In Medbot CMS you can [create an organisation](/cms/create-organisation) (with you as its first admin), [join one with a code](/cms/join-an-organisation), [claim a listed one](/docs/getting-started/claim-an-organisation) or open a [demo organisation](/cms/services/demo-services). See [Teams and Organisations](/docs/getting-started/organisations).

## Inbox

The Medbot app no longer has an **Inbox**. Every task in the Task Inbox belongs to a care service, so the Task Inbox moved to Medbot CMS, where it is **Tasks** (`/services/inbox`). The old `/inbox` address redirects there.

For notifications about your own work (finished runs, comments), use the **Notifications** bell at the top right of every page.

# Teams and Organisations

The Medbot app is your personal workspace: your notes, agents, datasets, Deep Research reviews, templates and clinical resources. The [research teams](/docs/teams) you belong to are reached from the same app.

Clinics and hospital groups are **organisations**, and they live in **Medbot CMS**. Their services, tasks, patients, staff, agents, settings and AI policy are there, and so are creating, joining and claiming an organisation. The Medbot app no longer has organisation pages, an organisation switcher or a **Role** menu.

## The space switcher

At the top of the sidebar, the switcher shows where you are: **Personal space** with "Only you", or a team's name with "Team · N members" while you are on that team's page.

Select it to open the menu:

- Under **Personal**, **Personal space** opens [Home](/docs/getting-started/home).
- Under **Teams**, each research team you belong to opens its team page. With no teams yet, the menu says "Share research and agents with colleagues in a team."
- **Create team** opens **Teams** (`/teams`), where **New team** makes one. See [Make a team](/docs/teams#make-a-team).
- **Join with invite link** opens the team invitation page (`/teams/join`). Team invitations come by email: open the link in the email to see the invitation and join. Opened from the menu, without an invitation link, the page shows **Invitation unavailable**: "This link is missing its invitation." See [Accepting an invitation](/docs/teams#accepting-an-invitation).
- **Organisations are in Medbot CMS** opens Medbot CMS.

A tick marks where you are. The sidebar's tools stay the same whichever you pick: choosing a team opens its [team page](/docs/teams#the-team-page), with the team's reviews and agents. Teams have no workspace of their own yet.

## Getting to Medbot CMS

There are three ways from the Medbot app to Medbot CMS:

- **Open Medbot CMS** on the card at the bottom of the sidebar (hidden while the sidebar is collapsed). If you belong to an organisation, the card is headed **Organisation work**: "Services, patients and clinic operations live in Medbot CMS." If not, it is headed **Run a clinic or service?**: "Set up your organisation, its services and its clinic in Medbot CMS."
- The same card, with the same **Open Medbot CMS** button, beside the welcome card on [Home](/docs/getting-started/home).
- **Organisations are in Medbot CMS** in the space switcher.

These links appear only where your Medbot has Medbot CMS set up. Sign in to Medbot CMS with the same Medbot account. To start there, see:

- [Getting Started](/cms/getting-started) in Medbot CMS: signing in and the first sign-in.
- [Create an Organisation](/cms/create-organisation), [Join an Organisation](/cms/join-an-organisation) and [Claim an Organisation](/docs/getting-started/claim-an-organisation).
- [Choosing & Switching](/cms/switching): the organisation, service and role switcher.
- [Staff App Overview](/cms/overview), the [Home assistant](/cms/home-assistant) that replaced the organisation Overview, [Services](/cms/services) and [AI policy](/cms/ai-policy).

## Old organisation links

Addresses in the Medbot app for pages that moved now redirect to the same page in Medbot CMS, keeping anything after the `?`. Bookmarks, links in emails and kiosk QR codes keep working.

| Medbot app address | Opens in Medbot CMS |
| --- | --- |
| `/services` and every page under it | The same address: [Services](/cms/services) |
| `/campaigns` and every page under it | The same address |
| `/kiosk` and every page under it | The same address: the [kiosk](/cms/services/kiosk) |
| `/organisations` and every page under it, including claims and **Create organisation** | The same address |
| `/patients` | `/patients`, the organisation's patients |
| `/inbox` (the Task Inbox) | `/services/inbox`, **Tasks** in Medbot CMS |
| `/organisation` (the organisation Overview) | `/home`, the [Home assistant](/cms/home-assistant) |
| `/organisation/members` and a member's page | `/staff`: see [Staff](/cms/staff) |
| `/organisation/settings` | `/organisation-settings`, which opens the current organisation's [settings](/cms/settings) |
| `/organisation/ai-policy` | `/ai-policy`: see [AI policy](/cms/ai-policy) |

Where your Medbot has no Medbot CMS, these addresses are not found.

The **Inbox** item has gone from the sidebar with the move, since every task in the Task Inbox belongs to a care service. An organisation's agents are on **Agents** in Medbot CMS; **Agents** in the Medbot app shows your own, shared, public, default and team agents. See [Agents](/docs/agents#who-can-see-an-agent).

## Organisation roles

Roles in an organisation (Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Receptionist and the rest), and switching between them, are in Medbot CMS. See [Roles & Permissions](/cms/roles-and-permissions) and [Members & Roles](/cms/members-and-roles).

The Medbot app always works for you personally, whichever organisation you last chose in Medbot CMS. Research teams have their own roles: see [Roles and seats](/docs/teams#roles-and-seats).

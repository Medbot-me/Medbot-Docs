# Getting Started

## Create an account

To create an account, head over to [medbot.me/login](https://www.medbot.me/login). The **Sign in to Medbot** page creates your account the first time you sign in with an email code.

1. On the **Email code** tab, enter your **Email** and select **Email me a code**.
2. Enter the 6-digit code from the email and select **Continue**. If the code does not arrive, select **Send a new code**.

The same page is how you sign in later. It has three ways in:

| Option | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Email code** | Medbot emails you a code each time. This also creates a new account. |
| **Password** | For an account that already has a password. If your email is not verified yet, Medbot sends a code to verify it first. |
| **Continue with Google** | Sign in with your Google account. It is shown only when your Medbot has Google sign-in set up. |

You cannot sign in with a phone number on the web.

The [desktop app](/docs/desktop-and-mobile/desktop#sign-in) signs you in through this same page in your browser. The [mobile app](/docs/desktop-and-mobile/mobile#sign-in) signs in with a password or Google.

## Onboarding

The first time you sign in, a four-step setup opens. It takes about a minute. A **Help** panel at the side offers **Guides**, **Report a problem** and **Running a clinic?** if you get stuck.

### Step 1: About you

Under the heading **Welcome to Medbot**, check your **First name**, **Last name** and **Email**, then select **Continue**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/getting-started/01-about-you.png" alt="The About you step of the Medbot setup, with first name, last name and email filled in" />

### Step 2: Your practice

Choose your **Country** and your role. Pick the one that fits from the **Your role** list:

- Doctor
- Nurse
- Pharmacist
- Allied health professional
- Medical student
- Researcher
- Clinic manager or administrator
- Other (a **Tell us your role** box appears so you can describe it)

Then enter your **Practice or clinic**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/getting-started/02-your-practice.png" alt="The Your practice step, showing country, role and practice or clinic" />

### Step 3: AI preferences

Under **How much should Medbot help while you write?**, choose how the writing assistant behaves. You can change all of these later in [Settings](/docs/advanced).

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Autocomplete while you write** | Medbot suggests the next few words as you type a note. Press `Tab` to accept a suggestion, or keep typing to ignore it. |
| **Suggestion length** | How much text each suggestion offers: **Short**, **Medium** or **Long**. |
| **Pause before suggesting** | How long Medbot waits after you stop typing: **Quick**, **Balanced** or **Relaxed**. Quicker feels more responsive; slower is less distracting. |
| **Match my writing style** | Lets Medbot learn from the notes you write so suggestions sound like you. It uses your own notes only. |

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/getting-started/03-ai-preferences.png" alt="The AI preferences step with autocomplete, suggestion length, pause before suggesting and match my writing style" />

### Step 4: Review

**Ready to set up your workspace** summarises your answers. Select **Edit** beside any section to change it, or **Back** to return to an earlier step. When you are happy, select **Finish setup**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/getting-started/04-review.png" alt="The Review step listing the details entered, each with an Edit button" />

Medbot then works through a short list, showing progress as it goes: **Creating your account**, **Adding starter notes, templates and research reviews** and **Applying your AI preferences**. If you arrived through an invitation link, you also see **Applying your invitation**.

When it is done, select **Go to Medbot**. You land on [Home](/docs/getting-started/home).

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/getting-started/05-all-set.png" alt="The all-set screen with the Go to Medbot button" />

If setup fails, choose **Try again**, **Edit my details** to correct your answers, or **Sign in again**.

> Starter notes, templates and example Deep Research reviews are added for you automatically. Agents are not added during setup: create your own from [Agents](/docs/agents).

## Credits

Finishing onboarding adds a **500-credit signup bonus** to your account. Your balance is shown on the **Credits Remaining** card on **Home**; select **View Usage** to open the Usage Dashboard. See [AI Usage & Credits](/docs/ai-usage).

## Finding your way around

The sidebar on the left is how you move between the tools in your personal space:

| Sidebar item | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your dashboard. See [Home](/docs/getting-started/home). |
| **Inbox** | Tasks assigned to you. |
| **Agents** | Build and run [agents](/docs/agents). |
| **Datasets** | Patient data for agents. See [Patient Data](/docs/agents/datasets). |
| **Notes** | Your notes and the [workspace](/docs/workspace). |
| **Deep Research** | [Systematic reviews](/docs/deep-research). |
| **Templates** | [Templates](/docs/workspace/templates) you insert into notes. |
| **Clinical Resources** | Your own [documents](/docs/clinical_resources) for AI to draw on. |

At the bottom of the sidebar, select your name to open the account menu: the **Settings** gear, **Theme** (light or dark), **Documentation**, **Report a bug** and **Log out**.

On a phone, the sidebar is hidden. Open it with the menu button at the top left.

If you belong to an organisation, the sidebar changes while you are working inside it. Use the switcher at the top of the sidebar to return to **Personal**. See [Organisations and roles](/docs/getting-started/organisations).

# Getting Started

## Create an account

To create an account, head over to [medbot.me/sign-up](https://www.medbot.me/sign-up), or select **Create an account** under "New to Medbot?" on the sign-in page. The page is headed **Create your Medbot account**: "For healthcare professionals. It takes a minute."

1. Select **Continue with Google** at the top, or fill in the form below "or":
   - **Full name**
   - **Email**: "We will email you a code to verify it. You can start using Medbot straight away."
   - **Password** and **Confirm password**, at least 8 characters.
2. Select **Create account**.

You are signed in at once and go on to [onboarding](#onboarding). "Already have an account?" with **Sign in** takes you back to the sign-in page.

Accounts are made only here, with Google, or (in the patient app) with Apple. An email code does not create an account any more, and Medbot sends no code to an address that has no account.

## Verify your email

A new account works before its email is verified, but Medbot emails you a 6-digit code when you register. Until you enter it, an amber banner sits at the top of the app: "**Verify your email.** We sent a code to &lt;your email&gt;. Verifying it lets you join organisations that invite you."

1. In the banner, select **Enter code**. (**Send a new code** sends another first.)
2. Enter the code and select **Verify email**. **Not now** closes the code box.
3. The banner says "Your email is verified." and then goes away.

The **×** at the end of the banner hides it on that browser without verifying.

A few steps wait for a verified email, because they trust the address:

- your [500-credit signup bonus](#credits)
- joining an organisation that invited you by email, and [creating or joining one in Medbot CMS](/cms/create-organisation#verify-your-email-first)
- the platform administrator role, for the address the deployment names as its administrator

## Sign in

The **Sign in to Medbot** page ([medbot.me/login](https://www.medbot.me/login)) is how you sign in later. It has three ways in:

| Option | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Continue with Google** | The button at the top of the page. The first time, this creates your account. It is shown only when your Medbot has Google sign-in set up. |
| **Password** | The tab selected when the page opens. Your **Email** and **Password**. |
| **Email code** | Enter your **Email** and select **Email me a code**: "If an account uses this address, we sent it a code." Enter the 6-digit code and select **Continue**. If it does not arrive, select **Send a new code**. |

You cannot sign in with a phone number on the web. "New to Medbot?" with **Create an account** opens the registration form, keeping any page you were on the way to.

The [desktop app](/docs/desktop-and-mobile/desktop#sign-in) signs you in through this same page in your browser. The [mobile app](/docs/desktop-and-mobile/mobile#sign-in) signs in with a password or Google, and can create an account too.

## Onboarding

The first time you sign in, a four-step setup opens. It takes about a minute. A **Help** panel at the side offers **Guides**, **Report a problem** and **Running a clinic?** if you get stuck.

### Step 1: About you

Under the heading **Welcome to Medbot**, check your **First name**, **Last name** and **Email**, then select **Continue**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/getting-started/01-about-you.png" alt="The About you step of the Medbot setup, with first name, last name and email filled in" />

### Step 2: Your practice

Choose your **Country** and your role. Pick the one that fits from the **Your role** list:

- Doctor
- Nurse
- Pharmacist
- Allied health professional
- Medical student
- Researcher
- Clinic manager or administrator
- Other (a **Tell us your role** box appears so you can describe it)

Then enter your **Practice or clinic**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/getting-started/02-your-practice.png" alt="The Your practice step, showing country, role and practice or clinic" />

### Step 3: AI preferences

Under **How much should Medbot help while you write?**, choose how the writing assistant behaves. You can change all of these later in [Settings](/docs/advanced).

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Autocomplete while you write** | Medbot suggests the next few words as you type a note. Press `Tab` to accept a suggestion, or keep typing to ignore it. |
| **Suggestion length** | How much text each suggestion offers: **Short**, **Medium** or **Long**. |
| **Pause before suggesting** | How long Medbot waits after you stop typing: **Quick**, **Balanced** or **Relaxed**. Quicker feels more responsive; slower is less distracting. |
| **Match my writing style** | Lets Medbot learn from the notes you write so suggestions sound like you. It uses your own notes only. |

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/getting-started/03-ai-preferences.png" alt="The AI preferences step with autocomplete, suggestion length, pause before suggesting and match my writing style" />

### Step 4: Review

**Ready to set up your workspace** summarises your answers. Select **Edit** beside any section to change it, or **Back** to return to an earlier step. When you are happy, select **Finish setup**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/getting-started/04-review.png" alt="The Review step listing the details entered, each with an Edit button" />

Medbot then works through a short list, showing progress as it goes: **Creating your account**, **Adding starter notes, templates and research reviews** and **Applying your AI preferences**. If you arrived through an invitation link, you also see **Applying your invitation**.

When it is done, select **Go to Medbot**. You land on [Home](/docs/getting-started/home).

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/getting-started/05-all-set.png" alt="The all-set screen with the Go to Medbot button" />

If setup fails, choose **Try again**, **Edit my details** to correct your answers, or **Sign in again**.

> Starter notes, templates and example Deep Research reviews are added for you automatically. Agents are not added during setup: create your own from [Agents](/docs/agents).

When you arrived from an invitation, such as a [research team](/docs/teams#accepting-an-invitation) invitation, onboarding ends back on that page instead of Home.

## Credits

Your account gets a **500-credit signup bonus** once onboarding is finished and your [email is verified](#verify-your-email). If you finish onboarding before verifying, the bonus is added on your first visit after you verify. Your balance is shown on the **Credits Remaining** card on **Home**; select **View Usage** to open the Usage Dashboard. See [AI Usage & Credits](/docs/ai-usage).

Which [plan](/docs/plans-and-billing) you are on, and its limits, are on **Settings → My Account**.

## Finding your way around

The sidebar on the left is how you move between the tools in your personal space:

| Sidebar item | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your dashboard. See [Home](/docs/getting-started/home). |
| **Agents** | Build and run [agents](/docs/agents). |
| **Datasets** | Patient data for agents. See [Patient Data](/docs/agents/datasets). |
| **Notes** | Your notes and the [workspace](/docs/workspace). |
| **Deep Research** | [Systematic reviews](/docs/deep-research). |
| **Templates** | [Templates](/docs/workspace/templates) you insert into notes. |
| **Clinical Resources** | Your own [documents](/docs/clinical_resources) for AI to draw on. |

At the bottom of the sidebar, select your name to open the account menu: the **Settings** gear, **Theme** (light or dark), **Documentation**, **Contact support** (see [Plans & Billing](/docs/plans-and-billing#contact-support)), **Report a bug** and **Log out**.

At the top of the sidebar, the space switcher shows **Personal space**. It lists your [research teams](/docs/teams), with **Create team** and **Join with invite link**. The top bar has the **Notifications** bell.

Organisations, with their services, tasks and patients, are in Medbot CMS. The **Open Medbot CMS** card at the bottom of the sidebar, and **Organisations are in Medbot CMS** in the switcher, take you there. See [Teams and Organisations](/docs/getting-started/organisations).

On a phone, the sidebar is hidden. Open it with the menu button at the top left.

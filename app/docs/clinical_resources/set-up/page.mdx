## Setting up your resources

In order to upload your clinical resources:

1. Navigate to home and click "Clinical Resources" from the sidebar.

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/clinical-resources/resources1.png)

2. Click "Create New Resource"

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/clinical-resources/resources2.png)

3.Enter your title , description and upload pdf.

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/clinical-resources/resources3.png)

4. After uploading, your will see your resource listed. Click on the eye icon under "Actions".

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/clinical-resources/resources4.png)

5. You will be navigated to the resource page like below. Click on "Create Resource Sections".This will take some time.

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/clinical-resources/resources5.png)

6. You will see the chunks. Select the chunks you think you need or simply select all chunks and click "Generate Document Embeddings".
This will take some time. You will see "Created" under "Embeddings" column in the table.

![DeepResearch Workflow](/images/clinical-resources/resources7.png)

You can delete any embedding you think isnot useful. If you want to skip all these steps, you can simply click "Automate Embeddings" to create embeddings for all your chunks directly.
You've successfully setup your resources enabling your agents to use them as tools. These resources can be directly referred and used in your workspace as well. Refer to [workspace](/docs/workspace) to know how.

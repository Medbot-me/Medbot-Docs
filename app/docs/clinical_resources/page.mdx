# Clinical Resources

## Introduction

Clinical Resources let you upload your own reference documents, such as guidelines, hospital protocols and spreadsheets of local data, so that Medbot's AI can draw on them. Answers that rely on a resource are grounded in the document you supplied rather than in general knowledge.

Your resources are private to you. Other users cannot open them.

## What you can upload

| Format | Largest file |
| --- | --- |
| PDF | 25 MB |
| Word (.docx) | 25 MB |
| Excel (.xlsx) | 10 MB |
| CSV | 5 MB |

Older Word and Excel files (.doc, .xls) are not supported: save them as .docx or .xlsx first. Macro-enabled files and template files (for example .docm, .xlsm, .dotx) are also refused.

## What happens after you upload

Medbot reads and divides the document into sections automatically. There is nothing to configure. The resource page shows a status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting to process** | Queued. |
| **Processing…** | Being read and split into sections. |
| **Ready** | Agents and chats can use it. |
| **Processing failed** | Something went wrong. The error is shown, and you can select **Reprocess**. |

PDF and Word documents are divided into sections by AI, which uses a few [credits](/docs/ai-usage). Excel and CSV files are divided into tables of rows (for example "Sheet: Wards, rows 1 to 50", plus an overview of the workbook), which uses no AI credits. For spreadsheets, questions about a particular sheet or row work best.

## Where resources are used

- **Agents** can search and read your resources as tools. See [Tools](/docs/agents/tools).
- In the [workspace](/docs/workspace), the **Resources** tab of the notes panel lists **My Clinical Resources** so you can refer to them while writing.

To add your first resource, follow [Setup Resources](/docs/clinical_resources/set-up).

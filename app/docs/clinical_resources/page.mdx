## Introduction

The Clinical Resources feature allows healthcare professionals to securely upload their own medical documents such as guidelines, hospital protocols, PDFs, and reference materials into Medbot. 
These resources are then processed using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), enabling clinicians to instantly retrieve relevant information and generate context-aware AI responses tailored to their clinical workflows.
This ensures that AI responses are more authentic and based on a resource you upload.

1. Upload Clinical Resources

Healthcare professionals can upload a variety of clinical documents in PDF format.

2. Generate Embeddings

After uploading, users can break down the documents into smaller, meaningful chunks. These chunks are then converted into embeddings.
The system uses these embeddings to quickly retrieve relevant sections from large documents, provide context-aware AI responses tailored to your clinical queries and enhance decision-making by ensuring responses are grounded in your own uploaded resources

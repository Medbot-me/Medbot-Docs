# AI Usage & Credits

Medbot tracks the AI you use with **credits**. Your credit balance and the history behind it are on the **Usage Dashboard**.

## Finding your usage statistics

The Usage Dashboard is at [medbot.me/ai-usage](https://www.medbot.me/ai-usage). It has no sidebar entry. You reach it from **Home**:

1. Open **Home** from the sidebar. (Home is in your **Personal** space; if you are inside an organisation, switch to **Personal** first.)
2. Find the **Credits Remaining** card. It shows your current balance and how many credits you have used to date.
3. Select **View Usage**.

The pill on the **Credits Remaining** card shows your state at a glance:

| Pill | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Usage Tracking** | You have credits left. |
| **Out of Credits** (red) | Your balance is zero or below. |

## What the Usage Dashboard shows

The page is headed **Usage Dashboard** and names the account it belongs to (**Account:** followed by your name). Credits are personal: the dashboard shows your own balance and activity, not your organisation's.

### Summary cards

Three cards sit across the top. All three cover the lifetime of your account. For a breakdown over a chosen period, see [AI usage](#ai-usage) below.

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Credits Remaining** | Your spendable balance across all AI features. It turns red at zero or below. |
| **Lifetime Granted** | Every credit ever added to your account: grants, purchases and adjustments. |
| **Lifetime Used** | Every credit spent on AI generations. |

### Credit Activity

The **Credit Activity** table lists the entries behind those totals, newest first.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Date** | When the entry was recorded. |
| **Type** | **Grant**, **Purchase**, **Usage**, **Refund** or **Adjustment**. |
| **Feature** | The AI feature an entry belongs to, or "—" when it has none. |
| **Description** | What the entry was for, such as "Signup bonus". |
| **Amount** | The change to your balance. Credits added are shown in green with a "+". |
| **Balance After** | Your balance once the entry was applied. |

You can sort by any column except **Description**. Use **View** to show or hide columns, and **Rows per page** to show 10, 20, 30 or 50 rows. On a narrow screen, **Feature**, **Description** and **Balance After** are hidden to save space.

The table shows your **20 most recent** entries. A new account shows "No credit activity yet." until its first entry.

## Where credits come from

| Entry type | When it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Grant** | Every new account gets a **500-credit signup bonus** when it finishes [onboarding](/docs/getting-started#onboarding). |
| **Adjustment** | A platform administrator granted you credits. The description is the reason they gave, or "Manual top-up by" their email address. |
| **Purchase** | A credit pack you bought with **Top up credits**. |
| **Usage** | Credits spent on an AI generation. |
| **Refund** | Credits returned to your balance. |

### Topping up

Select **Top up credits** on the Usage Dashboard to see the credit packs:

| Pack | Credits | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | 1,000 | US$10.00 |
| **Growth** ("Most popular") | 5,000 | US$45.00 |
| **Scale** | 12,000 | US$96.00 |

> **Card payments are not enabled yet.** The dialog says "Payments are not enabled yet — checkout is coming soon." Selecting **Buy** shows "Unable to start checkout". Until checkout opens, ask a platform administrator if you need more credits.

## How usage is charged

One credit is worth **US$0.01**. A generation is priced from the number of input and output tokens it used and the model that produced it, rounded up to a whole credit (at least one).

Every AI call is recorded. Medbot does not yet stop AI features when your balance reaches zero, but the **Credits Remaining** card turns to **Out of Credits**. On deployments that do enforce limits, you see an **Out of credits** message with a **Top up** action instead.

## AI usage

Below the three cards, the **AI usage** section lists "Every AI call billed to you" for a period you choose.

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/ai-usage/01-usage-dashboard.png" alt="The Usage Dashboard with the credits cards and the AI usage section for the last 30 days" />

- **Period** chooses the time range: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** (the default), **This month**, **Last 90 days** or **All time**.
- Four tiles summarise the period: **Model cost** (what the providers charged, with margin), **Credits charged** (whole credits taken from the balance), **AI calls** (with how many did not complete) and **Tokens** (in and out, plus minutes of audio).
- **By feature** and **By model** tables break the period down by calls, tokens and cost. Features include Autocomplete, Refine text, Continue note, Deep Research, Agent run, Resource ingestion, Encounter transcription and Dictation.
- The **AI calls** table lists each call with **Time**, **Feature**, **Model**, tokens in and out, **Cost**, **Credits** and **Status** (Completed, Failed, Stopped or In progress), 25 per page. Credits leave your balance in whole numbers once the fractions add up to one.

After paying through checkout, a **Payment received** or **Top-up canceled** message appears.

## When a model you chose is retired

Agents, tools, care contexts and your [refine and autocomplete settings](/docs/advanced) each use either a model you picked or the **Platform default**. An administrator can deactivate or delete a model. When that happens to a model you picked:

- **Your work keeps running.** The feature falls back to the platform default model.
- **An alert explains it.** Wherever the model is chosen, a **Model deactivated** or **Model deleted** alert says which model is now used instead, for example: _"X" has been deactivated by an administrator. This agent now runs on the platform default, "Y", until it is reactivated._ A deleted model also tells you to choose another model if you want a specific one.
- **A badge marks it.** Agent cards, tool and care-context lists, and a service's agents show a badge.
- **You are notified.** A notification such as _Your agent "N" uses a deactivated model_ appears in the **Notifications** bell on **Agents**, linking to the agent's edit page or to **Settings**. The same applies to your refine and autocomplete settings.

In a model picker, a retired choice stays visible as "(deactivated)" or "Deleted model" so you can see what was selected before you change it.

> **Deep Research reviews do not fall back.** A review pinned to a retired model stops until its owner chooses another model.

## For administrators

Medbot platform administrators see platform-wide numbers in the **Admin Panel**, reached from the **Admin Panel** button on **Settings**. See [Platform administration](/docs/admin) for the rest of the console.

- **Platform overview**: totals for users, agents and bug reports, and an **Agent usage** card with total, completed and background runs, a **Top agents by usage** table (**Agent**, **Runs**, **Last run**) and recent executions. These count runs, not credits or tokens.
- **Users** (superadmins only): a **Credits** column for each user, and a **Top up credits** row action that opens **Grant credits** with an **Amount** and an optional **Reason**, recorded as an **Adjustment** in that user's ledger. A **Usage** row action opens a side sheet with that user's AI usage for the period.
- **Models**: before you deactivate or delete a model, the confirmation lists what currently uses it (agents, tools, refine and autocomplete settings, care contexts, service agents, AI threads and research reviews). Everyone who chose it is notified when you go ahead. A model that is the default cannot be deleted until another model is made the default.

# Send Feedback

Send the Medbot team feedback, a question, a complaint, a suggestion or a comment from the web app, the desktop app or the mobile app. A person on the team reads every message.

## Where to find it

- **Web and desktop apps:** open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Send feedback**. It opens over the screen you are on.
- **Mobile app:** **More → Send feedback**.
- **Without signing in:** the form on the Medbot website at [medbot.me/contact](https://medbot.me/contact), also on the home page under **Get in touch**. It runs a quick Cloudflare check that you're human.

## Send a message

1. Choose **Feedback**, **Enquiry**, **Complaint**, **Suggestion** or **Comment**.
2. Choose how you'd like a reply: **Email**, **Phone call**, **WhatsApp** or **Any**, and give the email address or phone number it needs.
3. Write the message. Please don't include patient details.
4. Select **Send message**. You'll see a reference like `FB-7K3Q9M`, and a receipt is emailed to you if you gave an email address.

If you'd rather not be contacted, untick **Medbot may use these details to reply to me**. The form tells you we then can't reply, and you confirm with **Send without a reply**. Your contact details are not sent or kept.

## What is attached

The screen you were on, the app version, your browser or device, window size, and recent errors the app showed. Record ids are hidden. Select **See what's attached** to check before sending. Messages are kept for 24 months.

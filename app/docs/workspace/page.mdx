# Workspace

## Introduction

The workspace is where you write your clinical notes, have agents assist your writing, and chat with Medbot. Open it from **Notes** in the sidebar, or choose **Create Notes** on [Home](/docs/getting-started/home). A note opens in the editor with a panel on each side.

## Walkthrough

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/workspace/01-note-open-workbench.png" alt="A note open in the workspace, with the Notes panel on the left, the editor in the middle and the AI panel on the right" />

1. **Header.** Shows where you are, the note's folder path and title, and whether it is saved. See [Saving](/docs/workspace/notes#saving-a-note). The layout controls at the right of the header show or hide the panels.
2. **Notes panel (left).** Has three tabs:
   - **Notes**: your folders and notes, to move between notes.
   - **Templates**: [templates](/docs/workspace/templates) you can insert.
   - **Resources**: **My Clinical Resources**, your uploaded [documents](/docs/clinical_resources).
3. **The editor (centre).** Rich text with [autocomplete](/docs/workspace/editor#autocomplete), a [command palette](/docs/workspace/editor#command-palette) and a [bubble menu](/docs/workspace/editor#bubble-menu). Learn more [here](/docs/workspace/editor).
4. **AI panel (right).** Has four tabs:
   - **Chat**: talk to Medbot about your note. See [Chat](/docs/workspace/chat).
   - **Agents**: run [agents](/docs/agents/types/non-form) on the open note.
   - **Deep research**: ask a clinical question or start a [review](/docs/deep-research/start-a-review#from-the-notes-editor).
   - **Transcript**: the live transcript when you record a consultation.

## Make room to write

The layout controls in the header let you hide what you do not need.

| Control | Shortcut |
| --- | --- |
| **Hide notes panel** / **Show notes panel** | `Ctrl` + `\` (`Cmd` + `\` on Mac) |
| **Hide ai panel** / **Show ai panel** | `Ctrl` + `Alt` + `\` |
| **Focus the note, hide every panel** (leave with **Leave focus mode**) | `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `\` |

On a phone, the panels open as sheets over the note instead of beside it.

## Recording a consultation

In the workspace you can record a consultation and have Medbot draft a note from it:

- **Record encounter** records the consultation. The **Transcript** tab shows what is being said, with controls to pause, resume and stop.
- The arrow beside it opens **Previous sessions**.
- When you stop, an **Encounter summary** appears with a **Proposed clinical note**. Choose **Add to note** for each section you want.
- **Quick dictation** transcribes a short phrase straight into the note.

Recording is described in more detail under [Encounter Recording](/docs/services/encounter-recording).

# Workspace

## Introduction

The workspace is the most essential part of the Medbot application. This is where you write your clinical notes, have agents assist you in your writing, and chat with Medbot AI.

## Walkthrough

Following is a tour of the workspace, as shown in the image below.

1. The sidebar to the left has tabs for your Files, Chat, Deep Research, Templates, Clinical Resources, and Agents.
2. The toolbar at the top has text editing tools for the editor.
3. The title bar shows the title of your note, and a drop down to assign labels to your notes.
4. The editor.

# Templates

Templates are pieces of text you reuse in your notes, such as a standard examination or a handover layout. Open **Templates** in the sidebar to manage them.

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/workspace/06-templates.png" alt="The Templates page with the Workspace Templates and Document Templates tabs" />

## Workspace templates

The **Workspace Templates** tab lists your templates.

- **Load default templates** opens a list of Medbot's starter templates. Tick the ones you want (or **Select all**) and select **Load Templates**. The same button is on the **Templates** tab of the notes panel in the workspace.
- **Create New Template** opens a form. Enter a title ("Enter template title"), a smart phrase ("Enter template smart phrase") and the template text, then select **Create Template**.

To use one while writing, open the **Templates** tab of the notes panel in the [workspace](/docs/workspace), or choose **Templates** in the [command palette](/docs/workspace/editor#command-palette).

## Document templates

The **Document Templates** tab holds templates for generated documents such as referral letters, handoffs, discharge summaries and instruction sheets. Select **New template** to create one. These are used by [service](/docs/services) workflows.

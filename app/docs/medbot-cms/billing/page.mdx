# Billing

`/billing` is where `admin` and `receptionist` turn a visit's charges into an invoice.

## Accounts and charge items

The **Accounts** tab lists patient billing accounts — open a patient's chart **Accounts** section to create one if they don't have one yet. From an account's row menu, **Add items** attaches billable charge items, each with its own quantity and price, drawn from the [billable catalog](/docs/medbot-cms/catalog-and-inventory#billable-catalog-catalog).

## Generating an invoice

Once the charges are right, use the same row menu's **Generate invoice** — an account needs at least one charge item on it before an invoice can be raised. Issued invoices appear on the **Invoices** tab, and a completed visit's invoice is also what the patient sees and can download from their own [Patient Portal](/patient/visits).

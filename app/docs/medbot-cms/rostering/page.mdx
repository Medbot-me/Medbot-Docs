# Rostering

`/roster` is the clinic's staff schedule: a week grid, one column per day and one row per role (Doctors, Nurses, Reception), that `receptionist` and `admin` can both build and adjust. Only `admin` can publish a week — publishing is the step that makes a roster mean something operationally.

## Building a week of shifts

Move to the week you're planning with the arrows or **Today**. Select **Shift** in a day's cell to add one; the pencil icon edits or cancels an existing shift; the swap icon assigns a staff member to a shift or replaces the one already on it.

## Recurring shift patterns

**New recurrence** creates a repeating pattern in one step: weekdays, times, role, session, start date, and number of weeks. Running the same recurrence rule again is safe — it skips any dates it already created, so it's fine to re-run after adjusting a downstream week.

## The natural-language roster assistant

Where the **Roster assistant** panel is shown, describe the change you want in plain English (e.g. "give Dr. Tan Tuesday off and move her Wednesday shift to Thursday") and select **Preview**. The assistant shows the operations it would perform — with anything destructive flagged — before you commit to anything; select **Apply** to run them or **Discard** to back out.

## Publishing (admin only)

Select **Publish week** once a week is settled. Publishing does three things at once: it moves that week's shifts from draft to published, it seeds the day's clinic session if the published week covers today, and it starts [attendance](/docs/medbot-cms/attendance) tracking for those shifts. Until a week is published, its shifts stay drafts — they don't seed a session and don't count toward attendance. Reception can build and adjust drafts freely; only an admin can take the publish step.

## How it fits together

A published shift is what [Attendance](/docs/medbot-cms/attendance) measures against, and a published week covering today is what seeds that day's [Session Setup](/docs/medbot-cms/session-setup) staff list. There's no separate step to "start the day" once a roster is published for it.

# Clinic Messaging

Medbot CMS has two staff-facing surfaces for patient messages: the clinic-wide `/inbox`, and a **Messages** tab on each patient's own chart.

## The clinic inbox

`/inbox` is where `admin` and `receptionist` manage open conversations sent to the clinic through the [patient portal](/docs/medbot-cms/patient-portal#messaging-the-clinic), rather than to an individual clinician.

Staff can reply only while a thread is open and its Medplum patient record still exists. Read the full thread before replying — these are clinic-level responses, not a substitute for a clinician's clinical judgment, so anything clinical should go through the clinic's usual escalation process rather than being answered directly in an administrative reply.

If a patient's record has been deleted, the inbox disables replies on that thread and instead lets staff close the orphaned conversation.

## The patient chart's Messages tab

A patient's chart also has its own **Messages** tab, scoped to that one patient's thread. `admin`, `doctor`, `nurse`, and `receptionist` can all read it; only `admin` and `doctor` can send from it — a clinician communicating directly with their own patient, distinct from the front-desk inbox above. Sending checks the patient's [communication consent](/docs/medbot-cms/patient-records#communication-consent) for the **clinical updates** purpose on the delivery channel used — a missing or unselected grant blocks the message, deny-by-default like every other consent check in the app.

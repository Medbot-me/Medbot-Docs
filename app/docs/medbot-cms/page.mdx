# Introduction

## What is Medbot CMS?

**Medbot CMS** is the clinic-operations app: the day-to-day system a clinic's front desk, nurses, doctors, and administrators use to run a physical location — registering patients, booking and running appointments, managing a walk-in queue, rostering staff, recording clinical care, filing results, and billing. It sits alongside the patient-facing portal, where a clinic's own patients sign in to request appointments, read visit summaries, message the clinic, and manage their contact details.

Medbot CMS uses [Medplum](https://www.medplum.com) as its clinical data store — patients, practitioners, appointments, encounters, observations, immunizations, and documents are all FHIR resources in Medplum. A local Postgres database holds only the operational state Medplum doesn't model: queue tickets, clinic sessions, rosters and attendance, display-device tokens, and similar day-to-day scheduling detail.

## Key concepts

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Clinic role** | The four staff roles the app is built around: `admin`, `receptionist`, `doctor`, `nurse`. Every server action re-checks the signed-in user's role before reading or writing clinic data — see [Roles & Access](/docs/medbot-cms/roles-and-access). |
| **Clinic location** | A physical site. Sessions, rooms, staff assignments, and the queue are all scoped to one location at a time. |
| **Clinic session** | A location's "today" — which staff and rooms are active, opened from [Session Setup](/docs/medbot-cms/session-setup). |
| **Reception queue** | The live, location-scoped list of today's checked-in and booked patients — see [Reception Queue](/docs/medbot-cms/reception-queue). |
| **Roster** | The staff schedule — draft shifts, recurring patterns, and a publish step that seeds each day's session and starts attendance tracking. See [Rostering](/docs/medbot-cms/rostering). |
| **Patient portal** | A separate, patient-facing app where a clinic's patients sign in with their own Medbot account to manage their care. Staff connect a patient to it from the CMS — see [Patient Portal](/docs/medbot-cms/patient-portal), and the [Patient](/patient) tab for the patient's own view. |

## What you can do with Medbot CMS

1. **Run the front desk** — register patients, book and manage appointments, operate the reception queue, and reply to portal messages.
2. **Roster and track staff** — plan shifts (including recurring patterns), publish the week, and let attendance derive automatically from what staff actually do in the app.
3. **Deliver clinical care** — start encounters, record vitals and vaccinations, place orders, review diagnostic results, and issue documents and medical certificates.
4. **Run the clinic's back office** — manage [staff accounts and licences](/docs/medbot-cms/staff-management), configure locations/rooms/public displays, maintain the [medication formulary](/docs/medbot-cms/formulary) and [catalog/inventory](/docs/medbot-cms/catalog-and-inventory), and handle [billing](/docs/medbot-cms/billing).
5. **Give patients self-service access** — invite patients to the [patient portal](/docs/medbot-cms/patient-portal), where they request appointments, read visit summaries, invoices and results, message the clinic, and keep their own contact details current.

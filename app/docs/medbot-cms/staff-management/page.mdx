# Staff Management

`/staff` is where admins create staff accounts, keep role and licence details current, and deactivate access — `admin`-only.

## Adding a staff member

**Add staff** captures a person's email, name, role (`admin`, `receptionist`, `doctor`, or `nurse` — `superuser` isn't offered here, see [Roles & Access](/docs/medbot-cms/roles-and-access#the-superuser-role)), and, for clinical roles, their registration number: an **MCR number** for doctors and an **SNB number** for nurses, checked as it is typed. In Singapore organisations a doctor can also be given an **NRIC or FIN**, needed for SmartCMS claims and only ever shown masked. The staff table lists everyone's name, email, role, licence number, and whether the account is active or deactivated. See [Members & Roles](/cms/members-and-roles) for the formats.

## Editing and deactivating

A row's actions edit the role, correct the registration number and NRIC (**Edit registration**), or deactivate the member when someone leaves. Deactivating keeps their history intact and ends their access to **this organisation**; they are only locked out of Medbot entirely if they have no other active membership. Reactivate a returning staff member's existing membership rather than creating a second one.

## Why the role matters

The role set here decides which pages and controls that person sees everywhere else in the app — see [Roles & Access](/docs/medbot-cms/roles-and-access) for what each role can do. A new staff account also gets a linked Medplum `Practitioner` record automatically, which [communication consent](/docs/medbot-cms/patient-records#communication-consent) recording depends on.

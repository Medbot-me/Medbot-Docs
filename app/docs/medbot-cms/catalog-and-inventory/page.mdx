# Catalog & Inventory

Three admin-managed catalogs back the clinic's clinical and billing workflows, plus the physical stock records that track what's actually on hand.

## Billable catalog (`/catalog`)

`ChargeItemDefinition` records are the clinic's billable line items, used when [billing](/docs/medbot-cms/billing) a visit. `admin` and `receptionist` can browse the catalog; only `admin` can create, edit, or delete a definition. The same page also lists the vaccination services that reference these charge items — see [Vaccinations](/docs/medbot-cms/vaccinations#recording-a-vaccination).

## Clinical services and consumables (`/services`)

Clinical service definitions and non-medication consumables — devices and substances — are visible to `admin`, `doctor`, and `nurse`; only `admin` can create, edit, or retire an entry. Doctors and nurses reference these when placing [orders](/docs/medbot-cms/orders-and-order-sets), but can't change the catalog itself.

## Inventory (`/inventory`)

Inventory tracks physical stock — lot, batch, quantity, location, and expiry — as records distinct from the catalogs above. `admin`, `doctor`, and `nurse` can view stock; only `admin` can create or edit an inventory record; `admin` and `nurse` can adjust an existing record's quantity (receiving a delivery, or correcting a count) with a reason, and every adjustment is kept in the record's history. Setting or changing a reorder policy is `admin`-only.

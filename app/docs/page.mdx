# Introduction

## What is Medbot?
Medbot is a medical AI agent platform which allows healthcare professionals to create and integrate AI agents to augment their clinical work.

## Augment clinical workflows with AI agents (healthcare professionals)
Medbot augments clinical management with AI agents in several ways:
1. Autocomplete
2. AI refine
3. AI continue writing
4. Applying AI agent to specific text paragraphs
5. Customizing clinical documentation according to your style
6. Ask AI and RAG while writing clinical notes

## Applicaiton of AI agents at the heatlcare systems level (healthcare administrators)
Apart from applying AI agents as part of clinical workflows, Medbot provides healthcare administrators to apply AI agents at the healthcare systems level in several ways:
1. Batch run AI agents on multiple patients and clinical notes
2. Develop AI agents
2. Evaluate AI agents
3. Use AI agents on health systems level analysis for surveillance, health systems outcomes analysis, etc.

## Deep research (healthcare researchers)
Medbot's deep research focuses on using accepted medical and public health research methods to answer clinical questions. It is built for healthcare researchers performing systematic reviews and meta analysis.

Concurrently, Medbot's deep research can be extrapolated to the clician's "Ask AI" feature. As the approach to selecting and reviewing medical evidence is similar to that of systematic review and meta analysis, it is likely to be superior to the general "deep research" feature offered by most AI providers.

# Mobile app

The Medbot mobile app puts your personal workspace on your phone or tablet. It is available for iOS and Android, with layouts for iPad.

Use it to check on agents and research, read and edit notes, and see your Inbox when you are away from your computer. It uses the same account as the Medbot app on the web.

## Getting the app

The mobile app is not publicly available yet. There is no store listing. Your Medbot administrator will tell you how to install it while the app is in early access.

## Sign in

The app signs in to an existing account only. You cannot create an account, go through the setup steps or reset a forgotten password in the app. To do those, see [Getting started](/docs/getting-started) and use the web.

1. Open the app. The screen says **Sign in to Medbot** and asks you to use the same account as the web app.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password** and tap **Sign in**, or tap **Continue with Google**.

If your account needs an extra sign-in step, the app asks you to sign in on the web for now.

## Tabs

The tab bar at the bottom has:

- **Home**: shortcuts, your newest research reviews and recently edited notes
- **Agents**: browse, create, edit, run and refine agents, and review live calls
- **Notes**: write and organise notes and folders. Notes autosave, but the editor does not have AI in it yet.
- **Deep Research**: start a review, follow its stages, chat with Medbot, read the report and review articles
- **More**

**More** has:

- **Inbox**, with a badge showing how many updates are unread. Tapping an update opens the matching screen.
- **Templates**
- **Clinical Resources**
- **Datasets**, which opens in the web
- **Notifications**
- **Sign out**

On an iPad, the Agents and Deep Research tabs show the selected item beside the list when you hold it sideways. The app follows your phone's light or dark setting.

## Notifications

Turn on phone notifications when the app first asks, on your first visit to the Inbox, or later from **More** then **Notifications**, where the setting shows as **On**, **Off** or **Not set**. They work on real devices only, not simulators.

A notification shows the title of the update only, never its contents. Tap it to open the matching screen. Signing out stops notifications to that device.

## Working offline

When you have no connection, the app shows a notice and still lets you read your saved lists, such as agents, research reviews, templates, resources, note titles and your Inbox. These are stored encrypted on your device for up to 7 days.

Note contents, agent results and chats are never stored on the device, so they are not available offline. Saving or running something while offline fails straight away with a message that you are offline. Signing out removes the saved data from the phone.

## What is only on the web

These are not in the mobile app yet:

- AI in the note editor
- Running a workflow on dataset patients
- Background runs
- Datasets (**More** opens them in the web)
- Settings, AI usage and My account
- Report a bug
- Organisation features

## Clinical photos

Clinical photos are taken in the Medbot CMS in your phone's browser, not in this app. You can add the CMS to your home screen. See [Clinical photos](/cms/clinical-photos).

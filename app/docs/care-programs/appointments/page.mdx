# Appointments

Each care program has its own lightweight, first-party appointment book: care team members publish recurring weekly availability, and both staff and the patient agent book, move, and cancel appointments directly against the same open slots — so what's shown as "free" is always what's actually bookable, for a human and the agent alike.

## 1. Publish availability

Before anyone can book anything, at least one care team member needs a published availability window. From the program's **Care team** tab, see [Care Team → Availability](/docs/care-programs/care-team#availability) to add one.

## 2. Booking for a patient (staff)

From a patient's page, **Appointments** tab, click **Book for this patient**:

1. Optionally filter by **care role** (or leave it as "Any role").
2. Pick an open slot from the list — each shows the date/time, the care team member's name, and their role. Slots are computed live from published availability for the next 14 days, minus anything already booked.
3. Optionally add a **reason**.
4. Click **Book**.

The new appointment appears immediately under **Upcoming**.

## 3. Booking through the patient agent

If the [patient agent](/docs/care-programs/patient-agent) is enabled, a patient can ask it for a new appointment, to move an existing one, or to cancel one — it reads the exact same available-slots data as the staff booking flow above and books directly into an open slot; a confirmed booking is immediate, not a request awaiting review. Appointments booked this way are labeled **"booked by patient agent"** on the patient's Appointments tab so staff can tell them apart from ones they booked themselves.

## 4. Managing appointments

- **Upcoming** appointments can be **cancelled** directly from the patient's Appointments tab.
- **Past** appointments show their final status: **Completed**, **Cancelled**, or **No show**.

## How it works under the hood

- Availability windows are **recurring and weekly**, defined in UTC (e.g. "Tuesdays 09:00–17:00, 30-minute slots"). There are no per-date overrides for holidays or one-off leave yet — a care team member with time off simply toggles their availability window's **Active** switch off and back on, or a staff user cancels the affected appointments directly.
- A slot is considered open the moment no confirmed appointment occupies it for that care team member — checked again at the moment of booking, so a slot that looked free a few seconds ago is still safely booked.
- This is a program-local booking system, independent of any external scheduling system. It's designed as a swappable layer, so a future move to a different appointment backend wouldn't change how staff or the patient agent interact with booking day to day.

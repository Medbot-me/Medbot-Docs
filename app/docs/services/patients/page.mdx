# Patients & Cohort

## The organisation's patient directory

A service's patients come from **the organisation's own Medplum patient directory**, the same patients the clinic sees in the CMS. They are not owned by any one service, and the same patient can be in several services' cohorts.

New patients are added to the organisation, not to a service. An organisation **admin** does this from `/patients` with **Add a patient** (first and last name, email, phone, date of birth, gender, ID type such as NRIC or passport, address and postal code), or in bulk by [seeding from a CSV file](/cms/seed-data).

## Adding a patient to the cohort

From the service's **Patients** tab (or the wizard's last step, or `/services/[serviceId]/board`), click **Add patient to cohort**:

1. **Search by name** among the organisation's Medplum patients. Patients already in this cohort are not offered.
2. Optionally add **Clinical notes**: referral letters, consult notes, discharge summaries. They are saved as the patient's **first encounter note** for this service, and their **patient context** is generated from it.
3. Click **Add to cohort**.

To take a patient out of the cohort, use the **X** on their row and confirm. They stay in the organisation's directory and in any other service.

### What happens when a patient is added

Configured on **Settings → On patient added**:

- **Patient portal invite.** If the patient has no patient-portal account yet, Medbot creates a 7-day invite link for them. The link is **sent as part of the welcome message** ("You can also view your care plan and message your care team here: …"), so turn the welcome message on if new patients should receive it.
- **Welcome message** ("Send a welcome message when a patient is added to the cohort"). Type the **Message** inline, with `{{variables}}` such as `{{firstName}}`, and choose the **Channel**: SMS, WhatsApp or **Patient portal**.
- **Onboarding agent** ("Assess the patient with the onboarding agent"). Runs the chosen onboarding agent against the new patient. The default one raises a task only when the patient clearly does not belong in the service. See [Service Agents](/docs/services/agents#the-onboarding-agent).

A toast confirms what happened.

## The cohort roster

The roster shows, per patient: **Pending tasks**, **Notes**, **Messages** and **Last message**, so you can see who needs attention without leaving the page. Click a name (or the eye icon) to open the patient's page.

## A patient's page

Each patient has a page per service, `/services/[serviceId]/patients/[patientId]`:

- **Header**: name, gender, phone, age and address.
- **Draft awaiting review**: pinned above the tabs when the [patient agent](/docs/services/patient-agent) has a reply waiting for approval.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | **Escalations** from the patient agent, **AI encounters** (see [AI Encounters & Kiosk](/docs/services/encounters)), and **Encounter notes**. |
| **Patient Context** | The AI-maintained **agent context** for this patient, which can be edited or regenerated. |
| **Messages** | The SMS/WhatsApp thread, and a box to send a message. |
| **Appointments** | Upcoming and past bookings, and **Book for this patient**. See [Appointments](/docs/services/appointments). |
| **Settings** | **Messaging settings**: who shares this patient's phone number and how their replies are routed. |

### Encounter notes

Encounter notes are the patient's running clinical record in this service. Click **New encounter note**, write it, and **Save note** (or **Update note** when editing). Every save **folds the note into the patient context**. Notes can be deleted individually.

### Messages

Type in "Send a message to this patient…", choose WhatsApp or SMS, and send. Each message in the thread is badged by who or what sent it: **Staff**, **Agent** (the patient agent), **Service agent**, **Emergency escalation**, **Encounter link** or **Encounter summary**.

### Messaging settings

Several patients can share one phone number, for example a parent messaging for two children. **Messaging settings** shows everyone reachable on this patient's number, which service their replies are currently routed to, and the recent routing changes. Staff can **Pin to this service** so replies from that number come here, or **Reset context** to let routing start afresh.

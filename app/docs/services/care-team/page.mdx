# Care Team

The **Care team** item in a service's sidebar brings together its members, coverage, and availability.

## Roles come from the organisation

A service has no roles of its own. Each care team member acts under **the role or roles they hold in the organisation**:

| Role | Label |
| --- | --- |
| `admin` | Admin |
| `doctor` | Doctor |
| `nurse` | Nurse |
| `receptionist` | Receptionist |

A member can hold more than one organisation role. The care team table's **Role** column shows one badge for each. If someone's organisation roles change, their roles on every service change with them. The same four roles appear wherever a service asks for a role: coverage, the patient agent's escalation role, and the appointment role filter. Roles are managed in the organisation, not the service; see [Members & Roles](/cms/members-and-roles).

## Members

Click **Add care team member**:

1. Pick an **Organisation member**. Only people who are already members of the service's organisation can be added. The dialog shows the role they will join with ("Joins this care team as &lt;Role&gt;") and where it comes from.
2. Optionally add **Additional information on user preferences**, such as how they like to be contacted or what they handle. AI turns it into that member's **agent context**, which service agents are given when they work with or for this person.
3. Click **Add &lt;name&gt; to care team**.

The members table lists **Name**, **Email**, **Role**, **Agent context** (which can be edited) and **Actions**. To take someone off the service, click **Remove** on their row and confirm in **Remove from care team?**.

Staff are not invited to a service by email: add them to the organisation first, then to the care team.

## Coverage / rota

Coverage says **who is covering a role at a point in time**. Several behaviours, such as patient agent escalations, address "whoever is covering the Nurse role right now" rather than a named person, so a day off does not leave notifications with nowhere to go.

Add an entry from the **Coverage / rota** card: **Organisation member**, **Role**, **Starts**, and optionally **Ends**, then **Add coverage**.

## Availability

The **Availability** card defines each care team member's **recurring weekly booking windows**. Staff booking, the patient agent and patients booking in the patient portal all read these to work out open slots. See [Appointments](/docs/services/appointments).

Times are in **clinic time**: your organisation's timezone, or Singapore time if none is set.

1. Click **Add availability**.
2. Pick a **Care team member**.
3. Pick a **Day of week**, and set **Start (clinic time)** and **End (clinic time)**.
4. Set the **Slot length (minutes)**, between 5 and 480, e.g. 30.
5. Click **Add**.

The table shows **Member**, **Role**, **Day**, **Window (clinic time)**, **Slot**, **Active** and **Actions**. Toggle a window's **Active** switch to take it offline temporarily (for leave) without losing it, or delete it with the trash icon. If nobody has any availability, patients cannot book, and the card says so.

### "Not bookable — fix in Members"

A window with this badge belongs to a member who has no practitioner record in the clinic's schedule, so their times are not offered for booking. An organisation admin can create the record on the Members page.

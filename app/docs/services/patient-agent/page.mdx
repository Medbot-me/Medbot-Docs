# Patient Agent

The patient agent is an AI agent that enrolled patients chat with **in the patient portal**, in their own time. In the portal it appears as "&lt;Service&gt; assistant". It only ever **replies** to a message the patient sent. It never starts a conversation on its own.

SMS, WhatsApp and email do not carry the conversation. They only tell a patient that something is waiting in the portal (see [Patient Notifications](/docs/services/patient-notifications)). If a patient replies to one of those messages, they get an automatic reply that points them to the portal, and their message lands in the Inbox for the care team.

## What it can do

| Capability | What the patient can do | What the agent may do on its own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Appointments** | Ask for a new appointment, move an existing one, cancel one, ask when their next one is | Read the patient's own appointments; book or move one directly into an open slot on a care team member's published availability; cancel outright |
| **Health education** | Ask questions about their condition, an upcoming procedure, pre-procedure preparation, or medication they were given | Answer from the service's own content library and the patient's own context only; hand off anything outside that scope |
| **Symptom triage** | Report a new or changed symptom between consults | Classify urgency; on a red flag, immediately redirect to emergency services and escalate to whoever covers the escalation role |
| **Education & counselling** | Go through a counselling or education protocol, step by step | Take consent, walk through the protocol from the content library, and record what the patient understood or agreed (with the **Education & counselling sessions** tool) |
| **Full encounter** | Need a longer conversation than chat allows | Send a link to a full [AI encounter](/docs/services/encounters) on the patient portal (with the **Hand over for a full encounter** tool) |

## Setting it up

The patient agent is **on by default** for a new service: the **Patient agent** default agent is created and bound for you. You only need to act if you want to change its instructions or settings.

### 1. Author the agent

Rewrite the default **Patient agent**, or create another service agent with **Audience = Patient** on the [Agents page](/docs/services/agents#audiences). It gets the patient tool set, and the safety preamble is always added on top of your instructions.

### 2. Settings

On the service's **Settings**, the **Patient agent** card:

- **Let enrolled patients chat with this service's patient agent in the patient portal**: the on switch.
- **Where patients can chat**: **Patient portal** is **Chat**. **WhatsApp** and **SMS** show **Notifications only**.
- **Patient agent**: a dropdown of this service's active Patient-audience agents. If there are none, it points you to the Agents page.
- **Escalation role**: which organisation role (Admin, Doctor, Nurse or Receptionist) is notified on a red flag, a failed turn or a hand-off. It is resolved through [Coverage](/docs/services/care-team#coverage--rota) to whoever is covering that role right now.
- **Emergency number**: e.g. `995`.
- **Emergency message**: a template, seeded with a default, that must keep the `{emergencyNumber}` placeholder. Sent immediately and unedited whenever the red-flag screen fires.
- **Offer education & counselling sessions proactively**: lets a staff service agent message patients to ask whether they would like to go through a counselling protocol from the content library, based on where they are in their care. The patient agent still never starts a conversation; it handles the reply.
  - **Agent that sends the offer**: a staff agent that can message patients.
  - **Also check right after an encounter note is saved**: otherwise offers are only considered on scheduled agent runs.

Click **Save**.

### Subagents

A patient agent can call linked workflow subagents, but only those set to let the agent decide; **Every run** is not available to it. See [Service Agents → Subagents](/docs/services/agents#subagents).

## Safety design

A few things are true regardless of settings, and are not configurable away:

- **Emergency screening runs on the patient's message.** A hit sends your **Emergency message** and opens an escalation.
- **It never invents an appointment, clinician name, result, or clinical fact.** Appointments only ever come from the appointment tools; anything the agent doesn't know, it hands off to the care team rather than answering.
- **It never diagnoses, changes a prescription, or interprets an unreleased result.**
- **It discloses that it's an automated assistant**, not a clinician, at the start of every new conversation and on request.

## Drafts awaiting review

The patient agent replies in the portal directly. If a patient has an older stored reply that was drafted for review, a **Draft awaiting review** card appears at the top of their page (above the tabs, so it is never missed):

1. Read the drafted reply, along with its detected **intent** and **triage band** badges.
2. Either:
   - Click **Approve & send** to deliver it exactly as drafted, or
   - Edit the text in place, which changes the button to **Send edited reply**, or
   - Click **Discard** to drop the draft entirely (nothing is sent).


## Escalations

An **Escalations** card on the patient's page (General tab) lists everything the patient agent has flagged: a **Red flag**, an **Agent-flagged triage**, a failed turn (**Agent turn failed**), an **Out of scope hand-off**, or something **Staff flagged** manually. Each shows its severity, status, and timestamp.

- Click **Acknowledge** to mark an open escalation as seen.
- Click **Resolve** once it's been handled.

If no one was actively covering the configured escalation role at the time, the card notes that the service owner was notified instead as a fallback — a visible signal that the rota needs attention.

## Preview chat

Before patients use the agent (or after editing it), use **Preview chat** from that agent's row on the Agents page:

1. Pick a real cohort patient to test against. The preview uses their actual context in this service.
2. Type messages as if you were that patient and see the agent's replies in a sandbox chat window.

**Nothing in a preview is sent to the patient or saved to their message history** — appointment bookings and escalations run in a simulated dry-run mode only.

## Recommended rollout

Before the first real patients are enrolled, use **Preview chat** to walk through appointment, education, red-flag and out-of-scope scenarios, and keep an eye on the **Open escalations** and **Pending drafts** figures on the service **Overview**.

# Introduction

## What is a Service?

A **Service** is a named care pathway run by an organisation in the Medbot app. It is the container for everything needed to run one: a **care team** drawn from the organisation's members, a **cohort** of patients, an **SOP and Instructions** text and a **content library** that every AI agent draws on, **service agents**, AI **encounters** patients complete on the patient portal, **referrals** coming in, and a **Workstation** for running the service's day.

Examples of a service: "Colorectal SOC: Referral to Discharge", "Post-op Cardiac Rehab", "Diabetes Management: Primary Care".

> **Services were called Care Programs.** The feature was renamed and reworked: services now use the organisation's own roles instead of care roles, and patients come from the organisation's Medplum directory. See [What changed](#what-changed-from-care-programs).

Services belong to an organisation. With an organisation selected in the Medbot app, find them under **Services** in the sidebar.

## Key concepts

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Service** | The top-level container: name, specialty, setting, **mode of care** and status (`draft` or `published`). |
| **Mode of care** | How the service delivers care: **Clinic**, **Inpatient** or **Remote**. It decides what the Workstation shows. See [Modes of Care](/docs/services/modes-of-care). |
| **Care team** | The organisation members who staff the service. Each joins with the role or roles they already hold in the organisation (Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Receptionist). There are no service-specific roles. |
| **Coverage** | Who is covering a role right now (for a rota with leave), so Medbot can notify "whoever is the covering Nurse today" rather than a named person. |
| **Cohort** | The patients enrolled in this service, taken from the organisation's Medplum patient directory. A patient can be in several services' cohorts. |
| **SOP and Instructions** | Plain-language description of how the service runs, who does what and when to escalate. Medbot turns it into the agent context every agent run is given. |
| **Content library** | Reference material and counselling protocols the service's agents and AI encounters can draw on. |
| **Service agent** | An AI agent scoped to this service, with an **audience** (Staff, Patient or Onboarding), tools and markdown instructions. Every service starts with four default agents. |
| **Patient agent** | The service's AI agent that enrolled patients chat with in the patient portal. SMS, WhatsApp and email only notify patients that something is waiting there. |
| **AI encounter** | A structured conversation (history taking, health education or financial counselling) that the patient completes on the patient portal, or on a clinic device in the in-clinic **kiosk**. |
| **Patient context** | An AI-maintained briefing per patient, kept current from encounter notes, that every agent is given. |
| **Workstation** | The service's working view of the day. In a Clinic service it is the queue; in an Inpatient service the ward census; in a Remote service the caseload. It also holds the consult screen. |
| **Demo service** | A service filled with made-up patients, for showing or learning how a service runs. See [Demo Services](/docs/services/demo-services). |

## What you can do with a Service

1. **Set it up** through a guided seven-step wizard: care team, SOP and Instructions, referral criteria, agents, settings, content library, patients.
2. **Take referrals**: record them, screen them against the service's criteria and offer the patient a slot. See [Referrals](/docs/services/referrals).
3. **Manage a cohort**: add patients from the organisation's directory (they get an enrolment notice), track tasks, notes and messages per patient, and open any patient's page.
4. **Author and run service agents** for staff, for patients, or for onboarding, on demand or on a schedule, and link library workflows to them as subagents.
5. **Send AI encounters** to patients, or hand a clinic device to the patient for an in-clinic text or voice encounter.
6. **Record a consult** for up to an hour and review an AI-drafted clinical note section by section before adding it.
7. **Run the day** from the Workstation: the queue, the ward census or the remote caseload, with a pre-visit history board and an inbox for concerns patients raise.
8. **Let patients chat with a patient agent** in the patient portal, with safety screening built in.
9. **Run an appointment book** against the care team's published availability. Patients can book themselves in the portal, and every booking appears on the clinic schedule.
10. **Notify patients** by email, WhatsApp or SMS when something is waiting in the portal. See [Patient Notifications](/docs/services/patient-notifications).

## Who can see a service

Every member of the organisation can see the list of its services, and **any member can create one**. Opening a service is limited to:

- its **owner** (the person who created it)
- active members of its **care team**
- the organisation's **admins** (and `superuser` accounts)
- Medbot platform administrators

Anyone else gets "not found". The owner edits the service's details, finishes its setup, and can delete it.

## What changed from Care Programs

| Before | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Care Programs under `/programs` | Services under `/services`. |
| Six default care roles (Consultant, Clinic nurse, PSA, Dietitian, Care coordinator, Pharmacist) and a special `Patient` role, managed per program | No service roles. Each care team member's roles are their organisation roles. |
| Agents scoped to a care role | Agents have an **audience**: Staff, Patient or Onboarding. |
| **Workflow** tab and wizard step | **SOP and Instructions** (wizard step 2, and a card on **Overview**). |
| Platform-wide patient directory with add/edit/delete | The organisation's Medplum patients, managed from the organisation's patient list by admins. |
| Welcome message authored in the Content Library | A single enrolment notice sent by Medbot, with an optional note from the care team. |
| Patient agent over SMS and WhatsApp | Patient agent in the patient portal. SMS and WhatsApp only notify. |
| Freeform "Other skills" on agents | Removed. Old tags show as **Legacy tag** badges. |
| Sandbox page | Removed. Use **Preview chat** on a patient agent. |

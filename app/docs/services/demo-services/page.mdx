# Demo Services

A **demo service** is a fully configured service filled with made-up patients, so you can show how a service runs, or learn it yourself, without touching real records. Demo patients have no real phone, email or ID number, so nothing is ever sent to a real person.

Demo services live only in a **demo organisation**: your own organisation for showing Medbot, with synthetic data only. Loading demo patients there also makes **demo sign-ins** for the care team and the patients, so the people you show it to can sign in as each person and try every part themselves. Nothing demo remains in a clinic's real organisation.

## Your demo organisation

Anyone signed in may make one demo organisation, on a deployment where Medbot can host Medplum projects (Medbot's production service).

1. Open **Organisations** from the sidebar switcher. The dashed **Demo organisation** card says "Your own organisation for showing Medbot to clinics, with synthetic data only."
2. Select **Create demo organisation**. Medbot makes a Medbot-hosted organisation for it, in Singapore, with you as its first admin, and switches you into it. The same card is under the form on **Create organisation**.
3. Later, select **Open demo organisation** on the same card.

A demo organisation is marked **Demo** in your memberships list, and the switcher shows **Demo organisation** under its name. It does not count towards the one organisation you may create. If the card is missing, the server cannot make hosted organisations.

## Create a service from a template

1. In the sidebar, open **Services** and click **New Service**. In a demo organisation, the page says "Start from a blank service, or try a demo service: a template that comes fully configured for one clinical area and can be filled with demo patients." The organisation **Overview** also lists the templates under **Try a demo service**, each opening this page with that template chosen.
2. Under **How to start this service**, choose a template:

   | Template | Specialty | What it sets up |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | **Chronic disease management** | Family medicine | Pre-visit history before each review, red flags for low and high blood sugar, chest pain and stroke signs, foot and eye screening steps, and education and financial counselling agents for chronic care. |
   | **Joint replacement and recovery** | Orthopaedic surgery | Post-op reviews with wound check, X-ray and physiotherapy steps, red flags for wound infection, calf swelling and breathlessness, education on weight-bearing, exercises and pain relief, and pre-op readiness questions. |
   | **Medical retina clinic** | Ophthalmology | Visual acuity, pressure and dilation steps, red flags for sudden vision loss, flashes and floaters, and a painful red eye after injection, injection interval reminders, and a pre-visit history on vision changes. |
   | **IVF and assisted reproduction** | Reproductive medicine | Monitoring visits with scan and hormone blood-test steps, red flags for OHSS and for bleeding or pain in early pregnancy, injection-technique and cycle-stage education, and financial counselling for cycles. |

3. The **Name** is pre-filled with the template's name; change it if you like. The template sets the specialty, protocols, referral criteria, AI encounters, red flags, agents and visit steps. You can change any of them afterwards.
4. Click **Create from template**.

The service is published straight away, with no setup wizard, and shows an amber **Demo** chip beside its status ("Demo data - synthetic, not a real patient record"). Any member of the organisation can create one.

### Templates in a real organisation

The same templates are offered in every organisation, with the same configuration, but only in a demo organisation do they make a demo service. Elsewhere the new-service page calls them "a template that comes configured for one clinical area", the organisation **Overview** heads them **Start from a template**, and the service they create is an ordinary published service with no **Demo** chip and no demo patients.

## Load demo patients

The **Demo patients** panel (with a **Synthetic data** chip) sits at the top of a demo service's **Overview**. For a service you made without a template, owners and organisation admins find it at the bottom of **Settings**, above the **Danger zone**. Everyone with access to the service can see the panel; only the service owner or an organisation admin sees the buttons that change it, and the sign-ins.

1. Click **Load demo patients**.
2. In **Load demo patients**, choose a **Demo patient set**. Each shows a name, a **Bundled** or **From Medbot-Simulation** badge, and a description. A set already loaded in another service is greyed out.

   The bundled sets are **Chronic disease clinic — core six**, **Joint replacement — pre-op to 3 months**, **IVF cycles in progress** and **Medical retina clinic — injection list**.
3. Choose which patients to load. If the set has several conditions, archetypes or sites, filter with **Condition**, **Archetype** and **Site**. Quick buttons choose **One per archetype**, **Two per archetype**, **All shown** or **None**; or tick patients individually under **Patients (N shown)**.
4. Click **Load N patient(s)**.

<ImageZoom src="/images/docs/services/demo-services/02-load-demo-patients-dialog.png" alt="The Load demo patients dialog with the bundled set, the Condition filter, All shown and None, the patient list and the Load 6 patients button" />

Patients are dated relative to today, so their story is current whenever you load them. The panel's status line tracks progress ("Loading demo patients - N of M ready.") and ends with "N demo patients loaded." If some steps fail it says so, and you can try again.

Each loaded patient shows their name (a link to their patient page), a summary and tags. An **In patient portal** badge appears once their portal copy is ready. Demo patients carry a **Demo** chip in the cohort roster, on their patient page, and on the Workstation boards. Use the link at the foot of the panel to see them in today's queue on the Workstation.

## Demo sign-ins

A load also makes accounts for a cast of staff and for every demo patient, so a clinic being shown Medbot can sign in as each of them. They are listed in the **Demo sign-ins** section of the panel, for the service owner and organisation admins.

- **Who.** A set names its own cast. The IVF set has Dr Jonathan Lim (the lead doctor), Siti Nurhaliza (nurse, covering the Nurse role), Grace Ho (admin) and Dr Ruth Tan; any other set gets a generic lead doctor, nurse and admin. Each demo patient gets a sign-in linked to their own portal record. The lead doctor becomes the practitioner on the run's appointments and plans.
- **Addresses.** Plus-addresses of the email of the person who loaded the set, for example `you+orchard-lim@yourdomain` for a staff member and `you+orchard-mei-lin@yourdomain` for a patient. The accounts are created already verified, so no invitation or code is sent. The care team's ordinary notifications go to those addresses, so you receive them.
- **Password.** One per load, two words and four digits such as `maple-river-4821`, shown at the top of the section. "Every account below signs in with this password. A reset ends them, and the next load gives them a new one." **Copy password** copies it; **Copy all** copies the password and every address, with where each signs in, as text to paste into a message to the client.
- **Where.** The section says where the **Care team** signs in (the Medbot web app, with the clinic CMS for records, scans and the audit log) and where **Patients** sign in (the patient app).

While signed in with a demo patient's sign-in, every patient app screen carries an amber **Demo patient** banner: "A synthetic record for a demonstration. Nothing here is real."

## View as patient

Any care team member in a demo organisation can see what a demo patient sees without a sign-in. Once a patient shows **In patient portal**, click **View as patient**. The patient portal opens in a new tab as that demo patient, signed in as you. The first time, it asks you to complete a short sign-up.

If it fails, a message says "Could not open the patient portal".

## Remove or reset the demo

These buttons are for the service owner and organisation admins:

- **Refresh portal copies**: re-syncs the patient portal copies.
- **Reset and reload**: after you confirm **Reset and reload the demo?**, deletes everything the demo patients have (charts, appointments, visits, notes and portal copies, including anything you added while demoing) and reloads them dated from today.
- **Remove demo patients**: after you confirm **Remove the demo patients?**, deletes the demo patients and everything they touched. If removal does not finish, the panel says so and offers **Retry removal**.

A reset also ends the demo sign-ins: their sessions end, the staff leave the care team and rota, and the patient links are revoked. The next load reuses the same addresses with a new password.

A service that still has demo patients cannot be deleted. Remove them first, so their records are deleted from the patient portal too.

### Demo patients loaded before demo organisations

A service in a real organisation that was filled with demo patients before this change keeps them until you remove them. Its **Demo patients** panel says "Demo patients now load only in a demo organisation (open yours from Organisations). Remove the ones still in this service." and offers only **Remove demo patients**.

# Send Feedback

Tell the Medbot team what you think: feedback, a question, a complaint, a suggestion or a comment about Medbot Health. A person on the team reads every message. For a question about your own care, [message your clinic](/patient/messages) instead. For something that is broken, [Report a problem](/patient/report-a-problem).

## Where to find it

- In the menu, under **Help**, select **Send feedback**. It opens over the screen you are on, so you don't lose your place.
- On **Profile**, under **Help**.
- In the [mobile app](/patient/mobile-app), on **Profile**.
- On the Medbot website at [medbot.me/contact](https://medbot.me/contact), without signing in.

## Send a message

1. Under **What's this about?**, choose **Feedback**, **Enquiry**, **Complaint**, **Suggestion** or **Comment**.
2. Check **Your name**. It is filled in from your account.
3. Under **How should we reply?**, choose **Email**, **Phone call**, **WhatsApp** or **Any**.
   - **Email** needs your email address.
   - **Phone call** and **WhatsApp** need your phone number. Singapore (+65) is assumed. For another country, type the number with its `+` code. For a phone call you can pick the best time to call.
   - **Any** needs at least one of the two.
4. Optionally add a **Subject**.
5. Write your **Message**, at least 10 characters. Please don't include medical details or NRIC numbers.
6. Leave **Medbot may use these details to reply to me** ticked if you'd like a reply.
7. Select **Send message**.

The form works in every language the app offers.

## If you don't want to be contacted

If you untick **Medbot may use these details to reply to me**, the form explains: "Without your permission we can't contact you about this message. We'll still read it, but we won't be able to reply." It then asks you to choose:

- **Let Medbot reply** ticks the box again.
- **Send without a reply** sends your message with no email address or phone number. Nothing you typed in those fields is sent or kept, and the team cannot reply to you.

## What is attached

So the team understands what you were doing, the message carries the screen you were on, the app version, your language, browser or phone, window size, and any errors the app showed you just before. Select **See what's attached** to see exactly what goes. Record numbers in the screen's address are hidden, and your health records, results and messages are never attached.

## After you send it

You'll see **Thanks, we've got your message** with a reference like `FB-7K3Q9M`, and how we'll reply. If you gave an email address and agreed to be contacted, a receipt with the reference is emailed to you. Keep the reference if you need to follow up.

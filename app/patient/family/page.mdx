# Family

The **Family** page brings together everyone around your health record: the people you look after, and the people who can help with yours. You do not need a clinic to use it.

> Look after a child or relative, and choose who can help with your own record. You do not need a clinic to use this.

Open it from the **Family** card on Home ("Look after a child or parent, or share your record with someone you trust."), or from **Profile**, then **Family and sharing**.

The page has two cards:

- **People you care for**: records you can see because someone gave you access, and profiles you created. Select **Create a profile** to add one.
- **People who can access your record**: your own caregivers and invites. Select **Add someone who can help with your record**. See [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers).

While you are viewing someone else's record, the page only says that family settings are about your own account, and to switch back to your own record to change them.

## Key ideas

| Idea | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile for someone else** | A health record you keep for a child or family member who has no Medbot account. It holds only what you enter. |
| **Self-reported profile** | What such a profile is labelled: "Self-reported profile. No clinic records are connected." |
| **Hand-over** | Sending the person a link so they can take the profile over with their own account. |
| **Claim** | What the person does with that link: the record becomes their own. |

## When you sign up

After you confirm your own name and date of birth, Medbot asks **Who is this for?**:

> You can look after someone else's health record from your own account. You can also do this later under Family.

Choose **Just me**, or **Also set up a profile for a child or family member**. Select **Skip for now** to decide later.

## Create a profile for someone else

1. On **Family**, select **Create a profile**.
2. Enter their **First or given name**, **Last or family name** and **Date of birth**.
3. Under **You are their**, choose how you are related, for example Parent.
4. Select **Create profile**.

The profile then appears under **People you care for**. Select **View their record** to switch to it, the same way a caregiver switches records. See [Switching between records](/patient/caregivers#switching-between-records).

Creating a profile does not connect any clinic's records. To connect a clinic, the clinic sets up access. See [When the clinic sets up access](/patient/caregivers#when-the-clinic-sets-up-access).

## Give them their own access

When the person is ready to look after their own record, hand it over.

1. Under their profile, find **Give them their own access**:

   > Send a link to their email. Once they sign in, this becomes their own record and they see everything entered so far. They choose whether you keep access, and you stop seeing their AI chats either way.

2. Enter **Their email** and select **Send link**. Medbot says "Link sent to" the address, and until when it is valid.
3. If the email could not be sent, Medbot shows the link instead: "We could not email it. Share this link with them yourself. It is the only copy." Select **Copy link**.

To stop a hand-over, select **Cancel link**.

## Taking over your own record

If someone sent you a link to take over your record:

1. Open the link and sign in, or create an account. The page reads **Your health record**: "Someone has been looking after this record for" your name, with the date of birth on the record.
2. Check it is really yours. If the date of birth is different from the one you gave when you signed up, Medbot warns you.
3. Tick **This record is about me**.
4. Answer **Should the person who created this record keep access?**:
   - **Yes, keep their access**. They can still see your record until you change that under **Family**. They cannot see your AI chats.
   - **No, remove their access**.
5. Select **Take over this record**, then **Go to my record**.

From then on, the record is yours. Every choice made for you before, such as what each clinic could see, resets, and you choose for yourself. See [Sharing with Your Clinics](/patient/clinic-sharing#who-can-change-these-choices).

Chats written in Ask Medbot before you took over are not shared with anyone. Ask Medbot shows **Chats from before you took over**, and each message says who wrote it. Choose **Keep them** or **Delete them**.

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "This link was sent to a different email address…" | Select **Sign in with a different account** and use that address. |
| "Your account already holds health information, and records cannot be merged yet…" | Your account already has its own record. Nothing has been changed. Sign in with a different account. |
| "This link is not valid any more…" | Ask the person who sent it for a new one. |

## Stop looking after a profile

- **Stop caring for them**, then **Stop**, removes your own access. You can do this only while someone else also looks after the profile.
- If you are the only person looking after it: "You are the only person looking after this profile. To stop, hand it over to them or delete it."
- **Delete this profile**, then **Delete profile**, erases everything entered for them. It cannot be undone. A profile someone is taking over cannot be deleted.

## AI chats for a child or dependant without an account

A parent or legal guardian whom the clinic has confirmed, and who sees the whole record, can turn on Ask Medbot for someone who has no account of their own:

> This person has no account of their own, so you decide. Turn this on to use Ask Medbot for them. It ends if they ever claim their record.

Select **Turn on AI chats**. It reads **AI chats are on for you**, with **Turn off AI chats**. This choice, and the guardian's sharing choices for that person, end when they turn 21 or take over their record.

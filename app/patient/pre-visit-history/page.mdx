# Pre-visit History

When you book a visit with some services, Medbot can ask you a few questions beforehand. It takes about 10 minutes. Your care team can then read your answers before you arrive.

## How it starts

After you book, you may get a notification, **Tell Medbot about your visit**: "Answer a few questions before your visit". You can also find it:

- on **Appointment requests**, under **Before your visit**, with **Start** or **Continue**
- on Home, as a card

If you have no account yet, the clinic may send an invite by text message or WhatsApp.

## Before you start

The first screen says what will happen:

- Your answers are saved to your health record.
- They go to the service only if you choose, after you review them.
- If you mention a warning sign, Medbot tells you how to get urgent help and lets your clinic know.

Select **Start now**, or **Remind me the day before**. A caregiver with read-only access cannot start it.

## Answering the questions

Medbot asks in six steps: **Why you are here**, **When it started**, **Your symptoms**, **Your health**, **Medicines and allergies** and **Anything else**. It keeps a list of what it has recorded. It drafts a note for your care team, and nothing is sent until you review it.

Medbot is not a doctor. If you feel very unwell now, call 995.

## If you mention a warning sign

Medbot pauses and shows **This may need urgent care**. Depending on the service, you can choose **Call 995 for an ambulance**, **Alert the clinic now**, or **I feel safe, continue the questions**. If you alert the clinic, a nurse is told within minutes and may call you, and what you said so far is sent marked as a red flag. Your appointment stays booked unless the clinic brings it forward. You get a notification, **Your clinic saw your red flag**.

## Review and send

When you finish, you get **Review your pre-visit history**. Open the conversation and select **Review and send**.

1. On **Review and finish**, read **What Medbot recorded**. It has a **Summary for your care team** and **Questions to ask at your visit**.
2. Choose **Send to** the clinic, or **Keep in my record only**.

Sending puts it in the clinic's notes where your care team can read it. It cannot be unsent. To change an answer, tell your care team at your visit.

Afterwards the conversation says "Sent to" the clinic with a **See what was sent** button, or that it was kept in your record only. You can still send it before your visit. It is also in [Health Records](/patient/health-records) under **Before a visit**.

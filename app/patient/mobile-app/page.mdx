# Mobile App

The Medbot Health mobile app puts your health record on your phone or tablet. It is available for iOS and Android. On your phone it is called **Medbot**.

The app does most of what Medbot Health on the web does: Home and Ask Medbot, your log and health plan, appointments and visits, results, chats, family and caregivers, sharing with your clinics, connected devices and your profile. It uses the same Medbot account, so everything you see is the same in both.

## Getting the app

The mobile app is not publicly available yet. There is no store listing. Until it is, use Medbot Health in your phone's browser at **health.medbot.me**.

## Sign in

1. Open the app. The screen says **Sign in to Medbot**.
2. Tap **Continue with Apple** (iPhone and iPad only) or **Continue with Google** at the top of the screen. They create your account the first time you use them. Or, below them, enter your **Email** and **Password** and tap **Sign in**.
3. New to Medbot? Tap **Create an account**. Enter your **Full name**, **Email**, a **Password** of at least 8 characters, and **Confirm password**, then tap **Create account**. You are signed in straight away.

Medbot emails you a code to verify your address. Until you do, Home shows a **Verify your email** card. Enter the **6-digit code** and tap **Verify**, or tap **Not now**. You can use the app before you verify.

Signing in with a phone code or an email code is only on the web. See [Getting Started](/patient/getting-started#1-create-an-account-or-sign-in).

The first time you sign in, you confirm your name and date of birth, as on the web. See [Onboarding](/patient/getting-started#2-onboarding).

## Connect a clinic

Connect a clinic the same ways as on the web:

- **Open an invite link** from your clinic while you are signed in.
- **Connect it yourself.** Pick your clinic, enter the identifier and date of birth it has on file, then enter the code the clinic sends you.
- **Ask a clinic to see you.** In **Find a clinic or service**, tap **Connect** next to a clinic on Medbot. See [Ask a clinic to see you](/patient/connected-clinics#ask-a-clinic-to-see-you).

See [Connect a clinic](/patient/getting-started#4-connect-a-clinic). A clinic's self-registration link also works in the app. See [Invites & Join Links](/patient/invites).

## Tabs and menu

The tab bar at the bottom has the same five tabs as the web on a phone: **Home**, **Chats**, **Log**, **Appointments** and **Notifications**. A badge on **Notifications** shows how many you have not read.

Everything else is in the side menu. Tap the menu button at the top left of any tab to open it. It has:

- **Viewing**: whose record is on show, with your own record, everyone who gave you access, and invites sent to your email as **Pending**. Tap one to switch, or to review a pending invite. See [Switching between records](/patient/caregivers#switching-between-records).
- **My health record**: **Results**, **Medicines**, **Conditions**, **Allergies**, **Documents** and **Medical history**.
- **Care**: **Visits**, **Find a clinic or service** and **Connected clinics**.
- **Account**: **Family and sharing** and **Profile**.
- **Sign out**.

The avatar at the top right shows whose record is on show. Tap it to open the menu at **Viewing**.

Links in texts and emails from your clinic, and in notifications, open the same screen in the app. Anything the app does not have opens in your browser.

## Appointments

**Appointments** is the fourth tab. On the day of an appointment it starts with today's visit, where you register your arrival and follow the queue. It works as on the web: your upcoming appointments with **Cancel appointment** and **Change time**, every request you have made and where it stands, appointments your clinic has offered, and past and cancelled appointments with **Book again**. See [Appointments](/patient/visits#appointments) and [Today's visit](/patient/visits#todays-visit). **Visits**, your past visits, is in the menu under **Care**.

## Your details

Once your account is set up, your name and date of birth are locked. On **Profile**, tap **Request a change** to send a request with a photo of your ID, which a clinic you are linked to checks. It works as on the web. See [Change your name or date of birth](/patient/profile#change-your-name-or-date-of-birth).

## Photos and files

- **Scan a reading.** On the **Log** tab, tap **Scan a reading** and take a photo, or choose one from your photos. Check the numbers before you save. See [Scan a reading](/patient/health-log#scan-a-reading).
- **Ask Medbot.** Tap the paper clip to send a photo or a file, or use **Log something** on Home: **Photograph your monitor**, **Photo of your plate** or **Document**. See [Send a photo or a file](/patient/ask-medbot#send-a-photo-or-a-file).
- **Medical history.** Add documents and photos of medicine labels, and add, change or remove a condition. See [Medical History](/patient/medical-history).

Where the web only opens the camera, the app also lets you choose a photo you already took.

## Home

Home has your greeting, any cards from your clinic, today's goals from your health plan, the Ask Medbot box with its starter badges, your latest chat, a **Log something** button and **Your readings**. See [Home screen](/patient/ask-medbot#home-screen). Some things on the web's Home are not in the app yet: the Ask box does not turn your words into a draft log entry, and your readings still show how many days each was in range. Your family is in the menu, under **Account**.

The app follows your phone's light or dark setting.

## Connected devices

You can connect Garmin from **Profile → Preferences → Connected devices**, or from the **Add your Garmin data** card on Home. You sign in to Garmin Connect™ in your browser, then come back to the app. With Garmin connected, Home shows a **Garmin** chip, and **Log something** offers **Sync Garmin now**. See [Connected Devices](/patient/devices).

## Record services

You can order [Record Services](/patient/record-services) in the app. When you pay, the payment page opens in a browser inside the app. When you close it, the app shows your request again. Check its status there to see whether your payment went through.

## Downloads

You can download your health summary, your doctor's explanation as a PDF, and documents from your record.

- On an iPhone or iPad, PDFs and photos open inside the app.
- On Android, they open in your phone's viewer. If nothing can open them, the app offers to share them.

Downloaded files are deleted when you sign out, and after one day.

## Family and caregivers

You can invite a caregiver, choose what they can see, change or remove their access, accept an invite, create a profile for a family member, hand it over, and switch to the record of someone you care for, as on the web. See [Family](/patient/family) and [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers).

You can also choose what each clinic sees from **Profile → Sharing with your clinics**. See [Sharing with Your Clinics](/patient/clinic-sharing).

A few things work differently in the app:

- After you create an invite, tap **Share link** to send it with your phone's share options. The web has a **Copy** button instead.
- Removing a caregiver, stopping caring for someone and cancelling an invite each ask you to confirm first.

## Notifications

The **Notifications** tab lists your notifications, as on the web. See [Notifications](/patient/notifications). The app asks whether it may send you notifications on your phone the first time you open it. To change this later, go to **Profile**, then **Preferences**, then **Notifications**. It shows **On**, **Off** or **Turn on**. If you turned them off in your phone's settings, it says "Turned off in your phone's settings." Tap it to open those settings.

A notification shows only its title, never the details. Tap it to open the matching screen. Signing out stops notifications to that phone.

If you care for someone, you are not sent phone notifications about their record. You still see them in the **Notifications** tab when you view their record.

## Video consultations

A link to a video consultation opens in your phone's browser, where the call runs. You do not need to sign in. See [Teleconsult join links](/patient/invites#teleconsult-join-links).

## Read offline

Read offline is **off** unless you turn it on. Go to **Profile**, then **Preferences**, and turn on **Read offline** ("Keep visits, results and messages on this phone, encrypted, for 7 days.").

When it is on and you have no connection, the app shows "You're offline. Saved lists are still readable; changes need a connection." You can still read your lists of visits, appointments, results, log entries, your health plan, notifications, and who you can message.

The app never keeps what is inside a conversation, your doctor's explanations, documents, your records, your medical history, your profile, or today's queue. Nothing is kept while you are viewing someone else's record.

Turning **Read offline** off, or signing out, deletes the saved copy from your phone.

## App lock

App lock is **off** unless you turn it on. It asks for Face ID, a fingerprint or your phone's passcode before it shows your health record.

- After you first sign in, the app asks **Lock Medbot?** Tap **Turn on** or **Not now**.
- Or go to **Profile**, then **Preferences**, and turn on **App lock**. Your phone asks you to confirm it is you.

When it is on, the app asks each time you open it, and after it has been in the background for more than a minute. If you cancel, you see **Medbot is locked**. Tap **Unlock** to try again. Your phone's passcode always works, so you cannot be locked out.

If your phone has no Face ID, fingerprint or passcode set up, the app says "App lock isn't available". Set one up in your phone's settings first.

Whether or not app lock is on, the app hides your health record in the app switcher.

## Delete your account

Go to **Profile** and tap **Delete account**. It works as on the web. See [Delete your account](/patient/profile#delete-your-account).

- You must have signed in within the last 10 minutes. If not, tap **Sign in**. Once you sign in again, you come back to the same screen.
- After you tick the box and tap **Delete my account**, confirm with **Delete**.
- If you sign in with Apple, the screen reminds you to stop using Sign in with Apple for Medbot. In your Apple Account settings, open **Sign-In & Security**, then **Sign in with Apple**, choose **Medbot** and stop using it.

Deleting your account also clears what the app has saved on your phone.

## What is only on the web

- **Changing your email or mobile number.** **Manage Medbot account** opens Medbot Health in your browser. See [Change your email or mobile number](/patient/profile#change-your-email-or-mobile-number).
- **Signing in with a phone code or an email code.**
- **Reporting a problem.** See [Report a Problem](/patient/report-a-problem).
- **Describing what to log in words.** The Log tab and Ask box in the app do not turn your words into a draft entry, and its meal form does not estimate calories from a description. You can still photograph a meal.
- **Changing a log entry.** In the app you can delete an entry, but not change it.
- **Answering a few questions in an Ask Medbot chat before a referral**, and sending a concern to a clinic that is not on Medbot. The app can send to clinics on Medbot. It does not show the card that says which clinics were told about a red flag.
- **Video consultations**, which open in your browser.

# Mobile App

The Medbot Health mobile app puts your health record on your phone or tablet. It is available for iOS and Android. On your phone it is called **Medbot**.

The app does what Medbot Health on the web does: Home and Ask Medbot, your log and health plan, visits, results, messages, caregivers and your profile. It uses the same Medbot account, so everything you see is the same in both.

## Getting the app

The mobile app is not publicly available yet. There is no store listing. Until it is, use Medbot Health in your phone's browser at **health.medbot.me**.

## Sign in

1. Open the app. The screen says **Sign in to Medbot**.
2. Tap **Continue with Apple** (iPhone and iPad only) or **Continue with Google** at the top of the screen. They create your account the first time you use them. Or, below them, enter your **Email** and **Password** and tap **Sign in**.
3. New to Medbot? Tap **Create an account**. Enter your **Full name**, **Email** and a **Password**, then tap **Create account**. Enter the code we email you to confirm your address.

If you sign in before you have confirmed your email, the app asks for the code first ("Check your email").

Signing in with a phone code or an email code is only on the web. See [Getting Started](/patient/getting-started#1-create-an-account-or-sign-in).

The first time you sign in, you confirm your name and date of birth, as on the web. See [Onboarding](/patient/getting-started#2-onboarding).

## Connect a clinic

Connect a clinic the same two ways as on the web:

- **Open an invite link** from your clinic while you are signed in.
- **Connect it yourself.** Pick your clinic, enter the identifier and date of birth it has on file, then enter the code the clinic sends you.

See [Connect a clinic](/patient/getting-started#3-connect-a-clinic). A clinic's self-registration link also works in the app. See [Invites & Join Links](/patient/invites).

## Tabs

The tab bar at the bottom has the same five tabs as the web: **Home**, **Messages**, **Log**, **Visits** and **Results**.

- Tap the person button at the top of the screen to open **Profile**.
- On **Home**, the bell at the top opens your notifications.

Links in texts and emails from your clinic, and in notifications, open the same screen in the app. Anything the app does not have opens in your browser.

## Photos and files

- **Scan a reading.** On the **Log** tab, tap **Scan a reading** and take a photo, or choose one from your photos. Check the numbers before you save. See [Scan a reading](/patient/health-log#scan-a-reading).
- **Ask Medbot.** Tap the paper clip to send a photo or a file, or use the **Vitals**, **Meal** and **Document** cards on Home. See [Send a photo or a file](/patient/ask-medbot#send-a-photo-or-a-file).
- **Medical history.** Add documents and photos of medicine labels. See [Medical History](/patient/medical-history).

Where the web only opens the camera, the app also lets you choose a photo you already took.

## Record services

You can order [Record Services](/patient/record-services) in the app. When you pay, the payment page opens in a browser inside the app. When you close it, the app shows your request again. Check its status there to see whether your payment went through.

## Downloads

You can download your health summary, your doctor's explanation as a PDF, and documents from your record.

- On an iPhone or iPad, PDFs and photos open inside the app.
- On Android, they open in your phone's viewer. If nothing can open them, the app offers to share them.

Downloaded files are deleted when you sign out, and after one day.

## Caregivers

You can invite a caregiver, change or remove their access, accept an invite, and switch to the record of someone you care for, as on the web. See [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers).

A few things work differently in the app:

- After you create an invite, tap **Share link** to send it with your phone's share options. The web has a **Copy** button instead.
- Removing a caregiver, stopping caring for someone and cancelling an invite each ask you to confirm first.

## Notifications

The app asks whether it may send you notifications the first time you open your notifications. To change this later, go to **Profile**, then **Preferences**, then **Notifications**. It shows **On**, **Off** or **Turn on**. If you turned them off in your phone's settings, it says "Turned off in your phone's settings." Tap it to open those settings.

A notification shows only its title, never the details. Tap it to open the matching screen. Signing out stops notifications to that phone.

If you care for someone, you are not sent phone notifications about their record. You still see them in the bell when you view their record.

## Video consultations

A link to a video consultation opens in your phone's browser, where the call runs. You do not need to sign in. See [Teleconsult join links](/patient/invites#teleconsult-join-links).

## Read offline

Read offline is **off** unless you turn it on. Go to **Profile**, then **Preferences**, and turn on **Read offline** ("Keep visits, results and messages on this phone, encrypted, for 7 days.").

When it is on and you have no connection, the app shows "You're offline. Saved lists are still readable; changes need a connection." You can still read your lists of visits, appointments, results, log entries, your health plan, notifications, and who you can message.

The app never keeps what is inside a conversation, your doctor's explanations, documents, your records, your medical history, your profile, or today's queue. Nothing is kept while you are viewing someone else's record.

Turning **Read offline** off, or signing out, deletes the saved copy from your phone.

## App lock

App lock is **off** unless you turn it on. It asks for Face ID, a fingerprint or your phone's passcode before it shows your health record.

- After you first sign in, the app asks **Lock Medbot?** Tap **Turn on** or **Not now**.
- Or go to **Profile**, then **Preferences**, and turn on **App lock**. Your phone asks you to confirm it is you.

When it is on, the app asks each time you open it, and after it has been in the background for more than a minute. If you cancel, you see **Medbot is locked**. Tap **Unlock** to try again. Your phone's passcode always works, so you cannot be locked out.

If your phone has no Face ID, fingerprint or passcode set up, the app says "App lock isn't available". Set one up in your phone's settings first.

Whether or not app lock is on, the app hides your health record in the app switcher.

## Delete your account

Go to **Profile** and tap **Delete account**. It works as on the web. See [Delete your account](/patient/profile#delete-your-account).

- You must have signed in within the last 10 minutes. If not, tap **Sign in**. Once you sign in again, you come back to the same screen.
- After you tick the box and tap **Delete my account**, confirm with **Delete**.
- If you sign in with Apple, the screen reminds you to stop using Sign in with Apple for Medbot. In your Apple Account settings, open **Sign-In & Security**, then **Sign in with Apple**, choose **Medbot** and stop using it.

Deleting your account also clears what the app has saved on your phone.

## What is only on the web

- **Changing your email or mobile number.** **Manage Medbot account** opens Medbot Health in your browser. See [Change your email or mobile number](/patient/profile#change-your-email-or-mobile-number).
- **Signing in with a phone code or an email code.**
- **Video consultations**, which open in your browser.

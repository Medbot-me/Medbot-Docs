# Ask Medbot

**Chat** is the portal's landing tab. Its centre is the Ask Medbot box ("Ask Medbot about your health…"), with cards above it for anything the clinic is waiting on — see [Home screen and notifications](#home-screen-and-notifications).

## What it can answer

Ask Medbot answers against the patient's **own record only** — a bounded read of their recent visits and results. It is a record-search assistant, not a general medical chatbot and not a second opinion.

Every answer carries its **sources** as chips, so a patient can see which visit or report an answer came from, and a **limits card** stating plainly what Ask Medbot cannot do. Each answer also offers a hand-off to the clinic for anything that needs a person.

## Conversations

Each question starts a **conversation** that is saved, so the patient can ask follow-ups ("Ask a follow-up…") and come back to it later. Answers come back whole, not word by word.

- **Recent conversations** on the Ask Medbot screen (`/ask`) lists past conversations, titled from their first question.
- On Home, **Continue asking** reopens the latest conversation in place of the try-asking suggestions.
- The Messages hub shows the three most recent conversations, with **See all**.
- **Delete conversation** in a conversation's header removes it. Conversations are deleted automatically after 180 days.

## Red-flag screening

Before any model call, every message passes a deterministic red-flag screen. A message that trips it is **not** answered as a normal question: the limits card is replaced by an emergency banner, the hand-off to the clinic is always offered, and the escalation is sent to the clinic thread. When several clinics are connected, the escalation goes to all of them rather than guessing which one should see it.

This is why the portal is not an emergency service — the screen exists to get the patient to a human quickly, not to triage them.

## Sharing a concern with a clinic

Some worries are not an emergency but are worth a clinic seeing. When Medbot notices one in your chat, a card appears under its answer: **Would you like a clinic to see this?** It says what Medbot noticed and "Nothing has been shared with anyone."

Choose **Share with a clinic** or **Not now**. This is your choice. Medbot does not send anything until you agree.

1. After **Share with a clinic**, read "Share this with a clinic?". It lists what the clinic will receive: a short summary of what you told Medbot, your name, date of birth and phone number, and medicines and allergies from your record. It does not get the rest of the chat. Select **Read the summary before sharing** to see the summary first.
2. Tick the consent box, then select **Choose a clinic**. To keep it private, select **Not now, keep it in my record**.
3. On **Choose a clinic**, search by clinic or service. Your own clinics are listed first. Select **Send to** the clinic you want.
4. Follow it on the **Escalation** page. It shows whether the concern was sent, is being reviewed, or has a reply, and what the clinic suggests: book an appointment, try another service, go to the emergency department, or no action needed.

You can **Add something to the summary**. You can also select **Withdraw sharing with this clinic** at any time. This stops the clinic contacting you, and a copy stays in your record. A draft you never send expires after 7 days. Shared concerns also appear in [Health Records](/patient/health-records).

This is separate from red-flag screening above, which always alerts your clinics. A caregiver cannot share a concern for you.

## Guided care sessions

Alongside answering, Ask Medbot classifies the question for whether a **guided care session** would help. When one would, a consent card appears below the answer offering it. The three kinds are:

- **History taking**
- **Health education**
- **Financial counselling**

A session is only ever offered this way, with consent — there is no separate picker to start one from. Accepting creates a thread in [Messages](/patient/messages) with the session's own identity. A red-flagged message never triggers a session offer.

A session does not require the patient to be enrolled in any care program. When it completes, its summary is saved to the patient's own [Health Records](/patient/health-records), kept separate from any clinic's chart.

## Home screen and notifications

The **Chat** tab opens on **Home**. Besides the Ask Medbot box, Home shows cards when there is something for you to do:

- **An appointment offer or check-in.** For example "Sunrise Diabetes Service has offered you an appointment", with an **Open** button. See [Offers from your clinic](/patient/visits#offers-from-your-clinic).
- **Steps from your visit.** Steps your doctor confirmed at the end of your latest consultation. See [Steps from your visit](/patient/visits#steps-from-your-visit).
- **A session from your care team.** For example "Your care team at Sunrise Clinic would like to go through a history taking with you", with a **Start** button.
- **Today's visit.** On the day of an appointment, a card reads "Your appointment today at" and the time. It has a **Register and get my queue number** button. See [Today's visit](/patient/visits#todays-visit).
- **Your stay.** If you are in hospital, a card shows where you are admitted: the organisation, ward and bed, the date you were admitted, which day of your stay it is, and your expected discharge date.
- **Add your medical history.** Shown while your record has no conditions, medicines or allergies. See [Medical History](/patient/medical-history).
- **Tell Medbot about your visit.** A reminder to answer a few questions before a booked visit. See [Pre-visit History](/patient/pre-visit-history).
- **Continue asking.** Your most recent Ask Medbot conversation. With none yet, Home shows **Try asking** suggestions instead.

### The notification bell

The bell at the top of Home shows how many notifications you have not read. Select it to see the list, then select a notification to open it. With none, it says "No notifications yet."

| Notification | Why you get it |
| --- | --- |
| A session "encounter waiting" or "session waiting", for example **History taking encounter waiting** | Your care team or clinic has a guided care session for you. |
| A session "encounter completed" | A guided care session has finished. |
| **It is your turn** | Reception called you, and you turned on **Notify me when it is my turn**. See [Today's visit](/patient/visits#getting-told-when-it-is-your-turn). |
| **Your visit summary is ready** | Your clinician sent the summary of today's visit. See [Your visit summary](/patient/visits#your-visit-summary). |
| **Your care team replied** | A member of your care team replied in one of your sessions. |
| **Tell Medbot about your visit** | You booked a visit with a service that asks a few questions first. See [Pre-visit History](/patient/pre-visit-history). |
| **Your visit is coming up** | A reminder to answer those questions. |
| **Review your pre-visit history** | Medbot finished your questions. Check them, then choose whether to send them to the clinic. |
| **Your clinic saw your red flag** | The clinic has seen a warning sign you told Medbot about. |
| **Sent to** a clinic | A concern you chose to share has been sent. See [Sharing a concern with a clinic](#sharing-a-concern-with-a-clinic). |
| A clinic replied (**&lt;clinic&gt; replied**) | The clinic answered a concern you shared. |
| **Your doctor has explained your results** (or **Your doctor updated the explanation of your results**) | A doctor-approved explanation is ready. See [Your doctor's explanation](/patient/results#your-doctors-explanation). |
| Caregiver access changes | Someone was given or lost access to your record, or your access to someone else's changed. See [Caregivers](/patient/caregivers#notifications). |

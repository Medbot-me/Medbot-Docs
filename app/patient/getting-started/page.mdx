# Getting Started

You can use Medbot Health on your own, without a clinic: keep a log, add your medical history and scanned results, look after family, connect a watch and use Ask Medbot. To see a clinic's visits, results and messages, you also link that clinic.

## 1. Create an account or sign in

Open **health.medbot.me**. If you are not signed in, you see the Medbot Health welcome page, with the headline "All your health, in one place you control".

- New to Medbot Health? Select **Get the app** to create an account.
- Already have an account? Select **Sign in**.

At the top of the sign-in and sign-up pages you can choose your language: English, 简体中文, Bahasa Melayu or தமிழ். Medbot Health uses your browser's language to start with, when it is one of these. See [Language](/patient/language).

### Create an account

On **Create your account**:

1. Enter your **Full name**, **Email**, a **Password** of at least 8 characters, and **Confirm password**.
2. Select **Create account**.

Or select **Continue with Google**, when it is offered.

You are signed in straight away. Medbot also emails you a 6-digit code to verify your address. You can use Medbot Health before you verify it. See [Verify your email](#verify-your-email).

Already have an account? Select **Sign in** under "Already have an account?".

### Sign in

On **Sign in to Medbot**, choose how to sign in:

- **Continue with Google**, a button at the top of the page when it is offered. Below it, after "or", are the three tabs.
- **Phone** (the first tab). Enter your **Mobile number**, including the country code, and select **Text me a code**. Enter the 6-digit code and select **Continue**.
- **Email code**. Enter your **Email** and select **Email me a code**. Enter the 6-digit code and select **Continue**.
- **Password**. Enter your email and password and select **Sign in**.

A phone or email code only signs in an account that already exists. It never creates one. For your privacy the page always says "If an account uses this address, we sent it a code." If no code arrives, check you used the number or address on your account, or select **Create an account** under "New to Medbot?". To get another code, select **Send a new code**.

If you opened a link before you signed in, such as an invite or a page from a reminder, you go back to it once you are signed in.

Creating an account does not connect you to a clinic, and a new account has no health information in it yet.

You can also use Medbot Health on your phone. See [Mobile App](/patient/mobile-app).

### Verify your email

Until you verify your email, a **Verify your email** banner shows at the top of every page: "Verifying it lets you accept clinic invites sent to it."

1. Select **Enter code**.
2. Enter the 6-digit code from the email, and select **Verify email**. Select **Send a new code** if you need another one.
3. The banner says "Your email is verified." and goes away.

You can dismiss the banner and carry on. Nothing else in Medbot Health needs a verified email. The steps that trust your address ask you to verify it there and then, for example accepting a clinic's invite link.

## 2. Onboarding

The first time you sign in, Medbot Health asks you to confirm who you are. It asks for three things:

- First name
- Last name
- Date of birth

If you signed up from a clinic's invite, these are filled in from the clinic's chart. They may be out of date, so check them and correct anything wrong. If you signed up yourself, they start blank.

## 3. Who is this for?

Next, Medbot asks **Who is this for?** Choose **Just me**, or **Also set up a profile for a child or family member**, or **Skip for now**. You can do this later under **Family**. See [Family](/patient/family).

## 4. Connect a clinic

You do not have to connect a clinic. But until you do, your clinic visits, results and messages stay empty. The Health Log, Ask Medbot, Family, your medical history and connected devices all work without one.

Go to **Profile**, then **Connected clinics**, or use **Connect another clinic**. There are two ways to connect.

### Connect it yourself

1. On the **Connect a clinic** page, pick your **Clinic** from the list. If yours is not there, select **Browse the directory** in "Can't find your clinic?". See [Find Care](/patient/find-care).
2. Enter your **NRIC / FIN / Passport number** and your **Date of birth**.
3. Enter the **6-digit code** the clinic sends to the contact details it has for you.

The form gives the same answer whether or not a record was found, so no one can use it to check if someone is a patient. If the code runs out, start the step again.

### Use an invite from the clinic

Clinic staff can send you an invite link. Open it while signed in and your account is linked straight away. If the link has been used or has expired, use the form above instead.

If you sign up from an invite link, you come back to it automatically once your account is ready.

## After you connect

Connecting needs your **permission** before the clinic's records start to arrive. See [Connected Clinics](/patient/connected-clinics).

## No chart at the clinic yet?

If you have never been seen at the clinic, there is no chart to connect to. Ask the clinic for a **self-registration** link. See [Invites & Join Links](/patient/invites).

## Invited as a caregiver?

Sign in or create an account, then open the caregiver invite link. You do not need to connect a clinic of your own to help someone. You see their record. See [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers).

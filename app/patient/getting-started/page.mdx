# Getting Started

To see your own health information, you need three things: a Medbot account, a short set-up step, and a link to at least one clinic. You can use some parts of Medbot Health before you link a clinic, such as the [Health Log](/patient/health-log).

## 1. Create an account or sign in

Open **health.medbot.me**. If you are not signed in, you see the Medbot Health welcome page, with the headline "All your health, in one place you control".

- New to Medbot Health? Select **Get the app** to create an account.
- Already have an account? Select **Sign in**.

Creating an account does not connect you to a clinic, and a new account has no health information in it yet.

## 2. Onboarding

The first time you sign in, Medbot Health asks you to confirm who you are. It asks for three things:

- First name
- Last name
- Date of birth

If you signed up from a clinic's invite, these are filled in from the clinic's chart. They may be out of date, so check them and correct anything wrong. If you signed up yourself, they start blank.

## 3. Connect a clinic

You do not have to connect a clinic to open the app. But until you do, your visits, results and messages stay empty. Some other parts, like the Health Log, work without one.

Go to **Profile**, then **Connected clinics**, or use **Connect another clinic**. There are two ways to connect.

### Connect it yourself

1. On the **Connect a clinic** page, pick your **Clinic** from the list. If yours is not there, select **Browse the directory** in "Can't find your clinic?". See [Find Care](/patient/find-care).
2. Enter your **NRIC / FIN / Passport number** and your **Date of birth**.
3. Enter the **6-digit code** the clinic sends to the contact details it has for you.

The form gives the same answer whether or not a record was found, so no one can use it to check if someone is a patient. If the code runs out, start the step again.

### Use an invite from the clinic

Clinic staff can send you an invite link. Open it while signed in and your account is linked straight away. If the link has been used or has expired, use the form above instead.

If you sign up from an invite link, you come back to it automatically once your account is ready.

## After you connect

Connecting needs your **permission** before the clinic's records start to arrive. See [Connected Clinics](/patient/connected-clinics).

## No chart at the clinic yet?

If you have never been seen at the clinic, there is no chart to connect to. Ask the clinic for a **self-registration** link. See [Invites & Join Links](/patient/invites).

## Invited as a caregiver?

Sign in or create an account, then open the caregiver invite link. You do not need to connect a clinic of your own to help someone. You see their record. See [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers).

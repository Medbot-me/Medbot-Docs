# Getting Started

Three things have to happen before a patient sees any clinical data: a Medbot account, a one-time onboarding step, and at least one **account link** to a clinic. They happen in that order.

## 1. Create an account or sign in

The portal uses an ordinary email-and-password (or social) sign-in. Signing up creates a **Medbot account** and nothing more — a brand-new account has no clinical data behind it and is not yet known to any clinic.

## 2. Onboarding

Every account passes through a one-time onboarding screen that asks for three fields:

- First / given name
- Last / family name
- Date of birth

For a self-registered patient every field starts blank. For a patient whose account arrived through a clinic invite, the fields are pre-filled from the clinic's chart — which may be out of date, so confirming or correcting them is the point either way.

## 3. Connect a clinic

A signed-in account with no link is sent to the connect screen automatically; no portal tab will open until a link exists. There are two ways to get one.

### Self-service: identifier, date of birth, and a code

1. Pick the clinic from the list of clinics available to connect to.
2. Enter the identifier that clinic holds on the patient's chart (NRIC, FIN, or passport number) together with the date of birth.
3. Enter the six-digit code sent to the contact detail on that clinic's chart.

The first step answers the same way whether or not a record matched, so the form cannot be used to probe whether someone is a patient of a given clinic. An expired code means starting the step again.

### Clinic-issued invite

Staff can issue an invite from the CMS ("Send portal invite" on a patient's record). The invite is a link the patient opens while signed in; opening it consumes the token and links the account immediately. If the token has already been used or has expired, the self-service form above is offered as the fallback.

A patient who signs up from the invite link is brought back to it automatically once the account exists, so the link completes in one pass.

## After linking

Once one link exists, every tab has real data behind it. The link itself still needs the patient's **consent** before that clinic's records start syncing — see [Connected Clinics](/patient/connected-clinics), which also covers adding a second and third clinic.

## No chart yet?

A patient who has never been seen at the clinic does not have a chart to link to. Staff issue a **self-registration** invite instead, which collects the patient's details first and creates the chart. See [Invites & Join Links](/patient/invites).

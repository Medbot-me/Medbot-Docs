# Health Log

The **Log** tab is your own diary. Write down how you feel, your readings, your meals and your exercise. Your clinic can see what you log, but nobody watches it as it happens.

**In an emergency, call 995.** Do not use the log to ask for help.

## What you see

At the top is **Photograph a reading** with a **Scan a reading** button. Under **Log by hand** are four tiles: **Symptom**, **Vitals**, **Diet** and **Exercise**. Each shows your latest entry, such as "Last:" and when, or how many meals you have logged today.

Further down are **Latest vitals** (blood pressure, heart rate, SpO2, temperature and breathing rate) and **Recent entries**. An entry from a photo has a **From photo** badge. One added by a family member has **Entered by a caregiver**. Select the link to see your log next to your clinic's own measurements. With nothing logged yet, you see "Nothing logged yet" and a hint to start.

After you save, a banner says "Vitals saved.", "Symptom saved.", "Meal saved." or "Exercise saved."

## Log a symptom

1. Select **Symptom**.
2. Under **What are you feeling?**, pick one, for example Headache, Fever, Cough or Dizziness. Pick **Something else** to describe it yourself.
3. Under **How bad is it?**, choose from 0 (None) to 10 (Worst possible).
4. Say **When it started**. Tick **I still have it**, or say **How long it lasted**.
5. Optionally say where it is and add notes.
6. Select **Save symptom**.

Symptoms you log are saved to your health record as reported by you. The page reminds you: for chest pain, trouble breathing or signs of a stroke, call 995 now.

## Log vitals

1. Select **Vitals** and say **When you took them**.
2. Fill in only what you measured. Leave the rest blank. You can enter **Blood pressure** (top and bottom number, plus your position and arm), **Heart rate** and **SpO2**, **Breathing rate**, and **Temperature** (and where you measured it).
3. Select **Save vitals**.

If a number looks wrong, the screen tells you. For example, the top blood pressure number should be higher than the bottom one.

## Log a meal

1. Select **Diet**. Choose **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Dinner** or **Snack**, and **When you ate**.
2. Under **Food or drink**, add what you had. Portion, calories and type of food are optional. Select **Add another food or drink** for more.
3. Select **Save** for the meal.

## Log exercise

1. Select **Exercise** and choose an **Activity**, such as Walk, Run, Gym or Yoga.
2. Enter **When you started** and **Duration**. Distance, calories burned and **How hard was it?** (Light, Moderate or Hard) are optional.
3. Select **Save exercise**.

If you felt unwell while exercising, log a symptom as well.

## Scan a reading

Photograph a device or label and Medbot fills in the numbers for you to check. Your photo is not kept.

1. Select **Scan a reading**.
2. Choose what you are photographing: **Blood pressure monitor**, **Oximeter**, **Thermometer**, **Nutrition label**, **Meal** or **Watch or treadmill**. Medbot shows tips for each, for example to fill the frame with the monitor's screen and avoid glare.
3. Select **Take photo**, or **Choose from your photos**.
4. Wait while Medbot reads it. Then you see "Medbot read these from your photo".
5. Compare each number with your device and correct anything wrong. Nothing is saved until you save it.
6. Save. The entry shows **From photo**.

Medbot may flag a number it was unsure of, or an estimate, or say it could not see the unit. Check these. Select **Retake photo** to try again, or **Enter the numbers yourself** to type them in.

If a photo cannot be read or is too large, the screen says so and suggests typing the numbers in. If you scan many photos in an hour, you may be asked to type or try later.

## Log from Home or Ask Medbot

You can also log without opening the **Log** tab. On [Home](/patient/ask-medbot#log-something), the **Vitals** and **Meal** cards open your camera, and **Symptom** opens the symptom form. In Ask Medbot, the paper clip sends a photo of a reading or a meal.

Medbot reads the photo into a card in the conversation, with the same form as here, already filled in. Check the numbers, then save. Nothing is saved until you do, and your photo is not kept. The entry then appears here with **From photo**. See [Send a photo or a file](/patient/ask-medbot#send-a-photo-or-a-file).

Home also shows your logging streak and a summary of your recent readings. See [Your readings](/patient/ask-medbot#your-readings).

## Delete an entry

Select **Delete** on an entry, then **Delete** again to confirm (or **Keep**).

## Caregivers

A caregiver with "Can make changes" access can add entries. A read-only caregiver sees "You cannot add to this record." See [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers).

# Health Log

The **Log** tab is your own diary. Write down how you feel, your readings, your meals and your exercise. Your clinic can see what you log, but nobody watches it as it happens.

**In an emergency, call 995.** Do not use the log to ask for help.

## What you see

At the top is **Log something**, one button. It opens in place, so you do not leave the page. See [Log something](#log-something). Below it are your **Recent entries**: the latest 15, newest first. An entry from a photo has a **From photo** badge. A meal Medbot estimated from your words has an **Estimated** badge. One added by a family member has **Entered by a caregiver**. One synced from a connected watch or scale has a **Garmin** badge. Select the link under the list to see your log next to your clinic's own measurements. With nothing logged yet, you see "Nothing logged yet" and a hint to start, and **Log something** is already open.

Under your entries is **Your health plan**: your active goals with their progress, with **Add a goal** and **See your plan**, or **Set a goal** if you have no plan yet. See [Health Plan](/patient/health-plan).

After you save, a banner says "Vitals saved.", "Symptom saved.", "Meal saved." or "Exercise saved." After you change an entry, it says "Your changes are saved."

## Log something

Select **Log something**. The panel that opens has:

- **Tell Medbot what to log**, a box. Write it in your own words, for example "Chicken rice for lunch, BP 128/82", and select **Fill in the entry**. Medbot fills in the matching form for you to check: "Check what Medbot filled in. Change anything that is wrong before you save. Nothing is saved until you do." If it cannot find anything to log, it says so and you can try again or choose a kind below.
- Four tiles, **Symptom**, **Vitals**, **Diet** and **Exercise**, each showing your latest entry, such as "Last:" and when, or how many meals you have logged today.
- **Photograph a reading**, with **Scan a reading** for a monitor, plate, food label or watch.

Each of the symptom, vitals and exercise pages also starts with its own "describe it" box, with **Enter it yourself** under it for the form. The meal page works its own way. See [Log a meal](#log-a-meal).

If your words read as an emergency, Medbot shows **This may need urgent care** with the emergency advice and logs nothing.

## Log a symptom

1. Select **Symptom**.
2. Under **What are you feeling?**, pick one, for example Headache, Fever, Cough or Dizziness. Pick **Something else** to describe it yourself.
3. Under **How bad is it?**, choose from 0 (None) to 10 (Worst possible).
4. Say **When it started**. Tick **I still have it**, or say **How long it lasted**.
5. Optionally say where it is and add notes.
6. Select **Save symptom**.

Symptoms you log are saved to your health record as reported by you. The page reminds you: for chest pain, trouble breathing or signs of a stroke, call 995 now.

## Log vitals

1. Select **Vitals** and say **When you took them**.
2. Fill in only what you measured. Leave the rest blank. You can enter **Blood pressure** (top and bottom number, plus your position and arm), **Heart rate** and **SpO2**, **Breathing rate**, **Temperature** (and where you measured it), and your **Weight** in kilograms.
3. Select **Save vitals**.

If a number looks wrong, the screen tells you. For example, the top blood pressure number should be higher than the bottom one.

## Log a meal

1. Select **Diet**. The meal page opens on **Describe what you ate**. Write what you had, for example "Chicken rice with an extra egg, and a teh tarik", and select **Estimate calories**.
2. Medbot shows "Medbot estimated these from your description": one line for each food or drink, each number marked **Estimate**, with the totals as "about" a number. Estimates, not measurements. Select an item to change its portion, carbohydrate or protein, and change any number you know better. Select **Edit your description** to start again.
3. Choose **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Dinner** or **Snack**, and **When you ate**, then save. Nothing is saved until you do.

Other ways in, under the box:

- **Photograph your plate**: Medbot estimates from the photo, which is not kept.
- **Scan a nutrition label**, for packaged food and drinks.
- **Enter each item yourself**: add each **Food or drink**, and optionally its portion, calories, carbohydrate and protein in grams, and type of food. You can tick a **sweet drink**. Select **Add another food or drink** for more.

Calories, carbohydrate and protein count towards a [health plan](/patient/health-plan) goal such as carbohydrate a day or sweet drinks a week.

If Medbot cannot find any food or drink in your words, it says so, and you can try again or enter it yourself. There is a limit on how many estimates you can ask for in an hour. If your words read as an emergency, Medbot shows the emergency advice instead.

## Log exercise

1. Select **Exercise** and choose an **Activity**, such as Walk, Run, Gym or Yoga.
2. Enter **When you started** and **Duration**. Distance, calories burned and **How hard was it?** (Light, Moderate or Hard) are optional.
3. Select **Save exercise**.

If you felt unwell while exercising, log a symptom as well.

## Scan a reading

Photograph a device or label and Medbot fills in the numbers for you to check. Your photo is not kept.

1. Select **Scan a reading**.
2. Choose what you are photographing: **Blood pressure monitor**, **Oximeter**, **Thermometer**, **Nutrition label**, **Meal** or **Watch or treadmill**. Medbot shows tips for each, for example to fill the frame with the monitor's screen and avoid glare.
3. Select **Take photo**, or **Choose from your photos**.
4. Wait while Medbot reads it. Then you see "Medbot read these from your photo".
5. Compare each number with your device and correct anything wrong. A meal photo is shown as an estimate instead, one line per item, which you can change. Nothing is saved until you save it.
6. Save. The entry shows **From photo**.

Medbot may flag a number it was unsure of, or an estimate, or say it could not see the unit. Check these. Select **Retake photo** to try again, or **Enter the numbers yourself** to type them in.

If a photo cannot be read or is too large, the screen says so and suggests typing the numbers in. If you scan many photos in an hour, you may be asked to type or try later.

## Log from Home or Ask Medbot

You can also log without opening the **Log** tab. On [Home](/patient/ask-medbot#home-screen), type what you ate, felt, measured or did into the Ask Medbot box. Medbot drafts the entry in a card under the box for you to check, then **Save to health log**. See [Log something by telling Medbot](/patient/ask-medbot#log-something-by-telling-medbot). **Log something** on Home also opens the **Vitals**, **Meal**, **Symptom** and **Exercise** forms, and its **Photograph your monitor** and **Photo of your plate** items open your camera. See [Log something](/patient/ask-medbot#log-something). In Ask Medbot, the paper clip sends a photo of a reading or a meal.

Medbot reads a photo into a card in the conversation, with the same form as here, already filled in. Check the numbers, then save. Nothing is saved until you do, and your photo is not kept. The entry then appears here with **From photo**. See [Send a photo or a file](/patient/ask-medbot#send-a-photo-or-a-file).

Home also shows your logging streak and your latest entries. See [Your readings](/patient/ask-medbot#your-readings).

## Readings from a connected device

If you [connect Garmin](/patient/devices), readings arrive in your log on their own, marked **Garmin**: resting heart rate, steps, sleep, stress level, and blood pressure, SpO2 and weight when your Garmin devices record them. One reading is kept per measure per day, and a newer one for the same day replaces it.

Synced readings show on Home and to your care team like the ones you log. They do not count towards your streak, and Medbot does not check them for warning signs. When you disconnect, you choose whether to keep them.

## Change or delete an entry

Each entry you entered has a pencil to change it and a **Delete** button.

- **Change.** Select the pencil. **Change entry** opens the entry in the form it was logged with: "Change what you logged, then save. It replaces the entry in your health log." Change what is wrong and select **Save changes**. A change is checked for warning signs like any new entry.
- **Delete.** Select **Delete** on an entry, then **Delete** again to confirm (or **Keep**).

A reading synced from a connected device cannot be changed here, because it is the device's, so it has no pencil.

## Caregivers

A caregiver with "Can make changes" access can add, change and delete entries. A read-only caregiver sees "You cannot add to this record." See [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers).

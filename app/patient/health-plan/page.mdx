# Health Plan

Your **health plan** is a set of goals you track against what you log, such as fewer sweet drinks a week or 150 minutes of exercise. You build it with Medbot, in your own words. If you want, a clinic can check it and suggest changes, or your clinic can propose a plan for you after a visit. Nothing in it reaches a clinic unless you send it or choose to share it.

It lives on the **Log** tab, where **Your health plan** sits at the top. With no plan yet, the card says **Set a goal with Medbot**: "Write a goal in your own words, like eating less rice or walking more. Medbot suggests goals you can track here."

**Medbot's suggestions are not medical advice.** For a clinician's view, send your plan to your clinic.

## What a goal is

Each goal has:

- **What to track**, for example carbohydrate a day, calories a day, sweet drinks a week, exercise minutes or sessions, weight, blood pressure, LDL cholesterol, HbA1c, fasting glucose, taking your medicine, or **Something else**.
- A **target**: **At most** or **At least** a number, **each day** (optionally weekdays only), **each week**, or **by a date**. Some goals, such as taking your medicine or something else, have no number; you mark them done when you get there.
- **Your goal in words**, how you would say it, like "Fewer sweet drinks on weekdays".

Medical targets such as blood pressure, LDL cholesterol, HbA1c and fasting glucose can only be set by a clinic. If Medbot drafts one, it is added without a number, "as a goal to raise with them", and the form says "Your clinic sets this kind of target."

## Set a goal

1. On the **Log** tab, select **Set a goal** (or **Add a goal** on an existing plan).
2. Under **Your goal**, write it in your own words, for example "Eat less rice at lunch and more protein", and select **Suggest goals**.
3. Under **Medbot suggests**, Medbot proposes up to three goals it can track, each with a target. Untick any you do not want, or select one to change it. If Medbot needs more detail it shows a question under **Medbot asks**. To change the whole set, type under **Ask Medbot to change the suggestions** (for example "Make it easier to start with") and select **Update suggestions**.
4. Select **Add N goals to plan**.

Or select **Set a goal yourself** to fill in the form by hand, and **Save goal**.

If your words read as an emergency, Medbot shows **This may need urgent care** with the emergency advice instead of goals. If Medbot cannot suggest anything, it says so and you can say it another way or set a goal yourself. There is a limit on how many suggestions you can ask for in an hour.

## Goals from an Ask Medbot chat

When you talk about a goal in [Ask Medbot](/patient/ask-medbot), the answer can carry a card, **Goals from this chat**: "Medbot turned what you said into goals you can track. Untick any you don't want; you can change them on your plan later." Add them to your plan, or select **Not now**. A concern Medbot wants a clinic to see takes the place of this card.

## Your plan page

Select **See your plan** on the Log tab to open **Your health plan** (`/log/plan`): "Your goals and how your logging counts towards them. Your clinic only sees this plan if you send it to them."

Goals are listed under **Goals**, **Paused** and **Done**. Each shows its target, a bar, and facts about your progress, such as "5 of 7 days", "3 of the last 4 weeks", "Today: 150 g", "Latest: 72 kg on 3 Oct" or "Reached: 70 kg on 10 Oct". Progress counts what you log and, for weight, blood pressure and lab results, readings in your health record. Medbot never says whether you are doing well or badly. It only shows the numbers. A goal with nothing logged says "Nothing logged for this yet". One Medbot cannot track says "Medbot doesn't track this one. Mark it done when you get there."

A goal from your clinic carries a **Clinic** badge.

Select a goal to open it. From there you can **Change** it, **Pause** or **Resume** it, **Mark as done**, or **Remove goal**. A goal's "what to track" is fixed once saved. A clinic's goal can only be paused: "Your clinic set this goal. You can pause it, or ask them to change it."

**End this plan** at the bottom ends the plan: "Your goals stay in your health record, and you can start a new plan."

## Send your plan to a clinic for review

A clinician can check your goals and suggest changes. Nothing changes on your plan unless you accept it.

1. On your plan page, select **Send to a clinic for review**.
2. Under **Choose a clinic**, search, or pick from **Your clinics** (marked "Connected to you") and **Clinics near you**. Each shows how soon it usually replies. Only clinics that take concerns from patients are listed. If none is near you, use **Message your clinic instead**.
3. Check **What the clinic will see**: your goals and targets, your name and date of birth, and, unless you untick it, a summary of your last 30 days of logging. The summary says how many days you logged, and how often your readings were in the general range.
4. Optionally add **A note to the clinic**, for example "Are these targets realistic for me?".
5. Tick "I agree to share these with &lt;clinic&gt;. The clinic keeps what it receives as part of its records." If you are connected to the clinic, a note also goes into your record there.
6. Select **Send to &lt;clinic&gt;**.

Only you can send a plan. A caregiver cannot. One review at a time can be with a clinic.

### The clinic's reply

Follow the review from your plan page (**Review by &lt;clinic&gt;**, then **See review**). Its status is **Sent**, **A clinician is looking at it**, **Replied**, **Done** or **Withdrawn**. While it waits, the page says "We will let you know when they reply." **Withdraw from review** takes it back.

When the clinic replies, Home shows a card, "&lt;clinic&gt; replied about your health plan". The review page shows **Their advice** and any **Suggested changes**:

- a new goal
- a changed target, showing **Now** and **Suggested**, with **Why** when they gave a reason
- a goal to remove

For each, select **Accept** or **Keep mine**. **Accept all** takes them all. "Anything you do not accept stays as it is." A clinic target you accept can be paused but not changed. A clinic's reply about your plan is general advice. For anything urgent, call your clinic.

## A plan from your clinic

After a visit, a clinic you are connected to can propose a plan for you. You get a notification, "&lt;clinic&gt; proposed a health plan for you", and cards on Home and the Log tab. On your plan page, under **From your clinic**, the proposal shows **Their note**, **Targets your clinic set** ("You can pause these, but only your clinic can change them") and **Goals to go with them**.

- Tick **Share my progress with &lt;clinic&gt;** if you want them to see your goals and how you are doing against them, worked out from what you log and your results. You can stop at any time.
- Select **Start this plan**, **Not now** ("&lt;clinic&gt; will know you chose not to for now"), or **Message the clinic**.

If you already keep a plan, end it first or choose **Not now**. If a clinic proposes goals while you keep a plan, they arrive as suggestions on it instead, as in a review ("&lt;clinic&gt; suggested goals for your health plan").

While you share progress, your plan page shows **Shared with &lt;clinic&gt;**. Select **Stop sharing** there to stop.

## In your health summary

The plan you keep is copied into your health record as a plan of care. It appears in the **Plan of Care** section of the summary you download from Results. See [Download my summary](/patient/results#download-my-summary).

## Caregivers

A caregiver with "Can make changes" access can set, change, pause and finish goals, and accept a clinic's suggestions. A read-only caregiver can only view the plan. Only you can send the plan for review. See [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers).

# Messages

The **Messages** tab lists all your conversations in one place. Open any of them to read it and reply.

The screen has four groups: **Clinic**, **Care team**, **Ask Medbot** and **Assistants**.

## Clinic

One thread for each clinic you are connected to. With one clinic it is called "The clinic". With more than one, each thread is named after its clinic.

Use it for non-urgent questions to the clinic's staff. Staff see and answer it in their own app. Read what is already there before you add to it. Keep each message to one request, and say the date of the visit or appointment if it helps.

You do not see staff members' names in this thread.

## Care team

If a clinic enrolled you in a service, such as a diabetes service, you get a thread for it. Each thread is named for the service's assistant. Under the name you see the organisation that runs it, for example "Sunrise Clinic · AI assistant".

The thread explains what the assistant can and cannot do: "This assistant is run by the organisation that provides this service. It can look up your service details and hand off to a member of your care team for anything it can't answer. It can't diagnose. For anything urgent, please call the clinic."

To reach a person, select **Message the clinic**.

Each message names who sent it. A thread may also hold short check-in conversations that you open from it.

## Ask Medbot

Your three most recent [Ask Medbot](/patient/ask-medbot) chats. Select **See all** to see the rest.

## Assistants

Guided care sessions that you agreed to, such as a [pre-visit history](/patient/pre-visit-history). Each thread shows its own name and icon. A session's reply can carry a picture from the clinic's own library, for example a diagram or a device, under the message with its title and caption; select it to open it full size. Select **AI encounters** to see them all, grouped as waiting for you, completed and ended.

An ended session is read-only, with a badge on the list: **New**, **Completed**, **Expired** or **Ended**. In place of the message box you see one of these:

- A link to the session's summary, if it was completed. See [Health Records](/patient/health-records).
- A **Start a new session** form, if it was cancelled or left unfinished. It shows the same consent wording as before.

An unfinished session does not save a summary. A finished pre-visit history has a **Review and send** button (or **See what was sent**) in its thread.

## Red flags

If Ask Medbot or a session picks up a warning sign, it goes to your clinic's thread. If you are connected to several clinics, it goes to all of them.

## What Messages is not

Messages is not for emergencies. For anything urgent, call the clinic or your local emergency number.

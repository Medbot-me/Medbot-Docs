# Connected Clinics

The portal does not sign a patient into one clinic's system. It reads a patient-owned **health record**, and each clinic the patient connects syncs its own records into it. That is what lets one account carry a patient's care across several clinics.

Connected clinics are managed from **Profile → Connected clinics**.

## What a connection does

Connecting a clinic creates a link between the patient's health record and that clinic. Once the link is active, the clinic's records for that patient — lab and imaging reports, results and measurements, immunisations, documents, visit summaries — flow into the health record and appear on the portal's own screens, each tagged with the clinic it came from.

Every record row and every visit notes its originating clinic as soon as more than one clinic is connected, so a result is never ambiguous about which clinic issued it.

## Connection status

Each row on the Connected clinics list shows one of four states:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Awaiting confirmation** | The link exists but the patient has not consented yet. Nothing syncs until they do. |
| **Syncing** | The link is active and that clinic's records are flowing into the health record. |
| **No longer syncing** | The clinic discharged the patient; historical records stay in the health record. |
| **Syncing stopped** | The patient stopped the sync themselves. |

## Consent

A link created by a self-service connection or a clinic invite starts as **Awaiting confirmation**. The portal shows a consent card above the list explaining what the patient is agreeing to; confirming it moves the link to **Syncing**.

An active link can be stopped at any time with **Stop syncing**. Stopping a sync ends future flow from that clinic; it does not delete records already in the health record.

## Connecting another clinic

**Connect another clinic** on the profile screen runs the same flow as first-time linking, for a clinic that already has a chart for you: pick the clinic, enter the identifier and date of birth held on that clinic's chart, then the six-digit code. It returns to the profile when the code verifies. The list holds real clinics only. If you have not been seen at a clinic before, [ask it to see you](#ask-a-clinic-to-see-you) instead.

Connections matter beyond Results: the [Chats](/patient/messages) tab grows one front-desk thread per connected clinic, and an appointment request asks which clinic before it asks for a location. A clinic with no active link is not offered anywhere in the portal.

## What a clinic sees of yours

Connecting a clinic brings its records to you. It does not let the clinic read what you do in Medbot Health. An urgent message from Ask Medbot goes to the linked clinics that look after that kind of problem, and concerns go to your linked clinics unless you turn that off. Anything more, such as your chats or the lab reports you scanned, the clinic sees only if you choose. See [Sharing with Your Clinics](/patient/clinic-sharing).

## Ask a clinic to see you

If you have never been seen at a clinic on Medbot, there is no chart to connect to yet. Ask the clinic to see you instead. Select **Connect** next to a clinic near you after a guided session, or in the mobile app's [Find Care](/patient/find-care). On the web, open the clinic's page in Find Care and select **Book an appointment**. A clinic that takes bookings from Find care gets an [appointment request](/patient/find-care#book-an-appointment) instead, which connects you in the same way once it accepts. A clinic you are already connected to shows **Request an appointment** instead.

You can also show your [clinic card](/patient/clinic-card#a-clinic-with-no-record-of-you) at the clinic's front desk. A clinic with no record of you can then ask you to share your details and connect.

1. On **Ask to be seen**, under **Ask &lt;clinic&gt; to see you**, choose a **Service**. Under **What would you like help with?**, write a sentence or two.
2. Tick the consent: you agree to share your name, date of birth and contact details with the clinic so they can arrange your care, and to connect your Medbot account to your record there once they accept.
3. Select **Send request**.

The request goes to that service's reception. Staff there accept it by naming your chart there, which connects your Medbot account to it, and offer you an appointment time. Or they decline it. Either way you get a notification: **&lt;clinic&gt; accepted your request** or **&lt;clinic&gt; can't take your request**. A time they offer is in [Appointments](/patient/visits#offers-from-your-clinic). Once the clinic is connected, it shows under **Connected clinics** like any other, and you give permission as usual. See [Consent](#consent).

Your request is listed in [Appointments](/patient/visits#your-requests) as **Request to join a clinic**, with the status **Awaiting the clinic**, **Accepted** or **Declined**. You can ask a clinic once at a time: if a request is waiting, the page says "You've already asked &lt;clinic&gt; to see you."

Only clinics that are really on Medbot are offered. A clinic that is not taking requests through Medbot yet says so instead of **Connect**.

Not sure which clinic to pick? [Find Care](/patient/find-care) lists clinics on Medbot.

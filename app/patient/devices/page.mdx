# Connected Devices

You can connect your Garmin account so readings from your watch or scale arrive in Medbot Health on their own. They appear in your log and on Home, next to the readings you enter.

> Readings from a watch or scale you connect appear in your log and on your home page, next to the ones you enter.

Garmin is the only device service for now. If it is not offered where you are, the page says "Connecting a device isn't available yet."

## Key ideas

| Idea | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Garmin Connect™** | Garmin's own app and account. Medbot connects to it, not to the watch itself. |
| **Data you share** | The kinds of readings you let Medbot read. You choose them, and can change them at any time. |
| **Synced reading** | A reading Garmin sent. It shows **Garmin** in your log and on Home. |

Medbot only reads from Garmin Connect™. It never changes anything there.

## Connect Garmin

You can start from:

- **Home**, where a card reads **Add your Garmin data**: "Steps, sleep, heart rate and SpO2 show up here on their own, without typing them in." Select **Connect to Garmin Connect™**, or **Not now** to hide the card.
- **Profile**, then **Preferences**, then **Connected devices**, then **Connect to Garmin Connect™**.

Then:

1. On **Bring your Garmin Connect™ data into Medbot**, under **Choose what to share**, turn on what you want Medbot to read. You must choose at least one.

   | Data | What arrives |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Heart rate** | Resting heart rate each day |
   | **Blood pressure** | From a Garmin Index™ BPM, or entered in Garmin Connect™ |
   | **Sleep** | How long you slept |
   | **Steps and activity** | Steps each day, against your Garmin goal |
   | **SpO2** | Average blood oxygen for the day |
   | **Weight** | From a connected Garmin scale |
   | **Stress** | Garmin's daily stress level |

2. Select **Continue to Garmin Connect™**. You sign in to Garmin Connect™ and agree there, then come back to Medbot.
3. Medbot shows "Finishing connecting to Garmin Connect™…" and then your connected device.

If you said no at Garmin, you see "Medbot wasn't connected to Garmin Connect™. You can try again whenever you like."

One Garmin account can be connected to only one Medbot account.

## How readings arrive

New readings arrive shortly after your watch syncs with Garmin Connect™. You do not need to do anything.

Where Medbot can ask Garmin for your latest readings, you can also:

- select **Sync now** on **Connected devices**, or
- choose **Sync Garmin now** from **Log something** on Home.

Medbot says "Garmin is sending your latest readings. They usually arrive within a few minutes."

On Home, a **Garmin** chip beside **Log something** says when Garmin last synced, for example "Synced 5 min ago", or "Waiting for data" before the first readings.

### Where synced readings appear

- On the **Log** tab, with a **Garmin** badge. See [Health Log](/patient/health-log).
- On Home, under **Your readings**, with **Garmin** as the source. The list notes "Includes Garmin device-sourced data".
- To your care team, the same way they see the readings you log.

Synced readings do not count towards your logging streak. A day's reading replaces the one Garmin sent earlier for that day, so a re-sent summary is not counted twice. Medbot does not check synced readings for warning signs. Ask Medbot or call your clinic if you are worried about a reading.

## Manage the connection

**Profile → Preferences → Connected devices** shows:

- **Connected**, and **Last synced** with a time, or "Waiting for the first readings from Garmin"
- **Data you share**, with a switch for each kind of reading
- **Recent syncs**, each with how many readings arrived, or **Didn't sync**

If Garmin stops accepting Medbot's sign-in, the card says **Needs reconnecting**. Select **Reconnect Garmin Connect™**. If health data sharing for Medbot is turned off in Garmin Connect™, the page tells you to turn it back on there, under **Account information**.

### Disconnect

1. Select **Disconnect Garmin Connect™**. Syncing stops straight away.
2. Choose **Keep my synced readings** ("They stay in your record, marked as from Garmin.") or **Remove my synced readings** ("Every reading Garmin sent is deleted from your record.").
3. Select **Disconnect**.

## Who can connect a device

Only you. A caregiver viewing your record does not see the Garmin card or chip, and cannot connect or change a device: "Only the patient can connect or change a device."

Connected devices work the same way in the [mobile app](/patient/mobile-app).

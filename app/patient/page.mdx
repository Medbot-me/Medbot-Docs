# Introduction

## What is Medbot Health?

**Medbot Health** is your own health app, at health.medbot.me and in a [mobile app](/patient/mobile-app) for iOS and Android. You can use it on your own: keep a log of how you feel and what you measure, connect a watch, add your medical history and lab reports, look after your family's records, and ask questions about your health. Connect a clinic to also see your visits and results, message the clinic, and ask for appointments.

Your clinic may use Medbot CMS to run its own work. Medbot Health is the app made for you. It is separate from the clinic's system, and you decide which clinics to connect.

## Key ideas

| Idea | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Medbot account** | Your own sign-in. Having an account does not connect you to a clinic. |
| **Health record** | Your own copy of your health information, kept in one place. Records from each clinic you connect are added to it, and each one shows which clinic it came from. |
| **Connected clinic** | A clinic whose records are added to your health record, with your permission. You can connect more than one. See [Connected Clinics](/patient/connected-clinics). |
| **Caregiver** | A family member or carer who uses Medbot Health for you, with their own account. You choose which parts of your record they see. See [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers). |
| **Family** | Profiles you keep for a child or relative with no account, and hand over later. See [Family](/patient/family). |
| **Sharing with your clinics** | What each linked clinic may see of your Ask Medbot chats and scanned results, beyond the records it holds. See [Sharing with Your Clinics](/patient/clinic-sharing). |
| **Guided care session** | A short, guided chat with Medbot, for example to take your history before a visit. Medbot only offers one with your consent. |
| **Health plan** | Goals you track against what you log, built with Medbot, and optionally reviewed by a clinic or proposed by one. See [Health Plan](/patient/health-plan). |

## The tabs

Medbot Health has five tabs: **Home**, **Chats**, **Log**, **Appointments** and **Notifications**. On a phone they run along the bottom of the screen. On a computer they run across the top. **Home**, with the house icon, is where Medbot Health opens. The **Notifications** tab shows a badge with how many notifications you have not read.

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [**Home**](/patient/ask-medbot#home-screen) | The Ask Medbot box, today's goals from your health plan, your streak and latest entries, and the **Log something** menu. Also shows anything your clinic is waiting for. |
| [**Chats**](/patient/messages) | Your conversations with your clinic, your care team, Medbot's assistants and Ask Medbot. |
| [**Log**](/patient/health-log) | A diary of symptoms, vitals, meals and exercise, which you can fill in by describing it, and your health plan. |
| [**Appointments**](/patient/visits#appointments) | Today's visit and queue number, upcoming appointments, your requests to the clinic, and past and cancelled appointments. |
| [**Notifications**](/patient/notifications) | What Medbot wants you to know, grouped as today and earlier. |

## The menu

Everything else is in the menu. On a phone, select the menu button at the top left of any tab. Select your avatar at the top right to open the menu at **Viewing**. On a computer, the menu is the sidebar. It has:

- **Viewing**: whose record you are looking at. Your own is listed with each person who gave you access, and any invite waiting for you as **Pending**. See [Switching between records](/patient/caregivers#switching-between-records).
- **My health record**: [**Results**](/patient/results), **Medicines**, **Conditions**, **Allergies**, **Documents** and your [**Medical history**](/patient/medical-history).
- **Care**: [**Visits**](/patient/visits#visit-list-and-summaries), [**Find a clinic or service**](/patient/find-care) and [**Connected clinics**](/patient/connected-clinics).
- **Account**: [**Family and sharing**](/patient/family) and [**Profile**](/patient/profile), with your details, connected devices, language and how the clinic may contact you.
- **Help**: [**Report a problem**](/patient/report-a-problem).
- **Sign out**.

## What Medbot Health is not

Medbot Health is **not an emergency service**. For anything urgent, call the clinic or your local emergency number. In Singapore, call 995. Nobody watches your chats or your log as they happen.

Ask Medbot does not diagnose you or advise you on your medicines, and it can make mistakes.

Nothing you do here changes your clinic's own chart. If something looks wrong on a visit or a result, message the clinic.

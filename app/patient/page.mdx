# Introduction

## What is Medbot Health?

**Medbot Health** is your own health app, at health.medbot.me and in a [mobile app](/patient/mobile-app) for iOS and Android. You sign in to see your visits and results, ask questions about your health, message your clinic, keep a log of how you feel, and ask for appointments.

Your clinic may use Medbot CMS to run its own work. Medbot Health is the app made for you. It is separate from the clinic's system, and you decide which clinics to connect.

## Key ideas

| Idea | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Medbot account** | Your own sign-in. Having an account does not connect you to a clinic. |
| **Health record** | Your own copy of your health information, kept in one place. Records from each clinic you connect are added to it, and each one shows which clinic it came from. |
| **Connected clinic** | A clinic whose records are added to your health record, with your permission. You can connect more than one. See [Connected Clinics](/patient/connected-clinics). |
| **Caregiver** | A family member or carer who uses Medbot Health for you, with their own account. See [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers). |
| **Guided care session** | A short, guided chat with Medbot, for example to take your history before a visit. Medbot only offers one with your consent. |
| **Health plan** | Goals you track against what you log, built with Medbot, and optionally reviewed by a clinic or proposed by one. See [Health Plan](/patient/health-plan). |

## The tabs

Medbot Health has five tabs: **Home**, **Messages**, **Log**, **Visits** and **Results**. On a phone they run along the bottom of the screen. On a computer they are in the sidebar. **Home**, with the house icon, is where Medbot Health opens.

**Profile** is not one of the five tabs. On a phone, select your avatar at the top of the screen. On a computer, it is the last item in the sidebar.

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [**Home**](/patient/ask-medbot#home-screen-and-notifications) | Quick ways to log something, your logging streak and recent readings, and the Ask Medbot box. Also shows anything your clinic is waiting for. |
| [**Messages**](/patient/messages) | Your conversations with your clinic, your care team and Medbot's assistants. |
| [**Log**](/patient/health-log) | Your health plan, and a diary of symptoms, vitals, meals and exercise, or photograph a reading. |
| [**Visits**](/patient/visits) | Today's visit and queue number, past visit summaries, and appointments. |
| [**Results**](/patient/results) | Test results, your doctor's explanations, and the rest of your record. |
| [**Profile**](/patient/profile) | Your details, connected clinics, language and how the clinic may contact you. |

## What Medbot Health is not

Medbot Health is **not an emergency service**. For anything urgent, call the clinic or your local emergency number. In Singapore, call 995.

Nothing you do here changes your clinic's own chart. If something looks wrong on a visit or a result, message the clinic.

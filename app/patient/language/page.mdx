# Language

If your clinic offers other languages, you can choose the language Medbot Health uses for menus, buttons and replies in Chat.

To choose, go to **Profile**, then **Preferences**, then **Language**. The row shows the name of your current language.

On the **Language** page:

1. Under **App language**, pick a language. The one in use shows **Current**.
2. Select **Switch to** that language.

Languages are added one at a time, after a native speaker has checked them. Ones not yet available may be listed as coming soon. If only one language is available, there is nothing to choose.

## Your records are not translated

Clinic notes, results and medicine names stay exactly as your clinic wrote them. You can ask Medbot in Chat to explain any of them in your language.

Doctor explanations of results may be published in more than one language. See [Your doctor's explanation](/patient/results#your-doctors-explanation).

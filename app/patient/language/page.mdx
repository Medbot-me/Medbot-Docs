# Language

Medbot Health is available in **English**, **Chinese** (简体中文), **Malay** (Bahasa Melayu) and **Tamil** (தமிழ்). Your choice is saved on your account, so it follows you to every device and to the [mobile app](/patient/mobile-app).

## Choosing your language

You can choose:

- **When you sign in or create an account.** The language picker is at the top of the page. It is also on an invite, a clinic link and a record hand-over link.
- **The first time you use Medbot Health.** If you have not chosen, Medbot uses your browser's or phone's language, when it is one of the four, and saves it.
- **At any time.** Go to **Profile**, then **Preferences**, then **Language**. The row shows your current language.

On the **Language** page:

1. Under **App language**, pick a language. The one in use shows **Current**.
2. Select **Switch to** that language.

If your clinic's Medbot Health offers fewer languages, only those are listed, and any others may show as coming soon.

## What follows your language

Everything Medbot Health says to you:

- menus, buttons and messages on every screen, including error messages
- Ask Medbot's answers, even when your record is in English. See [Ask Medbot](/patient/ask-medbot).
- red-flag emergency advice
- notifications, texts, phone notifications, emails and sign-in codes

In Tamil, Ask Medbot answers with set wording only, not with written answers. Emergency advice is given in Tamil.

## Your records are not translated

Clinic notes, results and medicine names stay exactly as your clinic wrote them. You can use Ask Medbot to explain any of them in your language.

Doctor explanations of results may be published in more than one language. See [Your doctor's explanation](/patient/results#your-doctors-explanation).

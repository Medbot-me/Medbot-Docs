# Medical History

The medical history quick start helps you add your long-term conditions, medicines, allergies and old documents to your health record. You can skip any step and come back later.

Everything you add is labelled as reported by you until a clinician confirms it.

## Where to start

- On Home, the **Add your medical history** card. It shows while your record has no conditions, medicines or allergies. Choose **Start**, or **Review and add** if items are waiting ("You have N items waiting for your review."), or **Not now**.
- Or go to **Profile**, then **Preferences**, then **Medical history** (**Add or update**).

The first screen lists four steps: **Conditions**, **Medicines and allergies**, **Documents** and **Review**. Select **Start adding history**, or **Skip for now**. Use **Save and exit** on any step to stop.

If you would rather have someone do it, see [Record Services](/patient/record-services).

## Step 1: Conditions

1. Under "Any long-term conditions?", search, or pick from common ones such as High blood pressure, Type 2 diabetes or Asthma.
2. Add the year diagnosed (or Not sure), and mark it **Ongoing** or **Resolved**.
3. Select **Add a past surgery or hospital stay** if needed.
4. If none apply, select "I have no long-term conditions".
5. Select **Next: medicines**.

## Step 2: Medicines and allergies

1. Under "Medicines you take", include supplements and traditional medicine.
2. Photograph the labels, one photo per box or bottle. Names are kept exactly as printed and show **From label**. Or select **Type a medicine**. See [Typing a medicine](#typing-a-medicine).
3. Under allergies, answer "Are you allergic to any medicine, food or anything else?" with Yes, No or Not sure. For a yes, say what you are allergic to and what happened. Select **Add another allergy** for more.
4. Select **Next: documents**.

### Typing a medicine

As you type in **Medicine name**, Medbot suggests matching products registered in Singapore (from the Health Sciences Authority's list). Use the arrow keys or tap to choose one. A suggestion with a similar spelling, rather than the name you typed, says "Similar spelling. Check this is your medicine." Medbot never puts a look-alike name in for you.

"Not on the list? Just keep typing and we will save what you typed."

Then fill in what you know:

- **Strength (optional)**. A product you chose from the list fills this in.
- **Dose and how often (optional)**.
- **Route (how you use it)**, for example **By mouth (oral)**, **Breathed in (inhaler)** or **Skin patch**. A product from the list suggests its usual route. Choose **Not sure** if you do not know.

If you change the name after choosing a product, the strength and route that came with it are cleared.

## Step 3: Documents

Select **Take a photo** or **Choose files**. Use PDF, JPG or PNG files. Medbot reads each one and shows how many items it found. If a file cannot be read, it tells you why, for example because it is too blurry or not a medical document.

For a document of several pages, choose **Several pages** to send the photos as one document. If a photo looks blurry or cut off, Medbot suggests taking it again.

When Medbot finds lab values in a document, it shows **Check N values**. Select it to compare them with the paper and save them. See [Scanned Lab Reports](/patient/lab-reports).

Nothing is added until you approve it on the next screen. The original files are kept under **Documents** in your record. Select **Next: review**.

## Step 4: Review

On **Check before it is added**, untick anything that is wrong. If two documents show different doses, choose which you take now, or pick "Neither, or I'm not sure". Each item shows where it came from: you entered it, your label photo, or an uploaded document.

Select "Add N items to my record". Not sure what is right? Select "Ask the Medbot team to reconcile it" (a paid service, see [Record Services](/patient/record-services)).

When done, you see "Added to your record" with links to your conditions, medicines and allergies. They also appear under **More from your record** on the [Results](/patient/results#more-from-your-record) tab.

# Health Records

**Health Records** is the patient's own store of [guided care session](/patient/ask-medbot#guided-care-sessions) summaries. It is deliberately separate from [Results & Records](/patient/results), which reads the clinic chart.

Reach it from "More from your record" on the Results tab, or from the link that replaces the composer on an ended session thread.

## What is in it

One entry per **completed** guided care session, holding:

- the session's **summary**
- its **key points**
- its **next steps**
- links back to the session's thread and to the clinic

A session that was cancelled, or swept away as abandoned after sitting idle, writes nothing here.

## Who can see it

Only the patient. These summaries are written to a patient-owned store — they are never written into any clinic's chart and are never shown to CMS staff. The list and each entry are read-only; nothing in the portal edits them.

This separation is the point of the feature: a guided session is a conversation the patient had with Medbot, and its record belongs to them rather than becoming an unreviewed clinical note in a clinic's system.

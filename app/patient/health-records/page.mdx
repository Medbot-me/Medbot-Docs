# Health Records

**Health Records** holds your own summaries from guided care sessions with Medbot. It is separate from [Results & Records](/patient/results), which shows what your clinic recorded.

To open it, select **Session summaries** under "More from your record" on the Results tab. You can also open it from the link on an ended session in [Messages](/patient/messages).

## What is in it

One entry for each finished guided care session, including:

- the **summary**, key points and next steps
- links back to the conversation and the clinic

Entries come from your [pre-visit histories](/patient/pre-visit-history), other guided sessions, and concerns you shared (see [Sharing a concern with a clinic](/patient/ask-medbot#sharing-a-concern-with-a-clinic)).

Filter them with **All**, **Before a visit**, the other session types, or **Escalations**.

A session you cancelled or left unfinished saves nothing.

## Who can see it

You can. Nothing here reaches a clinic unless you choose to send it.

Each entry says where it is:

- **Shared with** a clinic: you sent it, for example a pre-visit history. The clinic's care team can read it.
- **Only in your record**: it stays with you.

These summaries are not written into any clinic's chart until you send them, and then only to the clinic you chose. You cannot edit them in the app.

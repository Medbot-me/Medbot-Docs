# Report a Problem

If something in Medbot Health is not working, looks wrong, or is hard to find, you can tell the Medbot support team. It is for problems with the app. For a question about your care, [message your clinic](/patient/messages) instead.

## Where to find it

- In the menu, under **Help**, select **Report a problem**. On a phone, open the menu from the button at the top left of any tab. On a computer, the menu is the sidebar.
- On **Profile**, under **Help**.
- On a screen that did not load. It says "Something went wrong" and offers **Try again** and **Report this problem**. The report then already names the screen, and **Something isn't working** is picked.

Reporting a problem is on the web only. It is not in the [mobile app](/patient/mobile-app#what-is-only-on-the-web).

## Send a report

The page says "Tell us what went wrong. The Medbot support team reads every report."

1. Choose the kind of problem: **Something isn't working**, **Something looks wrong**, **I can't find something** or **Something else**.
2. Under **What happened**, describe it, for example "I picked a time, tapped Book, and nothing happened." Please do not include medical details. The report goes to Medbot's technical team, not to your clinic.
3. Under **How much is it getting in your way**, choose **I cannot continue**, **It is getting in the way** (the starting choice) or **Just letting you know**.
4. Optionally add a screenshot, a PNG, JPEG or WebP image under 10 MB. Select **Add a screenshot**, or paste an image anywhere in the form, or drop it on the screenshot area. Select **Remove screenshot** to take it off.
5. Select **Send report**. Ctrl or ⌘ and Enter sends it too.

**Send report** stays off until you have picked a kind of problem and described it.

## What is sent

Under **Sent with your report**, the form lists what goes along with your words:

- **Screen**: the page you opened the form from.
- **Device**: your browser and screen size.
- **Reply to**: your account email. The support team may email you if it needs more detail.

Your health records, results and messages are never attached.

## After you send it

The page says **Report sent**: "Thanks for telling us. The support team has it, and we'll email you if we need more detail." It shows a **Reference**, the **Type**, how much it is getting in your way, and the **Status** (**Received**). It also offers **Message your clinic** if you need help with your care while Medbot looks into it, and **Report another problem**.

Reports reach Medbot's platform administrators, who read and triage them. Reports that stop someone from continuing are looked at first.

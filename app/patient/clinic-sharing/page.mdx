# Sharing with Your Clinics

Connecting a clinic copies its records **into** your health record. It does not let the clinic read your chats with Ask Medbot or the results you scanned yourself. What each linked clinic may see of those is up to you, clinic by clinic, on **Sharing with your clinics**.

Go to **Profile**, then **Sharing with your clinics** ("Concerns, chats and scanned results").

> Choose what each clinic you are linked to can see. Nobody monitors your chats in real time, whatever you choose here.

If you have no clinic linked, the page says "You have no clinic linked, so nothing here reaches a clinic."

## Key ideas

| Idea | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Urgent message** | A message Medbot's red-flag screen treats as an emergency. It always goes to your linked clinics, and you are told whether it did. This cannot be turned off. |
| **Concern** | Something Medbot noticed in your chat that a clinician should review, but that is not an emergency. On by default, per clinic. |
| **Your chats** | How much of your Ask Medbot chats a clinic's doctors and nurses may read. **Nothing** by default. |
| **Report** | Whether the topics of your chats are counted, with no names, in the clinic's report. Off by default. |
| **Care program assistant** | Whether a clinic's care program assistant may see your whole record, not only what that clinic holds. Off by default. |
| **Scanned results** | Lab reports you scanned yourself. Each one is shared only if you say so, and only with the clinics you choose. |

Only doctors and nurses at the clinic can read what you share. Receptionists and clinic administrators cannot. Every time a doctor or nurse reads it, that is recorded.

## Each clinic's choices

Each linked clinic has its own card.

### Concerns

> If Medbot notices a concern a clinician should review, &lt;clinic&gt; gets a short summary and your name, date of birth and phone number. Nothing else from your chat. You are told each time, and you can withdraw it.

This switch is on unless you turn it off. See [Concerns from Ask Medbot](/patient/ask-medbot#concerns-from-ask-medbot) for what happens when one is sent.

### Your chats

Choose one:

| Choice | What the clinic's doctors and nurses can read |
| --- | --- |
| **Nothing** | None of your chats. This is the default. |
| **Summaries and topics** | An AI-written summary and the topics of chats from now on. Not your words. |
| **Chats, from now on** | Everything in your chats from now on, plus summaries and topics. Not what you wrote before. |
| **Chats, including past chats** | Everything in your chats, including those you already wrote, plus summaries and topics. |

Choosing more than **Nothing** asks you to confirm first: "Share with &lt;clinic&gt;?" It tells you every read is recorded and that nobody watches your chats, so never rely on this for anything urgent. Select **I agree**, or **Cancel**.

You can stop at any time by choosing less. That stops further reading, but cannot undo what someone has already read. The card shows "Since" and the date your current choice started.

A summary is written about thirty minutes after a shared chat goes quiet. It is made from your own words only, never from Medbot's replies.

### Count my chats in the clinic's report

> Topics from your chats, such as questions about fasting or results delays, are counted with other patients'. &lt;clinic&gt; sees a topic only if at least 10 patients raised it, with no names and no quotes. General questions may be rewritten so they point at nobody, for the clinic to answer.

Off unless you turn it on. A question the clinic answers this way may later be quoted to patients as an [approved answer](/patient/ask-medbot#where-an-answer-comes-from).

### Let the care program assistant see your whole record

> With this off, the assistant sees only what &lt;clinic&gt; holds. With it on, it also sees what other clinics recorded and what you entered yourself. &lt;clinic&gt;'s staff can read this assistant's drafts.

Off unless you turn it on. It applies only to a clinic that runs a care program you are in.

## Scanned results

The **Scanned results** section lists the lab reports you scanned. See [Scanned Lab Reports](/patient/lab-reports).

> Each scan is shared only if you say so, and only with that clinic. They see it marked as scanned and checked by you, with the original. It never counts as a verified result.

Tick **Share with &lt;clinic&gt;?** under a report to share it. If sharing failed, the report says so, with **Try again**. Deleting a report's original stops every share of it.

## One chat at a time

Each Ask Medbot conversation has a **Who can see this chat** card. It says, for each clinic, whether it sees nothing, a summary and its topics, or the chat and a summary, and from which date. It shows the summary the clinic can read, if one has been written.

- Select **Don't share this chat** to keep that one chat from every clinic, whatever your clinic choices. A message in it that looks urgent still goes to your clinics.
- Select **Share this chat again** to undo it.
- **Manage sharing** opens this page.

## Who can change these choices

Only you. A caregiver can read the page but not change it: "Only the patient, or a parent or guardian of a child without an account, can change this."

The exception is a person who has no Medbot account of their own. For them, a parent or guardian the clinic has confirmed, or the person who created their profile, can choose, until the person turns 21. The page then says:

> You are choosing for someone else. If they get their own account, these choices stop counting and they choose for themselves.

When someone takes over their own record, every earlier choice resets to the defaults. See [Taking over your own record](/patient/family#taking-over-your-own-record).

# Profile & Preferences

The **Profile** tab covers who the patient is, how to reach them, which clinics they are connected to, and how those clinics may contact them.

On a phone, open **Profile** from your avatar at the top of the screen. On a computer, it is the last item in the sidebar.

## Personal details

Your first name, last name and date of birth. Change them and select **Save**. You see "Saved." when it worked. Your email and avatar come from your Medbot account.

## Contact details and home address

Your **Contact details** and **Home address** are shown here. Keep the contact details on your Medbot account up to date so the clinic can reach you.

## Connected clinics

The per-clinic list — its status, the consent card for a link still awaiting confirmation, "stop syncing" for an active one, and "connect another clinic". This has its own page: [Connected Clinics](/patient/connected-clinics).

## People who can access your record

The **People who can access your record** card lists every caregiver who can use the portal for the patient — family members or carers with their own Medbot accounts — and any invites still waiting to be accepted. From here the patient can **Add a caregiver**, switch someone between read-only and **Allow changes**, or **Remove** them, including caregivers the clinic added.

A patient who is themselves a caregiver also sees **People you care for**: "Records you can see because someone gave you access." Each entry has **View their record** and **Stop**.

Both cards are covered in [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers).

## How the clinic may contact you

One switch each for **Email**, **SMS** and **WhatsApp**. Each answers one question: may the clinic reach you this way for routine messages?

The card says: "Appointment reminders and result notifications only. We never send clinical details by SMS." Your switches cover appointment reminders and the delivery of your own documents.

Clinics may keep other permissions, which staff manage. Turning a channel off here does not change those.

## Preferences

The **Preferences** card has four rows:

| Row | Where it goes |
| --- | --- |
| **Medical history** (**Add or update**) | [Medical History](/patient/medical-history) |
| **Find a clinic or service** | [Find Care](/patient/find-care) |
| **Record services** | [Record Services](/patient/record-services) |
| **Language** | [Language](/patient/language) |

## When a caregiver is viewing

A caregiver acting for the patient sees the patient's details here. The connected-clinics and caregiver cards are replaced by a single **Clinics and caregivers** card, because only the patient can connect clinics or choose who can see their record. A caregiver who can make changes can update the patient's address. Everything else on this screen is the patient's alone.

## Sign out

**Sign out** is near the bottom of the screen. Below it: "For anything urgent, please call the clinic or go to A&E."

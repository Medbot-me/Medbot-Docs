# Profile & Preferences

The **Profile** screen covers who the patient is, how to reach them, which clinics they are connected to, and how those clinics may contact them.

Open **Profile** from the menu, under **Account**. On a phone, open the menu from the button at the top left of any tab. On a computer, the menu is the sidebar.

## Personal details

Your first name, last name and date of birth are shown here, with your national ID (NRIC, FIN or passport number) partly hidden, for example "S••••567A". The first time you set up your account you type your name and date of birth. After that they are locked. Your national ID cannot be changed here: "Ask your clinic to correct this." Your email and avatar come from your Medbot account.

Only you can ask for a change. A caregiver cannot.

### Change your name or date of birth

A change is checked by a clinic against your ID, so it needs a clinic you are connected to. If you have none, the form says "A linked clinic has to check this" and offers **Connect a clinic**.

1. Select **Request a change**. The page says "Change your name, your date of birth, or both. A clinic you are linked to checks a photo of your ID before anything changes."
2. Enter your **New first / given name** and **New last / family name**, or your **New date of birth**, or both.
3. Under **Why are you changing it?**, choose "It changed by law (for example marriage or a deed poll)" or "It was entered wrongly".
4. Choose the **Document you will photograph**: **NRIC**, **FIN card** or **Passport**.
5. Under **Photo of the front of the document**, select **Add a photo or file**. Use a JPEG, PNG, HEIC or PDF up to 10 MB. Add 1 or 2 files.
6. Tick the consent: the photo is shown only to the clinics you are linked to, for this check. It is deleted once the request is decided or cancelled, or after 30 days if nobody has checked it.
7. Select **Send request**.

You can have one request open at a time. While it is open, the page says "Waiting for &lt;clinic&gt; to check". Select **Cancel request** (and **Yes, cancel it**) to withdraw it, which deletes your photo.

A staff member at any clinic you are linked to compares your photo with your ID and approves or rejects the change. The first decision applies for every clinic. You get a notification, and Profile shows **Change approved** with the clinic and date, or **Change not approved** with the clinic's reason. You can then send a new request. Your photo is deleted once the request is decided. Medbot keeps who decided, when, which document was checked, the old and new details and the outcome.

An approved change updates your health record in Medbot. The clinic that checked it may also update its own chart. Other clinics you are linked to are not changed by this.

## Contact details and home address

Your **Contact details** and **Home address** are shown here. Keep the contact details on your Medbot account up to date so the clinic can reach you.

**Email on file** and **Mobile on file** show what your clinic records hold. You cannot type over them. Instead, change the email or mobile number on your Medbot account, and Medbot copies it to your clinic records. It only ever copies an email or number you have confirmed.

### Change your email or mobile number

1. Under **Contact details**, select **Manage Medbot account**. The **Your Medbot account** window opens and shows your current email and mobile number.
2. Under **Email** or **Mobile number**, enter the new one. Include the country code for a mobile number. Select **Send code**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code sent to the **new** email or number, and select **Confirm**. You see "Updated."
4. Medbot then copies your new details to your clinic records. You see "Your contact details are up to date."

To copy your details again at any time, select **Sync from Medbot account**. If nothing has changed, you see "Your Medbot account already matches your chart."

Only you can change these. A caregiver cannot.

## Connected clinics

The per-clinic list — its status, the consent card for a link still awaiting confirmation, "stop syncing" for an active one, and "connect another clinic". This has its own page: [Connected Clinics](/patient/connected-clinics).

## Sharing with your clinics

The **Sharing with your clinics** row ("Concerns, chats and scanned results") sits under the connected clinics. It sets, clinic by clinic, whether concerns from Ask Medbot are sent, how much of your chats a clinic may read, whether it may see your medical history, and which scanned results it sees. See [Sharing with Your Clinics](/patient/clinic-sharing).

## Family and sharing

The **Family and sharing** row opens [Family](/patient/family). There you:

- see and change **People who can access your record**: your caregivers and invites. See [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers).
- see **People you care for**, create a profile for a child or family member, and hand a profile over.

## How the clinic may contact you

One switch each for **Email**, **SMS** and **WhatsApp**. Each answers one question: may the clinic reach you this way for routine messages?

The card says: "Appointment reminders and result notifications only. We never send clinical details by SMS." Your switches cover appointment reminders and the delivery of your own documents.

Clinics may keep other permissions, which staff manage. Turning a channel off here does not change those.

## When a caregiver is viewing

A caregiver acting for the patient sees the patient's details here. The connected-clinics card and the sharing and family rows are replaced by a single **Clinics and caregivers** card, because only the patient can connect clinics or choose who can see their record. A caregiver who can make changes can update the patient's address. Everything else on this screen is the patient's alone.

## Help

**Report a problem** is at the bottom of Profile, above **Delete account**. It tells the Medbot support team when something in the app goes wrong. See [Report a Problem](/patient/report-a-problem).

## Delete your account

**Delete account** is near the bottom of Profile, above **Sign out**. It opens **Delete your account**, which explains what goes and what stays before you decide.

Deleting your Medbot account removes, for good:

- your health record in Medbot: what you logged, your medical history and documents, and the records copied from your clinics
- your Ask Medbot conversations, your notifications, concerns you have not sent, and your contact requests to providers not on Medbot
- your sign-in, on the web and in the app. Caregivers lose access to your record, and you lose access to anyone you care for.

What stays:

- Your clinics keep their own records of your care, including anything you sent or shared with them.
- Receipts for record services you paid for.

This cannot be undone. To use Medbot again, you would create a new account.

### Before you delete

If something needs settling first, the page lists it under **Before you delete your account**, and you cannot delete yet:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "This sign-in is also a clinic staff account…" | Ask your organisation's administrator to remove you first, or contact Medbot. |
| "Record service … hasn't started. Cancel it first…" | Select it and cancel the request. A paid one is refunded in full. See [Record Services](/patient/record-services). |
| "Record service … is under way…" | Wait until it is finished, then delete your account. |

### Delete it

1. Tick "I understand this deletes my Medbot account and can't be undone."
2. Select **Delete my account**. In the [mobile app](/patient/mobile-app), confirm with **Delete**.
3. You are signed out and see "Your Medbot account has been deleted."

For your security, you must have signed in within the last 10 minutes. If it was longer ago, the page says "For your security, confirm it's you before deleting your account." Select **Sign in**. You are signed out, and once you sign in again you come back to this page. Then delete your account.

Only the account holder can delete their account. A caregiver acting for someone does not see **Delete account**.

## Sign out

**Sign out** is near the bottom of the screen. Below it: "For anything urgent, please call the clinic or go to A&E." With no clinic connected, it says "For anything urgent, please call emergency services or go to A&E."

# Profile & Preferences

The **Profile** tab covers who the patient is, how to reach them, which clinics they are connected to, and how those clinics may contact them.

On a phone, open **Profile** from your avatar at the top of the screen. On a computer, it is the last item in the sidebar.

## Personal details

Your first name, last name and date of birth. Change them and select **Save**. You see "Saved." when it worked. Your email and avatar come from your Medbot account.

## Contact details and home address

Your **Contact details** and **Home address** are shown here. Keep the contact details on your Medbot account up to date so the clinic can reach you.

**Email on file** and **Mobile on file** show what your clinic records hold. You cannot type over them. Instead, change the email or mobile number on your Medbot account, and Medbot copies it to your clinic records. It only ever copies an email or number you have confirmed.

### Change your email or mobile number

1. Under **Contact details**, select **Manage Medbot account**. The **Your Medbot account** window opens and shows your current email and mobile number.
2. Under **Email** or **Mobile number**, enter the new one. Include the country code for a mobile number. Select **Send code**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code sent to the **new** email or number, and select **Confirm**. You see "Updated."
4. Medbot then copies your new details to your clinic records. You see "Your contact details are up to date."

To copy your details again at any time, select **Sync from Medbot account**. If nothing has changed, you see "Your Medbot account already matches your chart."

Only you can change these. A caregiver cannot.

## Connected clinics

The per-clinic list — its status, the consent card for a link still awaiting confirmation, "stop syncing" for an active one, and "connect another clinic". This has its own page: [Connected Clinics](/patient/connected-clinics).

## People who can access your record

The **People who can access your record** card lists every caregiver who can use the portal for the patient — family members or carers with their own Medbot accounts — and any invites still waiting to be accepted. From here the patient can **Add a caregiver**, switch someone between read-only and **Allow changes**, or **Remove** them, including caregivers the clinic added.

A patient who is themselves a caregiver also sees **People you care for**: "Records you can see because someone gave you access." Each entry has **View their record** and **Stop**.

Both cards are covered in [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers).

## How the clinic may contact you

One switch each for **Email**, **SMS** and **WhatsApp**. Each answers one question: may the clinic reach you this way for routine messages?

The card says: "Appointment reminders and result notifications only. We never send clinical details by SMS." Your switches cover appointment reminders and the delivery of your own documents.

Clinics may keep other permissions, which staff manage. Turning a channel off here does not change those.

## When a caregiver is viewing

A caregiver acting for the patient sees the patient's details here. The connected-clinics and caregiver cards are replaced by a single **Clinics and caregivers** card, because only the patient can connect clinics or choose who can see their record. A caregiver who can make changes can update the patient's address. Everything else on this screen is the patient's alone.

## Delete your account

**Delete account** is near the bottom of Profile, above **Sign out**. It opens **Delete your account**, which explains what goes and what stays before you decide.

Deleting your Medbot account removes, for good:

- your health record in Medbot: what you logged, your medical history and documents, and the records copied from your clinics
- your Ask Medbot conversations, your notifications, and concerns or referral requests you have not sent
- your sign-in, on the web and in the app. Caregivers lose access to your record, and you lose access to anyone you care for.

What stays:

- Your clinics keep their own records of your care, including anything you sent or shared with them.
- Receipts for record services you paid for.

This cannot be undone. To use Medbot again, you would create a new account.

### Before you delete

If something needs settling first, the page lists it under **Before you delete your account**, and you cannot delete yet:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "This sign-in is also a clinic staff account…" | Ask your organisation's administrator to remove you first, or contact Medbot. |
| "Record service … hasn't started. Cancel it first…" | Select it and cancel the request. A paid one is refunded in full. See [Record Services](/patient/record-services). |
| "Record service … is under way…" | Wait until it is finished, then delete your account. |

### Delete it

1. Tick "I understand this deletes my Medbot account and can't be undone."
2. Select **Delete my account**. In the [mobile app](/patient/mobile-app), confirm with **Delete**.
3. You are signed out and see "Your Medbot account has been deleted."

For your security, you must have signed in within the last 10 minutes. If it was longer ago, the page says "For your security, confirm it's you before deleting your account." Select **Sign in**. You are signed out, and once you sign in again you come back to this page. Then delete your account.

Only the account holder can delete their account. A caregiver acting for someone does not see **Delete account**.

## Sign out

**Sign out** is near the bottom of the screen. Below it: "For anything urgent, please call the clinic or go to A&E."

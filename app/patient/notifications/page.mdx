# Notifications

The **Notifications** tab lists what Medbot Health wants you to know about, in one place. It is the fifth tab, after **Home**, **Chats**, **Log** and **Visits**. On a phone it is in the bottom bar. On a computer it is across the top. A red badge on the tab shows how many notifications you have not read, up to "9+".

## The list

Notifications are in two groups: **Today** and **Earlier**. Each shows what it is about, a line of detail, and the time it arrived: for example "8:12 am", or "10 Nov" for an earlier day. A new one is in bold, with a dot.

- Select a notification to open the screen it is about. It is then marked as read.
- Select **Mark all read** to clear them all at once.

With none, the tab says "No notifications yet."

If you care for someone, the tab shows **their** notifications while you are viewing their record. See [Caregivers & Family Access](/patient/caregivers#notifications).

## What you may be told

| Notification | Why you get it |
| --- | --- |
| A session "encounter waiting" or "session waiting", for example **History taking encounter waiting** | Your care team or clinic has a guided care session for you. |
| A session "encounter completed" | A guided care session has finished. |
| **It is your turn** | Reception called you, and you turned on **Notify me when it is my turn**. See [Today's visit](/patient/visits#getting-told-when-it-is-your-turn). |
| **Your visit summary is ready** | Your clinician sent the summary of today's visit. See [Your visit summary](/patient/visits#your-visit-summary). |
| **Your care team replied** | A member of your care team replied in one of your sessions. |
| **Tell Medbot about your visit** | You booked a visit with a service that asks a few questions first. See [Pre-visit History](/patient/pre-visit-history). |
| **Your visit is coming up** | A reminder to answer those questions. |
| **Review your pre-visit history** | Medbot finished your questions. Check them, then choose whether to send them to the clinic. |
| **Your clinic saw your red flag** | The clinic has seen a warning sign you told Medbot about. |
| **Sent to** a clinic | A concern was sent to a clinic. See [Concerns from Ask Medbot](/patient/ask-medbot#concerns-from-ask-medbot). |
| A clinic replied (**&lt;clinic&gt; replied**) | The clinic answered a concern you shared. |
| **&lt;clinic&gt; accepted your request** or **&lt;clinic&gt; can't take your request** | A clinic you asked to see you with **Connect** has answered. If it accepted, it has connected your account to your record there and will offer you a time in [Appointments](/patient/visits#appointments). See [Ask a clinic to see you](/patient/connected-clinics#ask-a-clinic-to-see-you). |
| **&lt;clinic&gt; replied about your health plan**, **&lt;clinic&gt; proposed a health plan for you** or **&lt;clinic&gt; suggested goals for your health plan** | A clinic reviewed the plan you sent, or proposed one after a visit. See [Health Plan](/patient/health-plan). |
| **Your doctor has explained your results** (or **Your doctor updated the explanation of your results**) | A doctor-approved explanation is ready. See [Your doctor's explanation](/patient/results#your-doctors-explanation). |
| **Your medical history was reviewed** | A doctor at a clinic you share your history with coded, confirmed, removed or added conditions you reported. See [A clinic's review](/patient/medical-history#a-clinics-review). |
| **Your change of details was approved** or **Your change of details was not approved** | A clinic checked your ID for a change of name or date of birth. See [Change your name or date of birth](/patient/profile#change-your-name-or-date-of-birth). |
| Caregiver access changes | Someone was given or lost access to your record, or your access to someone else's changed. See [Caregivers](/patient/caregivers#notifications). |

## Phone notifications

The [mobile app](/patient/mobile-app#notifications) can also send you a notification on your phone. It shows only a title, never the details.

# Find Care

**Find a clinic or service** helps you find clinics, connect to the ones on Medbot, or ask to be referred to one that is not.

Open it from **Profile**, then **Preferences**, then **Find a clinic or service**. Only the patient can use it, not a caregiver.

## Search

The page lists clinics on Medbot first, then others Medbot knows of. Search by name, area or specialty and select **Search**.

Each result shows the name, address and up to three specialties, with a badge: **Connected**, **On Medbot** or **Not on Medbot yet**. The button depends on the badge:

| Button | When | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Connect** | A clinic on Medbot | Starts [connecting](/patient/connected-clinics) |
| **Request an appointment** | A clinic you are connected to | Opens the [appointment form](/patient/visits#booking-or-asking-for-an-appointment) |
| **View** | A clinic not on Medbot yet | Opens its details |

If nothing matches, you see "No clinics match your search."

## Ask to be referred

For a clinic that is not on Medbot yet, select **View**. The **Ask to be referred** page shows what the clinic offers and its phone or website, if known. You can also contact them yourself.

1. Choose a **Service**, or leave "The clinic, any service".
2. Check your name, email and phone. Give at least one way for the clinic to reach you.
3. Under "Why would you like to be referred?", say why.
4. Tick the consent box.
5. Select **Send my request**.

Medbot passes your request on to the clinic by email or phone, and they contact you directly. Only your name, contact details and reason are shared. Your health record, results and messages are not.

## Follow your requests

Select **Your referral requests**. Each request shows the service, the clinic, the date and a status: **Received**, **Passed to the clinic**, **In touch**, **Closed** or **Withdrawn**. Select **Withdraw** to cancel a request.

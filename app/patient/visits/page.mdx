# Visits & Appointments

The **Appointments** tab is the fourth tab, on a phone and on a computer. It covers every appointment and request, and today's visit:

- [today's visit](#todays-visit): registering when you arrive and following the queue, at the top of the tab on the day
- [upcoming appointments](#upcoming-appointments), with [cancel](#cancelling-an-appointment) and [change time](#changing-the-time)
- [requests you send to the clinic](#booking-or-asking-for-an-appointment), and [where every request stands](#your-requests)
- [appointments your clinic offers you](#offers-from-your-clinic)
- [the check-in before an offered appointment](#pre-visit-check-in)
- [telling Medbot about your visit](#telling-medbot-about-your-visit) before a booked one
- [past and cancelled appointments](#past-and-cancelled-appointments), and [booking again](#book-again)

**Visits**, your record of past visits, is in the menu, under **Care**, with a stethoscope icon:

- [past visits and their summaries](#visit-list-and-summaries)
- [after your visit](#after-your-visit): the summary your clinician sends and the steps they arranged

To check in by scanning a QR code, or to show the front desk who you are, see [Clinic Card](/patient/clinic-card).

## Visit list and summaries

Open **Visits** from the menu, under **Care**. It lists your completed visits, newest first. Each row shows **Clinic visit** and the date. With no visits yet, it says "No visits recorded".

Above the list, an **Appointments** card ("Upcoming appointments and your requests") opens the [Appointments](#appointments) tab. Today's visit is no longer on this screen. It is at the top of **Appointments**.

Opening a visit shows its **Visit summary**:

- the clinic it came from, for example "From Sunrise Clinic". If that clinic no longer syncs your record, it says "No longer updated by" and the clinic's name.
- **Diagnosis**, or "No diagnosis recorded"
- **Medications**: name, dose and instructions where available, and when each was prescribed. Or "No medications recorded".
- **Billing**: the invoice's status and total, or "No invoice available"

Nothing here can be edited. If something on a visit looks wrong, [message the clinic](/patient/messages). Do not try to correct it from the portal.

## Today's visit

On the day of an appointment, the **Today's visit** card appears at the top of **Appointments**, and on **Home**. Its second line tells you what to do next:

| Card says | Where you are |
| --- | --- |
| **Register your arrival** | You have not registered yet. |
| **Track your queue number** | You are checked in and waiting. |
| **It is your turn** | Reception has called you to a room. |
| **View your visit summary** | Your visit is complete. |

The card shows your earliest appointment of the day (Singapore time) at any connected clinic, and names its location. Teleconsults are not included. If you open the screen with no appointment today, it says "No appointment today", with a link to **Request an appointment**.

The card also opens your [clinic card](/patient/clinic-card), which you can show at the front desk instead of registering here.

### Registering your arrival

When you reach the clinic, open **Today's visit**. The card says "Your appointment today at" and the time. Under it you see your doctor, the clinic and the location.

1. Under **Paying with**, you can choose how you expect to pay: **Pay at the counter**, **Insurance / MediSave on file**, **Subsidy scheme** or **Company / corporate account**. The first is already chosen.
2. Register, as your clinic allows:
   - **One tap.** Select **Register and get my queue number**. Only at a clinic that lets you register from anywhere.
   - **Your location.** Select **Register my arrival**. Medbot asks for your location ("Location found (accurate to about" a distance) and checks you are at the clinic. Then select **Register and get my queue number**. This is what most clinics ask for.
   - **The code at the counter.** Select **Scan or enter the code at the counter instead**, enter the 6-digit **Code from the counter**, and select **Register and get my queue number**. Select **Use my location instead** to go back.

One step checks you in for this appointment and registers today's visit. If the clinic shows a check-in QR code at the counter, the card also says "You can also scan the QR code at the counter with your phone's camera." See [Check in at the clinic](/patient/clinic-card#check-in-at-the-clinic).

If Medbot could not get your location, it says "Could not get your location. Use the code at the counter instead.", or asks you to allow location for Medbot in your settings. If the clinic has not set up self check-in, the card says "Self check-in is not set up for this clinic yet. Please register at the counter." If you are too far away or the code is wrong, the screen shows the clinic's message. If it does not work, register at the counter.

### Following the queue

Once you are registered, **Today's visit** becomes a live queue tracker. It refreshes by itself every few seconds while the screen is open, so you do not need to reload it.

- **Your queue number**, in large type.
- **Ahead of you**: how many people are before you.
- **Estimated wait**, for example "About 10–18 min", or "You're next". It is an estimate, not a promise.
- **Now serving**: the numbers being seen now.

A panel tells you where to go, for example **Waiting area**: "Please take a seat. We will call you when it is your turn."

When reception calls you, the screen changes to a banner: **It is your turn**, with the room to go to, for example "Please proceed to Consult Room 2."

Below that, a step list shows how far through the visit you are. It starts with **Registered**. If the service has its own visit steps, they follow, and the current one says "Happening now". Otherwise you see **Consultation**, **Investigations** (only if tests or procedures were ordered today), **Payment and medicines** and **Visit summary**.

### Getting told when it is your turn

While you wait, you can turn on **Notify me when it is my turn**. When reception calls you, you get a notification in the portal and a text message (SMS): "It is your turn. Please proceed to" and the room.

### Sessions your clinician arranged

If your clinician arranged a guided care session during the visit, it is listed under **Sessions your clinician arranged**. Select **Start**, or **Continue** for one you have begun. It opens in [Messages](/patient/messages).

## After your visit

### Your visit summary

When your visit is complete, your clinician may send you a summary. You get a notification, **Your visit summary is ready**. The clinic may also send a text message. You can also open it from **Today's visit** with **View your visit summary**.

**Your visit summary** shows who sent it and when, then:

- **What we found**
- **Medicines started**, with dose and instructions
- **Next steps**. A follow-up has a **Book** link, which opens the appointment request form. A session has a **Continue** link, which opens it in Messages.
- **Billing**, where the clinic has recorded it

At the bottom, **Ask Medbot about this visit** opens [Ask Medbot](/patient/ask-medbot).

This summary is separate from the visit's entry in the visit list. That entry fills in once the clinic's record syncs to the portal.

### Steps from your visit

At the end of a consultation, the doctor may confirm further steps for you. For about a week after the visit, these show on Home under **Steps from your visit**, with the service and visit date.

- A step you can do in the portal, such as a guided care session, has a **Start** button.
- Other steps show their status: **To do**, **In progress**, **Done** or **Not needed**.

The card disappears once nothing is left to do.

## Appointments

**Appointments** is one place for every appointment and every request. It is the fourth tab. You can also open it from the **Appointments** card on **Visits**. On **Home**, an **Appointments** card shows your next appointment and how many requests need your reply or are waiting on the clinic. It appears only when there is something to show.

The page has, in this order:

1. **Today's visit**, only on a day you have an appointment. See [Today's visit](#todays-visit).
2. **From your clinic**: links waiting for you to confirm, and appointments the clinic has offered. See [Offers from your clinic](#offers-from-your-clinic).
3. **Upcoming**: your next appointments.
4. **Before your visit**: questions to answer first. See [Before your visit](#before-your-visit).
5. **Your requests**: every request you have made.
6. **Past and cancelled**: the last year.

The **Request an appointment** button at the top starts a new request.

If Medbot could not reach one of your clinics, a notice says so: "We could not reach &lt;clinic&gt; just now, so some of your appointments may be missing."

### Upcoming appointments

**Upcoming** lists the appointments you have booked or the clinic has booked for you, at every clinic you are connected to and for your services. Each card shows the date, who you will see, the clinic or service and where, the reason, and a status: **Booked**, or **Change requested** if you have asked to move it. A follow-up after a visit says **Follow-up visit**. With none, it says "No upcoming appointments."

### Cancelling an appointment

Select **Cancel appointment** on a card. Confirm with **Cancel appointment** again, or choose **Keep appointment**. You can add a **Reason** ("Reason (optional)"). The clinic is told straight away and the time can be offered to someone else.

You can cancel online until the clinic's cutoff, which the card shows: "You can cancel online until" a date and time. Each clinic sets its own cutoff, from 0 to 168 hours before the appointment, and 24 hours if it has not chosen. After it, the card says "Online cancellation has closed. To cancel, call the clinic on" the clinic's number. If you have already registered your arrival, the card says "Speak to the front desk to make any change."

An appointment you booked with a service can be cancelled the same way, with a plain confirmation: "Cancel your appointment on" the date. A caregiver needs "Can make changes" access.

### Changing the time

Select **Change time** on a card. The page, **Change appointment time**, shows the current appointment.

- **At a clinic.** Tell the clinic when suits you better: choose a **Preferred date** and **Time of day**, add a reason if you like, and select **Send request**. Your current appointment stays booked until the clinic confirms the new time. The card says "You asked to move this appointment. It stays booked until the clinic confirms a new time." The clinic can confirm, suggest another time, or decline. If it suggests one, you accept or decline it, as for any [proposed time](#responding-to-a-proposed-time).
- **With a service.** Pick a new time from its open times, then select **Move appointment**. It moves as soon as you confirm.

Under **Other options**, **Cancel this appointment instead** cancels it, and "Feeling worse or have a new symptom?" links to **Ask Medbot**.

You can have one open change request for an appointment. If you cancel the appointment, or the clinic cancels it or marks it as missed, the request closes.

### Past and cancelled appointments

**Past and cancelled** lists the last year, newest first. Each shows how it ended: **Attended**, **Missed**, **Past**, **Cancelled by you** or **Cancelled by the clinic**. An appointment that is over with nothing recorded is **Past**, never **Missed**. For an attended visit, **Visit summary** opens it. With none, it says "No past or cancelled appointments in the last year."

### Book again

Select **Book again** on a past or cancelled appointment to ask for another one like it. The **Book again** page shows the **Previous appointment**.

- **At a clinic**, it is a request like a [general appointment](#booking-or-asking-for-an-appointment): choose a preferred date and time of day, and the clinic confirms or suggests another time.
- **With a service**, pick a time from its open times.

Before booking again after a visit that was completed, Medbot checks whether you already have a follow-up. If you do, the page says **You may already have a follow-up booked** and names it: the follow-up linked to that visit, another appointment at the same clinic booked after it, or a request still waiting on the clinic. Choose **Change that appointment instead**, **Yes, I need another appointment**, or **Back to appointments**. If the check itself fails, the page says so, offers **Check again**, and still lets you ask for another appointment.

### Your requests

**Your requests** lists every request you have made and where it stands, so you do not have to remember where you sent it. Each row says its kind, its status, and when you asked. Under the list, a line explains each kind on show:

| Kind | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Appointment request** | Sent to a clinic you are connected to, which confirms a time or suggests another. This also covers a **Change of time** and a **Book again** request. |
| **Request to join a clinic** | Asks a clinic on Medbot to take you on. If it accepts, it offers you a time. See [Ask a clinic to see you](/patient/connected-clinics#ask-a-clinic-to-see-you). Its status is **Awaiting the clinic**, **Accepted** or **Declined**. |
| **Contact request** | Medbot passes your details to a provider that is not on Medbot. They contact you directly, and no appointment is booked here. See [Find Care](/patient/find-care#ask-them-to-contact-you). This was called a referral request. |

You can withdraw an appointment request the clinic has not yet answered, or a contact request that is still open: select **Withdraw**, and confirm "Withdraw this request?" with **Withdraw** or **Keep request**. Closed requests are hidden. Select **Show N closed requests** to see them. With nothing open, the list says "You have no open requests."

A caregiver does not see the person's contact requests here, because they are the signed-in person's own.

### Booking or asking for an appointment

Select **Request an appointment** (or **New request**). The **Request an appointment** form starts with **Clinic**. Pick one of your connected clinics ("Select a clinic").

If the clinic has services you can book, **What do you need?** lists one card for each service, and **I'm not sure** ("Send your request to the clinic. Their team will choose the right service and offer you a time.").

**Book with a service** you are enrolled in. Medbot shows "Finding open times…", then **Pick a time**. Open times are grouped by day, and hovering a time shows who you will see. Add a **Reason for the visit (optional)**, then select **Book appointment**. Your appointment is confirmed as soon as you book it. You can cancel it later from your appointments. If the service has nothing free in the next two weeks, the form says so and suggests asking the clinic instead.

**I'm not sure**, or a clinic with no services to pick. Fill in:

1. **Location**, if the clinic has more than one
2. **Preferred date**
3. **Time of day**: **Morning**, **Afternoon** or **Evening**
4. **Reason for the visit**

Select **Send to** the clinic (or **Submit request**). This is a request, not a confirmed booking: "No time is held yet. The clinic's team reads your note, chooses the service and offers you a time to accept." The clinic answers in the portal.

A link from Medbot can open the form with the clinic and service already chosen, for example **Book an appointment** on a clinic's [Find Care](/patient/find-care#a-clinics-page) page, or on a clinic's reply to a [concern](/patient/ask-medbot#following-a-concern). A service you are not enrolled in becomes a request for that service, which the clinic confirms.

You can also ask [Ask Medbot](/patient/ask-medbot#book-change-or-cancel-an-appointment) to take you to this form.

### Before your visit

If a service you booked asks questions first, a **Before your visit** section on this page has **Start** or **Continue**. See [Telling Medbot about your visit](#telling-medbot-about-your-visit). Do not confuse it with the five-question [check-in](#pre-visit-check-in) that has the same title.

### What happens to a general request

A general request does not book anything until the clinic answers.

Staff review it from the CMS's [Appointments → Booking requests](/cms/front-desk/appointments#responding-to-portal-booking-requests) screen, where a request to change a time or to book again is labelled as such. Each request shows where it stands:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Awaiting the clinic** | Submitted. Staff have not reviewed it yet. |
| **Clinic proposed a new time** | Staff suggested a different time. You need to respond. |
| **Confirmed** | Accepted. A real appointment now exists. For a change of time, the original appointment moves to the new time. |
| **Declined** | Staff turned the request down. |
| **You declined the proposed time** | You turned down the clinic's suggested time. |
| **Expired** | The request lapsed without an answer. |
| **Cancelled** | The request was cancelled. |

### Responding to a proposed time

When staff suggest a different time, the request shows "The clinic proposed" and the date and time, with any note from the clinic. Select **Accept proposed time** or **Decline proposed time**. Accepting creates the appointment at the clinic. Declining closes the request without one.

### A reminder's change link

A reminder from your care program may include a link to change your appointment. It opens **Appointments** with a card: "Need to change an upcoming appointment?"

- **Ask about changing it** asks Medbot for you.
- **Request a new time** opens the request form.

To change a particular appointment, use **Change time** on its card.

## Offers from your clinic

Some services, such as a specialist referral, offer you an appointment instead of waiting for you to ask. Offers appear on **Appointments** under **From your clinic**.

The clinic tells you about an offer by SMS or WhatsApp. That message only carries a link. The date, time and place are only shown in the portal. Home also shows a card, for example "Sunrise Diabetes Service has offered you an appointment" with an **Open** button.

If the clinic invited you and you have not confirmed your link to it yet, you see "Confirm your link with" the clinic "to see the appointment it has offered you." Select **Confirm** first. Only the patient can do this, not a caregiver.

### Answering an offer

The offer shows:

- the service
- the date and time, in Singapore time
- the location and address
- "With" the practitioner's name "or the team"

Select **Confirm this appointment** to accept it.

If the date does not suit you, select **I cannot make this date**. Under **Tell us the days that suit you**:

1. Choose one or more **Days**, Mon to Sat.
2. Choose a **Time of day**: **Mornings**, **Afternoons** or **Any time**.
3. Optionally add a note under **Anything else** (up to 500 characters).
4. Select **Send my preferred days**, or **Keep the offered date** to go back.

Sending your days releases the offered slot. The offer then reads "The clinic is finding another date for you" and repeats the days you chose. You get another message with a link when a new offer is ready.

### Once you confirm

The offer shows **Confirmed**, with a reminder to arrive 15 minutes early and what to bring. It stays on the screen until shortly after the appointment time.

If the service has a pre-visit check-in, a **Complete your check-in** button appears. Once you have sent it, the offer shows "Check-in sent to your care team".

### If something goes wrong

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "Choose at least one day that suits you." | Pick at least one day before sending. |
| "There is no offered slot to confirm." | The offer has changed. Reload the screen. |
| "This offer can no longer be changed." | The offer was already confirmed or replaced. |

## Pre-visit check-in

Before a confirmed appointment, some services ask how you have been, so your doctor can read your answers before the visit. Open it with **Complete your check-in** on the confirmed offer, or from the card on Home: "Tell your doctor how you have been before your visit".

The **Before your visit** screen has five short questions. It takes about two minutes. Today the questions are written for diabetes care:

1. How you have been feeling since your referral
2. Any low sugar episodes in the last two weeks
3. Your lowest and highest fasting readings this week, if you have them (optional, numbers between 1 and 35 mmol/L)
4. Whether you are taking your medicines as prescribed
5. Anything else you want the doctor to know (optional)

A note on the screen reminds you that this is a quick check, not a diagnosis. If you feel unwell now, call the emergency number shown or go to the nearest emergency department.

Select **Send to my care team**. You see "Thank you, your answers were sent". Your doctor reads them before the visit.

If you have not answered, you get a reminder message about 3 days before the visit, and another about 2 days before. Its link opens **Appointments**. If you still have not answered a day before the visit, clinic staff are asked to contact you by phone. You can still answer in the portal until the visit.

If an answer is not accepted, the screen tells you why:

- "Please answer the three questions with choices."
- "Readings should be numbers between 1 and 35, like 6.8."
- "The lowest reading cannot be higher than the highest."

## Telling Medbot about your visit

When you book a visit with a service that asks for it, Medbot invites you to answer a few questions beforehand, so your care team can read them before you arrive. This is covered on its own page: [Pre-visit History](/patient/pre-visit-history).

## Caregivers

A [caregiver](/patient/caregivers) sees the same Appointments and Visits screens for the person they care for.

| | Read-only | Can make changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See visits, visit summaries and steps from a visit | Yes | Yes |
| See the queue tracker | Yes | Yes |
| Turn on **Notify me when it is my turn** | No | Yes |
| Register when you arrive | No | Yes |
| Show the person's [clinic card](/patient/clinic-card#show-a-family-members-card) | No | Yes |
| Book with a service, or cancel a booking or appointment | No | Yes |
| Make an appointment request, ask to change a time or book again, or accept or decline a proposed time | No | Yes |
| Answer an offer from the clinic | No | Yes |
| Fill in a pre-visit check-in | No | Yes |
| Confirm a link to a clinic | No | No, only the patient |

## Notifications

Visits sends these notifications to the [Notifications](/patient/notifications) tab:

- **It is your turn**, if you turned on **Notify me when it is my turn**
- **Your visit summary is ready**

For the full list, see [Notifications](/patient/notifications).

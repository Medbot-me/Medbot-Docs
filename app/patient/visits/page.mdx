# Visits & Appointments

The **Visits** tab covers your visits from start to finish:

- [past visits and their summaries](#visit-list-and-summaries)
- [today's visit](#todays-visit): registering when you arrive and following the queue
- [after your visit](#after-your-visit): the summary your clinician sends and the steps they arranged
- [appointment requests](#appointment-requests) you send to the clinic
- [appointments your clinic offers you](#offers-from-your-clinic)
- [the check-in before an offered appointment](#pre-visit-check-in)
- [telling Medbot about your visit](#telling-medbot-about-your-visit) before a booked one

## Visit list and summaries

The **Visits** screen lists your completed visits, newest first. Each row shows **Clinic visit** and the date. With no visits yet, it says "No visits recorded".

Above the list are two cards:

- **Today's visit**, only on a day you have an appointment. See [Today's visit](#todays-visit).
- **Appointment requests** ("Request a visit or respond to the clinic"). See [Appointment requests](#appointment-requests).

Opening a visit shows its **Visit summary**:

- the clinic it came from, for example "From Sunrise Clinic". If that clinic no longer syncs your record, it says "No longer updated by" and the clinic's name.
- **Diagnosis**, or "No diagnosis recorded"
- **Medications**: name, dose and instructions where available, and when each was prescribed. Or "No medications recorded".
- **Billing**: the invoice's status and total, or "No invoice available"

Nothing here can be edited. If something on a visit looks wrong, [message the clinic](/patient/messages). Do not try to correct it from the portal.

## Today's visit

On the day of an appointment, the **Today's visit** card appears at the top of **Visits**. Its second line tells you what to do next:

| Card says | Where you are |
| --- | --- |
| **Register your arrival** | You have not registered yet. |
| **Track your queue number** | You are checked in and waiting. |
| **It is your turn** | Reception has called you to a room. |
| **View your visit summary** | Your visit is complete. |

The card shows your earliest appointment of the day (Singapore time) at any connected clinic. Teleconsults are not included. If you open the screen with no appointment today, it says "No appointment today", with a link to **Request an appointment**.

### Registering your arrival

When you reach the clinic, open **Today's visit**. It is on **Home** and at the top of **Visits**. The card says "Your appointment today at" and the time. Under it you see your doctor, the clinic and the location.

1. Under **Paying with**, choose how you expect to pay: **Pay at the counter**, **Insurance / MediSave on file**, **Subsidy scheme** or **Company / corporate account**. The first is already chosen.
2. Select **Register and get my queue number**.

That is all. You do not need to scan anything, share your location or enter a code. If something goes wrong, the screen shows the clinic's message. If it does not work, register at the counter.

### Following the queue

Once you are registered, **Today's visit** becomes a live queue tracker. It refreshes by itself every few seconds while the screen is open, so you do not need to reload it.

- **Your queue number**, in large type.
- **Ahead of you**: how many people are before you.
- **Estimated wait**, for example "About 10–18 min", or "You're next". It is an estimate, not a promise.
- **Now serving**: the numbers being seen now.

A panel tells you where to go, for example **Waiting area**: "Please take a seat. We will call you when it is your turn."

When reception calls you, the screen changes to a banner: **It is your turn**, with the room to go to, for example "Please proceed to Consult Room 2."

Below that, a step list shows how far through the visit you are. It starts with **Registered**. If the service has its own visit steps, they follow, and the current one says "Happening now". Otherwise you see **Consultation**, **Investigations** (only if tests or procedures were ordered today), **Payment and medicines** and **Visit summary**.

### Getting told when it is your turn

While you wait, you can turn on **Notify me when it is my turn**. When reception calls you, you get a notification in the portal's bell and a text message (SMS): "It is your turn. Please proceed to" and the room.

### Sessions your clinician arranged

If your clinician arranged a guided care session during the visit, it is listed under **Sessions your clinician arranged**. Select **Start**, or **Continue** for one you have begun. It opens in [Messages](/patient/messages).

## After your visit

### Your visit summary

When your visit is complete, your clinician may send you a summary. You get a notification, **Your visit summary is ready**. The clinic may also send a text message. You can also open it from **Today's visit** with **View your visit summary**.

**Your visit summary** shows who sent it and when, then:

- **What we found**
- **Medicines started**, with dose and instructions
- **Next steps**. A follow-up has a **Book** link, which opens the appointment request form. A session has a **Continue** link, which opens it in Messages.
- **Billing**, where the clinic has recorded it

At the bottom, **Ask Medbot about this visit** opens [Ask Medbot](/patient/ask-medbot).

This summary is separate from the visit's entry in the visit list. That entry fills in once the clinic's record syncs to the portal.

### Steps from your visit

At the end of a consultation, the doctor may confirm further steps for you. For about a week after the visit, these show on Home under **Steps from your visit**, with the service and visit date.

- A step you can do in the portal, such as a guided care session, has a **Start** button.
- Other steps show their status: **To do**, **In progress**, **Done** or **Not needed**.

The card disappears once nothing is left to do.

## Appointment requests

Appointment requests have their own screen. Select the **Appointment requests** card on **Visits**. The **Visits** tab stays highlighted while you are there.

The screen lists your requests. With none, it says "No appointment requests".

### Booking or asking for an appointment

Select **New request**. The **Request an appointment** form starts with **Clinic**. Pick one of your connected clinics ("Select a clinic").

If you are enrolled in services at that clinic, a **Book with** choice appears. Pick **General appointment (clinic confirms)** or one of your services.

**Book with a service.** Medbot shows "Finding open times…", then **Pick a time**. Open times are grouped by day, and hovering a time shows who you will see. Add a **Reason for the visit (optional)**, then select **Book appointment**. Your appointment is confirmed as soon as you book it. You can cancel it later from your appointments. If the service has nothing free in the next two weeks, the form says "This service has no open times in the next two weeks. Ask the clinic for a general appointment instead."

**General appointment.** Fill in:

1. **Location**, if the clinic has more than one
2. **Preferred date**
3. **Time of day**: **Morning**, **Afternoon** or **Evening**
4. **Reason for the visit**

Select **Submit request**. This is a request, not a confirmed booking. The clinic will answer in the portal.

### Booked with your services

Appointments you booked yourself with a service are listed on **Appointment requests** under **Booked with your services**. Each shows the service, the date and time, who you will see, and a **Confirmed** badge. To cancel, select **Cancel**, then **Cancel appointment** (or **Keep it**). A caregiver needs "Can make changes" access to cancel.

### Before your visit

If a service you booked asks questions first, a **Before your visit** section on this screen has **Start** or **Continue**. See [Telling Medbot about your visit](#telling-medbot-about-your-visit). Do not confuse it with the five-question [check-in](#pre-visit-check-in) that has the same title.

### What happens to a general request

A general request does not book anything until the clinic answers.

Staff review it from the CMS's [Appointments → Booking requests](/cms/appointments#responding-to-portal-booking-requests) screen. Each request shows where it stands:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Awaiting the clinic** | Submitted. Staff have not reviewed it yet. |
| **Clinic proposed a new time** | Staff suggested a different time. You need to respond. |
| **Confirmed** | Accepted. A real appointment now exists. |
| **Declined** | Staff turned the request down. |
| **You declined the proposed time** | You turned down the clinic's suggested time. |
| **Expired** | The request lapsed without an answer. |
| **Cancelled** | The request was cancelled. |

### Responding to a proposed time

When staff suggest a different time, the request shows "The clinic proposed" and the date and time, with any note from the clinic. Select **Accept proposed time** or **Decline proposed time**. Accepting creates the appointment at the clinic. Declining closes the request without one.

### Changing an upcoming appointment

A reminder from your care program may include a link to change your appointment. It opens **Appointment requests** with a card: "Need to change an upcoming appointment?"

- **Ask about changing it** asks Medbot for you.
- **Request a new time** opens the request form.

## Offers from your clinic

Some services, such as a specialist referral, offer you an appointment instead of waiting for you to ask. Offers appear on **Appointment requests** under **From your clinic**.

The clinic tells you about an offer by SMS or WhatsApp. That message only carries a link. The date, time and place are only shown in the portal. Home also shows a card, for example "Sunrise Diabetes Service has offered you an appointment" with an **Open** button.

If the clinic invited you and you have not confirmed your link to it yet, you see "Confirm your link with" the clinic "to see the appointment it has offered you." Select **Confirm** first. Only the patient can do this, not a caregiver.

### Answering an offer

The offer shows:

- the service
- the date and time, in Singapore time
- the location and address
- "With" the practitioner's name "or the team"

Select **Confirm this appointment** to accept it.

If the date does not suit you, select **I cannot make this date**. Under **Tell us the days that suit you**:

1. Choose one or more **Days**, Mon to Sat.
2. Choose a **Time of day**: **Mornings**, **Afternoons** or **Any time**.
3. Optionally add a note under **Anything else** (up to 500 characters).
4. Select **Send my preferred days**, or **Keep the offered date** to go back.

Sending your days releases the offered slot. The offer then reads "The clinic is finding another date for you" and repeats the days you chose. You get another message with a link when a new offer is ready.

### Once you confirm

The offer shows **Confirmed**, with a reminder to arrive 15 minutes early and what to bring. It stays on the screen until shortly after the appointment time.

If the service has a pre-visit check-in, a **Complete your check-in** button appears. Once you have sent it, the offer shows "Check-in sent to your care team".

### If something goes wrong

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "Choose at least one day that suits you." | Pick at least one day before sending. |
| "There is no offered slot to confirm." | The offer has changed. Reload the screen. |
| "This offer can no longer be changed." | The offer was already confirmed or replaced. |

## Pre-visit check-in

Before a confirmed appointment, some services ask how you have been, so your doctor can read your answers before the visit. Open it with **Complete your check-in** on the confirmed offer, or from the card on Home: "Tell your doctor how you have been before your visit".

The **Before your visit** screen has five short questions. It takes about two minutes. Today the questions are written for diabetes care:

1. How you have been feeling since your referral
2. Any low sugar episodes in the last two weeks
3. Your lowest and highest fasting readings this week, if you have them (optional, numbers between 1 and 35 mmol/L)
4. Whether you are taking your medicines as prescribed
5. Anything else you want the doctor to know (optional)

A note on the screen reminds you that this is a quick check, not a diagnosis. If you feel unwell now, call the emergency number shown or go to the nearest emergency department.

Select **Send to my care team**. You see "Thank you, your answers were sent". Your doctor reads them before the visit.

If you have not answered, you get a reminder message about 3 days before the visit, and another about 2 days before. Its link opens **Appointment requests**. If you still have not answered a day before the visit, clinic staff are asked to contact you by phone. You can still answer in the portal until the visit.

If an answer is not accepted, the screen tells you why:

- "Please answer the three questions with choices."
- "Readings should be numbers between 1 and 35, like 6.8."
- "The lowest reading cannot be higher than the highest."

## Telling Medbot about your visit

When you book a visit with a service that asks for it, Medbot invites you to answer a few questions beforehand, so your care team can read them before you arrive. This is covered on its own page: [Pre-visit History](/patient/pre-visit-history).

## Caregivers

A [caregiver](/patient/caregivers) sees the same Visits screens for the person they care for.

| | Read-only | Can make changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See visits, visit summaries and steps from a visit | Yes | Yes |
| See the queue tracker | Yes | Yes |
| Turn on **Notify me when it is my turn** | No | Yes |
| Register when you arrive | No | Yes |
| Book with a service, or cancel a booking | No | Yes |
| Make an appointment request, or accept or decline a proposed time | No | Yes |
| Answer an offer from the clinic | No | Yes |
| Fill in a pre-visit check-in | No | Yes |
| Confirm a link to a clinic | No | No, only the patient |

## Notifications

Visits sends these notifications to the bell on Home:

- **It is your turn**, if you turned on **Notify me when it is my turn**
- **Your visit summary is ready**

For the full list, see [Home screen and notifications](/patient/ask-medbot#home-screen-and-notifications).

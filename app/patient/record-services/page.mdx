# Record Services

Record services are paid services where the Medbot team helps with your health record. You pay once for each request. You approve every change before it reaches your record.

Open them from **Profile**, then **Preferences**, then **Record services**. Only the patient can buy them. A caregiver sees "Only the patient can buy record services." If the page says "Record services are not available yet", check back later.

## What you can ask for

The page is headed "Let the Medbot team do it". Choose one or more.

| Service | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Upload my documents** | You send or post your paper and digital records. An administrator scans, uploads and labels each one. |
| **Reconcile my history** | An administrator compares everything in your record, merges duplicates and lists conflicts, like two different doses, for you to settle. |
| **Get my NEHR record** | Shows **In development**. You cannot buy it yet. |

Each shows how long it takes, in working days, and any page limit.

## Request one

1. Choose the services you want.
2. Optionally add anything the team should know.
3. Tick the consent: you allow a Medbot administrator to view your health record for this request and prepare changes. You review every change before it is added.
4. Select **Continue to payment** and pay.

You can cancel for a full refund until the team starts work. Only the administrator assigned to your request can see your records, and only until it is ready for your review. Every view is listed on your request.

## Follow your request

Your requests are listed on the page. Open one to see **Request status**. It moves through: Paid and sent, Medbot administrator assigned, Administrator prepares your history, Ready for your review, and You review each change.

The status shows as Awaiting payment, Paid, In progress, Ready for your review, Completed, Cancelled, Payment not completed or Refunded.

From a request you can:

- **Attach files for the team**. For paper records, the page shows an address to post them to. Write the reference on the envelope. Originals are returned by post.
- **Review the changes**, once it is ready. Approve what is right.
- **Cancel request**. Before payment, you have not been charged. After payment, you can cancel and get a full refund to the way you paid, until work starts. Select **Keep it** to go back.

**Who has accessed your record** lists each time the team looked at it. At first it says "No one from the Medbot team has accessed your record yet."

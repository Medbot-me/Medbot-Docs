# Results & Records

The **Results** tab is where you read your test results and the rest of your health record. Records show which clinic they came from.

## Results list

Reports are listed newest first. Use **All**, **Laboratory** or **Imaging** to filter. If the clinic has asked you to do something about a result, it shows above the list.

Each report has a badge: **Normal**, **Abnormal** or **Critical**.

## When you see a result

You only see a result once the clinic has released it.

- A result is not shown as good or bad until a doctor has signed it off.
- A **Critical** flag is held back while the clinic is still trying to reach you, so you hear it from a person first.
- **What this means** is a note written by your clinician for you. It is not staff shorthand.

If a report says "Your doctor hasn't reviewed this report yet. It will be explained here in plain language once they have.", check again later. If the clinic withdrew a report, it says so. Message the clinic if you have questions.

## Opening a report

A report shows its badge, **What this means**, the clinic that issued it, and any follow-up. After review, you also see each individual test result. Each shows **Normal**, **High**, **Low**, **Critically high**, **Critically low** or **Abnormal**. You can go back to the list, message the clinic, or ask for an appointment from here.

A report your doctor has explained shows a **Reviewed by your doctor** badge and a link, **Read your doctor's full explanation**.

## Your doctor's explanation

Sometimes your doctor writes a plain-language explanation of your results and approves it. You find it in three places:

- A notification: **Your doctor has explained your results**, or **Your doctor updated the explanation of your results**.
- A section on the Results tab called **Explained by your doctor**. Each row says "Explained by your doctor on" and a date.
- The link on a report, **Read your doctor's full explanation**.

The page is called **Your test results, explained**. It shows the clinic and "Reviewed and approved by" your doctor and the date. It has these parts:

- **In short**
- **Your results**: each test with your result, the normal range, and whether you are below, within or above the range. It also shows if you are up, down or the same as last time.
- **What each test measures**
- **What your results mean**
- **What to do next**: a checklist. Ticks stay on your device and are not sent to the clinic. It also has **Healthy habits**.
- **When to get help sooner**: warning signs. "In an emergency, call 995 now."
- **Learn more**

At the bottom you can choose **Download PDF**, **Ask Medbot about this explanation**, or **Message the clinic**.

If your clinic offers other languages, a language switcher appears on the page. A computer translation says "Translated by computer from the English your doctor approved." A checked translation says "Translation reviewed by bilingual clinic staff."

If your doctor replaces an explanation, old links open the new one. If the clinic withdraws one, the row says **Explanation withdrawn**. The page says not to rely on it, with a **Message the clinic** button.

## More from your record

Under **More from your record**, the Results tab lists:

| Row | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Conditions** | Diagnoses, and the history you added yourself |
| **Medicines** | Prescriptions and medicines you take |
| **Allergies** | Allergies on your record |
| **Results & measurements** | Individual results, vitals and readings |
| **Immunisations** | Vaccines the clinic recorded |
| **Documents & letters** | Letters, referrals and uploaded files |
| **Session summaries** | Summaries of your guided care sessions. See [Health Records](/patient/health-records). |

Things you add yourself, for example with [Medical History](/patient/medical-history), are marked as reported by you until a clinician confirms them. Each row says which clinic it came from, and whether that clinic is still adding to your record.

## Download my summary

**Download my summary** saves your whole record as an International Patient Summary. This is a standard document you can give to another clinician. If you keep a [health plan](/patient/health-plan), its goals appear in the summary's **Plan of Care** section.

It covers your problems, immunisations, results and procedures. Medicines and allergies are combined across all your clinics, so a medicine recorded at two clinics appears once.

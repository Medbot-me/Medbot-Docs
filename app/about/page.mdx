# About Us

Medbot is made by Medbot AI Pte Ltd, in Singapore. It has three apps: the **Medbot app** for clinicians, researchers and care teams, **Medbot Health** for patients and families, and **Medbot CMS** for running a clinic.

## Pricing

Medbot is free to start, with pay-as-you-go credits. Paid plans add monthly credits, clinic organisations, research teams and support. See [medbot.me/pricing](https://www.medbot.me/pricing) and [Plans & Billing](/docs/plans-and-billing).

## Privacy

Medbot's privacy policy is at [medbot.me/privacy](https://www.medbot.me/privacy), linked from the **Legal** column of the site footer and from the app store listings. It covers what Medbot collects (including data from connected devices), how it is used, how AI features process it, when it is shared, consent and how to withdraw it, security and breach notification, how long it is kept, overseas transfers, your rights, and how to contact Medbot's Data Protection Officer.
